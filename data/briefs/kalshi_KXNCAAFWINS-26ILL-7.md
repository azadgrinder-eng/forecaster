# Research Brief: Will Illinois win at least 7 regular-season games in 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts for YES:** Illinois must have 7 or more wins in official 2026 regular-season games. Bowl games, the Big Ten Championship and College Football Playoff games do not count. Ties don't count as wins, but FBS games go to overtime, so a tie is practically impossible.
- **Where Illinois stands:** Illinois is **6–3 (4–2 Big Ten)**. It became bowl-eligible by beating Nebraska 24–13 on Nov 6 (ESPN recap, Nov 6, 2026; Illinois Athletics, Nov 6, 2026). **It needs one win from its three remaining games.**
- **Games left:**
  - Fri Nov 13 (tonight) at No. 4 UCLA, Rose Bowl.
  - Sat Nov 21 vs. Iowa, at home.
  - Sat Nov 28 at Northwestern.
  - Dec 5 is an open date (Ourlads; Fox32 preview).
- **Timing:** The regular season ends Nov 28, well before the market closes on Dec 20, 2026.
- **Who decides:** Kalshi resolves the market from official results.
- **Edge cases:**
  - If a game is cancelled and not made up, Illinois has fewer chances to get the win.
  - A forfeit or vacated win would raise the question of what counts as "official." That is very unlikely.
  - Kickoff for the UCLA game is listed as either 9 p.m. ET or 8 p.m. CT, depending on the source. Either way it finishes after 02:00 UTC on Nov 14, so its result may not be known when this forecast is made.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **Each remaining game, judged by the betting spread:**
  - **At UCLA:** UCLA is favored by about 9.5–10 points (DraftKings opened at 12.5; Covers and CBS list −9.5/−10). SP+ projects UCLA 32, Illinois 17 (Bettors Insider, Nov 12, 2026). Historically, a 10-point underdog wins outright about 20–25% of the time (my estimate).
  - **Vs. Iowa at home:** Iowa is 7–2, possibly ranked (ABC7 called it "No. 21 Iowa" before the Northwestern game). It allows only about 12.5 points per game and beat Northwestern 24–10 on Nov 7. Iowa hosts Purdue (3–6) on Nov 14 as a 16.5-point favorite (Dimers; BettorEdge).
  - **At Northwestern:** Northwestern is 6–3 (3–3 Big Ten) but has lost two straight. It plays at Ohio State on Nov 14 and at Minnesota on Nov 21 before hosting Illinois (Sports-Reference; Wikipedia).
  - **ESPN's FPI:** As of about Nov 5, with Illinois at 5–3, FPI expected Illinois to be the underdog in **all four** of its final games. It gave Illinois a 72.5% chance of reaching six wins (247Sports preview, ~Nov 5, 2026). Illinois then beat Nebraska as an underdog.
  - **Rough calculation:** Suppose Illinois has about a 23% chance at UCLA, 35–45% against Iowa and 40–50% at Northwestern, and the games are independent. Then it gets at least one win about 70–80% of the time. FPI's "underdog in all four" view points to the lower half of that range. Results are somewhat correlated (injuries, team quality), which lowers the estimate slightly.
- **Bowl-eligible 6–3 teams with three games left:** In the Power 4, it is common for such a team to finish 6–6 in a tough closing stretch, but most get at least one more win. A rough base rate of 75–85% is my judgment, not a counted figure.
- **Illinois under Bielema:** The program has won 19 games over the previous two seasons. The 2025 team went 9–4 (Wikipedia 2025 page; Yahoo). That suggests a stable, competent program, not one prone to collapse.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 13, 2026:** Illinois plays at UCLA (9–0, 6–0, No. 4) at the Rose Bowl on Fox. The line is UCLA −9.5 to −10, with an over/under of 47.5 (CBS; Covers). UCLA's run defense ranks 10th nationally at 87.7 yards per game (Daily Bruin, Nov 11). I found no final score yet.
- **Illinois injuries this week (247Sports, Champaign Room, ~Nov 12):**
  - RB Javari Barnett is out for the season after leg surgery.
  - RB Aidan Laughery is out 4–6 weeks, which likely covers the rest of the regular season.
  - C Jake Renfro (questionable) and DL Will Holmes (limited in practice) were both hurt against Nebraska.
  - Transfer WRs Alex Perry and Jayshon Platt are "pretty much done" for the year.
  - QB Michael Clayton II has a bone bruise in his hand and has a "good chance" to return at some point.
  - Running back depth is thin.
- **UCLA injuries:** UCLA will be without RT Hall Schmidt, WR Jalen Saint Paul, DL Ashton Sanders and edge rusher Ryan McCulloch.
- **QB situation, which is unclear:** Katin Houser has 1,955 yards, 16 TDs and 5 INTs, and threw for 292 yards and 3 TDs against Nebraska. One report says he broke his throwing hand in October and was upgraded from out to doubtful at the time. He clearly played on Nov 6, so this is probably an older note.
- **Nov 14 (Sat):** Iowa hosts Purdue as a 16.5-point favorite. Northwestern plays at Ohio State.
- **Nov 7:** Iowa beat Northwestern 24–10, moving Iowa to 7–2 and dropping Northwestern to 6–3.
- **Nov 6:** Illinois beat Nebraska 24–13 at home for its fourth straight win and bowl eligibility. WR Collin Dixon caught two TDs, giving him 12 for the season, a school record (ESPN; Yahoo).
- **Season path:** Illinois beat UAB 42–23 and Southern Illinois. It lost at home to Duke 31–27 on Sept 12 (an upset), lost at Ohio State 42–19, and lost a close home game to Purdue, falling to 2–3. Defensive coordinator Bobby Hauck left after the Ohio State game (SI; Yahoo). Illinois then won four straight, including a win at Maryland on Oct 31 (opening line Illinois −2.5) and the Nebraska win.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **Path to YES (one win needed):**
  - An upset at UCLA tonight. UCLA's missing players and a possible letdown help Illinois; UCLA's elite run defense against a depleted Illinois backfield hurts.
  - A home win over Iowa. This is a low-scoring matchup where Iowa's defense is strong, and it is likely close to a coin flip with Illinois slightly behind.
  - A win at Northwestern in the rivalry game. Northwestern is fading (two straight losses, with Ohio State next) and may be worn down. Its home venue is unclear because Ryan Field is being rebuilt, so home-field advantage may be weaker than usual.
- **Path to NO:** Illinois loses all three. That most likely happens if injuries pile up (QB, center, defensive line, running backs) and Illinois plays like the team that went 2–3 early, not the one that won four straight.
- **What it depends on:** QB health (Houser and Clayton), the running back shortage, whether Renfro and Holmes return, how Iowa and Northwestern come out of their Nov 14 games, and the Nov 21 and Nov 28 betting lines.

## 5. Signposts
- **UCLA result tonight:** A win settles the market as YES. A competitive loss (within a touchdown) is mildly positive for later games. A blowout or a new QB injury is negative.
- **Opening lines for Iowa (around Nov 15) and Northwestern (around Nov 22):** If Illinois is favored in either game, the probability goes up a lot.
- **Iowa vs. Purdue and Northwestern at Ohio State/Minnesota:** A Northwestern collapse to 6–5 or worse, or Northwestern injuries, helps Illinois. An Iowa loss to Purdue would be a sign that Iowa is weaker than its record.
- **Injury reports:** Watch Houser, Clayton, Renfro, Holmes and the running back depth chart.
- **Weather:** Late-November games in the Midwest favor Iowa's low-scoring style.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not get final results or scores for all nine games; most pages wouldn't load, so this relies on search snippets. The order of wins in Weeks 6–9 is unconfirmed. The 6–3 (4–2) record is confirmed by several sources.
- I could not get current FPI or SP+ win probabilities for the Iowa and Northwestern games, and those lines aren't posted yet.
- Houser's health is unclear (reports of a broken hand versus a 292-yard game on Nov 6).
- Northwestern's 2026 home venue and its home-field advantage are unconfirmed.
