# Research brief: Will Perplexity's NPM valuation reach $25B or more by Dec 31, 2026?

*Method note: every page fetch was blocked by the network proxy, so all figures below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. I could not open the NPM resolution page itself. Treat exact numbers as approximate.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES condition:** Perplexity's valuation, as measured by the NPM Price that Nasdaq Private Market publishes, is at or above $25B on any trading day from market creation through Dec 31, 2026. One print is enough because the question asks about the period high, not the year-end close.
- **Timing:** NPM publishes prices for trading days only and updates them at 1:00 PM ET the next calendar day. If data is late, the market can stay open until Jan 4, 2027. Later revisions are ignored unless they correct clearly erroneous data.
- **Source:** the NPM company data page at fe.secondmarket.com.
- **IPO path:** this path is effectively irrelevant. The CEO has only said he "won't rule out" an IPO *filing* in Q1 2027 (FourWeekMBA, ~Nov 16–17, 2026). A listing before Dec 31 is essentially impossible.
- **Key ambiguity: NPM publishes a per-share price, but the question is about a dollar valuation.** The valuation equals the NPM Price times NPM's own estimate of fully diluted shares, and NPM does not disclose that count (NPM Data & Intelligence page). Two ways to back out the count:
  - From NPM's own numbers: a third-party analysis tied an NPM mark of about $61 per share to about $17.9B (Sep 3, 2026). That implies roughly 293M shares. On that basis $25B needs about **$85 per share**.
  - From Forge's round data: Forge lists the new round at $106.90 per share and $30B post-money, which implies about 281M shares post-money. That count is lower than the NPM-derived one, so the two sources disagree.
  - **If NPM updates its share count after the new round**, adding roughly 28–56M shares, the implied valuation rises even if the per-share price stays flat. For example, 321–349M shares × $72.91 ≈ $23.4–25.5B. This is a real mechanical path to YES that I could not confirm.
- **Unknown creation date:** the window opens at market creation, which I don't know. NPM appears to have stayed well below $25B through 2026 (see section 3), so earlier dates probably add little.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **How NPM moves after a large priced up-round becomes public.** Other platforms reacted quickly once the Series F appeared in charter filings:
   - Forge's price jumped 53.7% to $106.90, matching the round price exactly (Forge, Nov 18, 2026). Forge's CNBC quote still showed $60.00 on Nov 12, so Forge picked up the round between Nov 12 and Nov 18.
   - Hiive's indicative price rose to $78.36 (Nov 18), from roughly $62–71 in Aug–Sep 2026.
   - NPM blends primary rounds, secondary trades, bids and offers, 409A valuations, and mutual-fund marks (NPM Data & Intelligence). It has historically sat at the low end of the platforms: $59.76 on NPM vs $69.50 on Forge and $71.54 on Hiive in early September 2026 (meikuio).
   - So NPM probably moves only partway toward the new round price. I found no NPM-specific base rate for this.
2. **NPM's own volatility.** It moved from about $59.76–61 (Sep 3–4) to $74.22 (Oct 9), a gain of about 21–24% in five weeks. It then eased to $72.91 (Nov 3). YES needs roughly +15–20% from the Nov 3 level within about six weeks, a move NPM has made recently.
   - Illustrative calculation: with ~60% annualized volatility and no drift, the chance that the running maximum over six weeks rises 17% or more is about 40%. A positive drift from the new round would push that higher.
3. **Perplexity's secondary price vs its last round.** Through most of 2026, NPM and other secondary prices sat at or below the $20B round (about $69.54 per share), even while run-rate revenue roughly quadrupled. That points to a persistent discount, especially on NPM.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES:**
- **NPM picks up the $30B round.** If NPM records the Series F from charter filings, Form D, or press coverage, its blended price could move toward $85–107. Even a 25–35% weighting toward the round price would clear about $85 per share.
- **NPM updates its share count.** Adding the new F-1/F-2 shares could lift the implied valuation above $25B even with a modest per-share move.
- **Official confirmation.** A Perplexity, Nvidia, or press announcement would add another public input to NPM's model, on top of the $1B run-rate news and IPO talk that already support higher marks.

**Paths to NO:**
- NPM lags or discounts the round heavily, as its consistent low position among the platforms suggests. It then drifts only to about $76–82 per share, roughly $22–24B.
- The Series F turns out to be a misread filing or a tranche-based or structured deal, and NPM ignores it.
- Only about six weeks of trading days remain, and the holidays bring thin activity.
- The broader AI market cools, for example if the Anthropic IPO (reported around mid-November) trades poorly.

## 5. Signposts
- **Most decisive:** the NPM price prints for Nov 4–20. A move above about $80 per share points strongly to YES. Staying in the $72–75 range points to NO.
- **Up:** a Reuters, Bloomberg, Information, or company confirmation of the $30B round or of Nvidia's participation.
- **Up:** a Form D filing for the Series F.
- **Up:** Hiive and other indicative prices continuing to rise toward $90 or more.
- **Down:** reports that the round is structured, for example with preferred terms or tranches, or that the price is inflated.
- **Down:** new negative news, such as lawsuits, slowing growth, or a weak Anthropic IPO.

## 6. Uncertainties
- The current NPM price and NPM's implied valuation since Nov 3. I could not access NPM's data directly.
- NPM's share count, and therefore the exact per-share price needed for $25B. It is roughly $83–89, or lower if NPM adds the new shares to its count.
- Whether the Series F actually closed and on what terms. Even Forge says it is unconfirmed.
- How strongly NPM's algorithm weights a new primary round, and how fast it reacts.
- The market's creation date, and whether NPM ever printed above $25B earlier in 2026. That seems unlikely given the figures above.
