# Research brief: Will Apple be the world's largest company by market cap at the close on Nov 30, 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Apple has the highest market cap of any company in the world at market close on **Monday, November 30, 2026**. The market resolves to whichever company is #1 then, and this question is YES only if that company is Apple. The deadline (2026-12-01 04:59 UTC) is 11:59 pm ET on Nov 30, so the regular U.S. close at 4:00 pm ET is the snapshot that matters.
- **Who decides:** "a consensus of credible reporting." In practice that means trackers like CompaniesMarketCap, Bloomberg, Reuters, CNBC and the Motley Fool rankings.
- **Calendar:** About 13 trading sessions remain (Nov 12–30). U.S. markets are closed on Thanksgiving (Thu Nov 26) and close early on Fri Nov 27.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Share counts differ by source.** Apple's cap is quoted anywhere from $5.06T to $5.16T on the same days. This only matters if the gap to #2 shrinks to under about 2–3%.
  - **Intraday vs. close.** Several recent lead changes, such as Microsoft passing Nvidia on Nov 10, happened intraday only. The question uses the close.
  - **Private companies** (e.g., OpenAI) are effectively excluded because they have no market cap. SpaceX now appears in public rankings, but well below the top three.
  - **Weak-consensus scenario.** If two companies are within about 1% of each other, sources could disagree on #1. That risk is negligible unless the gap collapses.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **(a) Persistence of a large #1 lead over about 3 weeks.** I could not compile a full historical dataset. From known episodes since about 2018 (Apple/Microsoft/Aramco/Nvidia), every #1 change happened when the top two were within roughly 0–10% of each other. Examples:
  - Microsoft passed Apple in Jan 2024 with a gap of under 3%.
  - Nvidia and Apple traded places repeatedly in 2024–2026 with gaps of a few percent.
  - Apple retook #1 from Nvidia on Oct 30, 2026 at $5.03T vs $4.93T, a 2% gap.

  I found no case of a #1 with a lead of more than 20% losing the top spot within 3 weeks.
- **(b) Size of the move needed.** Apple's lead today is about 25–33% over the next company (see §3), so a flip needs a relative swing of roughly 22–28% in log terms within about 13 sessions.
  - **Apple vs. Microsoft:** typical relative volatility is about 25–30% a year, which is roughly ±6–7% over 13 sessions. A 24% swing would be about 3.5–4 standard deviations, around 0.1% under a normal distribution, or perhaps 1% allowing for fat tails.
  - **Apple vs. Nvidia:** relative volatility is now much higher, about 50% a year or more, giving roughly ±11% over the period. Adding an earnings jump of about ±7–8% gives a combined spread of about ±13%. The roughly 28% needed is then about 2+ standard deviations, around 1–3% before adjusting for fat tails.
- **(c) Largest single-name mega-cap moves in recent history.** Nvidia fell about 17% in one day on Jan 27, 2025 (DeepSeek). Nvidia has gained about 15–25% across a few weeks after strong earnings. Apple's worst multi-week declines outside crashes have been about 10–15%. Microsoft rarely moves more than 10% in a month. It is plausible but rare for one stock to gain 20% while another falls 10% within 3 weeks.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**YES (Apple stays #1).** This is the default. It only requires that no rival makes up a lead of roughly $1.0–1.3T in about 13 sessions.
- A Huang ouster or prolonged uncertainty keeps NVDA depressed.
- Microsoft and Alphabet have no catalyst before Nov 30.
- Apple benefits as a "low-AI-capex safe haven."

**NO paths, roughly in order of plausibility:**
1. **Nvidia comeback.** The board clears or keeps Huang (a relief rally of perhaps +8–12%), then a blowout Nov 17 report and guidance adds another +10–15%, while Apple pulls back 5–10% on rotation back into AI. This needs every piece to go right. Starting from about $3.9T, Nvidia needs about +30% if Apple is flat.
2. **Apple-specific shock.** A sharp derating of 15–20% from an antitrust ruling (e.g., the Google search-payments remedy), tariffs or China, an iPhone 18 demand scare, or an executive or regulatory event, combined with a rival rally. Nothing like this is scheduled.
3. **Microsoft surge plus Apple decline.** Needs about a 24% relative move with no scheduled catalyst. Very unlikely.
4. **Data or reporting ambiguity.** Not relevant unless the gap falls below about 2%.

## 5. Signposts
- **Nvidia board outcome (biggest near-term mover):**
  - Huang retained or exonerated → NVDA rebounds and the gap narrows (a modest move toward NO).
  - Huang ousted with a messy succession → the gap widens (toward YES).
- **NVDA price after the Nov 17 earnings:** a jump of more than 15% would put Nvidia within striking distance and is the key NO signpost. A miss or weak guidance makes YES nearly certain.
- **Apple-specific news:** a large one-day drop (more than 5%) on regulatory, tariff or China news.
- **Gap tracking:** watch the ratio of Apple's cap to the #2 company's on CompaniesMarketCap. Below 1.10 (a 10% gap) with a week left, NO becomes realistic. Above 1.20 with 5 sessions left, YES is near-locked.
- **Broad AI rotation:** a CoreWeave-style rally in the AI trade (Nov 11) helps Nvidia and Microsoft relative to Apple.
