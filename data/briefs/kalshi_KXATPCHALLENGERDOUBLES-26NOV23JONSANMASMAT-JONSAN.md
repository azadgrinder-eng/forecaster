### 2. Reference classes and base rates
These rates are approximate, from my general knowledge of Challenger doubles. I could not verify them this session.
- **Wildcard teams in Challenger doubles first rounds:** Wildcards usually go to young local players with little doubles ranking. They typically win about 30–40% of first-round matches. The rate is higher when the opponents are also low-ranked local or alternate teams rather than seeded tour doubles specialists.
- **Better-ranked team in Challenger doubles:** The team with the better combined ranking wins about 60–65% of the time. That is lower than in singles because of no-ad scoring and the match tiebreak.
- **All-Japanese pairings at Japanese Challengers:** Matches between two lower-ranked home teams are close to a coin flip. They lean to whichever team has more Challenger doubles experience.
