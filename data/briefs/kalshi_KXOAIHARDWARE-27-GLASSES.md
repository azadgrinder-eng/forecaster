# Research Brief: What device will Jony Ive and OpenAI announce first? ("Glasses" outcome)

*Prepared 2026-11-26. I only had search-result summaries to work from, because every attempt to open a full article failed at the proxy. The details below come from those summaries plus a few facts I already knew, which are marked as such.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Before Jan 1, 2027, OpenAI announces its **first** hardware device from the Ive/io collaboration, and that device is **glasses**. If one device fits several categories, every matching category resolves YES. Glasses with built-in audio would therefore count as glasses.
- **Deadline:** The announcement must happen before 2027-01-01. The market closes 2027-01-01 15:00 UTC, so about **5 weeks remain**.
- **Who decides:** The market operator interprets "announces" and "first hardware device." The rules don't define either term.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Announced, not shipped.** The rules say "announces," so a reveal in December 2026 would count even though shipping is expected in 2027 or later.
  - **Roadmap mentions.** If OpenAI reveals a speaker and also says glasses are coming later, a strict reading gives NO, because the question is about the *first* device. A loose resolver might still count it. This is a small but real risk.
  - **Codex Micro.** This $230 macropad, made with Work Louder and on sale since July 15, 2026, is OpenAI-branded hardware but not an io/Ive product, and it isn't glasses. It shouldn't affect this outcome (Axios, 2026-07-15; 9to5Mac, 2026-07-15).
  - **No announcement by Jan 1, 2027.** Then glasses resolves NO, along with every other category.
  - **Name.** OpenAI has said it won't use the "io" brand (sworn court filing, Feb 2026). The product name doesn't matter for resolution.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Reported first form factor vs. actual first product, for well-sourced Bloomberg/The Information hardware leaks about a product's category.** Leaks this close to launch, from several outlets about a named product with named leads, are usually right on category. Gurman's leaks on Apple product categories within roughly 6 months of launch have very rarely been wrong. Every outlet now reports the first io device as a **speaker**, and puts glasses in **2028 or later**. **Base rate: glasses as the first device is well under 5%.**
2. **Whether the reveal happens before the end of the year at all, given the company's own targets and slips.** OpenAI first aimed to ship in 2026. A February 2026 court filing pushed shipping to "not before end of February 2027." By late November there was still no reveal. Big-tech hardware reveals that are still unscheduled with five weeks left in the target year usually slip. My rough estimate is that **30–50%** of them still happen in time. This only affects whether *any* category resolves YES.
3. **AI companies' first consumer devices, 2023–2025.** Humane chose a pin, Rabbit a handheld, Meta (not AI-first) glasses, Friend and Limitless pendants, and Sesame glasses. Only about 1 in 5 or 6 was glasses. Even so, the device-specific reporting below matters far more than this prior.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

- **NO, speaker revealed (most likely):** OpenAI unveils the speaker or companion in December, or the reveal slips into 2027. Either way glasses resolves NO.
- **NO, no reveal before Jan 1:** The Apple litigation, the Feb 2027 or later ship date, and no marketing materials as of February all make a slip plausible. Every category resolves NO.
- **YES paths (all low probability):**
  1. The reporting is wrong and glasses come first. This would contradict sworn filings saying the first device isn't a wearable, plus several Bloomberg and The Information reports.
  2. OpenAI announces a family of devices with glasses among them and the resolver counts it.
  3. The speaker turns out to be wearable or eyewear-like, which is very unlikely given the puck/doughnut descriptions.

## 5. Signposts

- **Big move toward NO:** An official speaker reveal, or confirmation that no reveal is coming in 2026.
- **Move toward YES:** An official event invite or teaser showing eyewear, or credible reporting (Gurman, The Information) that glasses were moved ahead of the speaker.
- **Only a small move toward YES:** A multi-device reveal that explicitly shows glasses, since this mainly creates resolution ambiguity.
- **Timing only:** Court rulings in Apple v. OpenAI, especially a preliminary injunction, affect whether any reveal happens in time, not which category wins.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I couldn't confirm what, if anything, was said about hardware at DevDay on Sep 29, 2026, or whether any news broke in Oct–Nov 2026.
