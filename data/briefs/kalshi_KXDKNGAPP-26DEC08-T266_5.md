# Research Brief: Will Carbon Arc's DraftKings app downloads index for November 2026 be above 266.5?

**Bottom line:** YES needs November 2026 DraftKings downloads to be more than **2.665×** November 2025, a 166.5% year-over-year rise. Standalone sportsbook apps (each operator's betting app on its own, without other products folded in) shrank year over year through 2025. A YES would therefore have to come from new demand: the nationwide "Super App" with prediction markets, the Predictions app, and heavy marketing. I could not find any published Carbon Arc index value for recent months, which is the most important missing input.

Research limits: every WebFetch call was refused by the network proxy, so this brief relies on search-result summaries rather than full articles. I did not look up or report any market prices.

---

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Carbon Arc's "DraftKings app downloads (November 2026)" index must be **strictly above 266.5**. The index compares a month with the same month a year earlier, where 100 means no change. So 266.5 means November 2026 downloads equal 2.665× November 2025. A value of exactly 266.5 resolves NO.
- **Which value counts:** the "finalized" Carbon Arc value recorded at **11:59 p.m. ET on Dec 7, 2026**. Kalshi resolves the market and Carbon Arc supplies the data. Kalshi also uses Carbon Arc data for other DraftKings/FanDuel download markets and for sector-wide indices (iGaming Times DKNG profile, Oct 23, 2026; Kalshi/Markets Media announcement of sector KPI contracts).
- **Ambiguities:**
  - **Which apps are counted.** The rules don't say whether "DraftKings app downloads" means only the flagship "DraftKings: Sports & Casino" app or the sum of all DraftKings apps (Sportsbook/Super App, Fantasy/DFS, Predictions, Casino, Jackpocket). This matters a lot. The Predictions app didn't exist in November 2025 (it launched Dec 19, 2025), so if it is counted, everything it adds is pure growth.
  - **Data provider and coverage.** Carbon Arc's source (a panel or a download estimator?), whether it covers iOS, Android, or both, and whether it is US-only are not stated.
  - **Revisions.** The base month (November 2025) may have been revised, and "finalized" values can differ from preliminary ones.
  - **Not available here:** past Kalshi/Carbon Arc settlement values for DraftKings.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Sportsbook app download growth, 2025 to early 2026 (Apptopia):**
   - DraftKings downloads fell **16.0%** in full-year 2025 (7.43M). All four major sportsbooks were down year over year in Q4 2025, with DraftKings at 2.2M against Kalshi's 3.8M (Apptopia investor note).
   - During the January 2026 NFL playoffs, DraftKings was the only major operator growing, at about **+1%** year over year (NEXT.io).
   - A later Apptopia weekly note showed DraftKings downloads at **−21.9%** year over year (the note's date is unclear).
   - On the standalone-app trend alone, an index of about 85–105 would be normal. A reading above 266 would be unprecedented for a mature sportsbook app outside a big new-state launch.
2. **New-market launch spikes:**
   - In past state launches (Ohio and Massachusetts in 2023, North Carolina in 2024, Missouri on Dec 1, 2025), state-level downloads went up many times over.
   - National downloads usually rose only tens of percent in the launch month.
   - The Super App is different. Because it brings a sports-like product to CA, TX, FL, GA and other non-sportsbook states (about 100% more addressable population, per SGI Europe), it resembles one very large new-state launch. A national 2–3× jump is plausible only in the first months after that launch.
3. **Prediction-market app growth (upper bound):**
   - Kalshi grew downloads several-fold year over year through 2025 and early 2026: about 3M+ in January 2026 (Apptopia), and it led the February 2026 download race (CLEATZ).
   - Even so, DraftKings' own prediction-market apps started slowly. FanDuel's and DraftKings' prediction apps together had fewer than 100K downloads in January 2026, against Kalshi's 1.9M (Sensor Tower via TradingView/Finance Magnates).
   - Later, DraftKings Predictions took about **12%** of prediction-market app downloads over a trailing seven days, up from 11% (Macquarie via search snippet; date unclear).

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 2026:** Google Play shows the DraftKings Predictions app at only **100K+** installs. The flagship Sports & Casino app shows 5M+ on Google Play; one aggregator says 12.5M+ across stores.
  - AltIndex estimates about **3.3K downloads per day**, "down 80% over 3 months." Treat this as low-reliability: it implies about 100K per month, which conflicts with Apptopia's scale of about 600K+ per month.
- **Nov 5, 2026: Q3 earnings.**
  - Revenue was $1.33B (+16%); sports revenue +32%; iGaming +2%.
  - Monthly unique payers were about **4.1M (+13% year over year)**.
  - Sales and marketing spending was **$605.7M (+68%)**, and adjusted EBITDA was −$316.9M.
  - More than **1.6M customers** have used Predictions year to date.
  - The company cut full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $6.35–6.55B to fund **$650–700M of Predictions investment**.
  - Sources: Covers, Nov 5, 2026; Affpapa; StockStory.
  - Heavy acquisition spending in Q4 points to strong November downloads. But payer growth of only +13% is far below a 2.66× download pace.
- **Oct 28, 2026:** Apptopia reported online-sportsbook daily active users "slightly down this football season" (headline only; no download figures found).
- **Sep 17, 2026:** Apptopia said NFL Week 1 pushed sportsbook daily active users above their World Cup peaks.
- **Jun 2026:** Apptopia said the World Cup broke the usual summer lull for betting apps.
  - Sports Illustrated says the combined "Super App" (Sportsbook, Predictions, Casino, Lottery; one wallet) launched **June 26, 2026**.
  - Other coverage from March 2026 said it would launch in March, or "before end of 2026" alongside Railbird, DraftKings' in-house designated contract market.
  - In non-sportsbook states, the app shows sports prediction markets.
  - The exact nationwide go-live date is unconfirmed.
- **Mar 2026:** At investor day, DraftKings announced the Super App to reach customers "everywhere" (Covers, Mar 2, 2026; Legal Sports Report).
- **Feb 2026:** Super Bowl LX. DraftKings had 54,097 downloads on game day (Apptopia).
- **Dec 19, 2025:** the standalone DraftKings Predictions app launched in 38 states, with sports contracts in about 14–17 states including CA, TX, FL and GA.
- **Dec 1, 2025:** Missouri launched online sports betting.
  - Pre-registration in mid/late November 2025 likely lifted the **November 2025 base** somewhat, which makes the index harder to beat.
  - The big Missouri spike (Sensor Tower iOS: about 146K weekly downloads in mid-December 2025) fell in December, not November.
- **Before the close date:** Thanksgiving NFL slate (Nov 26) and Black Friday promotions. No new-state sportsbook launches found for November 2026. Data is finalized Dec 7.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (index above 266.5):**
- **Super App adoption in non-sportsbook states.** If the Super App went national in mid-2026 and Carbon Arc counts it as "DraftKings app downloads," new users in CA, TX, FL, GA and elsewhere could push year-over-year multiples above 2.5×.
- **Predictions app counted.** If Carbon Arc sums all DraftKings apps, the Predictions app adds downloads against a zero November 2025 base.
- **Marketing.** Q3 sales and marketing spending was +68%, and more Predictions spending is guided for Q4. NFL plus college football in November is peak acquisition season.

**Paths to NO:**
- **Sportsbook decline continues.** If Carbon Arc tracks only the flagship app and growth outside sportsbook states is modest, the index likely sits around 80–150.
- **Kalshi and Polymarket competition.** Prediction-market rivals keep taking share of new installs; Kalshi's share of new installs was about 42% in June 2026 (Apptopia).
- **Strong November 2025 base** from Missouri pre-registration.
- **Fading launch effect.** If the Super App launched in March or June 2026, year-over-year growth may have been strongest right after launch and could be lower by November. Each month's index is measured against its own prior-year month, though, so this depends on how fast the new-state user base keeps growing.

## 5. Signposts
- **Carbon Arc's DraftKings index for Sep/Oct 2026.** This is the single most informative input; it is not published openly. If recent months were around 250–300, the strike is near the middle and the question is close to a coin flip. If they were around 100–150, NO is very likely.
  - Inference, not verified: Kalshi often sets several strikes around recent values. A strike as unusual as 266.5 hints that recent readings are well above 100, but it could also be a tail strike.
- **Carbon Arc's methodology** (which apps are included). Learning that Predictions and the Super App are included pushes toward YES; learning that only the flagship app is tracked pushes toward NO.
- **Apptopia or Sensor Tower November download reports**, especially Thanksgiving and Black Friday commentary, and any DraftKings app-store rank jumps in CA or TX.
- **Analyst notes** (Citizens/Bender, Macquarie/Beynon) on DraftKings download trends in Q4 2026.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not get any Carbon Arc DraftKings index value or any past Kalshi settlement value for these markets.
- Which apps Carbon Arc includes, and whether its coverage is iOS-only or US-only, is unknown.
- The exact date the Super App went live nationwide is unclear: sources say March 2026, June 26, 2026, or "by end of 2026."
- Absolute DraftKings downloads for November 2025 are unknown. A rough estimate from Q4 2025 (2.2M total, with a December Missouri spike) is about 600–750K, but that is an inference.
- Third-party estimates conflict: AltIndex's sharp decline doesn't fit with Q3 marketing growth.
