## Research brief: Will *Avengers: Doomsday* have the 2nd-best domestic 3-day opening weekend of 2026?

**Bottom line:** *Doomsday* resolves YES only if its Fri–Sun opening on Dec 18–20 falls between about $160M (*Toy Story 5*, currently #2) and $360.09M (*Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, currently #1). Getting past $160M looks almost certain. The real question is whether it breaks *Brand New Day*'s all-time record, which would push it to #1 and resolve this market NO.

**Note on sourcing:** Every page fetch was refused by the network proxy, including The Numbers, Box Office Mojo, Deadline and Wikipedia. All figures below come from search-result summaries of the named articles, not from reading the primary tables. Treat exact decimals as approximate unless stated otherwise.

### 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What YES needs:** *Avengers: Doomsday* must have the second-highest domestic 3-day (Fri–Sun) opening weekend among 2026 films. The figure comes from the "Weekend Box Office Performance" table on The Numbers, using final actuals rather than studio estimates. Box Office Mojo is the fallback. Ties are broken alphabetically, which is irrelevant at these sizes.
- **The window it has to land in:**
  - *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, released Jul 31, 2026, is #1 at $360,091,572 (reported final figure; Comic Shop Locator / Deadline, Aug 2026).
  - *Toy Story 5*, released Jun 19, 2026, is #2 at about $160M (studio estimate; no revised final found; Variety / AP, Jun 21, 2026).
  - So *Doomsday* is YES if $160M < its opening < $360.09M.
- **Paths to NO:**
  - It opens above $360.09M and becomes #1, leaving *Spider-Man* as #2. This is the main path.
  - It opens below about $160M.
  - It is delayed out of 2026.
- **Timing:**
  - Release is Friday, Dec 18, 2026. Thursday previews count toward Friday.
  - The 3-day window is Dec 18–20. Studio estimates come Sunday, Dec 20, and actuals usually Monday, Dec 21.
  - The market closes Jan 1, 2027. The resolution backstop is Jan 31, 2027.
- **Edge cases:**
  - If *Doomsday* lands near $360M, the estimate and the actual could straddle the record. *Brand New Day* itself went from a $355M Sunday estimate to $360M in Monday actuals (Hollywood Reporter / CNBC, Aug 2–3, 2026). Only the final figure counts.
  - There are no realistic late-December threats to the $160M #2 slot besides *Doomsday*. *Dune: Part Three* (Dec 18) is tracking at about $75M. *Jumanji 3* is likely Dec 25 (the schedule log shows it moving from Dec 11). *Angry Birds 3* is Dec 23.

### 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. Biggest December openings ever (domestic 3-day, from memory of standard charts):**

| Film | Year | Opening |
|---|---|---|
| *Spider-Man: No Way Home* | 2021 | $260.1M |
| *Star Wars: The Force Awakens* | 2015 | $247.97M |
| *The Last Jedi* | 2017 | $220.0M |
| *The Rise of Skywalker* | 2019 | $177.4M |
| *Avatar: The Way of Water* | 2022 | $134.1M |

- No December film has ever opened above about $260M. That is 0 of about 5 mega-tentpoles reaching even $300M.
- Pre-Christmas weekends are softer because many schools are still in session. This class favours YES, since it implies an opening well under $360M.

**B. Early tracking vs. actual openings for Marvel/MCU event films (approximate, partly from memory):**

| Film | Early tracking | Actual | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Endgame* | $200–250M (Variety, 3 weeks out) | $357.1M | 1.43× midpoint |
| *Infinity War* | ~$200–225M | $257.7M | ~1.15–1.29× |
| *No Way Home* | ~$135–150M early | $260.1M | ~1.7–1.9× |
| *Brand New Day* | ~$180M (NRG initial) | $360.1M | ~2.0× (per the reporting on *Doomsday* tracking) |
| *Deadpool & Wolverine* | ~$160M | $211M | ~1.3× |
| *The Marvels* | ~$75–80M | $46M | ~0.6× |

- *Doomsday*'s NRG three-week figure is $270M+. It needs about 1.33× that to pass $360.09M.
- Roughly 3 of the 5–6 biggest event films beat early tracking by 1.33× or more. That points to a meaningful chance (maybe a third to a half within this class) that *Doomsday* takes #1.

**C. Presale benchmarks:**
- *Brand New Day* set the Marvel presale record of about $150M ahead of its $360M opening (Comic Shop Locator, 2026).
- *Doomsday* was reported at more than $100M domestic presales about three weeks out. Deadline-sourced items said it had "not yet caught up to *Endgame* or *Brand New Day*" in running totals (CBR / Particle, ~Nov 25–27, 2026).
- If presales scale roughly with the opening, the ~$100M figure points to an opening in the high-$200Ms to low-$300Ms unless late sales accelerate. But *Doomsday*'s presale window is about 4× longer than *Brand New Day*'s 45 days, so the comparisons are noisy.

### 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **~Nov 26, 2026:** NRG three-week tracking puts *Doomsday* at $270M+ domestic opening. Deadline reported it as NRG's highest initial forecast ever. *Dune: Part Three* tracks at about $75M for the same weekend. Yahoo says *Doomsday* "could climb toward $300M" and is "tracking significantly below *Endgame*" (Main Street Mouse, Nov 26; Particle; Yahoo).
- **~Nov 25–27:** U.S. presales passed $100M three weeks out (CBR; Particle).
- **~mid-Nov:** Box Office Theory projected a $340–410M range (Dork, about two weeks before Nov 26). That range straddles the $360M threshold.
- **Nov 20–22:** *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opened to about $120M; The Numbers lists $119.3M. The Hollywood Reporter called it the #5 domestic opening of 2026 (Variety; THR, Nov 22–23).
  - That implies the current top 5: *Brand New Day* $360.1M, *Toy Story 5* ~$160M, *Super Mario Galaxy* ~$131.7M 3-day, *The Odyssey* ~$124.5M, *Hunger Games* ~$119–120M.
- **~Nov 17:** The Dec 18 date for both *Doomsday* and *Dune 3* is confirmed. *Doomsday* reportedly lost IMAX screens to *Dune 3* (AOL/ScreenRant "losing IMAX" piece). That caps upside, though Disney is leaning on its "Infinity Vision" premium format, which made up about 70% of early presales per Disney's CFO.
- **~Oct–Nov:** Tracker reports put domestic presales at more than $65M with about 50 days to go, and more than $70M with about 35 days to go.
- **Aug 18:** The first two days of general presales reportedly ran 65% ahead of *Brand New Day*'s first two days (TheWrap via Comic Shop Locator / Eastern Eye).
- **Aug 2–3:** *Brand New Day* opened to a record $360,091,572, passing *Endgame*'s $357.1M. Its Friday of $168M and previews of $72M were also records (CNBC; Deadline; THR).
- **Other 2026 openings for context:** *Minions & Monsters* ~$36.4M 3-day (Jul 5); *Moana* live-action ~$43M (Jul 12); *Toy Story 5* $160M (Jun 21); *Super Mario Galaxy* ~$131.7M 3-day (Apr 5).

**Scheduled before close:**
- Final tracking updates and Deadline's preview, around Dec 15–17.
- Thursday previews on Dec 17.
- The opening weekend on Dec 18–20, with the Sunday estimate on Dec 20 and actuals around Dec 21.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios
**YES ($160–360M), the central scenario:**
- December seasonality: no December film has opened above $260.1M.
- Loss of IMAX screens to *Dune 3*, which also splits premium-format and adult audiences.
- NRG at $270M, Yahoo's "toward $300M" ceiling, and running presales behind *Brand New Day*'s.
- Possible Avengers/MCU fatigue.
- Even a 25% beat on $270M only gets to about $338M.

**NO via #1 (above $360.09M):**
- Marvel event films have often beaten early tracking by 1.3–2×.
- Box Office Theory's range was $340–410M.
- The Robert Downey Jr.-as-Doom and ensemble draw, plus the record early presale pace.
- Premium "Infinity Vision" pricing and Christmas-week momentum.
- A previews figure above about $75M would suggest record territory.

**NO via below $160M:** This would take a catastrophic collapse. Presales alone already exceed $100M, so it is very unlikely (well under 2%).

**NO via delay:** Very unlikely. Tickets are on sale and the film is three weeks out.

### 5. Signposts
- **Final-week tracking:**
  - $300M or less → strongly YES.
  - $330M or more → the record becomes live, shifting toward NO.
- **Presale milestones:** passing *Brand New Day*'s ~$150M before opening points toward NO (record). Plateauing around $120–130M points toward YES.
- **Thursday previews (Dec 17):** under about $60M is likely YES. More than $72M (*Brand New Day*'s record) signals a $360M+ risk.
- **Reviews and social reaction around Dec 15–17:** a strong reception raises the odds of a record opening.
- **Friday, Dec 18 total:** about $150M or less is likely YES. More than $168M (*Brand New Day*'s Friday) points to NO.
- **A Sunday estimate within about $10M of $360M:** wait for Monday actuals. Studios sometimes estimate aggressively to claim a record.
- **Any weather or economic disruption, or the Dune 3/IMAX split being larger than expected:** moves toward YES.

### 6. Uncertainties
- I could not open The Numbers to confirm *Toy Story 5*'s final figure (assumed about $160M) or *Brand New Day*'s exact figure on that site ($360.09M reported elsewhere).
- I could not confirm *The Mandalorian & Grogu*'s or *Michael*'s domestic openings. THR's "#5" placement for *Hunger Games* implies both were below about $120M.
- The trajectory of NRG tracking for earlier Marvel films is reconstructed from secondary reporting and memory.
- I could not establish how many IMAX screens *Doomsday* actually lost.
- I could not tell whether the "$100M presales" figure is domestic gross or includes fees, or how it compares like-for-like to *Brand New Day* at the same point.
- *Jumanji 3*'s exact date (Dec 11 vs Dec 25) is unconfirmed, though it doesn't matter for the $160M threshold.
