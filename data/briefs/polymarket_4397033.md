# Research brief: Will Bilibili Gaming (BLG) win Worlds 2026?

*Prepared 2026-11-11. WebFetch was blocked in this environment, so every fact below comes from search-result summaries of the cited articles. I did not open any of the pages directly.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** BLG is declared winner of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship. Riot/LoL Esports is the source, and a consensus of credible reporting such as Liquipedia is also allowed.
- **Where it stands:** The tournament is down to one match. BLG plays Gen.G in the Grand Final, a best-of-five, on **Saturday, November 14, 2026, at Barclays Center, Brooklyn, NY**. It starts in the evening US Eastern time, reported as 6am AEDT on Nov 15 (Stevivor, Nov 2026; Inven Global, Mar 2026). Whoever wins that series is the champion. There is no bronze match or rematch.
- **"Other" outcome:** The market resolves "Other" only if the final is postponed past Nov 28, 2026, 11:59 PM ET, is cancelled, or produces no winner. With the final three days away and every venue confirmed, that risk is very small, probably under 1% (outage, security incident, force majeure). Riot has handled technical problems with same-day or next-day remakes before.
- **Ambiguities:** In practice there are none. The "multiple winners / alphabetical" rule cannot come into play in a single Bo5 final. The resolution sources are unambiguous, and the result will be known about 2 weeks before the market closes (Nov 29 04:59 UTC).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Worlds finals between the LCK (Korea) and the LPL (China):** 6 finals since 2013. The LCK has won 5 and the LPL 1: 2013 SKT d. Royal, 2014 SSW d. SHRC, 2020 DWG d. SN, **2021 EDG (LPL) d. DK**, 2023 T1 d. WBG, 2024 T1 d. BLG 3-2. **LPL team wins 1/6 ≈ 17%.** I counted these from my own knowledge of tournament history. The current LCK streak is four straight Worlds titles (2022–2025), and a Gen.G win would make it five straight and 11 overall (Stevivor; Gambling.com, Nov 2026).
2. **MSI finals between the LCK and the LPL:** 6 total, and the LCK has won only 2 (2024 Gen.G d. BLG, 2026 HLE d. BLG). The LPL won 2015, 2018, 2021 and 2022 (Sheep Esports/EarlyGame, Jul 2026). **Combining Worlds and MSI LCK-vs-LPL finals: LPL 5 of 12 ≈ 42%.**
3. **BLG in best-of-five finals at international events:** 1 win, 4 losses. They lost the MSI 2023 final to JDG, the MSI 2024 final to Gen.G (3-1), the Worlds 2024 final to T1 (3-2) and the MSI 2026 final to HLE (3-2). They won First Stand 2026, beating G2 3-1. They lost two of those finals 3-2, so the losses were close.
4. **Head-to-head with Gen.G:** BLG has a 0-2 series record against Gen.G since 2024, 2-6 in games, with both series at MSI 2024 (mid.gg). Before that, BLG beat Gen.G at MSI 2023 and in the Worlds 2023 quarterfinal 3-2 (Stevivor; Khan Sports). Both rosters have changed since 2024, and I found **no 2026 meeting** between them.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 9–11, 2026, final previews:** Analysts lean slightly toward Gen.G. u.gg predicts Gen.G 3-2. Win.gg backs Gen.G but says BLG can win "if its early leads hold" and can also collapse 0-3. EarlyGame calls Gen.G "slight favorites." Strafe notes that the old head-to-head says little because both rosters have changed.
- **Nov 8, semifinal: BLG d. T1 3-1** (defending three-time champions). This ended T1's four-peat attempt and a 13-series Worlds winning streak against LPL teams. Jungler Xun's smites decided Game 1, and Knight's Orianna set the tone (Sheep Esports; GameDaily; games.gg; Gambling.com).
- **Nov 7, semifinal: Gen.G d. Invictus Gaming 3-0.** Canyon was series MVP. Gen.G has not lost a series at Worlds 2026 (Strafe; Dot Esports; Khan Sports).
- **Nov 6, quarterfinal: BLG d. Dplus KIA 3-0.** Support ON was series MVP. DK had gone 3-0 in the Swiss Stage and had knocked BLG out of the Esports World Cup 2026 quarterfinal earlier in the year (Khan Sports; Inven Global; GosuGamers).
- **Nov 4, quarterfinal: Gen.G d. LYON 3-0** (Inven Global; GosuGamers).
- **Swiss Stage, Oct 23–31:**
  - Gen.G went 3-0 in series. They dropped Game 1 to LYON and won 2-1, and they also beat HLE (Inven Global; GosuGamers).
  - BLG went 3-2 in series:
    - Round 1: lost the opening match to Movistar KOI (GosuGamers).
    - Round 4: reverse-swept 1-2 by Top Esports on Oct 29.
    - Round 5: swept HLE 2-0 to take the last knockout spot (GosuGamers; Strafe).
  - So BLG's path was much harder and less steady. Their knockout run (6-1 in games) has been very strong.
- **Conflicting game records:** One preview lists Worlds game records of BLG 10-1 and Gen.G 11-4 (summary of mid.gg/previews). That does not match the series results above: from those, Gen.G looks about 11-1 and BLG has at least 4 game losses. Treat those figures as unreliable. The same preview gives 2026 full-season game records of Gen.G 130-53 (71%) and BLG 95-32 (75%).
- **2026 season results:**
  - **BLG** (Bin, Xun, Knight, Viper, ON): won First Stand (Mar), won LPL Split 1 (3-1 vs JDG), and per reports won three LPL titles in 2026 (Split 2 MVP: Knight). They lost the MSI final 2-3 to HLE (Jul 12) and lost the EWC quarterfinal to DK. There was an in-season scare when Bin went on a break and BLG promoted academy top laner Wenbo, but Bin was back by Aug 20 (Sheep Esports snippets) and has been playing at Worlds.
  - **Gen.G** (Kiin, Canyon, Chovy, Ruler, Duro): won the LCK Cup (Mar, 3-0 final), failed to qualify for MSI 2026, finished first in the LCK regular season at 19-7, and won the LCK final 3-1 over HLE (around Sep 13). This is Gen.G's first Worlds final since 2017 (as Samsung Galaxy) and Chovy's first ever. Gen.G previously lost in the Worlds semifinals in 2022, 2024 and 2025.
- **Stakes:** Both organizations are chasing their first Worlds title. A BLG win would be the LPL's first since 2021.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (BLG wins):**
- BLG's early-game identity (Xun and Knight setting tempo, ON roaming) keeps working. They swept DK and beat T1 that way, and in the HLE series they won by bullying the top lane early.
- BLG's knockout form (6-1 in games against two strong LCK teams) carries into the final. Viper's experience of winning big Bo5s against Gen.G-style opponents helps.
- Gen.G's history of struggling on the biggest stage repeats. They have three Worlds semifinal losses in four years and are playing their first final under the Chovy/Canyon core.
- The patch or meta favours BLG's skirmish-heavy style. I could not verify draft details.

**Paths to NO (Gen.G wins):**
- Gen.G is steadier. They are undefeated in series, had a one-sided bracket path (9-0 in knockout games), and have elite late-game macro and teamfighting (Chovy, Ruler).
- BLG's record in finals is poor (1-4 in international Bo5 finals), and their Swiss run was shaky (losses to MKOI and TES).
- The historical LCK advantage in Worlds finals (5-1) holds.
- **Note:** Gen.G's easier semifinal also gave it more time to prepare. Both teams have had about six days, so the rest difference is negligible.

## 5. Signposts (before and during the Nov 14 final)

- **Roster or health news**, such as a substitution, illness, or visa or travel problems for the move from Texas to New York. Any of these would be a large signal for whichever team is affected.
- **Draft and side selection:** who has first pick or side choice in Game 1. In recent finals the blue side's first-pick advantage has mattered by a few percentage points per game.
- **Game 1 result:** the Game 1 winner wins roughly 65–70% of Bo5s (general heuristic). For in-play updates, a 1-0 lead moves the series odds far more than anything published before the match.
- **Late reporting on scrims** (Korean and Chinese community sources). These are noisy, but they sometimes move expert consensus.
- **Postponement or venue news:** very unlikely, but it is the only path to "Other."

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not read full articles because WebFetch was blocked. Exact game-level stats (gold difference at 15 minutes, side win rates, champion pools) and any detailed analyst reasoning are missing.
- The Worlds game-record figures conflict between sources (see Section 3).
- I could not establish the exact patch or meta, or which team it favours.
- I could not confirm whether Bin's mid-season break has any lingering effect. He appears to be playing normally.
- How much the head-to-head matters is unclear. The only recent meetings are from 2024, with different supports and AD carries (Viper was on HLE then).
- The base rates use small samples (6 to 12 finals), and LCK dominance from 2022 to 2025 was driven heavily by one team, T1, which BLG has just beaten.
