## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **Rule text:** "If Elon Musk is cast in Superman before Dec 31, 2026, then the market resolves to Yes." The question closes 2026-12-31 15:00 UTC, about 6 weeks from today.
- **Series context:** The ticker is KXACTORELONMUSK-26 and the Kalshi URL slug is "elon-musk-in-what-movie," which suggests a general "Musk acting role" series (Kalshi page title "Next Superman film: Will Elon Musk be cast?"). One third-party mirror summarized the rule as Musk being "cast **as** Superman," which is stricter than "cast **in**." The official wording says "in." In practice the gap hardly matters, because either outcome is extremely unlikely.
- **What counts as "the next Superman":** Almost certainly *Man of Tomorrow*, James Gunn's sequel to *Superman* (2025). It stars David Corenswet, filmed from April to August 2026, and is scheduled for July 9, 2027 (TechRadar gives July 7). Kalshi does not appear to define "next Superman" more precisely.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **A cameo as himself.** Most people would read "cast in" to include this, but Kalshi would presumably need credible reporting or an official announcement before the close date.
  - **Secret cameos.** Cameos are normally kept secret until release. Musk's *Iron Man 2* appearance was only revealed when the film came out. Even if Musk were filmed for *Man of Tomorrow* in reshoots, it might not be public before Dec 31, 2026, and the film doesn't come out until July 2027. In effect, YES requires both the casting and public confirmation within about 6 weeks.
  - **Other Superman projects.** It's unclear whether animated projects, TV, or a different "Superman" project would count. A cameo by archive footage or a parody (for example, a Lex Luthor written to resemble Musk) should not count.
  - Who settles disputes: Kalshi's resolution team, presumably using major trade press (Variety, THR, Deadline) or official studio or Musk statements.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Musk's history of acting cameos.** His known screen cameos include *Iron Man 2* (2010, as himself, a superhero film), *Machete Kills* (2013), *Transcendence* (2014), *Why Him?* (2016), *Men in Black: International* (2019, uncredited), and voice or TV appearances such as *The Simpsons*, *The Big Bang Theory* and *Rick and Morty*, plus hosting SNL in 2021 (Elon Musk filmography; Snopes; IMDb list of 12 appearances).
   - That is roughly 10–12 appearances from 2010 to 2021, about 0.8 a year. Since about 2021 I found **no new scripted cameos**.
   - His only 2026 screen credit is archive footage in Alex Gibney's HBO documentary *Musk* (Oct 2026).
   - Over the last ~5 years his cameo rate is close to 0 a year. Even at the historical rate, the chance that a cameo lands in one specific film within a 6-week window is far below 1%.
2. **Real tech billionaires cameoing in Superman or DC films.** I found none across the Superman film history (1978–2025). Gunn's *Superman* (2025) cameos were actors and DC personalities (Slashfilm cameo explainer). Base rate: about 0 of ~10 Superman films.
3. **Late casting additions after principal photography wraps** (major superhero films whose shoot has already ended). Small cameos are sometimes added in reshoots and occasionally surface early through leaks. Even so, publicly reported new cameo castings in the window after the wrap and more than 6 months before release are uncommon. On the same day that Variety, THR and IndieWire reported the leadership change (Nov 2026), Gunn said "every major role" was already filled (TechRadar).

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 14, 2026:** Gunn denied overhaul rumors: "No one from Skydance has even seen the movie." The film is in post-production (The Playlist, Nov 14, 2026; Kotaku).
- **Nov 12, 2026:** Nexus Point News, relayed by World of Reel and Geeks + Gamers, reported possible mandatory reshoots and cut cameos under Skydance. Blue Beetle is most often named as the cut. A claim that Supergirl would be cut was later retracted.
  - Cosmic Book News (low reliability) separately claims there will be "no reshoots" and a possible streaming release.
  - None of this is officially confirmed. The studio says the film is still set for July 2027 (Dark Horizons; Fiction Horizon; ComicBookMovie).
- **~Nov 6–10, 2026:** James Gunn and Peter Safran are out as heads of DC Studios, effective Dec 1, 2026. Marvel veteran Jeremy Latcham becomes president and chief creative officer of DC Studios.
  - The exit followed Skydance's roughly $110–111B acquisition of Warner Bros., which closed in October 2026.
  - Reports cite cost-cutting and disputes over creative control. David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz reportedly met Gunn and Safran on the lot on Nov 6.
  - Gunn is staying on as writer of a DC project. Who holds final cut on *Man of Tomorrow* is unclear (Variety; THR; IndieWire; Consequence; Slashfilm; Gizmodo, Nov 2026).
- **Oct 2026:** HBO released the documentary *Musk*, which uses archive footage only (RogerEbert.com; Letterboxd).
- **~Aug 2026:** Gunn announced that principal photography had wrapped after 93 days (JoBlo; Dark Horizons; ComicBook.com).
- **Jul 2026:** Gunn said a reality-TV star (Rick Devens) had a planned *Man of Tomorrow* cameo that fell through because of a scheduling conflict (Express Tribune, Jul 20, 2026). This shows that minor celebrity cameos were part of the plan.
- **Announced cast:** Corenswet (Superman), Nicholas Hoult (Lex Luthor), Lars Eidinger (Brainiac), Rachel Brosnahan, Milly Alcock, Isabela Merced, Nathan Fillion, Edi Gathegi, Frank Grillo, Skyler Gisondo, Sara Sampaio, Neva Howell, plus Andre Royo, Matthew Lillard and Sinqua Walls in undisclosed roles. **Musk does not appear in any cast reporting I found.**
- **Musk–Superman links:** Critics compared Hoult's Lex Luthor in *Superman* (2025) to Musk; RogerEbert.com called it a "GQ-meets-Elon-Musk" take. Hoult said he did not base Luthor on Musk (Vanity Fair segment, via search summary).
  - *Superman* (2025) was attacked from the political right over Gunn's comments that Superman is an immigrant (NBC News; The Week, Jul 2025). That makes a Gunn-era Musk cameo culturally unlikely.
  - I found no statement from Musk about wanting a Superman role, and no reported Gunn–Musk contact.
- **Other Musk film news:** Luca Guadagnino's OpenAI film *Artificial* reportedly portrays Musk as a character played by an actor, which is not Musk being cast (AV Club).
- **Scheduled events before Dec 31, 2026:** Latcham takes over DC Studios on Dec 1. Possible decisions on reshoots, the cut or the release strategy for *Man of Tomorrow*. No trailer, convention or premiere date for *Man of Tomorrow* is confirmed before year-end.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (all remote):**
- **Skydance-ordered reshoots add a Musk cameo, and it is announced or leaked before Dec 31.**
  - This depends on reshoots actually happening (unconfirmed), new leadership wanting a politically charged celebrity cameo in a film already cut, Musk agreeing, filming or announcing within 6 weeks, and a credible public report.
  - The Ellison family (Larry Ellison is a long-time Musk ally) now controls Warner Bros. That is the only structural tie I found, and nothing I found links it to Musk and Superman.
  - Reshoots for a July 2027 film would more likely be scheduled for early 2027.
- **A loose resolution:** a credible report of some Musk appearance in a "Superman" project, such as a voice role or a non-Gunn project, that Kalshi accepts.
- **A surprise publicity stunt,** such as Musk or the studio announcing a cameo on X. I found no evidence of this.

**Paths to NO (default):**
- No casting occurs. This is overwhelmingly the status quo: the film wrapped in Aug 2026 and the cast is set.
- A cameo is filmed but kept secret until the July 2027 release.
- Studio turmoil leads to cutting cameos, not adding them. Current reporting points this way.

## 5. Signposts

- **Toward YES (large shift):**
  - Official or trade-press confirmation of *Man of Tomorrow* reshoots in Dec 2026 together with new cameo castings.
  - Any Variety, THR or Deadline report, or a post from Musk on X, about a Musk cameo.
  - Musk publicly lobbying Skydance or the Ellisons for a role.
- **Toward YES (small shift):** Skydance or Latcham publicly signalling a politically "rebalanced" DC slate.
- **Toward NO:** Latcham or Skydance confirming the film is locked or headed to streaming without reshoots, or reshoots scheduled for 2027. Each week without news also matters, because the window is only about 6 weeks.
- **Watch:** how Kalshi interprets "cast in" if a rumor surfaces from a low-reliability outlet such as Cosmic Book News.
