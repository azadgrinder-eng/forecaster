# Research brief: Will Carbon Arc's FanDuel app-downloads index for November 2026 be above 84?

Prepared 2026-11-18. Most article pages were blocked in this environment, so the facts below come mainly from search-result summaries of the cited pages. I did not look up or report any market prices.

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Carbon Arc's "FanDuel app downloads" index for November 2026 must be strictly above 84. The index compares the month with the same month a year earlier, with 100 meaning no change. So YES needs November 2026 downloads to be no more than about 16% below November 2025. A value of exactly 84 resolves NO.
- **Who decides and when:** Carbon Arc's finalized value, recorded at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 7, 2026. The market closes December 8, 2026 at 04:59 UTC. Kalshi runs these "KPI" markets, which settle on Carbon Arc data ("Prisms"). Kalshi announced sector-level versions around late October 2026 (Kalshi news; Markets Media, Oct 26, 2026).
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Which apps are counted.** The rules don't say whether "FanDuel app" means only FanDuel Sportsbook & Casino or the whole brand (Sportsbook, Fantasy, Racing, the FanDuel Predicts prediction-market app). This matters a lot. Predicts launched in December 2025 and so has almost no November 2025 base. If it is included, the index could be pushed well up.
  - **Geography and platforms.** Whether the count is US only, and whether it covers both iOS and Android, is not stated.
  - **Data quality.** Carbon Arc's method (panel-based estimate or store data) and how much its numbers get revised before "finalized" values are unknown. Carbon Arc's figures may not match Sensor Tower or Apptopia.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **FanDuel's recent year-over-year download trend (Apptopia and Sensor Tower):**
   - Full-year 2025: −14.6% (Apptopia investor note, early 2026).
   - Q1 2026: Apptopia's headline weekly figure of 143.2K was down 6.3% (Apptopia, "OSB market grows Q1'26," 2026).
   - NFL Week 1 2026 (September): 265K downloads, down 36% year over year (Sensor Tower via Legal Sports Report and Citizens, Sept 2026).
   - NFL Weeks 1–2 2026: 502K in total. The 2025 Week 1 figure was 384K, so if 2025 Week 2 was roughly 300K, that is about −25% to −30%. This is my inference.
   - NFL Week 4 2026: about 8% of 2.1M real-money gaming downloads, roughly 168K (Deadspin, Oct 2026).
   - **Takeaway:** the autumn 2026 run-rate looks like roughly 25–36% below last year. That would put the index at about 64–75, below 84.
2. **Months when last year's comparison included a new-state launch:** Sportsbook downloads fall sharply year over year when the prior-year month had a state launch or pre-registration. Citizens explicitly blamed weak autumn 2026 sportsbook downloads on "difficult year-over-year comparisons and the absence of new state launches" (LSR, Sept 2026). Earlier, 2025 NFL downloads fell when no new market launched (LSR/CDC, 2025). November 2025 contained Missouri's pre-registration window (Nov 17–30, 2025), so November 2026 falls in this class.
3. **Months with a sports-calendar boost:** In 2026, the World Cup lifted June activity: FanDuel daily active users were up about 48% year over year for June 1–15 (Apptopia, June 2026). November has no comparable one-off event, and the NFL and college football calendars are similar in both years.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 4, 2026:** Flutter CEO Dan Taylor said the company will soon have a "clearer view" on whether to keep spending on prediction markets. He also admitted FanDuel Predicts "was not competitive" until recently (CDC Gaming / Front Office Sports, Nov 4, 2026).
- **Q3 2026 results (early Nov 2026):**
  - Flutter revenue $3,874m, up 2%. Average monthly players 13.7m, down 3%. Adjusted EBITDA down 25%.
  - Management said it is using the FanDuel brand and Predicts to acquire customers in states without legal sportsbooks, at lower acquisition cost (Finviz/MENAFN, Q3 2026 release).
  - Implication: new-customer acquisition is moving toward the separate Predicts app.
- **Oct 26, 2026:** Kalshi and Carbon Arc expanded the KPI contracts (Markets Media).
- **Early Oct 2026, NFL Week 4:** real-money gaming downloads were 2.1M, up 24% year over year, but the growth went to Kalshi (32% share). DraftKings had 12%, Polymarket 11% and FanDuel only 8% (Deadspin, Oct 2026).
- **Sept 2026:**
  - NFL Week 1: about 3M real-money gaming downloads, up 11%, while FanDuel fell 36% and DraftKings 46%.
  - Through Week 2: Polymarket 1.37M, Kalshi 1.31M, DraftKings 805K, FanDuel 502K.
  - Caveat: the September 2026 window had 48 NFL games against 62 in 2025 (LSR, Sept 2026).
- **2026 generally:**
  - FanDuel Predicts expanded from 5 states (Dec 2025) to nationwide (2026). It had 227K downloads in April 2026 (Apptopia).
  - Kalshi passed FanDuel in Q4 2025 downloads: about 3.76M against 2.5M (Apptopia; PYMNTS 2026).
  - FanDuel's Q4 2025 figure implies roughly 830K a month on average, with December lifted by the Missouri launch.
- **November 2025 base:**
  - Missouri pre-registration ran Nov 17–30, 2025, with FanDuel pre-registration offers of $300–$500 in bonus bets.
  - About 188,000 pre-registered accounts were created across all operators before the Dec 1 launch, and more than 250K accounts were active on day one (Covers / 4for4, Nov–Dec 2025).
  - If FanDuel took about 35–45% of those, that is roughly 65–85K extra downloads in November 2025. That would be about 8–12% of a typical month (my estimate). This raises the comparison base for November 2026.
- **Scheduled before the Dec 7 snapshot:** No new state sportsbook launch is known for November 2026. Thanksgiving NFL games (Nov 26) and the usual NFL and college football slate fall in both years.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to NO (index at or below 84):**
- The autumn 2026 trend of roughly −25% to −36% simply continues.
- The Missouri pre-registration bump in late November 2025 makes the comparison harder still.
- Kalshi and Polymarket keep taking new-to-category bettors.
- If Flutter cuts acquisition spending or promotions, downloads fall further.
- This looks like the central case if Carbon Arc tracks only the Sportsbook app.

**Paths to YES (index above 84):**
- **Carbon Arc counts Predicts too.** If the index covers the brand or includes FanDuel Predicts, which had almost no downloads in November 2025, it could be lifted substantially.
- **Carbon Arc's data differs.** Its panel or method may show a much smaller decline than Sensor Tower, for example because it covers different platforms or geographies.
- **A late-autumn recovery.** Sensor Tower's Week 4 data shows the overall category growing 24%. If FanDuel's share stabilizes, its decline narrows. The Week 1 drop was partly a game-count calendar effect.
- **Aggressive November promotions** (Thanksgiving, Black Friday-style bonus offers) or a viral moment.
- **Noise or revisions** in Carbon Arc's finalized number.

## 5. Signposts

- **Carbon Arc's FanDuel index for September or October 2026**, or Kalshi's description of which apps are included. This is the most valuable item. A value in the 60s–70s with a sportsbook-only scope argues strongly for NO; values near or above 85, or a scope that includes Predicts, argue for YES.
- **Sensor Tower, Citizens or LSR weekly NFL download reports for October–November 2026.** A FanDuel share stuck around 8% with a falling year-over-year trend means NO; signs of the decline narrowing below 15% favor YES.
- **Any new state launch or pre-registration in November 2026.** None is known; one would be a large move toward YES.
- **Flutter cutting Predicts or marketing spend** (toward NO), or a large FanDuel promotion push in November (toward YES).

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not establish Carbon Arc's methodology, which apps are counted, or any historical Carbon Arc FanDuel values. None appeared in search results, and the pages that might have them were blocked here.
- There is no published FanDuel download count for November 2025 or for October–November 2026.
- The size of the Missouri effect is my estimate, and some of those downloads may have been reinstalls.
- The Week 1 2025 baseline differs between sources (384K in an earlier LSR report against about 414K implied by the 2026 "−36%" figure), probably because of scope differences such as US only versus US plus Canada.
- I found no Apptopia FanDuel data more recent than July 2026.
