## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (Bills win):**
- Large gap in team quality: elite QB play from Allen and a +10/game differential against a 1-8 team allowing about 25 points per game.
- Home field in late-November weather against a warm-weather team.
- A backup QB making his second start, which raises the chance of turnovers.
- Miami's kicking unreliability.

**Paths to NO (Dolphins win or tie):**
- Turnover variance, for example if Allen's interception tendency shows up.
- Buffalo letdown or looking ahead at 8-1, possibly coming into play with multiple secondary injuries (Alford, Bishop, Hairston).
- A strong McCord game against a banged-up secondary.
- Miami nearly won at Indianapolis, and close games are coin-flips at the end.
- Precedent: Miami beat Buffalo 30-13 in Nov 2025.

**Other risks:**
- A key injury to Allen announced during the week would be the biggest swing.
- Severe weather adds variance and lowers scoring, which slightly helps the underdog.
- A postponement beyond 48 hours means a "fair price" settlement, very unlikely.

## 5. Signposts

- **Toward NO:** Josh Allen on the injury report or limited in practice (large effect). Several Bills secondary or offensive-line starters ruled out (moderate). A heavy lake-effect snow forecast or a venue change (raises variance, small effect).
- **Toward YES:** Willis activated early is unlikely, and since he would be an upgrade for Miami, it would actually push slightly toward NO. A Miami kicker change could cut either way. Bills defenders such as Alford and Bishop cleared, or Miami adding more injuries on the offensive line or at WR, push toward YES.
- **In-game:** early turnovers.
