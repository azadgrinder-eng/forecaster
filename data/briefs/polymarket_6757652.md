## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (United win in 90 minutes):**
- A "new manager bounce" under Pochettino. United's squad quality per player and attacking threat (Cunha, Fernandes, Mbeumo and others) beat Sporting's in a game where Sporting are also adapting to a new coach.
- United score often even in bad form: 8 UCL goals in 4 games and 4 against Villa. A shootout-style game gives them chances.
- League-phase stakes motivate United. At 17th they need points to stay in the top 24, which rewards an all-out approach.

**Paths to NO (draw or Sporting win):**
- Sporting's home strength in Europe: they beat Man City 4–1 and Tottenham 2–0 at Alvalade in recent seasons, and are a solid Liga top-three side.
- United's defensive fragility: 4 conceded at Como and 4 at home to Villa, Maguire's back problem, Baleba out.
- Little coaching time: Pochettino has about 3 days of club preparation, all spent around the Liverpool trip, so tactical change is limited. Fatigue after Anfield.
- A draw is a large share of the NO outcome. Draws typically account for about 25% of these games.

**Key dependencies:** whether Pochettino actually starts before 22 Nov (paperwork, his USA commitments), United's injury list after Anfield, Sporting's lineup choices with Porto four days later, and Fonseca's early results.
