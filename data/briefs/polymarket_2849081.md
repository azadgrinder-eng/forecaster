# Research brief: Will Perpl launch a token by December 31, 2026?

*Prepared November 25, 2026. I couldn't open any page directly because the proxy blocked every fetch attempt. Everything below comes from search-result summaries of the listed pages, so details such as exact dates should be checked against the primary sources where possible.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Perpl, the perpetual futures DEX on Monad (X account @perpltrade, site perpl.xyz), must officially launch its own token, and that token must be "actively and publicly tradable" by 11:59 PM ET on December 31, 2026 (04:59 UTC on January 1, 2027).
- **What doesn't count:**
  - An announcement alone, including a TGE date, points-conversion details, or an airdrop checker, if trading hasn't started.
  - Stablecoins, memecoins, LSTs and synthetic tokens.
  - Pre-market or perp listings of an unlaunched token (e.g., "PERPL-PERP" pre-launch futures on a CEX). These are a judgment call, but they probably fail the "official token launched by Perpl" test.
- **Who decides:** Perpl's own X account is the primary source, backed by a consensus of credible reporting.
- **Edge cases:**
  - Solana tokens with the PERPL ticker already trade on Phantom. One showed a market cap of about $87K as of March 26, 2026. These appear to be unaffiliated copycats and should not count (Phantom token pages; "Perpl (PERPL) Airdrop" aggregator, 2026).
  - The "Purple Summer" mPoints campaign pays rewards in MON, Monad's native token, not a Perpl token. That payout does not count (MEXC blog, about Nov 12, 2026).
  - If a TGE happened on Dec 31 with trading opening a few hours later, the timezone cutoff could matter.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. How long perp-DEX points programs run before a TGE.** Durations below are worked out from public dates and are approximate.
- Hyperliquid: points from about Nov 2023 to the HYPE TGE on Nov 29, 2024, so about 12 months.
- Lighter: points from about early 2025 to the LIT TGE in late Dec 2025, about 11–12 months (blockeden.xyz, Apr 28, 2026; The Defiant).
- edgeX: points from 2024 to a TGE reportedly on Mar 31, 2026, so more than 15 months. Sources conflict on this date.
- Extended: points since Apr 29, 2025, with no confirmed TGE in the sources I found, so more than 19 months.
- Paradex: XP since about 2024, with Season 2 extended six months in late 2025. I couldn't confirm a token date.
- Pacifica: points live, TGE "TBA."
- Perpl: Season 1 points began June 10, 2026. A TGE by Dec 31 would mean about 6.7 months, faster than every comparable case above. Roughly 0 of 5–6 peer perp DEXs reached a tradable token within 7 months of starting points.

**B. How much warning projects give before a token trades.** Credible TGEs (Monad, Hyperliquid, Lighter) were typically announced or clearly signalled 1–4 weeks before trading. Monad announced its airdrop and mainnet date on Nov 5, 2025 and launched on Nov 24, 2025 (The Block, Nov 5 and Nov 24, 2025). With 36 days left and no signal at all, the historical rate of a TGE with no warning is low, probably under 10%.

**C. Points-season transitions.** Some projects launch the token at the end of a season. Hyperliquid did at the end of Season 2, and Lighter launched and then reserved supply for Season 3. Others roll into a new season with no token. Perpl has publicly announced a Season 2 with new incentives and no token mentioned, which looks more like the second pattern.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (all low probability):**
1. **Surprise TGE at the end of Season 1 in late December.** A fee waiver and boosted points shortly before a season ends can come just before a token. This would need an announcement within about 1–4 weeks, plus CEX or DEX listings, a market maker and legal work, all over the holidays. Nothing visible suggests that work is under way.
2. **The Season 2 launch includes a token**, for example one that powers "funding rate earnings" or fee-free trading. That seems unlikely, since the announced Season 2 features are point- and fee-based.

**Paths to NO (the default):**
- Season 1 ends, Season 2 points begin in about Jan 2027, and any TGE slips to 2027. That would match the 11–15+ month timelines of peer perp DEXs.
- Weak organic metrics (about $2.9M TVL, under $0.5M open interest) give the team a reason to keep farming incentives going longer before a TGE.
- Ecosystem tokens performing poorly after the MON launch may also argue for waiting.

## 5. Signposts
**Toward YES, large moves:**
- Perpl tweets a TGE date, tokenomics, an airdrop checker, or a "Season 1 snapshot and conversion" announcement.
- CEX listing announcements (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kucoin, Backpack) or a Binance Alpha listing for an official PERPL.
- A token contract published in Perpl's docs, or a foundation entity announcement. "PerplFoundation" already exists as a GitHub organisation.
- Echo/Sonar participants being told about a token claim.

**Toward NO:**
- Season 2 goes live with a points schedule.
- The team says "no token in 2026" or "TGE in 2027."
- Season 1 is extended into January.

## 6. Uncertainties
