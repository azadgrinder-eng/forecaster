# Research brief: Will Justin Bieber have more than 17M global daily YouTube views on any day in December 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Bieber's Global daily views on YouTube Charts must be above 17M on at least one day between Dec 1 and Dec 31, 2026. Kalshi reads the value from a New York IP: search the artist on YouTube Charts and set the location to "Global". "Above" probably means strictly greater than 17M, so a reading of exactly 17.0M would likely not count.
- **This is a maximum, not an average.** One spike day is enough. The question asks for the highest of about 31 daily values.
- **Timing:** The market closes Jan 4, 2027 at 15:00 UTC. YouTube Charts publishes daily data with a lag of a day or two, so the Dec 30 and Dec 31 values should be visible before the close. Check that these late-December days are in fact settled before close.
- **The metric is not the channel view count.** YouTube Charts artist views are built differently from the official channel's views. They count official videos plus user-generated, lyric and art-track uses of the artist's music. Songs where he is a featured artist, such as DJ Snake's "Let Me Love You", may also count. Shorts treatment is unclear. The chart's number could therefore be higher or lower than the channel-level figures from third-party trackers below. **I could not access YouTube Charts or kworb directly (the network proxy blocked them), so I have not seen the actual series Kalshi uses.** This is the biggest gap in the brief.
- **Other edge cases:** YouTube can revise daily figures after publication. Views can differ by IP location, which is why the rules name New York. Kalshi decides the outcome.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **His recent run-rate against the threshold (closest analogue).** Three third-party trackers give these levels for his official channel:
   - vidIQ: 399.0M views from Oct 8 to Nov 4, 2026, about **14.25M/day** (vidIQ, Nov 2026).
   - Chartmetric: daily video views of **14.8M** and monthly views of 415.9M (Chartmetric, late Nov 2026).
   - vidIQ's earlier 30-day figure: 340.7M, about 11.4M/day, undated but older. The level appears to have risen through autumn 2026.
   - ChartMasters' lifetime average is lower, about 10.8M/day.
   
   On these numbers, 17M is about 15–20% above his current average. Normal day-to-day swings (weekends, regional holidays) are often ±5–10%. If the YouTube Charts series tracks the channel level, the 17M line needs a real boost on top of that noise. If YouTube Charts runs higher because of UGC and featured songs, 17M could be close to or below his normal level. I cannot tell which.
2. **December seasonality for artists with a big Christmas song.** "Mistletoe" (2011) is a recurring holiday hit. In 2016 it was the most-viewed Christmas video on YouTube at about 261M views (CBS/Music Times, Dec 2016). In the week of Dec 19–25, 2025 it ranked #9 on the UK weekly songs chart with 1.07M views (PPC Land, Dec 2025). Global holiday views are probably on the order of 1–2M/day at the Dec 20–25 peak. This is my estimate, not a sourced figure. Under the Mistletoe album tracks add a little more. Seasonality points toward YES in the second half of December.
3. **Spikes from events.** In 2026 his views jumped around releases and big performances:
   - He was a top-10 artist on YouTube Music's Top Artists chart in April and May 2026, after Coachella (AceShowbiz, Apr and May 12, 2026).
   - "Beauty and a Beat" surged after his Coachella set.
   
   Without a release or major event, large one-day spikes (+20% or more) are uncommon. I found no historical count of how often his daily maximum in a month exceeded his monthly average by 20%.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES:**
- The YouTube Charts metric runs structurally above the channel figures (UGC plus featured songs), putting his baseline at 15–17M or higher. Normal noise or holiday lift then pushes one day over 17M. This is the most important unknown.
- Christmas lift from "Mistletoe" and the holiday catalog, plus "Baby" and "Let Me Love You" continuing to rise (both grew in early November), adds 1–3M/day in late December.
- A surprise release, a viral Shorts or TikTok moment, a holiday TV performance, or a new-music announcement. He has dropped music with little warning before: *SWAG* was announced only days ahead.

**Paths to NO:**
- His YouTube Charts daily level is near the ~14–15M channel figures. Holiday lift adds only about 1–2M, so the maximum stays around 15.5–16.5M.
- Global YouTube music viewing dips around Dec 24–25 in many large markets (India, Latin America and others), which partly cancels the Christmas-song lift.
- No new music in December 2026. Reports from May 2026 say no next album had a confirmed date (ad-hoc-news, May 2026).

## 5. Signposts
- **Most important:** His actual Global daily views on YouTube Charts for late November and early December 2026. If they are already 16M+, YES becomes likely. If they are around 12–14M, NO becomes likely.
- Whether his daily views were above 17M in December 2025, which had a similar holiday setup plus fresh *SWAG II* momentum. A YES then is a strong precedent.
- Any new single, video, Christmas cover or collaboration announced for December. Strongly bullish.
- Confirmation of 2027 Grammy nominations, or a big December TV slot. Mildly bullish.
- How "Mistletoe" moves on kworb's global daily and weekly charts in the first half of December.

## 6. Uncertainties
- **I could not see the YouTube Charts series that decides the market.** Every number here is from third-party trackers of his official channel (vidIQ, Chartmetric, ChartMasters), and these may differ substantially from the YouTube Charts artist metric.
- I have no December 2025 daily data for Bieber, so the size of his seasonal lift is my estimate (about +5–15%).
- I have no figures for how much his daily views vary within a month, so I cannot say how far his monthly maximum usually sits above his average.
- I could not confirm whether he received 2027 Grammy nominations or has any December plans.
- I don't know how Kalshi treats a value displayed as exactly "17.0M" or values revised after the market closes.
