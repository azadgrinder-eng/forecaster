# Research Brief: Will Taylor Swift be #2 on Spotify Wrapped 2026's Global Top Artists chart?

**Bottom line:** Third-party trackers that count every credit put Drake well ahead of Taylor Swift for #2 in 2026. Spotify's own counts have historically shrunk Drake's numbers much more than Swift's. Swift would be #2 under Spotify's method and Drake under the trackers', and that gap is what decides this question. Bad Bunny is very likely #1. *Note: page fetches were blocked by the network proxy, so the stream figures below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages and tweets. Tweet dates were worked out from tweet IDs.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES** only if Spotify's official 2026 Wrapped Global Top Artists list puts Taylor Swift at exactly #2. #1 or #3 or lower is NO.
- **Release date:** Spotify hasn't announced it. Third-party estimates cluster on Wednesday, Dec 2, 2026, with a window of Dec 1–3. Every Wrapped since 2021 launched on a Wednesday, and Thanksgiving falls on Nov 26 (Jamwise; SpotifyStats; Chartlex, Nov 2026). In 2025 the release was Dec 3 (Spotify Newsroom, Dec 3 2025). The market closes Dec 31, 2026, which leaves plenty of slack.
- **Tracking window:** Wrapped usually covers roughly Jan 1 to mid-November. Most of the 2026 data is therefore already counted, and December streaming doesn't matter.
- **Methodology:** This is the key ambiguity. Spotify doesn't publish how it credits streams for this list. Its own artist totals look far smaller for feature-heavy artists like Drake than the "all credits" totals on trackers such as kworb (see section 4).
- **Edge cases:** The question says "on the date that the chart is released," so later corrections shouldn't count. Spotify did correct a 2026 in-year record once, when "Make Them Cry" was relabelled after two tracks' streams were combined (Hypebeast, May 2026), so a correction isn't impossible. The question presumes Spotify publishes a global top-artist list, which it has done every year.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. Swift's global Wrapped rank, 2021–2025 (built from news coverage of each year):**
- 2021: #2
- 2022: #2
- 2023: #1
- 2024: #1
- 2025: #2 behind Bad Bunny, ahead of The Weeknd and Drake (Spotify Newsroom, Dec 3 2025; chart data on X, Dec 2025)

She was #2 in 3 of 5 years and in the top two in 5 of 5.

**B. Drake against Swift in Wrapped, 2021–2025:** Drake finished #4, #3, #4, #4, #4 and was **below Swift every year (5 of 5).** That includes his big album years:
- *Certified Lover Boy* in 2021: #4
- *For All The Dogs* in 2023: #4
- 2025: trackers credited him with about 18B streams for the full year across all credits, the most ever for a rapper (RatingsGameMusic, Dec 2025). Wrapped still ranked him #4, and one source puts his Wrapped figure at about 12.64B (search summary of an Nov 2026 analysis).

**C. Year-to-date tracker leader vs. final Wrapped order:** In 2025, all-credit trackers had Drake near the top, yet Wrapped put Swift and The Weeknd above him. Tracker rankings have been an unreliable guide to Wrapped order for Drake in particular.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Path to YES (Swift #2, Bad Bunny #1, Drake #3 or lower):**
- **Spotify discounts Drake much more than Swift, as it has before.** Comparing Spotify's April 2026 official totals with kworb's all-credits totals (my rough estimates for April) gives about 0.85 for Swift, 0.76 for Bad Bunny and 0.67 for Drake.
- **Projection with those ratios.** Extend the August tracker figures to mid-November at current daily rates, adding a modest Encore boost for Swift:
  - Trackers: Drake about 19B, Swift about 16.7B, Bad Bunny about 20B
  - After the ratios: Bad Bunny about 15.3B, Swift about 14.2B, Drake about 12.7B
  - That order (Bad Bunny, Swift, Drake) is a YES. The 2025 result fits this pattern: Drake had about 18B on trackers but placed #4 in Wrapped.

**Path to NO via Drake at #2:**
- Drake's 2026 streams come mostly from his own three albums, not features. That makes Spotify's discount on him smaller this year than his all-time ratio suggests.
- On all-credit trackers he led Swift by about 1.85B in late August and has out-streamed her daily since. Plausible ratios for 2026 (about 0.75–0.80 for Drake, about 0.9 for Swift) leave the two within a few percent of each other.
- This is the main NO risk.

**Path to NO via Swift at #1 (Bad Bunny falls to #2 or #3):**
- Unlikely. Bad Bunny led Swift by about 3.6B on trackers in August and still streams more each day.

**Path to NO via Swift at #4 or lower:**
- Very unlikely given the gap to Bieber, Bruno Mars and The Weeknd.

## 5. Signposts
- **The Wrapped list itself (about Dec 2)** settles the question. Nothing else on the calendar is likely to change it.
- **Any late-November leak of Wrapped data, or Spotify's US or genre lists:** a Drake #2 leak points to NO, a Swift #2 leak to YES.
- **A Spotify statement on whether features or "appears on" streams count:** if they count fully, that favors NO.
- **Tracker updates with lead-only year-to-date totals:** in the lead-only race, a Swift lead points to YES and a Drake lead of 5% or more points to NO.
- **Bad Bunny unexpectedly slipping below Swift** would push this toward NO, because Swift would then be #1.

## 6. Uncertainties
- No reliable 2026 year-to-date total from after August, lead-only or all-credits, could be found.
- The @Spotify_daily_d figures are unverified, and I couldn't confirm whether they count features.
- Spotify's Wrapped crediting rules are undisclosed. The discount ratios above are inferred from the all-time list, and kworb's April 2026 baselines are my estimates.
- Drake's 2025 Wrapped figure of 12.64B appears in only one secondary source.
- The size of Swift's Encore boost after September is uncertain. Her current daily streams are well below Drake's.
- The exact cutoff for the tracking window is unknown.
