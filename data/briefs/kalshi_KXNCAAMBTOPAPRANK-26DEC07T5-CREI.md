# Research Brief: Will Creighton be in the top 5 of the AP men's basketball poll for Week 6 (Dec 7, 2026)?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Creighton must be ranked No. 1–5 in the AP men's Top 25 that the AP itself labels "Week 6" of the 2026-27 season. That poll is expected on Monday, Dec 7, 2026. Only the AP's official site and labeling count. Other outlets' week labels don't.
- **Which week is Week 6:** This season the AP labeled the Nov 23, 2026 poll as Week 4 (Men's basketball AP poll: Houston jumps to No. 1, Yahoo Sports, Nov 23, 2026). So Week 5 should come out Nov 30 and Week 6 on Dec 7.
- **Games that count:** Everything played through about Sunday, Dec 6.
- **Ties:** A tie at No. 5 would presumably count as "top 5," though the rules don't say.
- **Close date:** Dec 21. That leaves time to verify the result, but it doesn't change what must happen.
- **Possible ambiguity:** The AP could shift its schedule or week numbering, for example around holidays. This is unlikely to matter here.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **Teams with zero votes in late November that reach the top 5 two polls later.** Creighton got no votes in the Nov 23 poll. Its name isn't in the "others receiving votes" list, which ran from Texas A&M at 96 down to Texas State at 1 (AP poll through Nov 23, Yahoo Sports, Nov 23, 2026). I could not open the College Poll Archive's page on the biggest jumps from unranked to ranked, so I have no exact count. From general knowledge of AP history (uncertain, not checked against a source): one-week jumps from unranked almost never land above roughly No. 10–15, and I know of no modern case of an unvoted team reaching the top 5 within two polls in November or December. The base rate is very likely well under 1%.
- **Big one-week jumps by teams already ranked.** Houston rose 7 spots, from No. 8 to No. 1, after winning the four-team Players Era 8, a field of ranked teams that included a win over the previous No. 1. Oklahoma once went from No. 24 to No. 9 in a week after beating three top-10 teams in a row (CBS Sports). The most extreme known moves are about 15 spots in one week. To finish top 5, Creighton would need two such moves in a row, starting from outside the top 25.
- **Rebuilding teams with a new coach after a losing season.** Creighton went 16–18 in 2025-26 (9–11 in the Big East). Greg McDermott retired, and Alan Huss is in his first season as head coach (Wikipedia; Sports-Reference). Preseason expectations were modest: odds of about +1250 to win the Big East regular season (Action Network preview). Only one Creighton player made a preseason All-Big East team: Oswin Erhunmwunse, on the third team. Teams like this seldom get into the top 25 by early December, let alone the top 5.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **Path to YES (extremely narrow):**
  1. Creighton beats TCU, wins its bracket final, possibly against a top-15 team, and wins the Players Era 16 title game on Nov 28, likely against another ranked team. That would make it 8-1 with three or four wins in Las Vegas.
  2. The Nov 30 poll moves it into roughly the 8–15 range. That would already be a historically large jump from unranked with no votes.
  3. It beats No. 20 Nebraska on Dec 5, while several of the top 5–10 teams lose in the busy early-December stretch: ACC/SEC Challenge-type events, Big 12/Big East matchups and early conference games.

  Even if all of that happens, voters tend to move a 9-1 team that started unvoted only into the 8–12 range. The Kansas State loss and its preseason profile also hold it back.
- **Paths to NO (dominant):**
  - Any loss in Las Vegas or to Nebraska.
  - A perfect run that only lifts Creighton into the 10–20 range.
  - The top 5 (Houston, Virginia, Florida, Illinois, Texas Tech) and the next tier (UCLA, Texas, Arizona, Tennessee, UConn, all with 0–1 losses) mostly keep winning.

## 5. Signposts
- **TCU result, Nov 26.** A loss makes YES essentially impossible. A win keeps the tiny path alive.
- **Nov 27–28 results.** Creighton would need to win the Players Era 16 title and beat at least one top-10 team.
- **Week 5 poll, Nov 30.** This is the strongest signpost. If Creighton isn't in the top 12 or so, two-week top-5 odds become negligible. A debut in the top 10, which has almost no precedent, would raise them meaningfully but still not past a low probability.
- **Nebraska game, Dec 5, and top-10 results Dec 1–6.** It matters how many of the current top 9 lose.
