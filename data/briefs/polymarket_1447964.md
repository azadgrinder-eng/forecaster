### 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES:** Dallas is the official 2026 NFC East champion when the regular season ends. If teams finish level, the NFL's division tiebreakers decide it: head-to-head first, then division record, then record in common games, then conference record, and so on.
- **Timing:** The season runs through Week 18. Dallas plays at Washington in Week 18, listed for about Sun, Jan 10, 2027 (Sports Brackets / CBS Sports schedule listings, Oct–Nov 2026). The market closes Jan 4, 2027, which is after Week 17 (Jan 3) but before Week 18. The close date does not change how it resolves, which is by the final champion. Forecasters should still expect the question may be unresolved at close, and that the Week 18 game at Washington could decide it.
- **"Other":** Resolves "Other" if the season is cancelled, postponed past Jan 17, 2027, or no champion is declared. That risk is negligible.
- **Ties matter:** The Giants already have a tie (5-3-1). So a Dallas finish with more wins than the Giants is usually enough outright. For example, 9-8 beats 8-8-1. Exact ties between Dallas and the Giants are less likely than usual.
- **Who decides:** Official NFL standings, or a consensus of credible reporting.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES**
- Dallas wins the two easy games (TEN, PHI), goes about 1-2 over SEA, LAR and JAX, then beats the Giants in Week 17. That splits the head-to-head and probably finishes 8-9 or 9-8.
- At the same time, the Giants go roughly 2-6 or 3-5 against their brutal schedule and finish around 7-9-1 or 8-8-1.
- Washington (3-5-1) stays at about 8 wins or fewer. Dallas beating Washington in Week 18 removes that threat and gives Dallas the season sweep.
- **Rough math:** Dallas expects about 3.8–4.3 more wins, for 8–8.5 total. The Giants' expected finish is about 5.5 plus 2.5–3.5 win-equivalents, roughly 8–9 win-equivalents. That is close to a coin flip, slightly favouring the Giants because of their current lead and the head-to-head win they already hold.

**Paths to NO**
- The Giants keep winning with Winston, steal one or two upsets (CLE and at PHI are winnable), and beat Dallas or hold the tiebreaker.
- Dallas's defence keeps leaking. Its record against good NFC teams suggests losses at SEA and LAR and possibly to JAX.
- Dallas loses the Thanksgiving trap game to the Eagles.
- Washington, led by Daniels, gets hot. It holds the tiebreaker over the Giants and the Week 18 home game against Dallas.

### 5. Signposts
- **Week 11 (Nov 22):** A Dallas win plus a Giants loss to JAX cuts the gap to half a game. That would push Dallas's chances up sharply, toward 40–50%. A Dallas loss to the 8-point-underdog Titans would cut its chances substantially.
- **Thanksgiving vs PHI (Nov 26):** Dallas needs this win. A loss would be close to fatal.

- **Giants QB news:** Winston's form, or a change to someone else.

- **Washington's results:** If it reaches .500, the race becomes three-way.
- **Week 17 Dallas vs NYG (Jan 3):** Probably decisive, and played before the market closes.
