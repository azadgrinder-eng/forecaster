# Research brief: Will Carbon Arc's Miller Lite Point of Sale Growth index for November 2026 be above 82?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Carbon Arc's Miller Lite Point of Sale Growth value for November 2026 must be strictly above 82. That means Miller Lite spend in Carbon Arc's receipt panel must fall by less than 18% from November 2025. A value of exactly 82.0 resolves NO.
- **What the index measures:** It compares spend with the same calendar days one year earlier, where 100 means flat (Carbon Arc "Intro to Prisms" docs, undated). It is built from Carbon Arc's receipt panel, which captures in-store and online purchases from receipt-scanning and rewards apps. It is a panel estimate, not a census, and it contains no absolute dollar figures.
- **When the value is fixed:** The finalized value is recorded at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 2, 2026. The market closes December 3, 2026 at 04:59 UTC, which is the same moment.
- **Who decides:** Kalshi settles against Carbon Arc's Prisms data (Kalshi "Announcing sector KPI markets with Carbon Arc," about Oct 2026; Markets Media, Oct 26, 2026).
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Data lag.** Carbon Arc says data "arrives with a natural delay." Purchases from November 28–30 may be under-captured when the value is recorded on December 2. Whether that biases the year-over-year figure depends on how the lag is treated: the same lag a year earlier would cancel out, but a fully filled 2025 base would not.
  - **Revisions.** Carbon Arc says its figures "may be revised." Only the value recorded on December 2 counts.
  - **Calendar mix.** The index compares the same calendar days, so weekdays don't line up across years. November 2025 had five Saturdays (the 1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th). November 2026 has four Saturdays and gains an extra Monday. This is a small mechanical headwind for beer, which skews to weekends. My rough estimate is 1–3 points. Thanksgiving falls in November both years (Nov 27, 2025 and Nov 26, 2026).
- **Scale change:** Kalshi's earlier Miller Lite contracts were quoted in percent (for example, "above −21%" for August 2026). Later contracts use the 100-based index (for example, 86 and 94 for October). The two scales are equivalent: 82 corresponds to −18%.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Carbon Arc's own recent Miller Lite readings.**
   - The public Prisms page, as surfaced in search, showed Miller Lite Point of Sale Growth at **91.5 (−8.5)**, with Point of Sale share at 25.6% (+0.4) (Carbon Arc Prisms page; snapshot date and month not visible, probably recent).
   - The August 2026 Kalshi contract had strikes from −9% to −25%, and the "above −21%" strike resolved YES. So August came in above −21% (index above 79). The strike ladder was centred around −17%, which suggests August readings were running noticeably weak.
   - October 2026 contracts used strikes around 86–94, which suggests readings in roughly the high 80s to low 90s.
   - **Implied base rate:** in every month I could see (August, the October setup, and the current snapshot), the index looks to have been above 82. However, August appears to have been within about 5 points of the line. Monthly swings of 5–10 points look plausible for this panel.
2. **Industry scanner data for Miller Lite, 2025–2026:**
   - Circana, 52 weeks to April 19 (probably 2026): volume −7% (Fingers newsletter).
   - Bernstein, 4 weeks to early June 2026: volume −9.5%, Coors Light −8.5%, Molson Coors −7.5% over 12 weeks (Vinetur, Jun 10, 2026).
   - Circana year-to-date, probably 2025: dollars −5.8%, volume −6.8% (Brewbound).
   - Brand-level scanner declines in high single digits mean dollar sales down roughly 5–9%. That would put a "true" index around 91–95, well above 82. I found no Circana or Nielsen period in 2025–26 where Miller Lite dollar sales fell 18% or more.
3. **Category-wide declines:**
   - U.S. beer shipments −3.9% for January–September 2026, with Miller Lite losing about 20 basis points of share (Citi via Vinetur, Nov 11, 2026).
   - U.S. industry down more than 4% in Q3 2026 (Molson Coors call, Nov 5, 2026).
   - Even severe brand shocks rarely reach −18% in dollars. The Bud Light boycott in 2023 did, which shows a tail of that size takes something extraordinary.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **YES (index above 82):**
  - Miller Lite keeps its 2026 run-rate of roughly −5% to −10% in dollars.
  - The receipt panel tracks that trend, as the 91.5 snapshot suggests.
  - Holiday stock-up for Thanksgiving falls inside November both years.
  - Improving share trends (from the Q3 call) and Brewers-related activity help a little.
  - This needs only "business as usual" with ordinary panel noise.
- **NO (index at or below 82):**
  - **Panel noise or composition change.** August's strike ladder hints the panel can print in the mid-to-high 80s or lower even when scanner data shows high single-digit declines. App-based receipt panels can shift with user churn and changes in retailer coverage.
  - **Calendar headwind.** Losing a Saturday plus Thanksgiving-week timing could subtract about 1–3 points.
  - **A strong November 2025 base.** If Miller Lite ran heavy holiday promotions in November 2025, the comparison gets harder. I could not verify this.
  - **Deeper promotional pullback.** Molson Coors is under margin pressure from aluminum costs and its guidance cut, which could mean less discounting this holiday season.
  - **Late-month data lag.** If the 2026 data is under-filled when recorded but the 2025 base is complete, the reading could be dragged down.

## 5. Signposts
- **The live Carbon Arc Prisms reading for November month-to-date** is the single most important signal. A reading in the high 80s to 90s by late November strongly favours YES. A reading near 85 or lower makes this close to a coin flip.
- **The final October 2026 value.** Above 86 is reassuring; below 82 is a strong NO signal.
- **Circana or Bernstein 4-week scans in November** showing Miller Lite dollars down more than 12% would move toward NO. Declines in the single digits would move toward YES.
- **Thanksgiving-week scans and any news of reduced Molson Coors holiday promotion.**
- **Any Carbon Arc methodology or panel change, or a large revision to prior months.**

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not get the actual Carbon Arc history for Miller Lite: the exact August, September and October values, or month-to-date November. The Carbon Arc, Kalshi and transcript pages were blocked or refused the connection. The 91.5 figure comes from a search-result snippet with no visible date.
- I don't know how well the receipt panel tracks scanner data, or how noisy it is from month to month. The August strike ladder hints the noise is meaningful.
- I don't know what Miller Lite did in November 2025, which is the comparison base.
- It is unclear how Carbon Arc handles reporting lag at the December 2 recording time, and whether 2025 base days are equally lagged.
- I found no brand-level Miller Lite scanner data for September–November 2026. The latest figures are June (Bernstein) and the Citi share data through September.
