## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Jewelry Bonney must "officially join" the Straw Hat Pirates. In practice that means the One Piece manga (Oda/Shueisha) shows her becoming a crew member. Official Straw Hat joinings are usually unmistakable: an explicit acceptance, a "new crewmate" moment, or a group spread. Jinbe's joining in Wano is an example; Oda drew a welcome spread for it (ComicBook.com, "One Piece Welcomes Jinbe to the Family in New Straw Hat Photo").
- **Close date:** 2026-11-22 07:59 UTC. The question was created about a year ago with a one-year window.
- **Who decides:** The market creator. They wrote: "If it's still unclear at that time I will extend." That makes it unclear whether a non-joining by the close date resolves NO or gets extended. This is the biggest ambiguity in the rules. A creator who reads "she hasn't joined yet and the arc isn't over" as "unclear" may push the question past the close date. The forecasters should decide whether they are pricing "joins by Nov 22" or "eventually joins," since the creator may treat them the same.
- **Edge cases:**
  - Allied or traveling status does not count. Bonney already sailed to Elbaph with the crew.
  - Honorary status (like Vivi), anime-only or filler content, merchandise, and drafts of Bonney wearing a straw hat do not count. A UNIQLO "Luffy & Bonney" shirt is merchandise, not canon (One Piece Merch News on X, 2026).
  - The anime is on hiatus after episode 1180 (Sept 27, 2026), so anything decisive would come from the manga (ComicShopLocator, Nov 2026).
- **Chapters left before close:** Chapter 1198 came out Nov 8, 2026. Oda is on break Nov 15. Chapter 1199 is due Nov 22, 2026 in Japan; one outlet says the date comes from an insider report and is not yet confirmed by Shueisha or Viz (FandomWire, Nov 2026). Digital Jump chapters usually go up around 15:00 UTC on the Saturday before the Japanese Sunday date, which would be about Nov 21 15:00 UTC, before the close. Text spoilers usually leak 2–4 days earlier. So **only one more chapter (1199) can plausibly come out before the close.**

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **How often new Straw Hats join (counted from memory of the manga's publication history):**
   - Nine members joined in the first ~10 years of the series (1997–2008): Zoro through Brook.
   - Since Brook joined at Thriller Bark (2008), only Jinbe has joined. He was teased as a crewmate from Fishman Island (about 2011–12), said he would join after Whole Cake (2017), and officially joined in Wano (about 2020). Jinbe was "the first full addition to the crew since Brook" (ComicBook.com). That is one addition in about 18 years, or roughly 0.06 per year.
   - For any single 2-week window with one chapter, the base rate is well under 1%.
2. **Characters heavily teased as possible joiners who traveled or fought alongside the crew (from memory of the plot):**
   - Vivi (Alabasta, 2001): did not join.
   - Carrot (Zou to Wano, 2016–2019): did not join; left to rule Zou.
   - Yamato (Wano, 2020–2022): openly wanted to join, but was not taken.
   - Jinbe: did join, after about 9 years of buildup.
   - Momonosuke and Kin'emon (traveled with the crew for long stretches): did not join.
   - Roughly 1 in 5 such teased characters ended up joining. Joinings happen at the end of an arc, at the departure scene, never in the middle of a climactic fight.
3. **Crew size and narrative structure:** Fan analysis often frames a "10th seat" as possibly never filled; Jinbe is usually counted as the 10th member (ninth to join) (myanimethoughts.com, July 2026; comingsoon.net). Oda reportedly said at Jump Festa that after Elbaph only two major arcs remain: Lodestar and Laugh Tale (Critical Hits Brazil, 2026 — a single non-English source). With so few arcs left, there are few chances to add another crewmate.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES before Nov 22**
- Chapter 1199 shows Bonney asking to join and Luffy accepting. This is extremely unlikely in the middle of the Imu climax; joinings come at an arc's end.
- A possible variant: a dramatic mid-battle declaration, as Jinbe did in Wano chapter 976. That happened at the *start* of a fight, after years of setup, and Bonney's status is not set up that way. Very low probability.

**Paths to YES later (relevant if the creator extends)**
- In the Elbaph departure scene (late 2026 or early 2027), Bonney leaves Kuma in Lilith's care and joins Luffy. This could tie into her Nika-inspired powers or Kuma's wish for her to be free.
- She could join in the Lodestar or Laugh Tale arcs.

**Paths to NO**
- Bonney stays on Elbaph with Kuma and Vegapunk Lilith while the lab is built and Kuma is cured. This is the most-cited reading of chapter 1134.
- She stays an ally or forms her own crew again. She was captain of the Bonney Pirates, a Supernova crew.
- Oda keeps the crew at 10 members.
- In the near term, the question resolves NO simply because chapter 1199 will almost certainly continue the Imu fight. Even then, the creator may extend instead of resolving.

## 5. Signposts
