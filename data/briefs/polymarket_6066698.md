# Research Brief: Will Anthropic IPO by November 20, 2026?

*Prepared 2026-11-07, a Saturday. Market closes at 11:59 PM ET on Friday, Nov 20, 2026. WebFetch could not reach any news site in this environment, so everything below comes from search-result summaries of the cited articles. I could not read the full text of Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ or EDGAR.*

**Key point:** the reported plan has Anthropic shares trading in the week of Nov 16–20. The deadline is the last day of that week. If the schedule slips by even a few trading days, this resolves NO.

---

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What YES needs:** Anthropic shares are listed on a public securities exchange and open for trading by 11:59 PM ET on Friday, Nov 20, 2026. The expected venue is Nasdaq Global Select Market, ticker "ANT" (CNBC, 2026-10-26; Axios, 2026-10-26). In practice the first trade has to happen on or before Nov 20.
- **Pricing is not enough.** IPOs usually price the evening before the first trading day. If Anthropic prices on Thursday Nov 19, trading would start Friday Nov 20, which is YES. If it prices on Friday Nov 20, trading would most likely start Monday Nov 23, which is NO. One secondary report cites CoinDesk saying pricing is expected on "19 or 20 November." That is exactly the knife-edge.
- **Automatic NO:** if a company that is already public acquires Anthropic. This is very unlikely within two weeks.
- **Who decides:** SEC filings (S-1/A, 8-A, 424(b) prospectus), Nasdaq listing confirmation, and Anthropic press releases. A consensus of credible reporting is the fallback.
- **Edge cases:**
  - Tokenized "Anthropic" products, such as Ondo's planned offering to non-US investors (Crypto Briefing, ~Nov 2026), are not an exchange listing.
  - Pre-IPO secondary platforms like Forge don't count either.
  - A direct listing would count if it trades on an exchange, but the filings describe an underwritten IPO.
  - A trading halt or delayed open on Nov 20 that never actually opens would most likely be NO.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. Time from public S-1 to first trade for recent mega and high-profile IPOs that first filed confidentially.** The S-1 must be public at least 15 days before the roadshow starts.

| Company | Public S-1 | First trade | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arm | Aug 21, 2023 | Sep 14, 2023 | ~24 |
| Reddit | Feb 22, 2024 | Mar 21, 2024 | ~28 |
| SpaceX | May 20, 2026 | Targeted Jun 12, 2026 | ~23 (I could not confirm the actual listing) |

- The typical gap is about 23–28 days. Applied to Anthropic's Oct 26 filing, that gives a first trade between about **Nov 18 and Nov 23**, which straddles the deadline.
- Roadshows for mega IPOs usually last 6–9 trading days: SpaceX about June 4 to 11, Arm about Sep 5 to 13, Alibaba Sep 8 to 18 (2014).
- The earliest permitted roadshow start is about **Nov 10** (Oct 26 + 15 days). From there, the fastest realistic first trade is about **Nov 18–20**.
- So even with no slippage, YES depends on Anthropic using close to the fastest possible schedule.

**B. Large IPOs that publicly filed or set a timetable during market turmoil or valuation pushback.**

Postponed:
- WeWork, 2019: public S-1, then investors pushed back on valuation and the deal was withdrawn.
- Saudi Aramco, Oct 2019: delayed its launch to wait for Q3 results, then completed in Dec 2019. This parallels Anthropic's blank October revenue.
- Klarna, 2025: public filing in March, paused in April after the tariff sell-off, listed in Sep 2025.
- Ant Group, 2020: suspended by regulators.

Completed roughly on schedule despite turbulence:
- Uber, May 2019 (trade-war sell-off), Visa (Mar 2008), Facebook (2012), Arm (2023).

From memory, I put this rough sample at about 40–50% postponing once the timetable came under visible market stress. That is a soft estimate, not a systematic count.

**C. Anthropic's own record.** Its timing has already slipped once. A target of late October, before the midterms, was reported in September and then moved to November (Motley Fool/Yahoo, 2026-09-26; PYMNTS, Sep 2026).

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (all need near-perfect execution):**
1. **On schedule.**
   - An S-1/A with a price range is filed about Nov 9–10.
   - Roadshow runs about Nov 10–18; the SEC declares the registration effective.
   - Pricing about Nov 18–19, first trade Nov 19–20.
   - This depends on markets stabilizing, Anthropic accepting a lower valuation (about $1–1.3T) or a smaller raise, and October revenue being added quickly.
2. **Compressed roadshow.** Demand is strong enough to accelerate the book. Mega IPOs sometimes close books early, but a $80–100B deal makes that harder.

**Paths to NO:**
1. **Short slip.** The roadshow starts about Nov 12–16 and the first trade lands Nov 23–25, before Thanksgiving. The reported target of "trading before Thanksgiving" fits this path, and it resolves NO.
2. **Pricing on Friday Nov 20,** with trading on Monday Nov 23.
3. **Postponement to December or 2027** because of the AI sell-off, valuation disagreement, or the wait for October or Q4 numbers. Reuters is explicitly reporting this as a possibility.
4. **Regulatory or political shock:** SEC comments, an escalation of the DoD dispute, or post-midterm AI policy risk.

## 5. Signposts (direction and rough size)

- **S-1/A with a price range and share count filed Nov 9–10:** strongly toward YES. It confirms the roadshow launch and the most likely first trade is about Nov 18–20.
- **No price-range S-1/A by about Nov 11–12:** strongly toward NO. There would be too little time for a normal 6-day roadshow.
- **Reports of the expected pricing date:** "prices Wednesday Nov 18 or Thursday Nov 19" points to YES. "Prices Nov 20" or "trades week of Nov 23" points to NO.
- **Form 8-A filed and Nasdaq listing approval:** these come just before trading and confirm a YES.
- **Order book reports:** "multiple times oversubscribed" or "books covered on day one" point to YES, or even early pricing. A price-range cut or a smaller raise means the deal is struggling but still alive, which is mixed.
- **Market moves:** AI-stock rebounds or weakness, particularly around Nvidia's earnings.
- **Reuters, Bloomberg or WSJ "postpone" headline:** decisively NO.
