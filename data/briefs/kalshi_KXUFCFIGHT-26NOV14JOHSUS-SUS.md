# Research Brief: "Baisangur Susurkaev wins" (Donte Johnson vs. Baisangur Susurkaev, UFC 334, originally Nov 14, 2026)

**Bottom line:** Susurkaev has pulled out of this fight. Several outlets report that Cesar Almeida replaced him against Donte Johnson at UFC 334, and that Susurkaev has since been booked against Damian Pinas at UFC 335 on Dec 12, 2026. Under the rules, this market will almost certainly be settled as a cancelled fight, at a "fair price." It will not be settled by a fight result.

*Method note: every page fetch was refused by the network proxy in this environment. All facts below come from search-result summaries of the cited articles. I could not read any article's full text.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES:** Susurkaev wins the Donte Johnson vs. Susurkaev fight "originally scheduled for Nov 14, 2026" (UFC 334, Madison Square Garden).
- **Draw or no contest:** settles at $0.50.
- **Postponement:** if the fight starts within 48 hours of its original start time, it still settles on the official result.
- **Cancelled, or not started within 48 hours:** "both markets will resolve to a fair price." Kalshi decides that price. The rules don't say how it is calculated; it could be something like the last traded price or a pre-cancellation value. **This is the main ambiguity, and it is the one that matters, because the fight appears to be cancelled.**
- **Replacement opponent:** Johnson vs. Almeida is a different matchup and should not count. A strict reading of "Donte Johnson vs Baisangur Susurkaev" means that bout does not settle this market.
- Results revised after expiration are ignored. The market closes 2026-11-17 00:00 UTC.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **Withdrawn UFC fights that get reinstated on the same card:** very rare. It mostly happens with weight or medical scares in fight week, not with a fighter who has already been rebooked elsewhere. Susurkaev is now booked for Dec 12 against Pinas, and Johnson has a replacement opponent, so I estimate the chance of reinstatement at well under 1%. This is a qualitative estimate from how the UFC usually books; I did not calculate a base rate.
- **Hypothetical fight result (only matters if the fight is somehow reinstated):** Susurkaev is 12-0 with 11 finishes (9 KO/TKO, 2 submissions) and 3-0 in the UFC. Johnson is 9-0 per most recent reports, 3-0 in the UFC (UFC.com showed 8-0 after his Mar 2026 fight). Among UFC fights between two unbeaten prospects, the more decorated finisher tends to win roughly 60–70% of the time. That is a rough heuristic, not a computed figure.
- **Susurkaev's reliability:** one report says he "had to pull out again," which suggests at least one earlier withdrawal. He was also reported to have suffered multiple injuries in his UFC 328 win in May 2026 (AgentMMA).

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **~Nov 4, 2026:** Susurkaev vs. Damian Pinas (11-1) is booked for UFC 335 on Dec 12 at T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas. Vestnik MMA reported it first. It replaces a planned Susurkaev vs. Ryan Gandra bout after Gandra dropped out. The stories say Susurkaev "had been scheduled to face Donte Johnson at UFC 334 before withdrawing" (Bloody Elbow, Nov 4, 2026; SI/Yahoo; BJPenn; MMASucka).
- **Between the UFC 334 withdrawal and early Nov:** Susurkaev vs. Gandra was targeted for UFC 335, then cancelled (AgentMMA news #91307, #91631).

Some outlets called the Almeida booking a report that UFC had not yet confirmed.
- **Some sites are out of date.** Combat Edge, UFCalendar, Tapology summaries and some full-card articles still list Johnson vs. Susurkaev. These look like stale pages.
- **Other UFC 334 changes:** Adrian Yanez and Jefferson Nascimento have also withdrawn. The headliner is Gane vs. Hokit on Nov 14.
- **Background:** Susurkaev beat Djorden Santos by rear-naked choke at UFC 328 (May 9, 2026). He KO'd Eric McConico at UFC 322 (Nov 15, 2025; Cageside Press). He debuted on late notice at UFC 319 (Aug 2025). He is a Kill Cliff FC teammate of Khamzat Chimaev.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **NO / fair-price path (dominant):** the fight stays cancelled and Kalshi settles both sides at its "fair price." The YES payout then depends entirely on how Kalshi calculates that price, which I could not establish.
- **YES path (remote):** the Johnson vs. Susurkaev fight is reinstated and starts within 48 hours of the original time, and Susurkaev wins. This would require him to drop out of the Dec 12 Pinas booking, the Almeida bout to collapse, and the UFC to re-pair the original fighters. All three are very unlikely.
- **Interpretation risk:** if Kalshi treated Johnson vs. Almeida as the same contract, Susurkaev could not win it anyway, so YES is still not possible.

## 5. Signposts
- An official UFC 334 card from UFC.com, or weigh-in results on Nov 13, showing Johnson vs. Almeida would confirm cancellation.
- Any Kalshi notice about this market, such as early determination or the fair-price method, would show what YES actually pays out.
- Susurkaev dropping off UFC 335, or Almeida withdrawing, would only slightly raise the tiny chance of reinstatement.

## 6. Uncertainties
- The exact date of the withdrawal and the reason for it. The reporting says "undisclosed," and it may be injury-related given the UFC 328 injury report.
- Whether UFC has officially confirmed Johnson vs. Almeida. Some outlets called it reported but not confirmed.
- How Kalshi calculates the "fair price" for cancelled fights.
- Johnson's exact record (8-0 vs. 9-0). One unconfirmed source reported a UFC 330 win over McConico.
