# Research brief: Will LSU beat Texas on Nov 14, 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES if:** LSU wins the Texas at LSU game scheduled for Saturday, Nov 14, 2026, at Tiger Stadium in Baton Rouge. The official final result decides it, so overtime counts. There is no tie in college football, so the market resolves YES or NO unless the game is cancelled.
- **Kickoff:** 6:30 p.m. CT (7:30 p.m. ET) on ABC. The SEC set this late on Saturday, Nov 7, after saying earlier that it would be either 2:30 or 6:30 p.m. CT (TigerDroppings, ~Nov 7–8, 2026; Texas Athletics game center). Some older pages show 1:00 p.m. ET or TBA. Those are out of date.
- **Postponement:** If the game is delayed but starts within 48 hours of the scheduled kickoff, it resolves on the actual result. If it is cancelled, or doesn't start within 48 hours, Kalshi settles at a "fair price" that it decides. The market closes Nov 16, 2026, 20:00 UTC.
- **Edge cases:** A mid-November night game in Baton Rouge carries very little weather or cancellation risk. Lightning delays only push the result later. Disputed calls don't matter: the official final score stands. Texas's Nov 7 win at Missouri included a contested fumble call, which shows how much officiating can matter, but it doesn't affect how this market settles (OutKick/Fox News, Nov 2026).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Home teams in evenly matched Power-conference games.** Home field is usually worth about 2.5–3 points, which turns a pick'em into roughly a 56–58% home win. When the teams are close in quality, the home side wins about 55–60% of the time. These numbers are general modeling conventions, not something I verified for this brief.
2. **Rating-based models for this game:**
   - ESPN FPI gave LSU **54.7% → 57.1% → 59.0%** across successive pre-Alabama snapshots (247Sports FPI series, Oct–early Nov 2026). All of these were taken when LSU was 6-2, before the 31-13 home loss to Alabama.
   - Updated FPI ratings after Week 10: **Texas No. 6 (23.9), LSU No. 7 (22.5)** (Saturday Down South / Yardbarker FPI update, ~Nov 8–9, 2026).
   - My rough estimate from those ratings: Texas is about 1.4 points better on a neutral field, and adding FPI's home edge makes LSU about a 1-point favorite, or roughly **52–55% LSU**. This is my inference, not ESPN's published number.
   - Pre-Alabama SP+ had LSU No. 4 (24.7) and Texas No. 7 (22.1) (Yahoo, late Oct 2026). LSU has probably dropped since.
   - AccuScore's simulation says LSU 62%, but its page calls both teams undefeated, so it is probably stale (AccuScore, accessed Nov 9, 2026).
3. **Coach records in big games:**
   - Lane Kiffin is **15-27 (36%) against ranked opponents** for his career, after the Alabama loss (SI/FanNation, Nov 2026). He is 0-6 against Alabama.
   - In 2026, LSU is **0-3 against ranked or quality SEC teams** (Ole Miss, Kentucky, Alabama). Its best wins are Clemson 51-10, Texas A&M 35-6, and Mississippi State 31-0.
   - Texas has three wins over teams ranked in the top 15 of the first CFP rankings: Ole Miss, Ohio State, and Tennessee (Burnt Orange Nation, Nov 3, 2026).
   - Kiffin's record against ranked teams doesn't separate home and away games, so it counts for less than reference class 1 or 2.
4. **Head-to-head:** The last meeting was LSU 45-38 at Texas on Sept 7, 2019. This is the first meeting since Texas joined the SEC. It is a sample of one and tells us little.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (LSU wins):**
- Home field at night in Tiger Stadium, against a Texas team on its second straight SEC road game.
- LSU's passing game moves the ball. It had 380 yards and 457 total against Alabama, and the losses came from red-zone and fourth-down failures, which tend to even out over time.
- Texas's injuries add up: a backup left tackle facing LSU's pass rush, no Smothers, Coleman limited.
- A letdown or travel spot for Texas after an emotional road win.
- Manning playing a "homecoming" game in Louisiana could cut either way.

**Paths to NO (Texas wins):**
- Texas has the better 2026 résumé and has played its best football in the last three games.
- Manning is playing efficiently, with zero turnovers at Missouri.
- LSU has lost every top-tier game it has played this year, and Kiffin's record against ranked teams is poor.
- LSU's motivation could fade now that its playoff chances are almost gone. Texas is playing for a playoff spot at 74.5% per FPI.
- LSU's defensive depth is thin at linebacker and safety, and it may now be missing a starting right tackle.

**Key dependencies:** how healthy the offensive lines are (Goosby is out for Texas, Davis is uncertain for LSU), whether Coleman plays, whether Weeks is back, and the turnover margin. In a game rated close to even, turnovers swing the result the most.
