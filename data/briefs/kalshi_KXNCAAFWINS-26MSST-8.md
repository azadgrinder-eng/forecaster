# Research brief: Will Mississippi State win 8 or more regular-season games in 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts for YES:** Mississippi State needs at least 8 wins in official 2026 regular-season games. Bowl games, conference championship games and College Football Playoff games don't count, and ties don't count as wins. Kalshi resolves the market.
- **Current state:** The Bulldogs are 4-4 overall and 2-4 in the SEC after 8 of 12 regular-season games (CBS Sports team page, Nov 2026; Wikipedia 2026 MSU page, as of Nov 1, 2026). Four games remain, so **YES requires winning all four (4-0 finish).** A 7-5 or worse finish resolves NO.
- **Remaining schedule (most outlets agree):**
  - Sat Nov 7: vs Vanderbilt (home, 3:15 pm CT, SEC Network)
  - Sat Nov 14: vs Auburn (home)
  - Sat Nov 21: vs Tennessee Tech (home, FCS)
  - Fri Nov 27: at Ole Miss (Egg Bowl, Oxford, 11 a.m., ABC). The school's May 12, 2026 release moved it to Black Friday. Some older pages still list Nov 21 or Nov 28.
- **Edge cases:**
  - No byes appear to remain that could hide an extra game.
  - A cancelled game that isn't made up would leave only 3 games, which makes 8 wins impossible.
  - The market closes Dec 20, after the regular season ends, so the timing is fine.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Treating the four remaining games as independent model probabilities.** TeamRankings simulation odds: Vanderbilt 82.2%, Auburn 55.1%, Tennessee Tech about 99.9%, at Ole Miss 13.8% (TeamRankings projections page, accessed Nov 2026).
   - Product: 0.822 × 0.551 × 0.999 × 0.138 ≈ **6.2%**.
   - Swapping in the Dimers model's 59.1% for Vanderbilt (SI analytics piece, ~Nov 5, 2026) gives about **4.5%**.
   - TeamRankings' most likely final record is 7-5, with 6.5 projected wins.
   - Note: these models may not fully reflect the quarterback injuries described in section 3.
2. **The Egg Bowl in Oxford when Ole Miss is a top-12 team.** Ole Miss is No. 10 in the first CFP rankings and 6-2 (Yahoo/CBS, Nov 3, 2026), coming off a 13-2 CFP season in 2025.
   - From memory, not verified this session: Ole Miss has won roughly 4 of the last 5 Egg Bowls (2021, 2023, 2024, 2025), and MSU's lone win was 24-22 in Oxford in 2022.
   - Teams given about 14% by a model win about that often. So the Egg Bowl alone caps YES at roughly 10–15% even if MSU wins the other three.
3. **Lebby-era Mississippi State and teams on long losing streaks.**
   - The team started 4-0 and has now lost 4 straight: LSU 0-31 (Oct 17), Oklahoma 15-23 (Oct 24), Texas 3-31 (Oct 31), plus one more conference loss.
   - Lebby is 0-10 in October games during his tenure (Yahoo/On3 Texas recap, Nov 1, 2026).
   - MSU went 1-7 in the SEC in 2025, and its preseason 2026 win total was reported at 4.5 (Covers/Yahoo win-total pages). The team has already beaten preseason expectations.
   - It is rare for a 4-4 SEC team on a four-game losing streak with a backup quarterback to win four straight to close the season, including a road game against a top-10 team. I estimate low single digits, as a rough judgment.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 4–6, 2026:** Vanderbilt preview.
  - Vanderbilt is 3-5 (0-5 SEC) on a five-game losing streak and comes in off a bye. Its starting quarterback is 5-star freshman Jared Curtis: 65% completions, 1,489 yards, 8 TD, 8 INT.
  - MSU is favored by about 2.5–3 points. Dimers gives MSU 59.1% and projects 26-22. ESPN FPI ranks MSU No. 42 and Vanderbilt No. 71.
  - MSU has won 5 straight in the series and leads it 15-7-2.
  - Sources: WKRN preview; SI "What the analytical models reveal"; Vanderbilt Hustler, Nov 4, 2026.

He was not listed on the first SEC availability report, so his status is unclear. Lebby gave no definitive update. Freshman Parker "Billy" Puckett may have to start; he struggled in two earlier starts. (Yahoo injury report; Yahoo "Three Bold Predictions," early Nov 2026.)
- **Nov 3, 2026:** First CFP rankings put Ole Miss at No. 10 (6-2, two ranked wins and two ranked losses); the AP poll has them at No. 12. Ole Miss hosts No. 2 Georgia on Nov 7. Ole Miss beat Auburn 45-15 on Oct 31. (Yahoo; CBS; ESPN.)

- **Auburn (Nov 14 opponent):** 4-4 (2-4) under first-year coach Alex Golesh. Lost 45-15 at Ole Miss Oct 31 and lost to LSU 38-17 earlier. Hosts Arkansas Nov 7, then visits Starkville. (ESPN schedule; Wikipedia 2026 Auburn.)
- **Injuries:** Yahoo reported Lebby calling the injury list "unlike anything" he has seen in his career.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **Path to YES (all of these must happen):**
  - Beat Vanderbilt. This is a modest favorite at about 60–80%, and depends on Swann's health.
  - Beat Auburn at home. Roughly a coin flip, about 50–55%.
  - Beat Tennessee Tech (FCS). Near certain, about 97–99%.
  - Win at Ole Miss. Roughly 10–15%, with the likeliest upset routes being:
    - Ole Miss gets eliminated from CFP contention (for example by losing to Georgia) and comes in flat.
    - Ole Miss has its own key injuries.
    - MSU's running game, led by Fluff Bothwell, controls the game.
- **Paths to NO:** any single loss. The most likely are Ole Miss (about 85%+), Auburn (about 45%) and Vanderbilt (about 20–40%). The quarterback situation is the biggest common factor and makes the losses correlated rather than independent.

## 5. Signposts
- **Nov 7 vs Vanderbilt:**
  - A loss ends the market at NO.
  - A win raises YES only modestly, to roughly 7–10%.
  - Watch who starts at quarterback: Swann helps, Puckett hurts.
- **Nov 7, Georgia at Ole Miss:**
  - If Ole Miss loses, it may drop out of the CFP picture. That could lower its motivation slightly, but it remains a strong home favorite.
  - Also watch for any Ole Miss quarterback or major injuries.
- **Nov 14 vs Auburn:** a loss ends the market. A win, plus a likely win over Tennessee Tech, makes the outcome mostly the Egg Bowl, at roughly 12–18% depending on the spread.
- **The Egg Bowl spread:** expect Ole Miss to be favored by about two touchdowns or more. A spread under 10 would raise MSU's chances meaningfully.
