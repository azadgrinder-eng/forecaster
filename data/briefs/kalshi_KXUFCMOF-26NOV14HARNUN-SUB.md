## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **Paths to YES**, which require a finish in round 4 or 5 if the live-blog evidence holds:
  - Harrison keeps her top control and takes Nunes's back or isolates an arm late, as she did against Peña and Holm. Nunes being tired late in a grappling-heavy fight makes this more likely.
  - Much less likely: Nunes catches a guillotine or armbar while Harrison is fatigued.
- **Paths to NO:**
  - A decision. If Harrison was up 30-27 or 29-28 going into the championship rounds, this is the most likely outcome.
  - A TKO from Harrison's ground-and-pound, which was already her main offence in round 3.
  - A Nunes knockout late in the fight, especially if Harrison faded after a hard weight cut.
