# Research brief: Will Mark Carney visit Ukraine by December 31, 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Carney must physically enter Ukraine's land or sea territory between market creation and Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET (close is 2027-01-01 04:59 UTC). Flying through Ukrainian airspace does not count. The question is resolved by a "consensus of credible reporting."
- **Time left:** As of Nov 18, 2026, about 6 weeks remain (Nov 19 to Dec 31).
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Unannounced trips.** Leader visits to Kyiv are kept secret until arrival or departure. Carney's Aug 2025 Ukraine leg was under a media blackout (Global News / CBC, Aug 24, 2025). A visit on Dec 30–31 might only be reported after the deadline. It would still count, because the question asks when he entered Ukraine, not when it was reported.
  - **Short or border-area visits.** A brief trip to western Ukraine (Lviv, Uzhhorod) would count.
  - **Already-past visit.** Carney's only confirmed visit was Kyiv on Aug 24, 2025. That is almost certainly before the market was created, so it should not count. I could not verify the creation date.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Canadian prime minister visits to Ukraine since the full-scale invasion.** There have been five in about 4.7 years:
   - Trudeau: May 2022, June 2023, Feb 24 2024, Feb 24 2025 (the last confirmed in UNN / Euro Pravda, Feb 24, 2025)
   - Carney: Aug 24, 2025 (CBC, Aug 2025)

   That is about 1.06 visits per year. Spread evenly over a 43-day window, it gives roughly an 11–12% chance (Poisson). But **none of the five fell between mid-November and December**. They cluster on symbolic dates (Feb 24 anniversary, Aug 24 Independence Day) or come attached to European tours. Adjusted for timing, the rate for this window is lower, probably in the mid-single digits.
2. **Carney's own record in 2026.** He has made zero visits in about 10.5 months, despite the two most likely occasions:
   - Feb 24, 2026 (fourth anniversary): EU, Nordic-Baltic and Croatian leaders went to Kyiv. Canada was not named among them (Euromaidan Press, Feb 24, 2026).
   - Aug 24, 2026 (Independence Day): Carney joined a virtual Coalition of the Willing meeting and issued a statement (PMO readout, Aug 24, 2026). Meanwhile UK PM Burnham and European leaders went to Kyiv in person (ABC/AP, Aug 24, 2026).

   This suggests Carney sees a once-per-term or once-every-year-or-two cadence as enough.
3. **G7 or major-ally leader visits to Kyiv in November–December.** These happen but are uncommon. From memory, not verified this session: Sunak in Nov 2022 and Scholz in Dec 2024. Visits in 2026 have mostly been by European leaders, often on short trips:
   - Tusk, Feb 5 (Euro Pravda)
   - Bulgarian PM, Mar 30 (Euro Pravda)
   - Starmer, Jul 16, days before leaving office (Ukrainska Pravda)
   - Burnham, Aug 24 (AP)
   - Finland's PM Orpo, Oct 22 (Euro Pravda)

   A non-European leader in December needs a stronger reason to make the trip.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 11–19, 2026:** Carney is on a trip to Ireland, the Philippines, Cambodia and China. It ends at the APEC summit in Shenzhen (Nov 18–19), and he is due back in Ottawa on Nov 19 (BNN Bloomberg / CTV, Nov 11, 2026). The trip does not include Ukraine.
- **Nov 12–13, 2026, European Political Community summit in Dublin:** Carney attended a peace meeting on Ukraine co-hosted by Burnham and Zelenskyy. He described "tiers" of security guarantees: Europe, with Canada, with the US (PMO, Nov 12, 2026; Radio-Canada International). Europe is still working out who represents it in peace talks. Zelenskyy asked for a decision by the end of November, and formalization may come in December (Boston Globe / Al Jazeera, Nov 13, 2026).
- **Nov 10–11, 2026:** US-mediated Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi (Witkoff and Kushner mediating, Medinsky leading Russia's side) were the first direct talks since February. They produced agreement in principle on a prisoner swap but **no ceasefire and no deal on territory**. Lavrov rejected European ceasefire demands (Washington Post / NPR / Atlantic Council, Nov 10–11, 2026).
- **Oct 2026:**
  - Carney and Zelenskyy spoke by phone on Oct 11 (PMO readout).
  - Zelenskyy had recently visited Canada, where $355M for air defence was announced.

  The relationship is being kept up through calls and visits to Canada, not trips to Kyiv.
- **Sept 3, 2026:** Carney said he would tell Putin to "get out of Ukraine" if they meet at the G20 (CKPG / CP).
- **June 16, 2026:** Carney met Zelenskyy at the G7 summit in Évian, France (PMO readout).
- **Jan 6, 2026:** Canada co-signed the Paris declaration of the Coalition of the Willing on post-ceasefire security. Carney called Canadian troops on the ground only a "possibility." Possible roles include training and logistics, and the Army is at its "force generation sustainability limit" (CTV / CP, Jan 2026).
- **Scheduled before Dec 31:**
  - G20 leaders' summit in Miami (Trump National Doral), Dec 14–15. Carney is expected to attend, but this is not confirmed.
  - A possible European decision on peace-talk representation and a Coalition of the Willing meeting in December.
  - The House of Commons usually sits until about mid-December (typical calendar, not verified for 2026).
  - Then the Christmas break.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES:**
- **(a) Breakthrough in the peace talks.** A ceasefire or framework deal, or a signing ceremony or Coalition of the Willing summit held in Kyiv, could draw leaders there. Canada's planned role in a multinational "reassurance force" would give Carney a reason to go. This depends on movement Russia has so far resisted.
- **(b) A trip added onto other European travel in December.** He has no announced European travel in December. He was just in Europe in mid-November and did not go to Ukraine then.
- **(c) A visit to Canadian troops or trainers in Ukraine.** Unlikely, since no deployment exists before a ceasefire.
- **(d) A symbolic winter-solidarity visit** during Russia's strikes on energy infrastructure, as Finland's PM did on Oct 22.

**Paths to NO (the default):**
- He has just finished a long trip and faces a domestic December, the G20 in Florida and the holidays.
- His pattern is to support Ukraine virtually or at summits.
- The talks are stalled, so there is no ceremony to attend.
- Security and logistics are harder in winter.

## 5. Signposts
- **Up:**
  - A ceasefire or framework agreement announced, or a leaders' meeting of the Coalition of the Willing or "Europe's representatives" set to be held in Kyiv.
  - Carney announcing European travel in December, for example to Brussels, Paris, London or Poland.
  - The PMO announcing travel with no stated destination.
  - Canada committing troops, trainers or a large new aid package that it would want to deliver in person.
- **Down:**
  - Confirmation that Carney is going to the G20 in Miami, combined with a full domestic agenda.
  - Talks collapsing or stalling into 2027.
  - The House sitting until late December, or a domestic political crisis.

## 6. Uncertainties
- The market creation date (whether the Aug 2025 visit is excluded).
- Whether some unreported or overlooked Carney visit to Ukraine happened in 2026. Several searches found none, and the Wikipedia trip list, as summarized in search results, shows none.
- Carney's December 2026 schedule and the Commons sitting calendar.
- Whether Carney will attend the G20.
- Where the Abu Dhabi/European talks track goes after late November.
- Several pages (Wikipedia lists, PMO releases) could not be fetched directly. Details come from search summaries, a few of which had dating errors.
