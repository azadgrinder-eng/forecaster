# Research brief: Will Comerciantes Unidos be relegated from the 2026 Peru Liga 1?

**Bottom line:** Comerciantes Unidos played its last match on November 15, 2026 and finished with about 37–38 points, in 13th place. None of the teams still fighting relegation can get above 33 points. Unless an official body takes points away from the club, it has stayed up. Note: the page-fetch tool was blocked by the proxy, so every figure below comes from search-result summaries of the articles, not from reading the pages directly.

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES** if Comerciantes Unidos loses its Liga 1 place for 2027 in any of three ways: its final position in the 2026 Liga 1, losing a relegation playoff, or "any other official league decision recognized by Peru Liga 1". That last path covers points deductions, sanctions or a licensing decision.
- **NO** if the season is cancelled, postponed past Dec 31, 2026 11:59 PM ET, or no team is confirmed relegated in time.
- **Who decides:** official Liga 1 / FPF announcements, or a consensus of credible reporting.
- **Format:** 18 teams play a Torneo Apertura and a Torneo Clausura of 17 rounds each, so 34 matches per team. Relegation is decided on the combined table for the year (*tabla acumulada*), and point deductions are applied to that table. 2026 coverage describes **two** relegation spots: UTC, plus one more decided on the last matchday ("¿quién acompañará a UTC en el descenso?", Caretas, Nov 2026). I found no relegation playoff (*promoción*) in 2026 reporting, but I could not confirm the regulation text.
- **Edge cases:**
  - Late points deductions from debt cases, decided by the FPF's dispute chamber (*Cámara de Conciliación*) or by FIFA. In 2026 UTC lost 10 points, FC Cajamarca 2 and ADT 1 this way (Libero, Nov 2026; Libero, Aug 15, 2026).
  - Rulings by the FPF disciplinary commission (CD-FPF) that award or remove match results.
  - Whether a licensing refusal for 2027 would count as "relegation". The wording is ambiguous.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **Teams clear of the relegation zone after their final match whose outcome is later reversed in Peru:** rare, but administrative changes are common.
  - In Nov 2025 the CD-FPF overturned an Ayacucho FC win and awarded Comerciantes Unidos a 3-0 result. That pushed Ayacucho into the relegation zone and helped save Comerciantes (ESPN; Peru21; Libero, Nov 20, 2025).
  - That reversal came from a case already pending before the season ended. I found no Peruvian case of a team about 5 points clear after its last match being relegated afterwards. This is a rough estimate; I did not check it systematically.
- **Points deductions in the 2026 season:** at least 3 of 18 clubs (about 17%) were docked points for debts or sanctions: UTC −10, Cajamarca −2, ADT −1. One more deduction against "UTC" was later suspended (Libero, May 7, 2026). Most of these were 1–3 points; only UTC, with repeated cases, lost more than 5.
- **Comerciantes' recent history:** it has fought relegation at the bottom of the table repeatedly. It was saved late in 2024 after Mannucci beat César Vallejo, and in 2025 through the Ayacucho ruling, finishing 13th of 19. This shows the club often finishes near the bottom, but those seasons were tighter than this one.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **NO (dominant):** the table stands as it is. Comerciantes cannot be caught on the pitch because its season is over.
- **YES paths (all administrative, all unlikely):**
  1. A points deduction of about 5–6 or more before Dec 31 that drops it below the second-to-last team. This would need several unpaid-debt rulings or a serious disciplinary case. I found no pending case.
  2. A retroactive forfeit ruling that both takes points from Comerciantes and adds points to a rival.
  3. A licensing or financial decision barring it from Liga 1 2027 that the league calls relegation. The wording is ambiguous.
  4. Larger relegation numbers or a restructuring of the league. No evidence of either.

## 5. Signposts
- Any FPF Cámara de Conciliación, CD-FPF or FIFA ruling against Comerciantes Unidos (↑ YES).
- Official confirmation of the final combined table and of the relegated clubs after the Cajamarca vs Alianza Lima match on Nov 21 (↓ YES, effectively settles it).
- 2027 licensing announcements (Dec 2026).
- Reports of unpaid players or staff at the Cutervo club (Comerciantes' home town).

## 6. Uncertainties
- Exact points: 37 or 38.
- I could not read the 2026 regulation directly. That leaves open whether exactly two teams go down, whether there is a playoff, and whether sanctions issued after the season apply to 2026.
- Whether any debt claims against Comerciantes are still pending; searches found none.
- How Cajamarca's last match and its tiebreakers turn out. This does not affect Comerciantes.
