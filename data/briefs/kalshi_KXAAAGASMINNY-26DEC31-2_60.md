## Research brief: Will AAA's New York average for regular gas be below $2.60 by Dec 31, 2026?

*Prepared Nov 24, 2026. The AAA, oil-news and EIA pages could not be opened directly because the proxy refused the connections. Every 2026 figure below comes from search-result summaries of local news and AAA/EIA pages, so treat exact cents as ±1–3¢.*

### 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES** if AAA's average price of regular gasoline for New York State is strictly below $2.60/gal "by Dec 31, 2026." The question is titled "above or below $2.60," and YES means below.
- **Data source:** AAA's daily state average at gasprices.aaa.com/?state=NY. The New York average is ~$4.01 today, so this is not the much lower national average.
- **"By Dec 31" is ambiguous.** It could mean the reading on Dec 31, or any reading on or before Dec 31. The market closes at 14:55 UTC on Dec 31, when AAA's daily figure for that date is normally already posted. In practice the ambiguity barely matters: the price would have to fall about $1.41 in about 5 weeks either way.
- **Edge cases:**
  - A NY-only metro reading is not the state average and does not count.
  - "Strictly lower" means exactly $2.60 resolves NO.
  - AAA publishes to tenths of a cent (e.g. $2.5999 vs $2.60). This only matters if the price ends up right at the threshold.

### 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **A: The largest-ever short drop in US gasoline prices (late 2008).**
  - From memory of EIA weekly retail data (approximate; I did not re-check it): the US average fell from about $3.48 (Oct 6, 2008) to about $2.2–2.3 (around Nov 10, 2008). That is roughly $1.2 (about 35%) in 5 weeks, during the financial crisis, as crude fell from ~$90–100 to ~$60.
  - New York needs about $1.41 (about 35%) in about 5 weeks. Only one episode in roughly 35 years of AAA/EIA data comes close.
  - The 2014 collapse took about 6 months to fall $1.14 (DOE Fact #858, Feb 2, 2015). The 2022 decline was about $1.40 over roughly 5 months.
  - Rough base rate for a ≥35% drop in 5 weeks: about 1 such window in 1,800+ weekly windows, i.e. below 0.1%. Even conditional on a crude crash already under way, it is rare.
- **B: How often New York's average is below $2.60 at all.**
  - Over the past ~5.5 years, AAA's NY average has essentially never been below $2.60. My recollection, not re-checked, is that the last time was around early 2021 during the pandemic.
  - Throughout 2025 New York stayed around $2.95–3.20. NY averaged $3.06 for 2025; EIA's weekly NY low was $2.93 in the week of Dec 29, 2025; AAA showed $3.04 on Dec 29, 2025 (USGasGauge NY 2025; AAA Dec 29, 2025 survey via search).
  - So even the pre-war, oversupplied market of late 2025, with the national average under $3, did not get New York to $2.60.
- **C: Price structure.**
  - New York retail ≈ RBOB wholesale futures + about $0.50–0.55 in taxes (18.4¢ federal + NY excise, petroleum business tax and sales tax) + distribution and margin.
  - Late-2025 New York of ~$3.05 went with RBOB of about $1.90–2.00. A New York price of $2.60 implies RBOB of roughly $1.45–1.55, which historically goes with WTI of about $45–55.
  - Today RBOB is about $2.47 and WTI about $80–84.

- **Nov 23, 2026, crude and Hormuz:** Brent about $89.5. The Strait of Hormuz is still effectively closed: about 2 commercial transits on Nov 15 versus about 85 per day before the crisis. Tanker attacks were reported Nov 20–21. The US blockade of Iranian ports continues, and new US Treasury sanctions were announced (straits.live, Nov 24, 2026; UANI, Nov 18, 2026).

  - WTI is reportedly down more than 22% in November (FXEmpire).
- **Nov 19, 2026:** The national average fell 18¢ in a week to $3.72 (AAA release via search). It is down about 50¢ in three weeks, the steepest three-week drop since 2008 (NBC Palm Springs, Nov 19, 2026).

  - Brent forecast about $104 in November and $99 in December. Crude has since come in well below that.
  - US retail gasoline forecast $3.89 average for 2026 and $3.73 for 2027 (EIA press release 594; aroundprague.cz summary).
- **Background:**
  - The Iran war began in late February 2026. The national average was above $4 from April to early November (MoneyTalksNews).
  - A June ceasefire collapsed on July 8, and the US blockade resumed July 14.
  - Crude neared $100 in late October (AAA, Oct 22, 2026).
- **NY gas tax:** Lawmakers pushed through spring 2026 for a state gas-tax holiday. The 2022 holiday cut about 16¢/gal. I found no confirmation that a 2026 holiday was enacted; Hochul was resistant (NY Senate releases, Mar–May 2026; Spectrum).
  - Rockland County caps its sales tax on the portion of the price above $3 from June 2026 to March 2027. This is a county measure with negligible effect on the statewide average.
- **Scheduled before Dec 31:**
  - Thanksgiving, Nov 26.
  - OPEC+ meetings, likely late November or early December.
  - EIA's December STEO, around Dec 9.
  - Weekly EIA inventory reports.
  - AAA's weekly Thursday releases.
  - Ongoing Iran/Hormuz diplomacy and military events.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **Baseline path (NO):**
  - If wholesale holds near $2.45, New York retail should keep slipping toward about $3.50–3.70 by late December as the lag unwinds and winter demand stays soft.
  - Even an aggressive continuation (8–10¢/week for 5 weeks) gives about $3.50–3.60.
- **Fast-collapse path (YES needs all of these):**
  - A durable Hormuz reopening or peace deal, together with an OPEC+ supply flood or a global demand shock.
  - Crude falling from about $80 to roughly $45–50 within 2–3 weeks.
  - Retail passing that through almost immediately. Retail usually falls slowly ("rockets and feathers"), so an extra $1.41 in New York within 5 weeks has no precedent; 2008 is the only near-analog.
  - A state gas-tax suspension would add only about 16¢ and would need legislative action. The legislature is normally out of session in December.
- **Upside-risk path (firmly NO):** New tanker attacks, an end to the partial ceasefire, or Gulf infrastructure damage push crude back toward $100.

### 5. Signposts
- **Toward YES (each still leaves a large gap):**
  - Announcement of a comprehensive Iran settlement with Hormuz transits back near 85/day.
  - WTI below $55 or RBOB below $1.70 by mid-December.
  - New York's AAA average below $3.30 by Dec 15.
  - Emergency NY gas-tax suspension.
- **Toward NO:**
  - New York's weekly declines shrinking below 5¢.
  - Brent rebounding above $95.
  - More tanker attacks or mine reports.
  - The December STEO keeping Brent in the $90s.

### 6. Uncertainties
- I could not open AAA's NY page directly. The $4.01 reading (Nov 23) is secondhand.
- Exact current RBOB and crude settlements vary by source: WTI about $80–84, Brent about $89–93.
- The 2008 weekly figures and the "last time NY was below $2.60 (around early 2021)" claim are from memory, not re-checked in this session.
- Whether any 2026 NY gas-tax holiday is in effect is unconfirmed; the evidence suggests none.
- Some Hormuz tracker sites are of uneven reliability.
- The "by Dec 31" wording (any day vs. on Dec 31) is unresolved, but it barely matters here.
