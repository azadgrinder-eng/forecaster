# Research brief: Will the US Secretary of State (Marco Rubio) visit Switzerland between issuance and Jan 1, 2027?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** The sitting US Secretary of State, currently Marco Rubio, makes a real visit to Switzerland after the market's issuance date and before Jan 1, 2027. Close time is 2027-01-01 15:00 UTC.
- **What doesn't count:** Layovers, and visits to foreign embassies inside the US. Meeting Swiss officials in Washington is therefore not enough. In Aug 2025 Rubio met the Swiss president in Washington, and that would not count (Anadolu, Aug 2025).
- **Time left:** About 5 weeks (Nov 24 to Dec 31, 2026).
- **Issuance date matters, and I don't know it.** Rubio was in Geneva Nov 22–24, 2025 (State Dept countries-visited page; ABC/NPR, Nov 2025) and in Davos around Jan 21–22, 2026 (JNS/Trend/Fox, Jan 2026). If the market was issued before either trip, it should arguably already be YES. Since it is still open, the market was presumably issued after late January 2026. Forecasters should confirm this.
- **No source named:** The rules don't say what evidence decides resolution. The State Department publishes "Secretary Rubio's Travel to…" notices, daily public schedules, and a countries-visited page, so any visit would be well documented.
- **Edge cases:**
  - A day trip, such as flying into Lugano for a few hours, would almost certainly count.
  - A personal or non-official trip is not addressed by the rules.
  - A refuelling or transit stop at a Swiss airport is a "layover" and doesn't count.
  - If Rubio left office, the question presumably transfers to whoever holds the post.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. US Secretary of State attending the annual OSCE Ministerial Council in December.** This is the main scheduled path to YES. Approximate record, from my own recall except where sourced; treat it as roughly ±1:
- 2017 Vienna: Tillerson attended.
- 2018 Milan: Pompeo did not; a deputy went.
- 2019 Bratislava: Pompeo did not.
- 2020: virtual meeting; Pompeo did not attend in any real sense.
- 2021 Stockholm: Blinken attended.
- 2022 Łódź and 2023 Skopje: I believe Blinken did not attend in person at either; I am less sure about these two.
- 2024 Malta: Blinken attended.
- 2025 Vienna: Rubio skipped. The US was led by Brendan Hanrahan, a senior bureau official in the European affairs bureau, described as "likely the most junior" US representative ever at an OSCE ministerial (OSCE MC.DEL/56 US statement, Dec 2025; SHR Monitor, Dec 2025).

That gives roughly 3 of 9 overall, and roughly 1 of 5 (or 0 of 1 for Rubio) under Trump administrations.

**B. Rubio's own visits to Switzerland.** He has made 2 trips in about 22 months in office (Geneva Nov 2025 for Ukraine talks; Davos Jan 2026 for the Board of Peace launch with Trump). That is about 0.09 visits a month. Over a 5-week window with no scheduled reason to go, that implies roughly 10%. Both visits were driven by an event: high-stakes talks hosted in Geneva, or a Trump appearance. He had made about 31 trips abroad by mid-October 2026 (AP via PBS, Oct 18, 2026).

**C. Whether Rubio goes to multilateral European ministerials in December.** He skipped both the December 2025 NATO foreign ministers' meeting and the December 2025 OSCE ministerial (PBS/AP, Dec 2025; OSCE, Dec 2025). He did attend the G7 foreign ministers' meeting in Paris on Nov 8–10, 2026 (State Dept, Nov 2026) and the Munich Security Conference in Feb 2026 (CNN, Feb 14, 2026). The pattern is that he attends G7 and major security events but skips the December NATO and OSCE meetings.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES**
1. **Rubio attends the Lugano ministerial (Dec 3–4).** This is the most likely path. It is more likely if:
   - he goes to the NATO meeting in Brussels on Dec 2 and can combine the two;
   - the US wants to use the OSCE for Ukraine diplomacy, since Lavrov might attend and the meeting is a natural place to bring Europeans in, which matches Rubio's Nov 10 comments;
   - the US wants to reward the OSCE's budget reforms or press for more.

   It is less likely given Rubio skipped both the 2025 NATO and OSCE December meetings, the administration is sceptical of multilateral bodies, Rubio also serves as national security adviser, and he has tended to send lower-level officials to the OSCE.
2. **Ad hoc talks in Geneva in December.** This could be a Ukraine–Russia round with Europeans included, Iran nuclear talks, or another crisis negotiation that Rubio leads himself, as he did in Nov 2025. It depends on progress in the Abu Dhabi track.
3. **Other one-off visits:** a bilateral or UN-related event in Geneva, or a Trump trip to Switzerland that Rubio joins. Unlikely in late December.

**Paths to NO**
- Rubio skips the NATO meeting, the OSCE meeting, or both, again, and sends a deputy such as Landau or an Under Secretary, or a bureau official.
- Peace talks stay in the Gulf, the holiday period slows travel, and domestic or crisis demands (national security adviser duties, Western Hemisphere issues) keep him in Washington.
- The next natural Swiss trip, Davos in January 2027, falls just after the close.

## 5. Signposts
- **Strong move toward YES:**
  - A State Dept notice "Secretary Rubio's Travel to Belgium and Switzerland," likely posted around Nov 27–Dec 2.
  - The State Dept public schedule for Dec 3–4 listing Lugano.
  - Swiss or OSCE confirmation of the US head of delegation.
  - Reports that Lavrov is going to Lugano together with US–Russia–Ukraine contacts there.
- **Moderate move toward YES:**
  - Rubio confirmed for the Brussels NATO meeting on Dec 2, since Lugano would then be geographically convenient.
  - Announcements of Ukraine or Iran talks in Geneva in December at foreign-minister level.
- **Strong move toward NO:**
  - Announcement that Deputy Secretary Landau or another official leads the US delegation to NATO and/or the OSCE.
  - Rubio scheduled elsewhere on Dec 2–4, for example with Trump or on a Latin America or Asia trip.
  - After Dec 4 with no Lugano visit, only ad hoc Geneva talks remain as a path, so the probability should fall sharply, to around the low base rate from class B.

## 6. Uncertainties
- **Issuance date of the market:** this decides whether the Nov 2025 or Jan 2026 visits could count. Presumably it was issued after Jan 22, 2026.
- **The OSCE track record from 2018 to 2023** is partly from memory, not verified here, especially 2022 and 2023.
- **Whether Rubio attends NATO on Dec 2:** no information found.
- **The latest Swiss reporting** I found on attendance is about three weeks old; there may be newer confirmations. I couldn't open several primary pages (State Dept travel log, Wikipedia trip list, RTS, swissinfo) because the proxy refused the connection, so some details rely on search-result summaries.
- **US policy toward the OSCE in late 2026,** for example whether a withdrawal threat is still live or the US is re-engaging, is unclear beyond the March 2026 budget deal and ambassador nomination.
- **No visibility** on any December Geneva talks for Ukraine or Iran.
