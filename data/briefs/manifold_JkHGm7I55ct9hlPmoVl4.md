# Research brief: "Will Usopp die?" (closes 2026-12-06 04:59 UTC)

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What the question asks:** No resolution criteria are given, so it goes by the plain meaning. "Ussop" is almost certainly a misspelling of **Usopp**, the Straw Hat sniper in Eiichiro Oda's *One Piece*. A search for "Ussop" found no real person or other plausible subject, only unrelated "USOP" poker and pool events. Forecasters should still allow a little probability that the creator meant something else.
- **What counts as YES:** Usopp has to die, presumably in the canonical manga (or, less likely, the anime or the Netflix live action), in content released or reliably leaked by **2026-12-06 04:59 UTC**. That is midnight on Dec 6 Eastern time.
- **Content that can come out before close:**
  - Chapter 1198 came out Nov 8 (digital) / Nov 9 (print).
  - There is a break with no chapter on Nov 15 (Gamerant; FandomWire, Nov 2026).
  - Chapter 1199 is expected around **Nov 22–23** (Gamerant; FandomWire).
  - After that, roughly 1–2 more chapters (about 1200 around Nov 29–30, and 1201 around Dec 6–7) if there are no further breaks. ClayStage's fan-made projection is 1200 on Nov 30 and 1201 on Dec 7, with a break on Dec 14. It already conflicts with the other sources on the November dates, so treat it with caution.
  - The official English release on Manga Plus/VIZ is Sunday at about 15:00–16:00 UTC, so a Dec 6 chapter would officially come out **after** close. Text spoilers usually leak 2–4 days before release, though, so 1201's contents could be known by about Dec 3–4.
  - In total, about **2–3 chapters** of new content will be visible before close.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Fake-out deaths.** Usopp often "dies" for comedy or in apparent mortal danger and comes back. A creator resolving in a hurry on a cliffhanger panel of Usopp seemingly dead could cause a dispute. Note that the anime is far behind the manga.
  - **Non-canonical deaths** (alternate timelines, dreams, SBS jokes, the live action) should not count under the plain meaning.
  - **Wrong subject.** If the question creator meant a real person, pet or game character called "Ussop", none of this research applies.
  - **Who decides:** presumably the market creator, which is unspecified.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Straw Hat deaths across the whole series.** No core Straw Hat has died in about 1,198 chapters (1997–2026, about 29 years). That is 0 deaths over about 10 members × about 1,198 chapters, or 0 in roughly 12,000 member-chapters. With a Laplace-style prior (about 1 in 12,000 per member-chapter), a 3-chapter window gives about 0.025%.
2. **Oda's track record on major sympathetic characters.** On-panel deaths of major heroes or allies are very rare: Ace (Marineford, 2009), Whitebeard, Pell/Bell-mère/Kuina-style backstory deaths, Pedro (Whole Cake), and Vegapunk (Egghead, 2025). Even generously, that is fewer than 10 major ally deaths in 29 years. Oda reportedly prefers ending arcs with celebrations and as few deaths as possible. One fan site cites a 2018 Asahi Shimbun interview for this, but I could not verify the primary quote (animesenpai / escapist summaries).
3. **Usopp's own history.** He has faced at least ten near-death or "fake death" scenes, including Enies Lobby, Dressrosa (the Sugar faint, beaten by Trebol), Wano and Elbaph. He has never actually died. Fan commentary widely describes his "plot armor" (FandomWire).

**Rough base-rate takeaway:** under 0.1% for any given 3-week window. Even if the final saga raises the chance of a Straw Hat death overall, it is still well under 1% within this window.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (all very unlikely):**
- Oda kills Usopp in a heroic sacrifice during the Elbaph climax. That would break 29 years of practice and cut against the setup for Usopp's warrior-of-the-sea dream, which points toward him surviving to fulfil it.
- A loose resolution on a fake-out: Usopp is shown apparently dead in a cliffhanger, and the creator resolves before the reveal.
- A different "Ussop" turns out to be the subject (unknown chance).

**Paths to NO (dominant):**
- Usopp keeps a support role (rescuing Gaban, reacting to Luffy) through chapters 1199–1201.
- The final saga's real dangers (Imu, Holy Knights) mostly fall on Luffy, Zoro, Sanji and allied characters such as Loki and Gaban.
- Breaks reduce how much content comes out before close.

## 5. Signposts
- **Chapter 1199 leaks (about Nov 19–20) and 1200 leaks (about Nov 26–27).** The probability would rise only if Usopp ends up in direct combat with Imu or a Holy Knight, or a cliffhanger shows him gravely wounded. Even then, the base rate says recovery is very likely: Chopper and Gerd are both on Elbaph.
- **Schedule.** More breaks would push the probability down by shrinking the window.
- **The market creator's identity or comments.** These could show that "Ussop" means something other than the One Piece character.
- **Any official Oda or Jump statement** that a main character will die, which is unlikely to happen.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not read primary chapter pages (One Piece Fandom wiki, Comic Watch, Wikipedia; access was blocked). The chapter 1198 details come from search-result summaries of leak write-ups and may be slightly off.
- The exact dates for chapters 1199–1201, and whether there are more breaks before Dec 6, are not confirmed. The December dates come only from a fan projection.
- I could not verify the claimed Oda interview about avoiding main-character deaths.
- It is not completely certain that "Ussop" means the One Piece Usopp, and there is no stated rule for how fake-out or non-canonical deaths would be handled.
