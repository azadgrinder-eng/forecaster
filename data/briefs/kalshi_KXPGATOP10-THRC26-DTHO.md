## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES** if Thompson finishes in the top 10, ties included, at the 2026 RSM Classic. The event runs Nov 19–22, 2026, at Sea Island Golf Club in St. Simons Island, Georgia. It uses two courses: Seaside (par 70) and Plantation (par 72). It is the last event of the FedExCup Fall (Yahoo Sports prize-money article, Nov 2026; Golf Digest viewer's guide, Nov 2026).
- **Ties count.** Because of ties, "top 10" can cover 10–15 players. A T10 resolves YES.
- **Withdrawal or disqualification:** He has already teed off (8:52 a.m. ET Thursday, Seaside No. 1, with Harris English and Keith Mitchell). Any withdrawal, disqualification or missed cut from here resolves **NO**. The "Fair Market Price" clause, which applies only before tee-off, no longer matters.
- **Cut:** There is a 36-hole cut, normally the top 65 and ties at the RSM. If he misses it, the result is NO.
- **Timing:** The scheduled finish is Sunday, Nov 22. The market closes Nov 30, which leaves room for a weather-delayed Monday finish. The final official PGA Tour result decides the outcome.

1. **Thompson at the RSM (home event; he is a Sea Island-based player).** He has no top 10 in about 5–6 starts. 2. **Thompson's 2026 season.** A PGA Tour profile snapshot lists **4 top 10s in 2026**, out of roughly 22–26 starts, so about 15–18% per start. Known top 10s: solo 4th at the Puerto Rico Open, T8 at the Rocket Classic, T7 at the 3M Open; also T14 at the Wyndham (Aug 9, 2026) (Rotowire; PGA Tour profile). He ranked 168th in Strokes Gained: Putting (−0.932) and 8th in greens in regulation (73.98%) (PGA Tour Players betting profile, Mar 2026). I could not find any results from his fall 2026 events.
3. **Conditional on his round-1 position.** A mid-pack player who is about 6 back of the lead and 3–4 shots outside the top 10 after 18 holes, in a 156-man field, usually finishes top 10 roughly **5–12%** of the time. This is my estimate from typical golf simulation models: a per-round standard deviation of about 2.7–3 strokes gives about 5 strokes over 54 holes, and he would need to pass about 40 players. RSM scoring is low and the leaderboard is bunched, which makes it easier to jump many places but also means many rivals are moving at the same time. He also has to make the cut first.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES:**
- A low round on Plantation on Friday (65 or better, −7) puts him near the top 15 at halfway, and then two solid Seaside rounds (66–67).
- A wind-affected weekend on Seaside would favour his strong ball-striking (top-10 in greens in regulation) and reduce the edge of hot putters.
- Local knowledge and card motivation.

**Paths to NO:**
- Missing the cut. At about T51 after one round, with the cut at top 65 and ties, the risk is meaningful: roughly 25–35% if his 68 is right.
- Making the cut but finishing outside the top 10, the most likely outcome.
- Persistently poor putting (season SG: Putting −0.93).
- Bubble pressure, or playing conservatively once his card looks safe.
- His history at this event shows no finish better than T23.
