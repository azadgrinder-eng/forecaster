# Research brief: will Black Bird Group's net-advance figure for November 2026 be negative?

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What decides it:** Black Bird Group (BBG), a Finland-based OSINT group, posts a "Russian net monthly advance" figure on X (@Black_BirdGroup). The question resolves **YES** if the November 2026 figure is **negative**, meaning Russia lost more ground than it took. It resolves **NO** if the figure is **positive**.
- **How BBG measures it:** BBG uses its own front-line assessment and measures "net change between the first days of consecutive months." The November number is therefore roughly 1 Nov → 1 Dec 2026. Monthly posts have appeared in the first days of the next month (e.g. the September figure was posted about Oct 3, 2026). Expect the November post around Dec 1–7, 2026. The question closes Nov 30, but the outcome depends on a post that comes after that.
- **N/A condition:** If BBG publishes no new assessment using the same method by Jan 1, 2027, the question resolves N/A.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **A figure of exactly 0** is not covered by the rules. It is unlikely, since BBG rounds to "approximately X km²."
  - **Late confirmation of earlier moves.** BBG books changes when it confirms them, so gains from one month can land in the next. In March 2026 BBG said its first-half-of-March net was negative because "some Ukrainian penetrations from February were only confirmed in early March" (BBG March thread, Apr 6, 2026). Late October moves could therefore count toward November.
  - **Ukraine only.** The June 2026 post speaks of net-negative months "within Ukraine," so fighting on Russian territory appears to be excluded.
  - **Other trackers can disagree on sign.** BBG differs from ISW and DeepState, sometimes in the direction of the result. For October, a sitrep said the differences arose because "some analysts included part of the AFU's advances in their October statistics" (CIT Sitrep Nov 2–5, 2026). Only BBG's number counts.

- **2026 to date:** 3 of 10 months negative (30%). Since February, when Russian momentum collapsed, it is 3 of 9 (33%).
- **Recent run:** 3 of the last 6 months negative (June, September, and the near-zero October counts as positive). Nearly every month since February has been within about ±200 km² of zero, so small swings can flip the sign.

**B. BBG's longer record, mid-2023 to 2025.** Feb 2026 was "the first net loss since November of 2023." So from December 2023 to January 2026 there were 0 negative months out of about 26. That period was Russia's high-tempo phase (e.g. July 2025 was about 5× July 2026). It is a weak guide to today's near-stalemate, though it shows how quickly Russian momentum can come back.

**C. Other trackers in 2026, as a cross-check on direction:**
- **ISW:** March roughly 0 net (AFP analysis, Apr 3, 2026); April −116 km², its first net loss since August 2024 (Euromaidan Press, May 3, 2026); September −81 km², with 176 gained and 256 lost; October about −27 km².
- **Zelensky's October claim:** Ukraine freed 96 km² and Russia took about 86 km², a net gain of about 10 km² for Ukraine (Defence Matters, Nov 2026).
- **DeepState, via Russia Matters:** Russian net gain of about 47 km² over the four weeks to Oct 26. The four-week Russian gain fell from 57 sq mi to 20 sq mi (about 52 km²) as of Nov 3.
- **Pattern:** ISW tends to be more favourable to Ukraine than BBG (April and October differ in sign). DeepState tends to be less favourable. BBG falls in between.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 6, 2026:** 180 clashes. Ukrainian forces holding in the Zaporizhzhia and Kostiantynivka directions (112.ua, Nov 6). ISW, via RBC-Ukraine (about Nov 3–6), reports Russian attacks in Sumy and several other sectors failed to gain ground. Control of Zarichne is disputed, with even a Russian-aligned source questioning Russian claims.
- **Nov 4–5, 2026:** Ukraine's General Staff (not independently verified) reported 184–208 clashes a day. The heaviest pressure was on the Pokrovsk direction (16–17 attacks a day) and Kostiantynivka (up to 24 a day, toward Kostiantynivka and Druzhkivka). There were also about 9 a day near Huliaipole (Zlahoda, Ternuvate, Dolynka, Hirke, Yehorivka) (Ukrainska Pravda, Nov 4 and Nov 5; Ukrinform). An Estonian defence official said Russia's main effort remains Pokrovsk and Kostiantynivka and that the front line "has barely budged" (ERR). Ukraine reported a record 31,398 ground-robot missions in October (112.ua).
- **Nov 3–4, 2026:** Russia Matters (DeepState data): Russia's four-week net gain was about 20 sq mi, a slowing tempo.
- **Early November 2026:** The October totals disagree. BBG reportedly has Russia at +21 km² (CIT, Nov 2–5). ISW has −27 km². Zelensky claims a net Ukrainian gain of about 10 km². UNITED24 headlined that ISW says Ukraine has "wiped out nearly all of Russia's 2026 territorial gains." Another UNITED24 piece says Putin's claim of 6,500 km² seized is 45× the reality.
- **Oct 21–24, 2026:** After Witkoff met Putin in St Petersburg, the Kremlin agreed to resume three-way US–Ukraine–Russia talks after Nov 10, likely in Abu Dhabi around mid-November (AP via WRDW, Oct 21; Boston Globe, Oct 24). A local humanitarian ceasefire was agreed for a cut-off town in Kherson. Both sides remain far apart: Moscow wants a full deal before any truce and demands Ukraine withdraw from four oblasts; Kyiv refuses to give up land.
- **Sept 2026:** Ukraine's Lyman-area operation ("Operation Vivaldi") drove the −190 km² September figure, together with a push toward Komar in western Donetsk. Newsweek reports it ended in September after blocking Russian encirclement of Lyman. RFE/RL (Sept 15) called it a "quiet" counteroffensive and a "rout."
- **Through 2026:** Ukraine's southern counteroffensive, starting in February in the Huliaipole and Oleksandrivka area, retook ground. In February Russia lost 192 km² around Huliaipole by BBG's count. Syrskyi claimed 600 km² retaken by June 8; Zelensky claimed about 300 km². ISW: from November 2025 to April 2026 Russia took about 1,443 km², against 2,368 km² a year earlier. BBG analyst Kastehelmi (The World, May 29, 2026): "this spring, the Russians have not achieved practically anything." Commentators credit Ukrainian mid-range strikes on logistics and the drone "kill zone."
- **Scheduled before the Dec 1 cutoff:** three-way talks in Abu Dhabi around mid-November (not confirmed to have happened). Autumn mud and early winter conditions apply.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (BBG figure negative):**
- **Local Ukrainian counterattacks.** Ukraine has shown it can produce net-negative months with local pushes (Huliaipole in February, Lyman/Komar in September). A new limited operation of about 100–200 km² would very likely outweigh Russia's current tempo, which DeepState puts at about 50 km² a month.
- **Russian tempo stays near zero.** Russian advance has been near zero since February. Late-confirmed Ukrainian gains from October could also be booked in November.
- **Timing of talks.** Ukraine may want to show battlefield gains ahead of or during the talks.

**Paths to NO (BBG figure positive):**
- **Default pattern.** In 6 of 9 months since February, Russia posted small net gains. Russian pressure on Kostiantynivka, Pokrovsk and Huliaipole continues at about 180–200 clashes a day, so even 20–100 km² of creeping gains with no Ukrainian operation means NO.
- **Seasonal conditions.** In 2024 and 2025, autumn fog and bare tree lines helped Russian infiltration, and November 2025 was a strong month for Russia.
- **Ukrainian priorities.** Ukraine may conserve forces for winter or shift effort to defending infrastructure.
- **BBG's conservatism.** BBG tends to be stricter than ISW about crediting Ukrainian gains (October: BBG +21 vs ISW −27).

**Wildcard:** a front-wide ceasefire before Dec 1 is very unlikely given the stated positions. It would freeze the lines and push the figure toward zero or slightly positive.

## 5. Signposts

- **BBG's own October post and its tone.** Check whether it confirms +21 km² and whether it flags Ukrainian penetrations still awaiting confirmation (YES-leaning) or a Russian pickup (NO-leaning).
- **Weekly trackers in November:**
  - Russia Matters/DeepState four-week numbers (around Nov 11, 18 and 25).
  - ISW daily maps.
  - DeepState four-week Russian gains below about 20 km², or ISW showing net Ukrainian liberations: strongly YES.
  - DeepState over about 80 km²: strongly NO.
- **Signs of a named Ukrainian operation:** reports of a Ukrainian counteroffensive in a named sector (Huliaipole, Kupiansk, Lyman, Kostiantynivka, Sumy) push toward YES. Confirmed Russian captures of towns such as Zarichne or villages near Kostiantynivka or Huliaipole push toward NO.
- **BBG mid-month commentary** (BBG and Kastehelmi sometimes post interim updates).
- **Outcome of the Abu Dhabi talks:** any front-wide truce, or a sharp escalation such as a Russian winter offensive.

## 6. Uncertainties

- **October BBG figure.** I could not confirm it from BBG directly. +21 km² comes from a single secondary source (CIT). Page fetches were blocked in this environment, so I worked from search snippets only.
- **April BBG figure** was not found; "positive" is an inference from the June post.
- **No BBG data for November 2025** was retrieved for a direct seasonal comparison.
- **How BBG handles disputed areas** (e.g. Zarichne, grey zones) and late confirmations could flip a near-zero month either way.
- **No independent November front-line maps.** The early-November daily reports are mostly from Ukraine's General Staff and say nothing about net territorial change.
- **Unexplained figure gaps.** A Russia Matters item reportedly cited a −406 km² September figure, which differs from BBG's −190; I could not reconcile the two. A Newsweek ISW October figure appears internally inconsistent.
