# Research brief: Will Jensen Huang say "China"/"Chinese" during the NVIDIA GTC Washington, D.C. keynote originally scheduled for December 1, 2026?

**Most important finding:** Jensen Huang resigned as NVIDIA's President, CEO and board member on **November 12, 2026**, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. This contract asks about something *Jensen Huang* says in an official capacity. If he does not give the keynote, YES is almost impossible, whatever happens on stage. All page fetches were blocked by the proxy during this research, so the facts below come from search-result snippets and could not be checked against the full articles.

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES requires** Jensen Huang to audibly say "China" or "Chinese" (plurals and possessives such as "China's" count) during the NVIDIA GTC Washington, D.C. keynote originally scheduled for **Tue Dec 1, 2026, 2:00 p.m. ET**, at the Ronald Reagan Building (NVIDIA GTC DC FAQ / keynote page). Kalshi decides from the live video, with transcripts as backup.
- **"Speaking in an official capacity."** This is the critical edge case. Huang no longer holds any NVIDIA office (NVIDIA 8-K, Nov 12, 2026). Suppose he somehow appeared, for example as a guest or in pre-recorded content. It is unclear whether that would count as "official capacity," and previously recorded content is excluded.
- **If someone else gives the keynote** (e.g., Kress or another executive), the featured speaker is not Huang. The likely result is "Event does not qualify" = YES, with every word market, including this one, resolving NO. A guest or video clip saying "China" would not count either way.
- **Cancellation or failure to qualify:** all word markets resolve NO.
- **Postponement:** markets stay open only if the postponement is announced by the end of the next calendar day and the new date is within 14 days. Market close is Dec 15, 2026.
- The title's wording, "originally scheduled for December 1," suggests Kalshi already expects the event might be rescheduled or changed.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. Does a CEO who just resigned give the company's flagship keynote 2–3 weeks later?**
- I know of essentially no precedent for a CEO who resigned, or was pushed out, amid scandal and an independent board investigation coming back to headline the company's own conference within weeks.
- Comparable cases include Uber's Kalanick in 2017, Intel's Krzanich in 2018 and McDonald's Easterbrook in 2019. None of them made official company keynote appearances after leaving. My estimate for this class is <5%, and probably ≈1–2%.
- The only real path is reinstatement before Dec 1. On Nov 14, Altimeter and Atreides asked the board for a *slow, deliberate* CEO search that keeps a possible return open (CNBC, Nov 14, 2026). That points to months, not days.

**B. If Huang does give a GTC keynote, how often does he say "China/Chinese"?**
- **March 2025 San Jose keynote:** he addressed China's DeepSeek directly, saying Dynamo/Blackwell would run DeepSeek R1 30x faster (Fortune, Mar 18, 2025). DeepSeek is a model name, so this does not prove the word "China" was spoken.
- **Oct 28, 2025 DC keynote:** the coverage I found centers on pro-America themes, the Nokia investment and DOE supercomputers. His sharpest China remarks that week came at press events and a Q&A, not clearly in the keynote. These included the 95%→0% market-share comment, the argument that China's developers should run on NVIDIA, and the risk of being "ill-prepared" for Chinese open-source software (Yahoo/Reuters, Oct 2025; Mashdigi, Oct 2025). I could not confirm a keynote-transcript mention.
- **2026:** Semafor reported that China mentions in Huang's interviews rose roughly sixfold after the 2025 export curbs (Semafor, Nov 11, 2026). At GTC Berlin in Oct 2026, in a Yahoo Finance interview, he said nearly half of the world's AI researchers are of Chinese origin and praised Chinese open models (Crypto Briefing, Oct 2026).
- **My conditional estimate:** if Huang gives a normal 90-minute keynote, there is about a 55–80% chance he says "China" or "Chinese." Open models are a listed keynote theme, and Chinese open models such as Qwen and DeepSeek are hard to avoid there. On the other hand, a DC keynote framed as pro-American and a live spy scandal would both push him to avoid the word.

**C. NVIDIA keynote cancellations:** GTC 2020 was the only cancellation found (COVID) (TechRadar, 2020). Cancelling the whole event is rare. Replacing the speaker is the more likely change here.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 14, 2026:** Altimeter Capital and Atreides Management urged the board to consider a "potential return of Jensen Huang" through a slow CEO search. They did not ask for immediate reinstatement (CNBC, Nov 14, 2026).
- **Nov 13, 2026:** NVIDIA stock fell further. A further affair allegation emerged (Yahoo Finance live, Nov 13). Forbes and TechCrunch published timelines of the scandal.
- **Nov 12–13, 2026:** At least one outlet said the GTC DC site still featured Huang's "previously planned-for" keynote and that the event faced disruption (Events for Gamers, ~Nov 13). I found no official NVIDIA notice replacing, cancelling or postponing the keynote.
- **Nov 12, 2026:** NVIDIA's 8-K said Huang resigned as President, CEO and director, effective immediately, without giving a reason. Kress became interim CEO while staying CFO, and Harvey C. Jones became the first board Chair. The board said operations and the product roadmap would continue "without interruption" (SEC 8-K; CNN; Fox Business; Al Jazeera, Nov 12, 2026).
- **Nov 12, 2026 (before the resignation):** The WSJ reported the board was preparing to oust Huang. At that point he was still listed as the GTC DC keynote speaker (The National, Nov 12).
- **Nov 10–11, 2026:** Huang cancelled several summit appearances but stayed listed for GTC DC (The National, Nov 10/11). Congress from both parties called for investigations, and Trump defended Huang (The National; CNBC Nov 10).
- **~Nov 9, 2026:** Huang admitted an affair with Alice Huang, an executive linked to Megaspeed whom U.S. officials reportedly believe is a Chinese MSS agent. Beijing called the allegation a smear. No charges have been reported (Al Jazeera, Nov 9; Wikipedia).
- **Scheduled events:**
  - Possible congressional testimony or closed-door briefing around **Nov 18** (Wikipedia / search snippets; not confirmed).
  - GTC DC runs **Nov 30–Dec 3**, with the keynote on **Dec 1 at 2 p.m. ET**.
  - Background policy: H200 sales to China are permitted, Blackwell stays blocked, and Commerce is reportedly drafting a rule on remote chip access.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **NO via "Event does not qualify," the dominant path.** NVIDIA replaces Huang with Kress or another executive or a product-team keynote, or scales back or cancels the keynote. This depends on board decisions and needs no further news to happen.
- **NO via Huang speaking without saying the word.** This first requires the unlikely reinstatement. He could then avoid China deliberately given the spy scandal.
- **YES path (narrow):**
  1. The board reinstates Huang, or makes some arrangement that lets him speak in an official capacity, before Dec 1.
  2. The keynote goes ahead, or is postponed within the 14-day window.
  3. He says "China" or "Chinese," for example about Chinese open models, the export-control debate, Chinese AI researchers, or "competing with China."

  A rough chain: P(official Huang keynote) ≈ 1–4% × P(says the word | speaks) ≈ 0.6–0.8.
- **Wildcard:** NVIDIA keeps a "Jensen Huang keynote" listing by inertia, but he cannot speak in an official capacity. That still resolves NO.

## 5. Signposts
- **Strongly down (toward ~0):**
  - NVIDIA updates the GTC DC keynote page or speaker to Kress or another executive.
  - The keynote is cancelled.
  - A permanent CEO is named.
  - Huang's lawyers or Congress schedule appearances that conflict with the event.
  - The FBI or DOJ opens a probe.
- **Strongly up:**
  - The board announces Huang's reinstatement or an interim return.
  - NVIDIA explicitly confirms Huang will still deliver the Dec 1 keynote.
  - If he does speak, the keynote agenda includes open models or sovereign AI, which makes China mentions likely.
- **Moderate:**
  - Huang's tone in congressional testimony (~Nov 18). Defensive and China-avoidant suggests he would avoid the word even if reinstated.
  - Whether the investor push for his return gains momentum quickly.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not access the GTC DC keynote page after Nov 12 to see whether it still names Huang. All fetches failed, and the information I have may be 2–3 days old.
- There is no confirmed official statement on the keynote speaker after the resignation.
- Kalshi has not stated how it would treat a substitute speaker. "Event does not qualify" seems most likely but is unconfirmed.
- I have no verified transcripts of the Oct 2025 DC or Mar 2026 San Jose keynotes, so the conditional rate for saying "China" in a keynote (as opposed to interviews) is an estimate.
- Details of the scandal, such as the third affair, the Nov 18 testimony and the MSS link, come mostly from snippets and some lower-reliability outlets.
