## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** either spouse **files for divorce**, or either spouse (or an authorized representative) **announces a definitive intention to divorce**. Both count only if they happen by **Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET**. What happens to the divorce afterwards doesn't matter.
- **What doesn't count:** saying they are "considering" or "open to" divorce. Speculation in the media doesn't count either, and there is a lot of it (settlement estimates of $63–81B).
- **Sources used to decide:** statements from Jensen, Lori, or their representatives; court records (the likely venue is Santa Clara County Superior Court, California); or a consensus of credible reporting.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Legal separation.** In California, a petition for legal separation is a different filing from a petition to dissolve the marriage. It would probably not count as "filing for divorce," but this is disputable.
  - **Separation announcements.** A statement like "we have decided to separate" is ambiguous. It probably falls short of a "definitive intention to divorce" unless it says so.
  - **Discreet filings.** Wealthy couples sometimes file quietly or settle terms privately before filing. A filing late in December might not be reported until after the deadline, and court records may lag.
  - **Fake or AI content.** Fakes have already circulated in this story (see section 3), and in the Andy Byron case a fake Facebook page posted a "decision to divorce." Resolvers will need to check that any statement is authentic.
- **Time left:** about 5.5 weeks as of Nov 23, including the holiday season. About 2 weeks have already passed since the affair admission on Nov 8 with no filing reported.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES**
- **More revelations.** Lori files or announces after further humiliating details come out, such as the Powell affair being confirmed, more affairs, or FBI or congressional disclosures. The three-plus reported affairs are a stronger trigger than a single lapse.
- **A pre-planned joint statement,** in the style of Gates or Bezos, after private negotiation. But dividing trusts and estate plans worth more than $100B usually takes months, which makes a filing before Dec 31 less likely.
- **A quick protective filing** by Lori's counsel to set a date of separation, which matters for valuing community property while Nvidia shares are volatile and falling.

**Paths to NO (default)**
- Jensen is publicly committed to repairing the marriage.
- Lori has stayed silent for 2+ weeks.
- The marriage is 41 years old, and the couple is roughly 63–65 years old.
- Their finances are deeply intertwined through joint trusts and the foundation, and both children work at Nvidia.
- Legal advisors generally counsel slowness.
- A separation without a divorce filing, or a quiet negotiation that continues into 2027, would both resolve NO.
- Pressure from the national-security investigation may also push the couple toward a unified front, for example over spousal privilege and reputation.
