# Research brief: Will Alexandre de Moraes stop being a Brazil Supreme Court (STF) justice by Dec 31, 2026?

**Bottom line for forecasters:** About six weeks remain (Nov 18 – Dec 31, 2026). The STF is in recess for part of that time. Senate President Davi Alcolumbre has repeatedly refused to move impeachment forward, and the new, more right-leaning Senate is not seated until February 2027. The remaining realistic paths are a voluntary resignation or retirement (which Moraes has reportedly ruled out), an announced negotiated exit, or death or incapacity. The Banco Master/Vorcaro scandal is the main source of pressure and makes this riskier than an ordinary period. I could not open any articles directly (every page fetch was blocked), so everything below comes from search-result summaries. One important outcome, the September 15 plenary ruling, is still unknown.

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Moraes "ceases to be a Justice of Brazil's Supreme Federal Court for any period of time" between market creation and **Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET**.
- **An announcement is enough:** If his resignation or removal is announced before the deadline, the market resolves YES immediately, even if it takes effect later. A credible announcement in December of a 2027 retirement would count.
- **Who decides:** Official information from Moraes or the Brazilian government (for example, a retirement decree in the *Diário Oficial*, or Senate impeachment records). A consensus of credible reporting can also be used.
- **Ways he could leave the court:**
  1. Voluntary early retirement (*aposentadoria voluntária*) or resignation (*exoneração*). This is the fastest route; Barroso announced his early exit in October 2025.
  2. Impeachment under Lei 1.079/1950. The Senate President decides whether to accept a complaint. Conviction needs two-thirds of the Senate (54 of 81 votes) (NDMais, ~Nov 2026).
  3. Death or permanent incapacity.
  4. Mandatory retirement at 75. Not relevant: he was born Dec 13, 1968, so that falls in 2043.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Suspension during impeachment:** Under Lei 1.079, a justice is suspended from duties once the Senate formally accepts the accusation. Whether a suspension means he "ceases to be a Justice" is unclear. Being taken off a case, as Fachin did with the Fake News inquiry on Sept 9, 2026, clearly does not count.
  - **Leave of absence:** A voluntary leave would also be ambiguous.
  - **Rumors:** A denied rumor of resignation, or a deal talked about but never announced, should not resolve YES.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. Voluntary early exits from the STF since 1988.** My approximate count from memory has about 6 clearly early departures: Rezek (1990 and 1997), Jobim (2006), Ellen Gracie (2011), Joaquim Barbosa (2014) and Barroso (October 2025). That is over roughly 400 justice-years (11 seats × ~37 years), or about **1.5% per justice per year**, which works out to **~0.2% for a 6-week window**. Exits under scandal pressure have no clear precedent. Moraes is also a poor fit for this class: he is 57, his career depends on the court, and he is reportedly next in line to become STF president in September 2027 (Jornal Opção) — a strong reason to stay.

**B. Impeachment removals of STF justices.** **Zero** in the history of the republic, despite many complaints. By Sept 3, 2026, 55 impeachment requests against Moraes had been filed with the Senate (Wikipedia). Alcolumbre has cited 109 requests against STF justices overall (Aos Fatos). No Senate President has ever accepted one. The only forced removals were the military regime's AI-5 retirements in 1969 (three justices), which was outside the constitutional order.

**C. Death in office.** Two STF justices died in office since 1988: Menezes Direito in 2009 and Teori Zavascki in the 2017 plane crash. That is roughly 0.5% per justice-year. Actuarial mortality for a 57–58-year-old Brazilian man is about 0.7–1% a year, or **~0.1% over 6 weeks**. He faces higher-than-normal security threats.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 17, 2026:** Moraes is still active as a justice. He authorized four Vivo technicians to enter the home where Jair Bolsonaro is serving house arrest (Mixvale, Nov 17, 2026).
- **~Nov 16–17, 2026:** Lower-house Speaker Hugo Motta and Alcolumbre asked Moraes, as rapporteur, to bring the "Dosimetria" law (which cuts sentences for Jan. 8 defendants) to a plenary vote this year. Moraes said he would reply soon (O Liberal; Brasil 247). This is routine work and gives no sign he is leaving.
- **Mid-November 2026:**
  - It is uncertain whether Moraes will attend a meeting Lula is organizing with STF justices. Ministers close to Lula said Moraes was one target of Lula's criticism of the judiciary in a Nov 8 statement (Diário do Centro do Mundo).
  - Gazeta do Povo columns say Alcolumbre still blocks impeachment.
- **~Nov 9–11, 2026:** The Federal Police removed delegates Daniel Mostadeiro Cola and Wedson Cajé from strategic posts. They had found 300+ interactions between Vorcaro and Moraes. Reports say the police will redo the reports without the analyses and cross-checking (NDMais). A November report says a police document the force "rejected" internally raised suspicion that Moraes previewed a vote for Vorcaro (Jornal de Brasília / Terra Brasil, Nov 2026). The removals point toward the investigation being softened, not escalated.
- **Oct 25, 2026:** Lula was re-elected with 51.79% against Flávio Bolsonaro's 48.21% (Senado Notícias; Folha do Leste). The new term starts Jan 5, 2027. A Flávio win had been seen as the bigger threat to Moraes.
- **Oct 4, 2026:** Two-thirds of the Senate was renewed. Counts of senators-elect backing STF impeachment differ: 30, 37, or "41 in favor, 21 undecided, 19 against" (Gazeta do Povo; other trackers). Even 48 right-leaning votes would fall about 6 short of 54 (NDMais). **The new Senate takes office Feb 1, 2027, after this market closes.**
- **Oct 1, 2026:** Lula said Moraes "owes society an explanation" about a R$131 million contract between Banco Master and his wife's law firm, and that "if guilty, he must pay" (TV Sim; RIC).
- **Sept 15, 2026:** The STF plenary began a session described as historic on whether a Federal Police report on Moraes–Vorcaro messages was valid and could justify investigating him. The Prosecutor-General (PGR) and Moraes both argued it should be annulled. **I could not find the result.** The November reports that the police are redoing their reports suggest it was annulled or left without effect.
- **Sept 10, 2026:** Moraes cancelled a planned public statement. According to CNN, he told allies he **is not considering early retirement** (CNN Brasil, Gustavo Uribe, Sept 10, 2026).
- **Sept 9, 2026:** STF President Fachin removed Moraes as rapporteur of the Fake News inquiry (Tribuna do Sertão, Sept 10, 2026).
- **Sept 1–3, 2026:**
  - Senators filed new impeachment requests (Senado Notícias, Sept 1, 2026).
  - Prosecutor-General Gonet asked the court to annul the police report (Agência Brasil, Sept 2026).
  - Alcolumbre called the 109 requests "not normal" but gave no sign he would act on them.
  - Lula gave a televised statement calling the Master case the largest theft in Brazil's history (Band).
- **Around September–October 2026:** Part of Bolsonaro's party, the PL, floated early retirement for Moraes and Dino as an alternative to impeachment. Senators said a negotiated exit would be hard to arrange and would not stop impeachment efforts (Agenda do Poder; Goiás 24h). The Correio Braziliense *Blog da Denise* column ran the headline "crescem as apostas sobre aposentadoria de Moraes" ("bets on Moraes's retirement are growing").
- **Earlier background:** Vorcaro was arrested in November 2025. Justice Toffoli left the Master case after a police report. From memory, not checked here: the US imposed Magnitsky sanctions on Moraes in July 2025 and reportedly lifted them in December 2025.

**Scheduled events before Dec 31:**
- STF sessions until the court recess starts around Dec 19–20, with a possible Dosimetria plenary vote in December.
- The Senate's last sessions before recess, with Alcolumbre still presiding.
- Possible results from the redone police reports or further plenary action on the Master case.
- No impeachment vote is scheduled.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (all low probability in the time left):**
1. **Scandal-driven resignation or announced retirement.** This would need damaging new evidence, for example a vote sold to Vorcaro or proof of the R$131 million flows, plus loss of support from fellow justices or Lula. It could take the form of an exit announced now that takes effect in 2027, made to get ahead of the new Senate. This is the most plausible YES path, but Moraes has denied it and the institutions are currently protecting him: the PGR sought annulment, Fachin centralized the case, and the police are softening their reports.
2. **Negotiated exit with the PL or the Senate.** Floated in September–October, but senators said it was impractical. No sign of progress.
3. **Impeachment before Dec 31.** Close to impossible. Alcolumbre would have to accept a complaint, then a committee and plenary process would follow, and conviction needs 54 votes in the outgoing Senate.
4. **Death or incapacity:** about 0.1%.

**Paths to NO:**
- The status quo holds through the holidays. Lula's re-election takes away the most hostile executive scenario.
- The court closes ranks; Moraes keeps ruling on high-profile cases and remains on track for the STF presidency in 2027.
- Real impeachment risk only begins in 2027, with the new Senate and the February 2027 election of the Senate President.

## 5. Signposts

**Would raise the probability of YES (sharply):**
- Credible reports (Folha, Globo, Estadão, CNN Brasil) that Moraes is negotiating an exit, or that fellow justices or Lula are urging him to step aside.
- New hard evidence in the Master case: Vorcaro plea-deal testimony naming Moraes, the redone police reports, or the PGR or STF opening a formal investigation of him.
- Alcolumbre accepting any impeachment complaint.
- Moraes taking leave, or reports of a serious health problem.

**Would lower it:**
- Confirmation that the plenary annulled the police report and closed the matter.
- Moraes scheduling or reporting major December rulings (Dosimetria).
- The year-end recess starting with no new revelations.
- Moraes publicly confirming he will run for STF president in 2027.
