## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Fedez must be named in Rai's **first official list** of artists competing in the Campioni/Big category of Sanremo 2027. If he appears as part of a duo or joint entry (e.g. "Fedez & X"), that counts. Withdrawals or changes after the first list don't matter.
- **Deadline:** The list must be published by **Feb 15, 2027, 11:59 PM ET**. If no list is out by then, the market resolves NO.
- **Who decides:** Official Rai information is the primary source; a consensus of credible reporting can also be used.
- **When the list is expected:** Stefano De Martino, the new artistic director and host, will reveal the Big in **prime time on Rai 1 on Friday, Dec 11, 2026, from 21:30 CET** (15:30 ET), during the "Sarà Sanremo" special and the Sanremo Giovani final. That is earlier than the market's close (Dec 12, 04:59 UTC = Dec 11, 11:59 PM ET). This time the names will **not** be announced on the lunchtime TG1 news, as in past years (Everyeye Serial, Nov 2026; KissKiss, Nov 2026; Virgilio, Nov 2026).
- **Conflicting dates:** One older report said the announcement was "reportedly set for Nov 29 on TG1 at 13:30," and another said proposals were due to Rai by Nov 23. The later, more specific reports on the Dec 11 prime-time reveal look authoritative. In either case the list would come before the close.
- **Edge cases:**
  - Under the "unprecedented" format, names may be revealed one by one during the show. The first full list will still almost certainly be out on Dec 11.
  - If Fedez appears only as a guest, a duet partner on covers night, or a co-host, that does **not** count. Only the competing list does.
- **Size of the field:** 24 Big in a single competition. **23 are chosen by the artistic direction** (De Martino with Fabrizio Ferraguzzo), and 1 comes from Sanremo Giovani (Wikipedia, Sanremo Music Festival 2027; Libero, Nov 2026). That is fewer slots than the 2025 and 2026 editions (Conti had about 30 entries in 2026), so fewer veterans can be squeezed in.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. Fedez's own record.**
- He competed as a Big in **2021** (with Francesca Michielin, 2nd), **2025** ("Battito", 4th) and **2026** (with Marco Masini, "Male necessario", 5th with 16.5% of the final vote) (Wikipedia, Battito / Male necessario; Quotidiano di Puglia, Mar 1, 2026).
- From memory, not verified this session: he also appeared outside the competition in 2023 (the stage moment with Rosa Chemical) and did not compete in 2022, 2023 or 2024.
- That is 3 of the last 6 editions (50%), and 2 of the last 2. YES would mean a third year in a row.

**B. Big artists returning straight after competing the year before.**
- This is my rough estimate from memory, not from a source. In recent editions roughly 10–20% of a year's Big competed in the previous edition. For 2025→2026 the returnees I could identify were only a few, such as Fedez and Serena Brancale.
- So for any single prior-year competitor, the chance of returning is roughly **10–20%**, and probably lower with a new artistic director and only 23 chosen slots.

**C. Three editions in a row as a Big.**
- Since about 2015 this has been rare under Conti and Amadeus. I can't recall a clear case among top-tier pop or rap acts; artists usually skip a year or more between appearances (e.g. Achille Lauro competed in 2019, 2020 and 2022 with a 2021 guest year in between; Irama in 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025).
- I couldn't compute an exact frequency, but it looks like well under 10% of eligible artists.

**D. A new artistic director.**
- New directors (Amadeus 2020, Conti 2025, now De Martino 2027) tend to bring their own picks.
- Fedez's 2025 and 2026 entries both came under Conti. There is no documented relationship between Fedez and De Martino; my targeted search found nothing linking them.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES:**
1. **De Martino wants a big draw.** Fedez's last two entries were commercially strong (top 5 both times, heavily streamed), and he brings huge social reach and talk value. De Martino could see him as a ratings asset.
2. **A pairing or collaboration.** He could come back in a joint entry (as with Masini in 2026) or via a big-label package deal. This requires his label to have pushed him to an artistic direction that seems to prefer newer and "punk" names.
3. **A comeback story.** He could use Sanremo to launch the album he promised after his July health scare. Rumour lists missed some names in past years, and surprise picks happen.

**Paths to NO (seem more likely given the evidence):**
1. Absent from every rumour list two to three weeks before the reveal. Insiders such as All Music Italia and Parpiglia usually catch most of the eventual names.
2. A third straight year would be historically unusual, and his two recent entries were Conti's picks under a director who has since left.
3. Health: doctors ordered rest in July, the September Forum date was cancelled, and there is no new music since.
4. His current focus is the podcast and media, not a release cycle.
5. Fewer slots (23 chosen) and a crowded field of A-list names (Mengoni, Geolier, Ghali, Mahmood, Elodie, Emma and others).

## 5. Signposts

- **Strongly toward YES:**
  - Any credible insider (All Music Italia, Parpiglia, Davide Maggio, Fiorello on Radio2) naming Fedez, or a joint entry featuring him, before Dec 11.
  - Fedez announcing a new single or album with a February 2027 release.
  - Fedez teasing Sanremo on social media or *Pulp*.
- **Toward NO:**
  - Fedez saying he will skip 2027, or new health news.
  - Final insider lists before Dec 11 that still leave him out.
  - Confirmation that Warner's or Sony's allocations are full of other names.
- **Decisive:** the Dec 11 prime-time reveal on Rai 1.

## 6. Uncertainties

- My web-page fetching failed, so I couldn't read the full All Music Italia list or De Martino's Nov 16 interview. Fedez's absence from the rumours rests on search-result summaries, though they were consistent.
- I couldn't confirm Fedez's current label or health status after July 2026. An EarOne listing shows early-November arena dates, but these may be stale or from a previous year.
- I couldn't verify whether he submitted a song.
- The base rates for returning and three-in-a-row Big are rough estimates from memory, not systematically computed.
- There is a small chance the Dec 11 reveal is staggered or delayed. That shouldn't affect resolution as long as a list comes out by Feb 15.
