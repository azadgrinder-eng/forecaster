# IHOP Credit Card Spend, November 2026 (Carbon Arc index above 92?): research brief

*Research notes: every page fetch was blocked by the network proxy, so everything below comes from search-result summaries, not full documents. Treat figures as reported, not checked against the full filings. I went one search over the 12-search budget (13 used). By accident, one search returned crowd prices for this contract. ## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES condition:** Carbon Arc's IHOP Credit Card Spend index for November 2026 must be strictly above 92. A reading of exactly 92.0 resolves NO.
- **What the index measures:** IHOP spend in Carbon Arc's US credit and debit card panel, compared with the same period a year earlier and set so that 100 means no change. A reading of 92 means spend about 8% lower than November 2025. To resolve YES, the panel must show IHOP spend down by less than about 8% year over year.
- **Timing:** The value used is Carbon Arc's "finalized" figure as of 11:59 p.m. ET on December 4, 2026. The market closes December 5, 2026 at 04:59 UTC. Kalshi settles the market using Carbon Arc data delivered through Carbon Arc's "Prisms" product (Kalshi, "Announcing sector KPI markets with Carbon Arc," Oct 23, 2026).
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - The panel "updates daily as new data arrives." Only four days after month-end, the last days of November (Thanksgiving week and Black Friday) may not be fully posted. If late transactions are missing from November 2026 but fully counted in November 2025, the index would be pushed down.
  - The public rules don't say how Carbon Arc assigns transactions to IHOP. Delivery orders through DoorDash or Uber Eats, gift cards, and dual-brand Applebee's/IHOP restaurants may or may not be counted.
  - The rules don't say whether panel churn is normalized. Card panels often lose accounts over time, which pushes year-over-year readings down.
  - No published methodology document for the IHOP series turned up.
- **Strike structure:** This is contract design, not a price. Kalshi lists strikes for this November contract running down to 79.5, plus intermediate levels in the high 80s and at 92. A ladder centered this far below 100 strongly suggests that recent prints of this Carbon Arc series have been well below 100, even though IHOP's reported same-store sales are roughly flat. I could not find the actual earlier monthly values.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **IHOP's reported domestic same-restaurant sales, 2024–2026.**
   - Q3 2026: +0.8% (Dine Brands 8-K, Nov 4, 2026). Q3 2025: about −1.5% (Yahoo Finance, Q3 2025).
   - From memory, not checked: IHOP comps have stayed between roughly −2% and +1% in every quarter since 2024.
   - Fewer IHOP restaurants and unit closures across Dine Brands reduce total spend by perhaps another 0.5–1.5 points. Dine reported 98 closures and 60 openings across the system in Q1–Q3 2026, according to TheStreet; this is not split by brand and I did not verify it.
   - On fundamentals alone, IHOP's total US sales should be about 97–101 on a year-over-year index. Over the past roughly 10 quarters, I found no quarter where reported IHOP system sales fell close to 8% (my estimate: 0 of about 10).
   - On this reference class alone, 92 would be easy to clear. But the contract resolves on Carbon Arc's panel, not on company-reported sales.
2. **How card-panel year-over-year figures relate to reported sales.** Card panels such as Consumer Edge, Earnest, Bloomberg Second Measure and Carbon Arc often differ from reported comps by several points. They tend to read low because of panel attrition, lost merchant matches, and spend moving to third-party delivery apps. IHOP's off-premise share is 21% (Dine 8-K, Nov 4, 2026). The strike ladder suggests Carbon Arc's IHOP series runs roughly 8–15 points below reported sales. If so, the question is really whether that gap stays the same, widens or narrows in November, not what IHOP's actual sales do.
3. **Family dining as a segment.** Black Box Intelligence called family dining the only segment with falling same-store sales in 2026 through June. It was also last in both sales and traffic in October 2025 (Black Box monthly reviews, Oct 2025 and Jun 2026). Even so, IHOP has beaten Black Box on traffic for seven straight quarters and on sales for four (Q3 2026 earnings call, Nov 4, 2026). The segment backdrop is weak, but IHOP is beating it.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 24–25, 2026:** Surveys show consumers dining out less and hunting for deals; one in three are going out less, and 47% look for offers. The National Restaurant Association projects real US restaurant sales growth of only about 1.3% in 2026, led by higher-income diners while lower- and middle-income diners pull back (Restaurant Business and others, Nov 2026). IHOP's customers skew toward the price-sensitive middle- and lower-income groups.
- **Nov 26, 2026:** Thanksgiving. Thanksgiving 2025 was Nov 27, so both fall in November and Black Friday is in-month both years.
- **Calendar mix:**
  - November 2026 starts on a Sunday, so it has 4 Saturdays, 5 Sundays and 5 Mondays.
  - November 2025 started on a Saturday, so it had 5 Saturdays and 5 Sundays.
  - November 2026 therefore swaps one Saturday for one Monday. Weekends are IHOP's peak, so this is a modest drag, perhaps −0.5 to −1.5 points (my estimate).
  - Veterans Day is a Wednesday in 2026 and was a Tuesday in 2025. IHOP has historically run free veteran meals on that day.
- **Nov 4, 2026 (Q3 results):**
  - IHOP domestic comps were +0.8% in Q3 2026, the quarter ending Sep 27, with off-premise at 21% of sales. Applebee's comps were +0.9%.
  - Dine missed on EPS, beat on revenue, and pointed to the low end of its guidance.
  - The company is increasing investment at the restaurant level.
  - Dine had 49 dual-brand restaurants at the end of Q3 and is targeting 80 by year-end.
  - I found no quarter-to-date (October/November) commentary in the snippets.

  Sources: Dine 8-K, Nov 4, 2026; NRN; 247wallst; Finviz.
- **Oct 26–29, 2026:** Dine rejected a $39-per-share nonbinding takeover bid from Arkhouse and Cobblers Cove and adopted a one-year poison pill (NRN, FSR Magazine, late Oct 2026). This has no direct effect on November spend.
- **Oct 23, 2026:** Kalshi launched Carbon Arc sector KPI markets, so there is very little public history of these series.
- **IHOP promotions in 2026:**
  - The $6 Everyday Value Menu continues, with BLT additions. Lower check prices could reduce dollar spend unless traffic rises.
  - Bottomless pancakes ran through Mar 30, 2026.
  - I found no November 2026 limited-time offer.

  Sources: Food Network, AOL, IHOP news, 2026.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (index above 92):**
- Carbon Arc's structural gap versus true sales is moderate (under about 7 points), and IHOP's underlying sales are flat to slightly up, in line with Q3's +0.8%.
- The data is close to complete by December 4, so the Thanksgiving and Black Friday weekend counts fully.
- The October 2026 print (not found) was already in the low-to-mid 90s, and November continues that trend.

**Paths to NO (index at or below 92):**
- The panel normally runs in the high 80s for IHOP, which the strike ladder implies. Carbon Arc's year-over-year figure would then reflect panel and coverage effects more than IHOP's actual sales.
- Several drags stack up:
  - one fewer Saturday;
  - fewer restaurants;
  - a lower average check from the value menu;
  - weak family-dining and lower-income demand;
  - more off-premise spend going through delivery apps rather than IHOP's own card transactions.
- Late-month data isn't fully posted by December 4, pulling the "finalized" value down.

**What it hinges on:** mainly where the series has been printing, and how volatile it is month to month, rather than IHOP's fundamentals.

## 5. Signposts
- **Biggest:** the Carbon Arc IHOP readings for October 2026 and earlier months, and how much preliminary values get revised. If recent prints were around 88–90, 92 is a stretch. If they were 93 or higher, YES is favored.
- **Other chains:** Carbon Arc readings for Denny's, Cracker Barrel and Applebee's in November. These show whether the panel is depressing all family-dining chains.
- **Industry data:** Black Box or Circana November 2026 family-dining same-store sales and traffic, likely published mid-December, after resolution. Holiday-week card-spend reports from Bank of America Institute or Chase.
- **Company commentary:** any quarter-to-date comment from Dine at December investor conferences. Positive comps would raise the odds of YES somewhat.
- **Data events:** news of Carbon Arc panel changes or data delays around Thanksgiving. A delay points toward NO.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not find any historical value of the Carbon Arc IHOP index, its volatility, or its revision behavior. This is the most important missing input.
- I could not find Carbon Arc's methodology: panel normalization, merchant mapping, and whether delivery and dual-brand sales are counted.
- I could not find IHOP's October or November 2026 trends or Q4 guidance, and Q2 2026 comps were not confirmed.
- I could not confirm IHOP's own domestic net unit change for 2026 (the 98 closures and 60 openings cover all Dine brands).
