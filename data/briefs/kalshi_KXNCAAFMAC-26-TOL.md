# Research Brief: Will Toledo win the 2026 MAC Football Championship Game?

*Prepared 2026-11-28. Every page fetch was refused by the proxy, so all facts come from search-result summaries. Where those summaries disagreed, I say so.*

---

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **YES** if Toledo wins the 2026 MAC Football Championship Game. **NO** in any other case, which now in practice means a Buffalo win.
- **Matchup is set.** Toledo (10-2, 7-1 MAC) plays Buffalo (9-3, 7-1 MAC). The game is on **Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, at noon ET at Ford Field in Detroit**, on ESPN. The site is neutral (Yahoo Sports, Nov. 27–28, 2026; AP via ABC News, Nov. 27, 2026).
- **Who decides:** the official final score of that game. Overtime counts, as it does in any college game.
- **Edge cases:**
  - The market closes Jan. 3, 2027, which leaves about four weeks if the game is postponed.
  - A cancellation or forfeit is not covered by the text shown. Kalshi's general rules would apply.
  - One source lists Toledo at "7-2" in the MAC. AP and the other sources say 7-1, which looks like the correct figure. It does not affect the matchup.
  - Yahoo calls this possibly the "(final?)" MAC title game at Ford Field because the conference hasn't decided its future format. That has no bearing on the 2026 game.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **College conference title rematches.** From 1999 to 2015 there were 33 conference title games that repeated a regular-season matchup. The team that won the first meeting won **20 of 33 (about 61%)** (hornfans.com thread compiling data, via search summary; this is not a primary source). In NFL conference title rematches, the regular-season winner is 7-2 (CBS Sports). Across all NFL playoffs since 2002 it is 63-44, about 59% (Washington Post, via search summary). **Buffalo won the regular-season meeting**, so this class leans Buffalo at roughly 59–61%.
2. **Better overall record / likely betting favorite.** Toledo is 10-2 against Buffalo's 9-3. Toledo's losses came at Michigan State (20-30, Sept. 4) and to Buffalo. Buffalo lost at FIU (20-33), at Penn State (13-55), and to Western Michigan (17-20). In FBS, a favorite of about 2–3 points typically wins about 55–60% of the time. I could not find a 2026 spread, FPI, or SP+ number for this game, so whether Toledo is actually favored is unconfirmed.
3. **Recent MAC title games are close to a coin flip.** There have been six different champions in the last six seasons, and Toledo won in 2022. A Buffalo win would make seven in seven years (Yahoo Sports, Nov. 2026). Toledo is 1-1 in its recent title-game appearances: it beat Ohio in 2022 and lost to Miami (OH) in 2023. In 2024, Ohio beat Miami (OH) 38-3 after losing to the RedHawks in the regular season. That game is a recent example of the first-meeting loser winning the rematch, though this detail is from my memory, not a source checked here. Several of these title games were upsets.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov. 27, 2026 (Fri):** Toledo won **34-31 at Ohio** on Reice Griffith's 22-yard field goal as time expired. The drive went 65 yards in 7 plays and 44 seconds, helped by a 15-yard Ohio face-mask penalty. CJ Miller ran for 109 yards and 2 TDs, including the tying score with 1:33 left. Ohio was playing a freshman backup QB, Levi Davis, because starter Nick Poulos was hurt. Toledo needed a last-second kick to beat a team without its starting QB (AP via ABC News / theScore, Nov. 27, 2026).
- **Nov. 27, 2026:** **Buffalo beat Akron 17-9** to clinch its spot. Messiah Burch ran for 130 yards and a TD, and QB Jason Wright threw a 21-yard TD to Terrance Shelton Jr. It was Buffalo's **7th straight win**, tying the program record (AP / Lethbridge Herald, Nov. 27, 2026; Yahoo Sports).
- **Buffalo's run of wins since Oct. 10:**
  - at Toledo 23-8
  - UMass 24-18 (OT)
  - Bowling Green 31-24
  - at Miami (OH) 34-31 (OT)
  - at Ball State 28-7
  - Central Michigan 40-21 (Nov. 18)
  - Akron 17-9

  Two of these went to overtime. Earlier in the season Buffalo beat FCS Robert Morris by only 31-28 (CBS Sports schedule, via search).
- **Oct. 10, 2026, head-to-head: Buffalo 23, Toledo 8 at the Glass Bowl** (Toledo's homecoming).
  - Buffalo outgained Toledo 351-308.
  - Toledo went 3 of 14 on third down, committed 3 turnovers, and was penalized 15 times for 137 yards.
  - Burch's 87-yard TD run with about 4:30 left put the game away.
  - The loss ended Toledo's 9-game home winning streak and dropped the Rockets to 4-2. It was Buffalo's first win over an FBS team in six tries.

  Toledo's mistakes (turnovers, penalties) are the kind of thing that doesn't necessarily repeat. Buffalo's yardage edge was modest (UT Athletics; UB Athletics; ESPN recap; By The Belt, Oct. 10, 2026).
- **Since Oct. 10, Toledo has won 6 straight**, including wins over Western Michigan and Bowling Green (moved to Nov. 20) and the win at Ohio.
- **Coaching:** Toledo is in **Mike Jacobs's first season** after Jason Candle left. Buffalo's Pete Lembo is in his 2nd or 3rd season (sources differ).
- **Preseason expectations:** ESPN's preseason MAC preview projected Toledo at about 6.9 wins (5.1 in the MAC) and reportedly **108th in SP+**. Toledo has done much better than that. The SP+ figure is unverified.
- **Before the final week (about Nov. 24):** a Pro Football Network model gave Toledo 74%, WMU 61%, Buffalo 31%, Ohio 21%, and Miami 14% to reach the title game. Buffalo was the less likely of the two finalists.
- **Program history:** Toledo is making its 3rd title-game appearance in 5 seasons and last won in 2022. Buffalo's last MAC title was in **2008**.
- **Before the close date:** only the Dec. 5 game itself, plus bowl and transfer-portal news and any coaching-carousel moves (see Signposts).

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (Toledo wins):**
- Toledo's overall body of work is stronger: 10-2, a competitive 10-point road loss to a Big Ten team, and a road win at Ohio. If the market or power ratings make Toledo a small favorite, it should be slightly better than a coin flip.
- The Oct. 10 loss was driven by turnovers and penalties, which tend to regress toward normal levels.
- Toledo's running game (CJ Miller) is producing.
- Toledo has recent title-game experience, and the game is at a neutral indoor site.

**Paths to NO (Buffalo wins):**
- Buffalo dominated the only meeting, on Toledo's home field.
- The rematch base rate is about 60% for the team that won the first game.
- Buffalo has the hotter streak (7 straight) and a strong run game (Burch) plus kicking (Hautanen made 3 FGs on Oct. 10).
- Toledo has been living on the edge lately: it needed a walk-off kick against a backup QB.

**Things that suggest Buffalo is weaker than its record:**
- Two OT wins, a 3-point win over an FCS team, and a 33-20 loss to FIU point to a team whose record may be better than its underlying strength.

**Coaching-carousel risk:**
- Early December is when coaches leave for bigger jobs. Losing a head coach or coordinator before Dec. 5 would hurt whichever team it happened to.

## 5. Signposts

- **Point spread and power ratings (FPI/SP+) at kickoff.** This is the best single summary of team strength. Toledo -3 or more would mean about 58–62% for Toledo. A pick'em, or Buffalo favored, would mean about 50% or less. I couldn't find any line.
- **Injury news,** especially at QB. Toledo's starting QB in 2026 was not identified in my searches; Connor Walendzak scored the Rockets' TD on Oct. 10. Also watch Buffalo's QB Jason Wright and RB Burch.
- **Coaching departures** at either school, such as Lembo or the coordinators being hired elsewhere.
- **Season-long turnover margin and penalty rates.** If Toledo's Oct. 10 sloppiness was a one-off, that favors YES.
- **Weather is not a factor** because Ford Field is a dome.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I found no current spread, FPI, SP+, or other power rating for either team, so I can't say who is favored.
- I couldn't identify Toledo's 2026 starting QB or its injury status, or get season statistics for either team (yards per play, turnover margin).
- Sources disagree on records: early Wikipedia snapshots versus CBS and AP, and "7-2" versus "7-1" for Toledo in the MAC. AP's 10-2 / 7-1 for Toledo and 9-3 / 7-1 for Buffalo look correct.
- The 61% rematch base rate comes from an older (1999–2015) fan-forum compilation, not a primary dataset.
- My MAC title-game history (2022–2025 results) partly relies on memory, not sources fetched here.
