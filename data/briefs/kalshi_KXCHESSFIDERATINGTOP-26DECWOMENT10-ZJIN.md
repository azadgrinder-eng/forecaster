# Research brief: Will Zhu Jiner be in the top 10 of FIDE's Top Women Players standard list in December 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Zhu Jiner (CHN) appears at rank 1–10 on FIDE's December 2026 Top Women Players standard (classical) list. This is the "Top 100 Women" page at ratings.fide.com.
- **Timing:** FIDE publishes monthly lists on or around the 1st of the month, so the December list should appear around Dec 1, 2026. It covers rated games reported in roughly November 2026. The market closes Jan 7, 2027, which leaves time for any delay in publication.
- **Ties at 10th place:** These are broken first by more rated games in the period, then by the younger player, then by surname alphabetically. This only matters if she ends up level on rating with the 10th-place player. She is far from that today.
- **Edge cases:**
  - FIDE's top lists include only active players. A player with no rated game in the previous 12 months drops off. Zhu played the September 2026 Olympiad, so she is clearly active.
  - Sources conflict slightly on the ranks of players further down the list, for example the order of Lei Tingjie and Goryachkina. Nothing found suggests any doubt about Zhu's own rank.
  - Only the standard list counts. Rapid and blitz ratings are irrelevant.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **How much a player rated ≥2400 can lose in one month.** FIDE uses K=10 for players who have ever reached 2400, so Zhu's rating moves slowly.
  - A loss to a player rated ~2500 costs her about 10 × 0.56 ≈ 5.6 points.
  - A loss to a ~2700 player costs only about 2.4 points.
  - Losing the ~45 points that separate her from 10th place would take roughly 8 straight losses to ~2500 opposition in a single month, with no draws or wins.
  - My own rough estimate (not from a database) is that a top-50 woman's monthly rating change has a standard deviation of about 5–10 points. A 45-point fall in one month would be a 4–9 sigma event. I know of no top-10 woman who has dropped that far in one month in recent decades.
- **Players ranked #2 on one month's women's list who are still top 10 the next month.** As far as I know this has happened essentially every time. I did not compute this from a database; it follows from the rating math above.
- **Zhu's own record in the top 10.** She has been in the top 10 throughout 2025–26:
  - She climbed to #2 in October 2025 (FIDE Oct 2025 list).
  - She peaked at 2579 in November 2025 (Wikipedia).
  - She took back #2 in October 2026 (FIDE Oct 2026 list).
  - Nothing I found shows her outside the top 10 at any point in this period.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **YES (dominant path):** She plays nothing rated in classical chess in November, or plays normally. She stays around 2540–2570 and somewhere in ranks 2–5. This requires nothing unusual.
- **NO path 1 – catastrophic results:** She plays a long classical event in November and loses about 45 points. Given K=10, this is close to impossible in one rating period.
- **NO path 2 – mass overtaking:** Eight more players pass her. This would need several players currently at 2514–2553 to gain 10–45 points each in the same month while she stays flat or drops. That is essentially impossible.
- **NO path 3 – administrative issues:**
  - FIDE wrongly removes her from the list or flags her inactive. Very unlikely given her September 2026 games.
  - Kalshi misreads the list, or the December list is delayed or not published. Low probability, and the January 7 close leaves slack.
  - A change of federation does not affect the women's list.
