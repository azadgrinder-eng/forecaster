## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES** only if PSG lead on the scoreboard at the end of 90 minutes plus stoppage time. A draw resolves this market NO (a separate "Tie" market resolves YES), and so does a Nice win. Extra time and penalties don't apply to a league match anyway.
- **Kickoff:** Saturday 21 Nov 2026 at 17:15 Paris time (16:15 UTC), Allianz Riviera, Nice (PSG.fr fixture page; Sortiraparis). One ESPN listing shows 11:15 AM, which is almost certainly a US time-zone conversion. The market closes at 2026-11-23 16:15 UTC, exactly 48 hours after kickoff.
- **Edge cases:** If the match is cancelled, or moved more than 48 hours away, Kalshi pays out a "fair price" at its own discretion rather than a clean YES or NO. A delay within 48 hours still counts. Abandonments and results awarded by the league are not explicitly covered. In practice, the 90-minute scoreline as reported by the league or major data providers decides it.
