# Research brief: Will DeSean Bishop (Tennessee RB) win the 2026 Maxwell Award?

**Bottom line:** Bishop was not named one of the three 2026 Maxwell finalists on November 24, 2026. The finalists are Ohio State WR Jeremiah Smith, Miami QB Darian Mensah and Utah QB Devon Dampier. For YES, the Maxwell Football Club would have to give the award to someone who is not a finalist. No precedent for that turned up in this research.

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES:** Bishop is named the 2026 Maxwell Award winner (college football's player of the year). The winner will be revealed on the ESPN/Home Depot College Football Awards show. Reports give the date as Friday, December 11, 2026. Tennessee's August release said December 10. Either way it comes well before the market closes on January 7, 2027. The formal presentation is at the 90th Maxwell Awards Gala on March 13, 2027, in Atlanta (Maxwell finalist coverage, Nov 24 2026).
- **NO:** Anyone else wins. The market also resolves NO if Bishop doesn't play in the 2026 season. He has played, so this clause doesn't matter here.
- **Who decides:** The Maxwell Football Club. Past cycles used a voting body of media, coaches and club members. For semifinalists the club now works with PFF and Phil Steele as selection partners (Last Word on Sports, Nov 11 2025).
- **Edge cases:** If the award is shared, a YES share pays $1/N. That would only matter if Bishop were a co-winner, and he can't be one without being a finalist. Finalist lists are announced, so a non-finalist winning would be extraordinary.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **Non-finalists winning after a finalist list is published.** In the modern format (semifinalists, then three finalists, then a winner), I know of no Maxwell winner who wasn't a named finalist. This comes from my own knowledge of recent cycles; I couldn't check every year in the allowed searches. Examples: 2020 finalists were Lawrence, Mac Jones and DeVonta Smith, and Jones won (ESPN/Fox, 2020). 2022 finalists were Williams, Stroud and Hooker, and Williams won (Fox Sports, 2022). Base rate for a non-finalist win: roughly 0 out of 25+ cycles.
- **Running backs as winners.** RBs have won only a few Maxwells this century, for example Ashton Jeanty finishing second in 2024 and earlier RB winners such as Ricky Williams in 1998 and Larry Johnson in 2002. QBs win most years. Even if Bishop were still in contention, the prior on a non-Heisman-favorite RB winning would be low.
- **Tennessee winners.** Tennessee players have historically rarely won this award. That adds little beyond the points above.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 24, 2026:** Maxwell finalists announced: Jeremiah Smith (Ohio State WR; 1,482 receiving yards and 17 TDs through 11 games, leads the nation in both), Darian Mensah (Miami QB; 75.4% completions, 3,317 yards, 29 TDs, Miami 10-1 and in the ACC title game) and Devon Dampier (Utah QB; 2,535 passing yards, 24 TDs, Utah 11-0) (On3; Yahoo Sports; Utah Athletics; Miami Athletics; Eleven Warriors; ACC — all Nov 24 2026). Bishop is not on the list.
- **Nov 10–11, 2026:** Maxwell semifinalists were scheduled for Nov 10. One Tennessee aggregator page (roundtable.io, Nov 11 2026) shows a graphic labeling Bishop a "Maxwell Award Semifinalist." I couldn't confirm this against the official list. He was also listed as a Walter Camp Player of the Year semifinalist, one of three RBs (roundtable.io, Nov 11 2026).
- **Nov 7, 2026:** Bishop ran for 109 yards and 2 TDs in Tennessee's home win over Kentucky (Field Level Media, Nov 7 2026).
- **Oct 17, 2026:** 159 yards and 2 TDs as No. 14 Tennessee beat No. 6 Alabama 26-10 (AP via KSAT/AJC, Oct 17 2026).
- **Oct 3, 2026:** Career-high 220 yards and 3 TDs against Auburn (TSN/AP, Oct 2026).
- **Season totals:** Sources disagree. PFF showed 837 yards and 10 TDs as of about Oct 19. RotoBaller showed 1,066 yards and 13 TDs at some later date. Either way, these are strong numbers but not national-award-leading.
- **Aug 3, 2026:** Named to the Maxwell preseason watch list (UT Athletics, Aug 3 2026).

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **YES path:** Practically none. It would take the club picking a non-finalist (unprecedented) or some bizarre administrative reversal, such as all three finalists being disqualified and the club then choosing Bishop from the semifinalists. Each step is extremely unlikely, and the last one is implausible on its own.
- **NO path:** The default. Smith, Mensah or Dampier wins on Dec 10/11.

## 5. Signposts
- An official correction to the finalist list that adds Bishop would be a large move toward YES. I found no hint of one.
- Announcement of the winner on Dec 10/11 settles the question. Nothing between now and then is likely to matter, including Bishop's rivalry-week or SEC postseason games, since the finalists are already set.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I couldn't open the official finalist pages directly; the proxy refused the connection. The finalist names come from several matching search snippets of team-site and media articles dated Nov 24, 2026.
- Bishop's semifinalist status and his exact season stats are unconfirmed.
- There's a one-day discrepancy in the winner reveal date (Dec 10 vs Dec 11). It doesn't affect resolution.
- I couldn't verify the full Maxwell history for any case of a non-finalist winner, but I'm not aware of one.
