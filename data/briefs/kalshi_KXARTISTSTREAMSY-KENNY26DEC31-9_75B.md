# Research Brief: Will Kendrick Lamar have more than 9.75B Luminate worldwide streams in calendar 2026?

The best available evidence puts Kendrick's 2026 total somewhat below 9.75B, roughly 8.5–9.2B, unless he releases a major project in the last six weeks. That estimate rests on indirect data, though. I found no year-to-date Luminate total for him, and every page I tried to open directly was blocked, so all figures below come from search-result summaries.

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts for YES:** Kendrick Lamar's Luminate "Worldwide Streams" for January 1 – December 31, 2026 must be above 9.75 billion. Luminate may publish the figure up to about 48 hours after December 31. The market closes January 2, 2027 at 15:00 UTC.
- **Who decides:** The exchange settles the contract from Luminate data. The same "Worldwide Streams" wording appears on the weekly artist-stream markets, which say outcomes are "verified from Luminate" (Kalshi KXARTISTSTREAMS market pages, Apr 2026).
- **Ambiguities:**
  - **What "Worldwide Streams" includes.** It is unclear whether this is on-demand audio only or audio plus video. It is also unclear whether Kendrick gets credit for songs where he is only featured (for example "Like That" or Baby Keem's "Good Flirts"), or only for songs where he is lead artist. The weekly figures look large for an audio-only count: Rihanna had about 272M in one week (Kalshi "Rihanna streams up this week", Apr 2026). That suggests either a broad definition or generous crediting.
  - **Week alignment.** The weekly markets use Friday–Thursday weeks, but this market uses a custom calendar-year window. Luminate can produce that figure, but nobody can check it outside Luminate.
  - **Publication timing.** If the year-end figure comes out late or gets revised, settlement could be delayed.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **His own recent trajectory, using Spotify as a stand-in.**
   - 2025 was his biggest year ever, about 11B Spotify streams. That came from the GNX release cycle, the Super Bowl LIX halftime show and "Not Like Us" (Ratings Game Music, Nov 25 and Dec 17, 2025). After the Super Bowl his catalog jumped 154–175% (Billboard, Feb 2025).
   - 2026 has no new solo album and is running much slower. He passed 1B Spotify streams around mid-February, about 46–50 days into the year (Ratings Game Music, Feb 19, 2026; HotNewHipHop, Feb 2026). He passed 2B around April 11, about 101 days in (Hip Hop All Day on X; the date is my inference from the post ID).
   - That is roughly 20M Spotify streams a day in the first half, or about 7B a year, about 35% below 2025.
2. **Catalog decay after a big album cycle.** Without a new album, a hit catalog typically loses 20–40% a year after its peak year, and loses more in the second half of the year. Kendrick fits that pattern: big moments (GNX, the Super Bowl, the "Luther" chart runs) concentrated in 2025, and no new lead album in 2026 so far.
3. **Luminate weekly run-rate (the best direct evidence).** The weekly "streams up this week?" markets set their target equal to the previous week's actual count. Those targets show Kendrick's Luminate Worldwide Streams at about **162.9M** for the week ending about July 2, 2026 and **157.7M** for the week ending about July 9 (Kalshi KXARTISTSTREAMSU-KENNY26JUL09 / JUL16; CryptoStruct).
   - At 158–163M a week, a full year comes to about **8.2–8.5B**.
   - Clearing 9.75B needs an average of **187.5M a week** for all 52 weeks.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 16, 2026:** In the 2027 Grammy nominations, Taylor Swift matched Kendrick's record of five straight Album of the Year nominations, so he has no AOTY nomination this cycle. Kendrick is credited on "Good Flirts" (Baby Keem featuring Kendrick Lamar & Momo Boyd), nominated for Best Rap Performance (Sentinel Assam, Nov 2026; HotNewHipHop Grammy list, Nov 2026). The Grammys are in early 2027, so they cannot lift 2026 numbers.
- **Nov 11, 2026:** iHeartRadio's year-end Rewind named Kendrick one of the two most popular hip-hop artists of 2026, citing GNX catalog tracks ("luther," "tv off," "Not Like Us") (Power 105.1/iHeart, Nov 11, 2026). Catalog demand is still strong.
- **Nov 2, 2026:** There is unverified gossip about Kendrick (Adam22, Wack 100, Akademiks). I found **no confirmed announcement of a new Kendrick album** for late 2026.
- **Aug 3, 2026:** "Not Like Us" passed 2B Spotify streams (Ratings Game Music, Aug 3, 2026).
- **July 2026:** His Luminate weekly worldwide streams were about 158–163M (see section 2).
- **Jul 15, 2026:** Luminate's midyear report showed global on-demand audio streams up 9.8% year over year, to 2.8 trillion in the first half. The leaders it highlighted were Ella Langley, Olivia Dean and Bad Bunny; Kendrick was not among them (Luminate 2026 Midyear Report, Jul 15, 2026).
- **May 11–12, 2026:** GNX and "euphoria" briefly disappeared from Apple Music, TIDAL and Amazon. The "Not Like Us" and "Luther" videos were pulled from YouTube. Most of it came back within hours to a day, though Digital Music News said some re-uploaded videos were removed again. His label said it did not know why (Hot97; Complex; The Source, May 12, 2026). Spotify was not affected. The effect on the annual total is probably small, but this may point to a label or distribution change.
- **May 2026:** "Like That" (Future, Metro Boomin and Kendrick) passed 1B Spotify streams (Ratings Game Music, May 12, 2026). Drake's album ICEMAN set a 2026 first-day Spotify record (Independent, May 2026).
- **Apr 1, 2026:** One digest claimed a surprise album called "Chromatic." Nothing else corroborates it, and the April 1 date makes an April Fools' item likely. Later reporting still calls GNX his latest album (Independent, May 2026).
- **~Apr 11, 2026:** Over 2B Spotify streams in 2026 across all credits.
- **~Feb 15–19, 2026:** Over 1B Spotify streams in 2026.
- **Jan 2026:** GNX was Spotify's most-streamed rap album of 2025, with 2.98B streams (Hypebeast, Jan 2026). Luminate's 2025 year-end report showed "luther" (with SZA) at 1.672B global on-demand audio streams, seventh among songs worldwide. Global streams overall rose 9.6% to 5.1 trillion (Luminate Year-End Report, Jan 2026).
- **Scheduled before close:** There is no known Kendrick release. Other factors before December 31:
  - Spotify Wrapped and year-end playlists in early December, a small catalog lift.
  - The usual holiday-season dip for hip-hop in late December.
  - Possible features on other artists' albums.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Rough arithmetic.** Assume the second half of the year runs at about 155M a week (July levels, slightly decayed), so July through mid-November is about 20.6 weeks, or 3.2B. Assume mid-November to December 31 (about 6.4 weeks) runs at about 150M a week, or 0.96B.

Under those assumptions, the first half would have needed about **5.6B, or about 215M a week**, to reach 9.75B. The Spotify pace in the first half (about 20M a day, or 140M a week) and an implied Luminate-to-Spotify ratio of about 1.3 put the first half nearer **180–190M a week, or 4.7–4.9B**. That gives a central estimate of about **8.5–9.2B**, short of the line.

**Paths to YES:**
- A surprise album or major single in late November or December. GNX's debut week alone drew about 380M US on-demand streams (Billboard, Dec 2024); its global first week was probably 600M or more. That plus spillover to the catalog could add 1B or more within six weeks.
- The first half was much stronger than the Spotify proxy suggests. February had the Grammys (Feb 1, 2026) and the first anniversary of the Super Bowl show.
- Luminate's definition is broader than assumed (video included, generous feature crediting). That raises his level overall, but it is already built into the July weekly figures.

**Paths to NO:**
- Catalog keeps decaying with no new release, which is the status quo.
- The holiday-season dip.
- Lingering effects from the May takedowns.

## 5. Signposts
- **Strongly toward YES:**
  - A confirmed surprise Kendrick album or high-profile single before about December 15. Earlier is better, because each week of a debut adds 300–600M or more.
  - Any published Luminate year-to-date figure for Kendrick above about 8.3B as of mid-November.
- **Toward NO:**
  - Recent weekly worldwide-stream targets (October and November) at or below about 150M.
  - A year-to-date figure below about 8.0B as of mid-November.
- **Moderate:**
  - Large features (for example on a Baby Keem or SZA project), if Luminate credits features.
  - Spotify Wrapped visibility.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not get Kendrick's actual Luminate year-to-date or first-half 2026 Worldwide Streams figure. This is the biggest gap.
- I could not get his Luminate 2025 total, so year-over-year decay is estimated from Spotify only.
- I don't know the exact Luminate definition (audio vs. audio plus video; lead vs. all credits). The ratio of Luminate to Spotify (assumed about 1.3) is a rough estimate from one month of data.
- I found no weekly figures after July 2026.
- I can't rule out a late-2026 release. Kendrick has surprise-dropped before; GNX came out in November 2024.
