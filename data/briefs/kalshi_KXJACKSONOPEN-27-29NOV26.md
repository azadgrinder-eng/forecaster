# Research brief: Will Jackson Hole Mountain Resort open before Nov 29, 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** JHMR's first day of "Seasonal Opening Operations" in the 2026‑27 season must fall before Nov 29, 2026, Eastern Time. In practice that means on or before **Sat Nov 28**. The scheduled opening is **Fri Nov 27**, the day after Thanksgiving (Nov 26). So the resort can slip by at most one day and still resolve YES.
- **Minimum bar:** One passenger lift carrying the general public uphill for downhill skiing or riding, on terrain JHMR designates as open, is enough. Any number of lifts, any amount of terrain and any operating duration count. A single beginner lift on man‑made snow (Teewinot, Après Vous, the "slope of hope") would qualify.
- **Pass access:** 2026‑27 season‑pass holders must be able to ride without an additional purchase. Passholder‑only days and free reservations count. Ikon and Mountain Collective holders need reservations at JHMR; JHMR season passholders don't (Outpost JH blog). That shouldn't block YES.
- **What doesn't count:** Employee, media, private‑event or paid early‑access days; scenic rides; uphill‑only access; and announced or projected dates.
- **Edge cases:**
  - An opening on Nov 28 still resolves YES.
  - A one‑week delay, which is the usual pattern (Dec 3–4), resolves NO.
  - An earlier opening than Nov 27 is possible in principle; it happened in snow‑rich 2022. That is very unlikely this year.
  - The ET rule doesn't matter for a morning opening in Mountain Time.
- **Who decides:** Kalshi, based on what actually happened. Market close is Dec 5, 2026.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. JHMR hitting its scheduled opening date, recent seasons.** I could not confirm every year, so the record is uneven.
- **Confirmed delays:**
  - 2016: Nov 24 pushed back one week to Dec 1 (Unofficial Networks, Nov 21, 2016; AP/Washington Times, Nov 29, 2016).
  - 2025: Nov 28 pushed back to Dec 4 (Unofficial Networks, Nov 25, 2025; Cowboy State Daily, Nov 27, 2025; TGR "confirms Dec 4").
- **Confirmed on time or early:**
  - 2022: opened Nov 25 with five lifts after pre‑season snow (JHMR blog, "5 lifts opening Friday 2022").
  - 2024: opened Nov 29 with three lifts on snowmaking: Teewinot, Après Vous and the Sweetwater Gondola (Buckrail).
- **Scheduled Nov 24 but outcome not confirmed:** 2023. In December 2023 the News&Guide reported "34 inches since Dec. 1… opens additional terrain", which suggests the resort was already open on reduced terrain.
- **2017–2021:** Not confirmed. A Teton‑area radio report said 2016 was the last delay before 2025, which implies no delays in 2017–2024.
- **Rough rate:** about 2 delays in the last ~10 seasons, so roughly 80% on time unconditionally.

**B. On‑time openings given a very poor early snowpack.** This is the more relevant class.
- In the two known delay years, the delay was announced 3–6 days before the target date: Nov 18 in 2016 and Nov 25 in 2025. Both delays ran about one week.
- **2011** is the closest snowpack analog: upper‑mountain depth on Nov 11 was tied with 2026's for the lowest in 25 years. I believe JHMR's scheduled 2011 opening was already in early December (around Dec 3), but I could not verify the scheduled or actual date.
- In poor‑snow years, roughly half or more of Western destination resorts that target Thanksgiving slip. In 2025, Jackson Hole, Sun Valley, Brighton and Park City all reset their openings (KUNC, Dec 2, 2025; PlanetSKI, Nov 26, 2025).

**C. Nearby resorts this season.**
- Grand Targhee, 45 miles away, slipped two days from its Nov 20 target and opened Sun Nov 22 with only the Shoshone lift and one partly groomed run (Jackson Hole News&Guide; DeepArrival, ~Nov 21–22, 2026).
- That is evidence for a short slip rather than a long postponement.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES:**
1. **Snowmaking‑only opening (most likely YES path).** JHMR opens on Nov 27 with one to three lower‑mountain lifts on man‑made snow, as in 2024. This needs:
   - enough cold nights from about Nov 15–26 to build a base (signs point to yes: guns running, "slope of hope" forming, near‑normal temperatures);
   - a resort willing to open on minimal terrain, which Targhee just did.
2. **Natural snow helps.** The Nov 26–28 storm delivers several inches or more and adds cover. Even then, JHMR would most likely open lower‑mountain snowmaking terrain only.
3. **Short slip to Sat Nov 28.** This still resolves YES.

**Paths to NO:**
1. **Delay announced this week** (Nov 23–25), as in 2025 (announced Nov 25). Likely if the snowmaking base is too thin or temperatures were marginal. Past delays ran about a week, to around Dec 3–5.
2. **Storm‑driven postponement.** JHMR pushes the opening to grade and pack new snow or to open more terrain. This is less common, but resorts sometimes prefer a bigger opening.
3. **Operational problems.** Wind holds or a lift fault on opening day could push to Nov 28 (still YES) or later (NO).

**Key dependencies:**
- How much snowmaking JHMR has done by about Nov 24.
- Overnight temperatures Nov 23–26.
- Management's willingness to open on a "white ribbon". In 2025 they delayed instead of doing this, but 2025 was much warmer, which hampered snowmaking.
- Holiday revenue pressure from Thanksgiving weekend pushes toward opening.

## 5. Signposts

**Toward NO (strong):**
- Any JHMR or Buckrail post this week saying "postponed", "delayed" or "new date TBD".
- Overnight warmth that halts snowmaking.

**Toward YES (strong):**
- JHMR naming lifts for opening day, for example "Teewinot, Après Vous, Sweetwater open Friday", or opening‑day logistics posts on Nov 24–25.

**Moderate signals:**
- Toward YES: verified totals from the Nov 26 storm of 6" or more.
- Toward YES: Snow King or other regional resorts holding their dates.
- Toward NO: other regional resorts slipping again.
- Toward NO: a storm that fizzles, as the GFS model shows.

**Timing:** In past cycles a delay was announced 3–6 days before the target date. With no announcement by Nov 23 (4 days out), the chance of a delay is somewhat lower, but 2025's notice came only 3 days out, on the Tuesday of Thanksgiving week.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not view JHMR's official snow report, opening‑day page or social posts. Fetches were blocked, so the latest status (about Nov 19, "still anticipating Black Friday") may be out of date.
- The actual snowmaking progress and base depth on the lower mountain are unknown.
- The opening history for 2017–2021 and 2023 is unverified, as are the scheduled and actual dates for 2011, the best snowpack analog.
- The Thanksgiving storm forecast varies widely between models.
- I don't know whether JHMR's snowmaking capacity changed for 2026‑27.
- The "8% of average" Targhee figure may be a reporting error.
