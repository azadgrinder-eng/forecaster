# Research brief: Will Polymarket's trading volume exceed $4B in December 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** No resolution criteria were given, so the plain reading is that Polymarket's trading volume for the calendar month 1–31 December 2026 is more than $4,000,000,000. The final number won't be known until early January 2027, but the market closes on 28 Nov 2026, so forecasters must predict a month that hasn't started.
- **Where the question probably comes from:** It looks like part of a Manifold series by user "MikhailTal" ("Polymarket exceeds $Xb trading volume", with versions at $4B, $5B, $6B and $10B). Earlier markets in that series named Dune Analytics, Polymarket's own reporting, The Block's Polymarket volume tracker, or DeFi Rate as sources. Their resolutions have used different numbers for the same month. For March 2026, resolutions cited $10.15B, $10.57B (Dune / The Block) and $12.22B (DeFi Rate). A commenter complained that some cited sources returned 404 errors or didn't support the quoted figure (search snippets of the Manifold series pages, 2026). I could not read the text of this specific market because access was blocked.
- **Main ambiguity: which venue counts as "Polymarket"?** The answer almost decides the question.
  - **Polymarket US** (the CFTC-regulated exchange) did about $10B in October by The Block's count, or $14.77–14.9B by TickerTracker and DeFi Rate, measured at face value (Tokenpost / Cointribune / KuCoin, early Nov 2026). This venue alone is likely 2–4× the threshold.
  - **Polymarket Global** (the international, on-chain platform) is near the threshold and falling. The Block's October report says Kalshi traded 18.7× Polymarket Global and that Kalshi was about $65B. That implies roughly $3.5B for Global, possibly measured before the month ended (my arithmetic, from CoinPaper / KuCoin, ~29 Oct 2026). DeFi Rate shows $5.2B in contracts but only $2.6B in dollars actually paid for 3 Oct–1 Nov. Totalis shows $2.69B for October.
  - **Both venues combined** would be about $13–20B in October, far above $4B.
- **Second ambiguity: how volume is measured.** Trackers use notional or face-value contract volume, dollars actually paid, or one side versus both sides of each trade. Paradigm (Dec 2025) showed that many dashboards counted each trade twice, once for the buyer and once for the seller. DefiLlama, Allium and Blockworks said they would fix this. Polymarket's analytics arm disputes that its own figures double-count. Face value and dollars paid differ by roughly 2× (DeFi Rate's $5.2B vs $2.6B for Global in October).
- **Third ambiguity: "exceeds."** The volume needs to be strictly above $4B. That matters only if the figure lands near the line.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Combined or US-venue monthly volume in 2026.** Every reported month from February to October 2026 was above $4B for Polymarket overall:
   - Feb $7.26B (DeFi Rate)
   - Mar about $10.5–12.2B
   - Apr about $9–10.2B
   - May about $7.1B (Global alone, Dune)
   - Jul $7.9B Global plus $5B US
   - Aug $8.16B combined, the low point (The Block)
   - Oct about $13.5B+ combined

   Polymarket US alone has been above $4B every month since June ($4.04B in June, $5B in July, $8.7B in September, $10–14.8B in October). **Base rate if combined or US counts: 9 of 9 months above $4B in 2026.**
2. **Polymarket Global alone.** It was above $4B every month from about February to August 2026: $10.5B in March, about $9B in April, $7.1B in May, $7.9B in July, and roughly $4.5B in August (my estimate from The Block's $8.16B combined figure minus about $3.6B for US). From there it trends down. Q3 international notional volume was $17.1B, down 36% from Q2 (Cryptonews, ~11 Nov 2026). October came in at about $3.5B (The Block, implied), $2.6–2.7B (dollars paid, DeFi Rate / Totalis) or $5.2B (contracts, DeFi Rate). **The trend is down about 40–60% since spring, and October is roughly at or below $4B on most measures.**
3. **Seasonality, December versus October/November.** In December 2025, DeFi Rate showed Polymarket at $2.17B, against about $3.7B in November 2025 on the double-counted Dune figure, or $1.87B on DefiLlama. January 2026 was $4.19B. Last year December was not much weaker than November. This December has no election and no World Cup, but it does have the NFL, college bowl games, the NBA and crypto markets. October and November 2026 were boosted by the US midterms (3 Nov), so December may slip from the Oct/Nov level, perhaps by 10–30% (my estimate).

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **11 Nov 2026:** Polymarket confirmed a "company-wide reduction in force." The cuts hit marketing, customer experience and engineering, and more than 50% were contractors. Polymarket denied that 30% of staff were cut. The company describes the move as a push to compete with Kalshi and to "professionalize" (Reuters / Bloomberg / Fox Business, 11 Nov 2026).
- **~11 Nov 2026:** Q3 notional volume was $17.3B for US and $17.1B for international. US volume was 2.4× its Q2 level, while international fell 36% (Cryptonews, 11 Nov 2026).
- **Early Nov 2026:** Around the midterms, Polymarket's political markets did $396M in the first week of November, up 34% week over week. Politics was about 12% of total volume, which implies roughly $3B+ per week across Polymarket overall. On the US exchange, politics was only 2.2% of November volume, and sports dominates (KuCoin / Crypto-Economy, Nov 2026; CNBC, 9 Nov 2026).
- **Early Nov 2026:** October volume was $14.77B on Polymarket US by TickerTracker's face-value count, with combo contracts at $8.6B (58%) and sports at $4.57B. US monthly volume is up 266% since June. Polymarket US handled 74M trades in October. Active traders hit a record 477,850 in October; this figure is reported by The Block and may cover both venues (Tokenpost / Cointribune / Crypto Briefing, early Nov 2026).
- **29 Oct 2026:** International trading was briefly halted after a third-party provider was compromised. It resumed the same day, and Polymarket promised to reimburse affected traders. A similar vendor compromise happened in June (Cryptonews, Nov 2026).
- **28 Oct 2026:** The Block's October report put Kalshi at about $65B, Polymarket US at about $10B (+15% month over month) and Kalshi at 18.7× Polymarket Global (The Block newsletter, 28 Oct 2026; CoinPaper / KuCoin).
- **2 Sep 2026:** August combined Polymarket plus US volume was $8.16B, down 36.7% from $12.89B in July (The Block, 2 Sep 2026).
- **Regulatory background:** State cease-and-desist orders went to Polymarket US over sports contracts from Tennessee (Jan 2026), Connecticut and Illinois. The CFTC sued Arizona, Connecticut and Illinois in April 2026 to defend its exclusive authority. I found no November update on these cases.
- **Scheduled before or during December:** the NFL regular season (December is mid-to-late season), college football conference championships and the start of bowl season, the NBA and NHL, an FOMC meeting in mid-December, and crypto price markets. There is no major election.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES**
- **Resolution counts combined or US volume.** This is very likely YES. Polymarket US alone would have to fall from $10–15B to under $4B, a 60–75% drop in two months, without a shock. Possible shocks: a court or CFTC action stopping US sports contracts, a major outage or hack, or a methodology change.
- **Resolution counts Global on a face-value basis (DeFi Rate contracts).** October was about $5.2B, so YES needs December to fall by less than about 23%. Roughly a toss-up given the downward trend and the end of the midterms.

**Paths to NO**
- **Resolution uses Polymarket Global only, measured by The Block, dollars paid, or a one-sided Dune figure.** October was already about $2.6–3.5B and falling. Without a reversal, December is likely $2–3.5B.
- **The resolver picks the lower of conflicting trackers**, or the question creator intended "Polymarket" to mean the on-chain platform. Earlier markets in the series relied on Dune and The Block, which in 2026 sometimes reported the two venues together and sometimes separately.
- **Shocks:** further security incidents, the effects of the layoffs, US users leaving the offshore platform, and Kalshi taking market share.

## 5. Signposts

- **Most important:** the exact wording and data source of this market, including any creator comments on US versus Global and face value versus dollars paid. If it means combined or US, the probability moves strongly toward YES. If it means Global or Dune on-chain only, it moves toward NO.
- November 2026 full-month figures from The Block, Dune and DeFi Rate, published 1–3 December, and especially Polymarket Global's post-midterm level. Global under $3B would push toward NO on a Global reading. Global above $4.5B would push toward YES.
- Weekly DeFi Rate and Dune numbers in late November and December. US weekly volume above $1.5B means combined is comfortably YES.
- Any court ruling, CFTC action or state enforcement that halts Polymarket US sports or combo contracts (would push toward NO).
- Further outages or security incidents, or fallout from the layoffs (would push toward NO, mainly on Global).
- Any tracker methodology changes, such as a move to dollars paid or one-sided counting (would push toward NO).

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not read this market's resolution text or creator comments, because fetches of Manifold and its mirrors were blocked.
- I could not open the primary dashboards (Dune, The Block data, DeFi Rate). The proxy refused them, so the figures above come from search snippets and secondary news coverage. Some numbers are month-to-date or rolling windows, not calendar months.
- The October figure for Polymarket Global is implied from a ratio (about $3.5B), not reported directly, and trackers range from $2.6B to $5.2B.
- I have no November 2026 full-month data. December seasonality for the new US venue, which launched in late 2025, has no history to compare against.
- I don't know whether the 477,850 active-trader record (The Block) covers both venues.
