# Research brief: Will Lana Del Rey release an album in 2026?

*Prepared 2026-11-25. My page fetches were blocked by a proxy, so everything below comes from search-result summaries. Exact publication dates are marked where I'm unsure of them.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** a new Lana Del Rey album must be officially available to stream or download on official platforms between market creation and **Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM PT**. If labels or formats are ambiguous, the tie-break order is Spotify > Apple Music > YouTube Music > Deezer.
- **What doesn't count:** live albums, re-releases, deluxe editions, compilations and greatest-hits albums, unless at least 50% of their tracks have never been released before.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Stove** (the album that began as *Lasso*, then became *The Right Person Will Stay*) would clearly count. Its released singles ("Henry, Come On", "Bluebird", and "White Feather Hawk Tail Deer Hunter" from Feb 17, 2026) are only a small share of the expected tracklist.
  - **Spyda** (working title of the companion album) is described as "compiled by as many people as I could find" and a "large" companion LP. If it turns out to be mostly reworks or previously released material, the 50% rule could come into play. More likely it is mostly new material.
  - **These don't count:** a song on *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* (the "Crazy For You" track, released Nov 20, 2026) or a feature on someone else's record (the Zach Bryan duet "Lies", Oct 16, 2026, on his EP).
  - **Vinyl first, streaming later:** if vinyl arrives before streaming, it would not count. The album has to be on streaming or download platforms.
  - **Calendar:** the Friday release dates left in 2026 are Nov 27, Dec 4, Dec 11, Dec 18 and Dec 25 (Christmas, rarely used). Jan 1 falls in 2027. That leaves about 5 weeks.
  - **A summarizer error to ignore:** one search summary said Lana's "10th major album, *First Light*" came out in April. That is almost certainly a mix-up with her theme song for the video game *007 First Light* (Stereogum, early 2026). Her 10th album, Stove, has not been released.

**D. Major-label albums released in December**

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES**

1. **Stove announced at short notice for early to mid-December.** This fits her July timeline (companion done plus about 3 months of vinyl lead time). The GTA VI exposure and new country audience give a marketing reason to move soon.
   - Requires: an announcement within roughly the next 1–3 weeks; the label (Interscope/Polydor) accepting a December slot; vinyl ready or digital going first.
2. **Surprise digital drop** of Stove, or of Stove plus Spyda, before Christmas or on the final Fridays.
   - She has never done this for a studio album, but she is unconventional and controls her rollouts directly through Instagram.
3. **Spyda released first or on its own** (less likely, since she framed the two as a pair).

**Paths to NO**

1. **More slippage:** perfectionism, more songs added, a coordinated vinyl release pushing into Q1 2027, and her pattern of missing every self-set date since 2024.
2. **Bad timing:** the label avoids a December release that competes with holiday season and year-end lists, and a January or February 2027 slot is more normal.
3. **Other priorities:** her attention is on GTA VI promotion, side collaborations (Zach Bryan) and one-off live shows.
4. **Tooling gap:** no preorders, tracklist or cover art have surfaced. Normal campaign infrastructure takes weeks to set up.

## 5. Signposts

**Strongly toward YES**
- Instagram post with a firm date, cover art or tracklist.
- Apple Music or Spotify pre-save pages.
- Preorder listings on her official store, Interscope, Polydor, Rough Trade or similar.
- Retailer vinyl listings with December dates.
- A new single with a "the album is out [date]" message.

**Moderately toward YES**
- Reports of test pressings.
- Interviews or posts saying "next month" or "before Christmas."
- An industry blog leak like Tonspion's (discount this, since the last one was wrong).

**Toward NO**
- Any 2027 date announced.
- Further new-song or companion-album additions.
- Silence through about Dec 12. After that, only a surprise drop on Dec 18 or 25 could resolve YES.
- Vinyl preorders listing Q1 2027 ship dates.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not read full articles, so there may be November 2026 statements about Stove's timing that I missed. The newest Stove-specific item I found is from about October 2026.
- I don't know whether the companion album was actually finished in August as planned, or whether vinyl went to the plants.
- The exact dates of some Stereogum pieces, and of the post saying "still coming / may have a companion", are inferred.
- It's unclear whether Spyda is mostly original material, which matters for the 50% rule if it came out alone.
- The market creation date is unknown. It is probably irrelevant, since no qualifying album has come out in 2026 so far.
- I can't tell how much leverage the label has to block a December release, or whether she would release digitally ahead of vinyl.
