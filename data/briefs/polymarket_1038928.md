## Research brief: Will Ansem appear on a new UpOnly episode by Dec 31, 2026?

**Main finding:** Coinbase's $25M purchase of the UpOnly NFT in Oct 2025 obliged hosts Cobie and Ledger to make eight new episodes. I could not confirm whether any of them have been released, or who has appeared. All direct episode listings were blocked (Apple, Simplecast, Podbay, Podchaser, YouTube), and search indexes show no 2026 episodes. Ansem is a past guest and part of Cobie's circle, but I found no announcement that he will be on the revived show.

### 1. Resolution mechanics
- **YES** requires a *new* full UpOnly episode featuring Ansem (@blknoiz06), publicly released on an official UpOnly platform (YouTube, Spotify, Apple, or the official feed) by **Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET**. About 5 weeks remain from Nov 24.
- Clips, previews and teasers don't count. The decision rests on UpOnly's official channels and/or "consensus of credible public reporting."
- **Edge cases:**
  - Ansem's two existing UpOnly appearances (Nov 30, 2021 main show; Jan 2022 "Untitled Crypto Podcast" ep 1) are not new and should not count.
  - Unclear whether these would count:
    - A brief call-in or cameo, or Ansem as one of several panelists. The original show often had 3–6 guests per episode.
    - A spinoff or side-feed show (the 2022 "Untitled Crypto Podcast" ran on the UpOnly feed).
    - A live X/Twitch stream that isn't later posted as an episode.
    - An episode recorded but published after Dec 31.
  - The rules say "the specified guest," so this appears to be one line of a multi-guest market ("Who will appear as a guest on UpOnly?"). Other lines resolve independently.

### 2. Reference classes and base rates
- **How often Ansem appeared in the original run:** about 1 of ~78 main-feed episodes (≈1.3%). Counting the UpOnly-produced spinoff, about 2 appearances in roughly 15 months of activity, 2021–22 (IMDb episode listing; UpOnlyTV tweet, Jan 2022; Podbay lists 78 episodes). Per episode, that's roughly a 1–3% chance of Ansem being on any given episode.
- **Applied to an 8-episode revival:** the original multi-guest format often put several traders on one episode (e.g. "Kaleo, Rookie, Loma, Altcoin Psycho, and Mayne"). Assuming 1–4 guests per episode and that Ansem stays a top crypto-Twitter trader with existing ties to Cobie, a reasonable prior is that he appears somewhere in an 8-episode season perhaps 15–35% of the time. This assumes the season is actually made. It is my estimate, not a measured frequency.
- **Whether contractual or commissioned revivals deliver on time:** the NFT terms said a season would start within ~3 months of the burn, i.e. by about Jan 2026 (Crypto Briefing, Oct 2025). Another report gives Oct 21, 2025 as the start (CoinJournal, Oct 2025). Cobie is famously idiosyncratic and is now inside Coinbase after the ~$375M Echo acquisition (Unchained, Oct 2025). Even if episodes are being released, fewer than all 8 may be out by year-end, which cuts the number of chances.

### 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 22–24, 2026:** Ansem is active publicly. He is posting bearish macro and short-term trade calls (ZEC, HYPE, PUMP bottoming Nov 23–24), "holding cash," and expects a crypto bull market around Q2 2027 (Bitcoin Sistemi / ChainCatcher, Nov 22–24, 2026; TokenPost, Nov 15 and Nov 3, 2026). He is available and commenting on markets, which fits UpOnly's trading-guest format.
- **~early Oct 2026:** A multi-guest market on Versus ("Who will appear as a guest on UpOnly?") listed Ansem as a candidate, about 7 weeks before today. This suggests the season was not finished by October and that Ansem's appearance was unconfirmed then. I did not use its price.
- **2026 generally:** None of my 12 searches found reporting of a new UpOnly episode, a guest announcement, or a delay update. The index may lag; direct checks of the channels failed (proxy blocks).
- **Oct 2025:** Coinbase bought Cobie's UpOnly NFT for $25M USDC; Brian Armstrong confirmed the show is coming back. Cobie and Ledger are obliged to produce 8 episodes. The terms let them "ignore the buyer completely or call them idiots" (The Block, Oct 20, 2025; Cryptonews, Oct 2025; NFT News Today, Oct 29, 2025). The purchase was bundled with Coinbase's ~$375M acquisition of Echo (Unchained, Oct 2025).
- **Dec 2022:** Last episode of the original run, around the FTX collapse. FTX had been a sponsor (The Block, Oct 2025).
- **Jan 2022:** "Untitled Crypto Podcast, ep 1" with Meltem Demirors, Avi Felman, Cobie and @blknoiz06 (Ansem), released through UpOnly (UpOnlyTV on X, Jan 2022).
- **Nov 30, 2021:** UpOnly episode "Hentai Avenger and Ansem on trading, trends, and market cycles," 2h23m (IMDb).
- **Scheduled events before close:** I found no announced episode dates. The YouTube channel's old listing says "Thursdays at 8 pm UTC" for the main show (UpOnly YouTube). Thursdays left before close are Nov 26 (US Thanksgiving), Dec 3, 10, 17, 24 and 31.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES**
- The season is underway with episodes left to release, and one of the remaining slots features Ansem, alone or in a trader panel. A Q4 2026 market-outlook or trader roundtable episode would naturally include him given his current high profile.
- A year-end "wrap-up" or multi-guest special that includes him.

**Paths to NO**
- The 8 episodes are already done without Ansem, or the season has stalled (no releases at all) or slipped into 2027.
- Only a few episodes come out in the last 5 weeks, which leaves few slots. The holiday period also cuts recording output.
- The new run uses a different guest mix: Coinbase or industry executives, builders, policy people. Or Cobie, now inside Coinbase, avoids a memecoin-associated trader. Ansem has faced memecoin-promotion controversies.
- Ansem appears only on a live stream or in clips that never become a full official episode.

**What it mostly depends on:** (a) whether the revival season is live and how many episodes are left, (b) the format (trader panels vs. one-on-one interviews), and (c) whether Ansem appears with Cobie or Ledger anywhere else.

### 5. Signposts
- **Strongly toward YES:** an UpOnly, Cobie or Ledger post teasing or announcing Ansem; Ansem saying he's recording with them; a clip with Ansem on UpOnly accounts (clips don't count, but they usually precede the full episode).
- **Toward YES:** confirmation that the season is active with 2 or more episodes left before Dec 31, especially a trader or market-outlook episode.
- **Strongly toward NO:**
  - All 8 episodes already released without him.
  - The season has not started, or a 2027 start date has been announced.
  - Guest lists announced for the remaining episodes that leave him out.
  - Quiet official channels into mid-December.
- **Mildly toward NO:** Ansem traveling or offline over the holidays; Cobie saying the season is about something else, such as Coinbase or builders.

### 6. Uncertainties
- **Most important:** whether the revival season has started and how many episodes have been released. Direct checks of the feeds (Apple, Simplecast, Podbay, Podchaser, YouTube) were all blocked, and search indexes showed no 2026 episodes. A forecaster should check youtube.com/@UpOnly and the podcast feed directly.
- The conflict between the Oct 21, 2025 start date and "within three months of the burn" in the NFT terms is unresolved.
- Whether the new run keeps multi-guest trader panels, and whether it is still published on the same YouTube channel and feed.
- How the resolver would treat cameos, side or spinoff shows, or live-only streams.
- The state of the Cobie–Ansem relationship in 2026 (no recent evidence either way).
