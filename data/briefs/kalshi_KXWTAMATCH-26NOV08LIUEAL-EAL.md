## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (Eala wins):**
- A large gap in quality and ranking: a top-15-level player against a No. 102.
- Experience winning big finals, including the comeback against Pegula in Washington.
- Her left-handed patterns against a counterpuncher.
- Strong support from Filipino fans in Hong Kong.

**Paths to NO (Liu wins):**
- Liu's form: she hasn't dropped a set all week and has already beaten a seed.
- She has beaten Eala before, coming from a set down.
- Eala may be tired after a long, rain-broken three-setter the day before, while Liu's semifinal was quicker.
- Pressure on Eala as the heavy favourite in front of a crowd that expects her to win.
- Eala has twice this week let leads slip: the Sakkari third set, and a tight second-set tiebreak against Valentová.

**Other outcomes:**
- Eala retiring mid-match resolves NO.
- An injury withdrawal before the match resolves to the fair price.
