## 1. Resolution mechanics

**What counts for YES**
- Rubio's hourly "Odds of Happening" value in the "2028 Republican Nomination" listing on PredictionMarketOdds.com (PMO) must be **≤15.0%** (compared at 0.1% precision) for **4 consecutive published hourly values**.
- All four must be for datapoints between market creation and **Jan 1, 2027, 12:00 AM ET**. That leaves about 37 days, or roughly 890 hourly windows.

**How runs are counted**
- Any hour above 15.0% ends a run.
- Hours shown as "NO_PRINT" are ignored: they neither count toward a run nor end it. So four ≤15% prints separated by gaps still count.
- **Who decides:** PMO's published hourly series, on the listing page and on Rubio's "Odds History" detail page. Prices on individual exchanges (Polymarket, Kalshi, etc.) don't count.

**Edge cases**
- **Clearly wrong values:** there is a 48-hour window for PMO to publish a correction, which then replaces the original. If no correction appears, the original value stands. A data glitch that prints Rubio near 0% for 4+ hours could therefore resolve YES unless PMO fixes it within 48 hours.
- **PMO stops publishing Rubio** (for example, removes him from the listing): the question resolves on values published up to that point.
- **Underlying market decided early:** YES if Rubio is not the outcome. In practice this can't happen before Dec 31, 2026.
- **Methodology unknown:** I couldn't find PMO's method. I don't know if it averages several exchanges (as ElectionBettingOdds.com does with bid/ask midpoints) or uses last trade, nor how it builds an hourly value. If it uses last trade on a thin market, a single odd trade could print outliers. A smoothed multi-exchange average makes brief dips much less likely.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

These are approximate, from my background knowledge; I couldn't verify the price paths in this session.

**A. Big, fast drops for a nomination co-favorite in a quiet ~5-week window more than a year before the primaries.**
- Historically rare without a discrete shock (a candidate ruling himself out, a scandal, or a clear endorsement of a rival).
- In the 2024 GOP cycle, DeSantis fell from roughly co-favorite to the mid-teens over about 6–9 months (early to late 2023), not over weeks.
- Big single-step drops usually follow a decisive event, such as a candidate ruling himself out or the dominant figure endorsing someone else.
- Rough guess: in a 5-week window with no decisive event, a front-runner losing more than half its implied probability happens well under 10% of the time.

**B. Early "invisible primary" leads that collapse.**
- Early polling leaders often fade over a full cycle: Giuliani in 2008, Jeb Bush in 2016, DeSantis in 2024.
- Those collapses took months and came with campaign events.
- That supports some NO-side decay later, but not much inside 37 days.

**C. Heir-apparent dynamics.**
- When a sitting VP or the incumbent's anointed successor declares, rivals' odds usually drop sharply (for example, field-clearing around Gore in 2000).
- An actual Vance announcement plus a Rubio endorsement before Dec 31 is the main fast route to YES. Its frequency depends on announcement timing (see section 4).

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (Rubio at or below 15% for 4 straight hours)**
1. **Vance announces or firmly signals a run, Trump backs him, and Rubio reaffirms he'll support Vance and won't run.** This is the cleanest route. It probably needs Vance to make his decision in December. Reports so far say he is undecided and weighing things.
2. **Rubio rules out 2028 on his own,** for example by choosing the 2032 or private-sector path, or by reaffirming he'll stay at State through Jan 2029.
3. **Trump publicly anoints Vance or someone else** (a less likely reversal given his recent praise of Rubio).
4. **A Rubio-specific shock:** an Iran-war disaster tied to him, a scandal, health, or a fallout with Trump.
5. **A PMO data or methodology artifact:** a thin market, a stale exchange feed, or a reweighting that prints ≤15% for 4+ hours and isn't corrected within 48 hours.
6. **Rubio's starting level is already close to 15%.** Unknown to me; check it.

**Paths to NO**
- The status quo through the holidays. Rubio's momentum from the midterms and polls continues, he keeps saying "not discussing 2028," and Vance stays undecided.
- With strong recent polls and draft talk, the information flow is currently tilted in Rubio's favor.
