## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What resolves YES:** the finalized Carbon Arc "Casino Ad Spend" value for November 2026 must be strictly above 68.5. A value of exactly 68.5 resolves NO.
- **What the index measures:** year-over-year change, where 100 means spend equal to November 2025. 68.5 therefore means a 31.5% decline. YES needs casino ad spend to fall by less than 31.5% YoY (or to grow).
- **When it is fixed:** the value Carbon Arc shows at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 5, 2026 counts. The market closes Dec 6, 04:59 UTC. Revisions after that snapshot do not matter.
- **Who decides:** Kalshi (ticker family KXCASINOADS), using Carbon Arc data. Kalshi's sector KPI contracts settle on Carbon Arc "Prisms" data (Kalshi/Carbon Arc announcement; Markets Media).
- **Most likely source series:** the public Carbon Arc Prisms page has an "Advertising › Growth" table with a "Casino" row. Carbon Arc describes Prisms as free daily reference charts that track a named set of brands against the same period a year earlier, "through the latest available day of data." The values are relative, not dollar amounts. Carbon Arc also says the panel "does not include every transaction" and is best used for direction and growth (Carbon Arc Prisms page; Carbon Arc docs, "Intro to Prisms").
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - I could not confirm which brands are in the "Casino" group. It could be land-based operators, online casino brands, or both.
  - I could not confirm which ad channels are measured (digital, TV or other).
  - The value is captured only 5 days after month end. If late ad data has not arrived by Dec 5, the value may be provisional, and it is unclear whether the YoY comparison then uses the same partial window from last year.
  - The value is computed daily, so it can move during the month and up to the snapshot.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (value above 68.5):**
- The index stays in its recent range (about 75–90). This is the persistence case and the most likely path if September's roughly 79 is representative.
- November 2025 was a weak month for the panel's casino brands, which makes the comparison easy.
- New iGaming launches or casino promotions in Q4, or measured prices rising after the election.

**Paths to NO (68.5 or below):**
- The casino brands' ad budgets keep shrinking. Operators are visibly reallocating: BetMGM, cost cuts at Bally's, and UK tax pressure on international operators.
- Political crowd-out in early November cuts casino ad volume relative to a non-election November 2025.
- November 2025 was unusually strong, for example a big holiday or iGaming launch push.
- Panel or methodology quirks: incomplete data at the Dec 5 snapshot, or brand-coverage changes, could push the index down sharply. The 46.5 strike hints the exchange sees this tail as plausible.

The outcome mostly depends on where the Casino index stood in October and early November 2026, which I could not see.
