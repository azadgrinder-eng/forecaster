# Research Brief: Will Diable's next team be Bilibili Gaming (BLG) for 2027?

*Prepared 2026-11-25. Every page fetch was blocked by the proxy, so everything below comes from search-result summaries. I could not read any article in full, so quotes and dates may be slightly off. The Chinese sources are mostly bloggers and self-media, and the evidence should be weighted with that in mind.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **YES (BLG)** requires BLG or Diable (Nam "Diable" Dae-geun, a Korean ADC) to officially announce that he has signed with BLG's main League of Legends roster. The announcement must come **by Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET** (Jan 1, 2027, 04:59 UTC). A consensus of credible media reports can also be used, but the primary source is the acquiring team's announcement.
- **The first official signing ends the market.** If JD Gaming or any other team announces him first, BLG resolves NO right away, even if BLG was interested.
- **"Other"** applies if he joins an unlisted team, has no announcement by the deadline, retires, or ends up unsigned. Re-signing with Nongshim RedForce (NS) does not count, and that path now looks closed because NS released him (see §3).
- **Edge cases:**
  - A signing to an academy or development roster, or a non-player role, does not count. This is unlikely for him.
  - A deal agreed in December but announced after Dec 31 resolves "Other." This matters little, because LPL teams usually announce their rosters in late November or December, before the January season start.
  - Contract start date, league registration and first match date are all irrelevant.
  - Leaks, insider reports and GCD (the Korean league's Global Contract Database) entries are not enough on their own unless they add up to a "consensus of credible media."

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Named-destination prediction for a free agent with several suitors.** Chinese and Korean leaker reports in this period link Diable to two LPL teams, JDG and BLG, with weaker talk of LEC or LCS teams. When a free agent is credibly linked to two specific teams, the eventual destination is usually one of them, but the split is rarely 50/50. My rough heuristic from past LoL off-seasons (not a measured statistic) is that the team named "most often linked" in Western outlets ends up signing the player somewhat more than half the time. Western coverage (DPM.LOL, Sheep Esports) calls **JDG the most-linked team** for Diable (DPM.LOL LCK free agency 2027, Nov 2026).
2. **Conditional chain.** BLG only needs a new ADC if Viper (Park Do-hyeon, the incumbent ADC) leaves. Reports frame Diable as BLG's option **if Viper leaves**. So P(BLG) ≈ P(Viper leaves) × P(BLG picks Diable over Elk, JiaQi or others | Viper leaves) × P(JDG or someone else doesn't sign him first). For example, 0.75 × 0.45 × 0.8 ≈ 0.27. These numbers are illustrative, not measured.
3. **Timing.** Nearly all top free agents in the LCK and LPL off-season are announced between mid-November and mid-December. Players as in-demand as Diable ("the hottest young AD of this window," per Sina) very rarely stay unsigned past Dec 31. A rough estimate is that more than 90% of starting-calibre ADC free agents have an announcement before January.

"If Viper's talks fall through, BLG's bot lane next year is very likely him" (Sina 电竞热点小时报, Nov 24 2026). Another Sina piece says one analysis frames BLG's AD as a choice between **Elk and Diable**. It also notes Diable has been seen learning Chinese (Sina "都在猜 BLG 的 AD 人选", ~Nov 22–24). One leaker thread claims BLG is considering JiaQi, Elk and Diable. Another claims BLG is *not* considering Diable. A Chinese headline claims "BLG management firmly rejects Elk; new AD seemingly decided," which is unverified.
- **Nov 23:** Viper's agent was quoted saying "KRX.Viper is already 95%" and that they will respect Viper's choice either way (直播吧/zhibo8, Nov 23 2026). KRX here is Kiwoom DRX, an LCK team. This is the strongest signal so far that Viper is leaving BLG.
- **~Nov 21–22:** A rumor from the leaker Yuuu says that before final negotiations, BLG is prioritising keeping the same roster, and that Bin is likely to extend. Reports say BLG went to Korea to try to keep Viper and put a large retention package on the table, described in one source as a salary in the tens of millions of RMB (Sina "Viper 加盟 KRX 传闻反转"; Sohu). A Tencent piece, based on fan reports, says Viper wants to return to Korea for his mother's cancer treatment (Tencent News, Nov 21 2026).
- **Nov 20:** China.com reports that "multiple sources" say Viper rejected KRX, so the picture is contradictory. As of Nov 20, neither BLG nor Viper had announced a renewal or departure.
- **~Nov 19:** JDG's ADC slot was reportedly down to **Diable or Taeyoon**, and both were trialling with the team. JDG planned to settle its mid and jungle players first, because of limits on import slots. Its support, Vampire, reportedly prefers a domestic AD (smzdm, ~Nov 19). Separately, the Yuuu rumor said "KT Taeyoon locked." If Taeyoon goes to KT, JDG's main alternative to Diable disappears, which works against BLG.
- **Nov 17:** Viper's BLG contract expired. His GCD end date was moved on Nov 12 from 2027 to 2026. He went back to Korea after BLG won Worlds 2026 (DPM.LOL; Insider Gaming). Korean paper Kukmin Ilbo reported Viper had agreed to join DRX for 2027, together with Lucid and Scout. Nothing was official as of the latest results.
- **Nov 16–17:** NS officially parted ways with Lehends, **Diable**, Scout and Sponge (Sheep Esports on X). Deft, returning from military service, is reported as NS's new ADC.
- **Earlier 2026:** In April, Diable was traded from BNK FearX to NS in exchange for Taeyoon. This followed his demotion to FearX's second team after he refused to attend a fan meeting. Sheep Esports described him as having "burned bridges" (Dot Esports; NetEase 163; Sohu).
- **Other BLG context:** Knight, ON and Xun are reportedly under contract through 2027. Bin's contract was ending, and he is reported likely to extend. BLG would have import room for a Korean ADC once Viper leaves.
- **Scheduled events:** No fixed dates are known. Expect a wave of official LPL and LCK announcements through December. The KRX or BLG announcement about Viper is the key upcoming event.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES**
- KRX officially announces Viper (the agent says 95%). BLG then moves quickly for Diable, who reportedly wants BLG and shares Viper's agency. The fact that they share an agency could make it easier to line up the two moves.
- JDG chooses Taeyoon or a domestic AD, which the reported Vampire preference supports, or JDG delays. That leaves BLG as Diable's best LPL offer.

**Paths to NO (another team)**
- **JDG signs him first.** JDG is the most-linked team in Western outlets, he has trialled there, and Tabe (JDG's head coach) reportedly rates him. If Taeyoon is "locked" to KT, JDG's alternative is gone.
- **Viper stays at BLG.** BLG has made a large retention offer and reportedly prioritises keeping its roster. In that case BLG has no opening.
- **BLG picks Elk, JiaQi or another ADC.** That could be for chemistry or language reasons, or because of concerns about Diable's attitude after the FearX incident.
- Diable goes to an LEC, LCS or other LPL team. This looks unlikely.

**Path to "Other" by timing:** He is not announced by Dec 31. This looks unlikely.

## 5. Signposts

- **Official KRX announcement of Viper, or BLG farewell post:** strong move up for BLG.
- **BLG announces Viper's renewal:** BLG falls close to 0.
- **JDG announces any ADC:** Diable signing → BLG resolves NO. Taeyoon or a domestic AD → move up for BLG.
- **KT officially signs Taeyoon:** move toward JDG, so down for BLG.
- **BLG announces Elk or JiaQi:** BLG falls close to 0.
- **Reports from reliable insiders** (Sheep Esports, Kukmin Ilbo, well-known LPL leakers) that Diable has agreed with BLG: large move up.
- **BLG or Diable teasers,** such as social-media hints or Chinese-language content.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not read any article in full, so dates and attributions come from search summaries.
- Most BLG–Diable sourcing is streamers or self-media. One leaker directly says BLG is not considering Diable.
- The sources conflict on Viper's status: the agent says 95% KRX, while China.com reports he "rejected KRX."
- I don't know the exact LPL import-slot rules for 2027, or how they interact with BLG's and JDG's other Korean players.
- I don't know how far along JDG's mid and jungle decisions are, which reportedly have to be settled before it picks an AD.
- I don't know how much Diable's past discipline issue weighs with BLG's management.
