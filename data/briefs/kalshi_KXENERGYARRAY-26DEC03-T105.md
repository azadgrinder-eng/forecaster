# Research brief: Will Carbon Arc's Energy Drinks Array for November 2026 be above 105?

**Access limits:** I could not open Carbon Arc's Prisms page, the SEC filings or Kalshi. Each fetch was refused or blocked, so I have no published history of this array or of any of its constituent indices. Everything below comes from search-result summaries of earnings releases, call transcripts and trade press. I also ran 13 searches, one more than the 12-search limit.

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Carbon Arc's full-month Energy Drinks Array value for November 2026 must be strictly above 105. A value of exactly 105.0 resolves NO. The value is taken as Kalshi records it at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 2, 2026. Later revisions don't count.
- **How the array is built:** It is the equal-weighted mean of six year-over-year indices of convenience-store point-of-sale spend: Red Bull, Monster Energy Drinks, Celsius, Alani Nu, C4 Energy and Rockstar Energy, each weighted 1/6. Each index equals 100 × (Nov-2026 spend ÷ Nov-2025 spend). YES therefore needs the six brands' average YoY growth to be above 5%.
- **Equal weighting is the key feature.** A small, fast-growing brand (Alani Nu) counts as much as Monster or Red Bull. Shrinking small brands (Rockstar, possibly C4, the Celsius brand) pull the average down just as much. The array is *not* the category's growth rate.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Publication timing:** It's unclear whether Carbon Arc will publish the full November value by December 2. Data lag or a partial-month value could matter, and the rules don't say what happens if no value is posted.
  - **Unknown data source:** I could not tell whether Carbon Arc's "point-of-sale" data is scanner data from store chains or a card/receipt panel. A panel would make the small-brand indices noisier.
  - **Brand mapping:** It's unclear how sub-brands are assigned. For example, Monster's "Monster Energy Drinks" may or may not include Reign or Bang, and C4 may or may not include C4 Smart Energy.
  - **Membership changes:** Constituents are fixed "at listing," and the rules don't say whether they could change.
- **Calendar:** November 2026 starts on a Sunday and November 2025 started on a Saturday. Thanksgiving falls inside the month both years (Nov 26, 2026 vs Nov 27, 2025), so calendar effects should be small.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **US convenience-store energy-drink dollar growth (category level):**
   - Full-year 2025: +10% to more than $16B, with units +8% (CStore Dive citing Circana, early 2026).
   - 52 weeks to July 12, 2026: +10.5%, with non-aseptic energy +11.1% (CSP Daily News citing Circana, mid-2026).
   - Early 2026: +7% (Talk Business citing Nielsen, April 2026).
   - All outlets, 13 weeks to October 24, 2026: +12.4% including shots (Monster Q3 call citing Nielsen, Nov 6, 2026).
   - Over the past roughly two years the category has grown 7–12% in every period I found. Casey's grew energy-drink dollars per store per day at a 16% three-year CAGR.
   - However, a category average dominated by Monster and Red Bull is not the same as an equal-weighted brand average.
2. **Equal-weighted average built from the latest brand data (Q3 2026, mostly all tracked channels):**

   | Brand | Latest growth figure | Source and date | Implied index |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Alani Nu | +57.9% (13 weeks to Sep 27); about +50% excluding the Witch's Brew limited flavors | Celsius release, Nov 5, 2026 | ~158 |
   | Celsius brand | −7.0% | Celsius release, Nov 5, 2026 | ~93 |
   | Rockstar | −13.2%, from planned SKU cuts | Celsius release, Nov 5, 2026 | ~87 |
   | Monster | Ultra +19%, Juice Monster +21% (Nielsen, Q3) | Monster call, Nov 6, 2026 | ~112–118 |
   | Red Bull | +5.5% in c-stores (52 weeks to Jul 12) | CSP / Circana, mid-2026 | ~105 |
   | C4 | No figure found; Ghost is KDP's growth focus | — | assumed 90–100 |

   - The Monster figures are family-level, and one transcript instead gives 42% for Ultra, so treat them with caution.
   - The plain average of the implied indices is about **108–110**.
   - C-store-specific Alani Nu growth may be well above the all-channel figure: Alani Nu's top-five c-store item grew dollars +114.6% (CSP / Circana, 52 weeks to July 2026), helped by PepsiCo store-delivery distribution gains.
3. **Sensitivity of the equal-weighted average:** Each brand moves the array by 1/6 of its own index change.
   - A pessimistic mix gives about **104.7**: Red Bull 103, Monster 110, Celsius 90, Alani Nu 160, C4 85, Rockstar 80.
   - A central mix gives about **111**: 105 / 113 / 93 / 175 / 92 / 87.
   - An optimistic mix gives about **118**: 106 / 115 / 95 / 200 / 100 / 90.
   - YES mainly depends on Alani Nu's c-store growth staying above roughly +50–60%.

- **Scheduled before the close:** No major company earnings. Circana or Nielsen four-week reads may appear in trade press or analyst notes. Kalshi records the November value on December 2.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (array above 105):**
- Alani Nu's c-store distribution build (PepsiCo store-delivery rollout, new cooler space) keeps its index around 150–200.
- Monster stays in the 110s and Red Bull stays at or above 103.
- Category momentum holds at about +10–12%.

**Paths to NO:**
- Alani Nu's growth slows sharply as it laps November 2025, a period of heavy distribution gains and seasonal limited flavors.
- Rockstar's SKU cuts deepen to −20% or worse in c-stores, and C4 is weak (possibly falling as KDP prioritizes Ghost).
- The Celsius brand keeps falling in c-store planograms (cooler shelf-space resets).
- Carbon Arc's measurement differs systematically from scanner data. For example, a card panel could under-represent fast distribution gains, or small-brand indices could be noisy.
- Broader c-store traffic weakens. Traffic was reported as reduced in early 2026 (Talk Business, April 2026).

**Main dependencies:** Alani Nu's c-store growth rate, and the gap between Carbon Arc's data and Circana/Nielsen figures.

## 5. Signposts
- **Biggest signal:** Carbon Arc's or Kalshi's reported values for September or October 2026 of this array or its constituents. If recent months were around 110 or higher, YES is likely. If they were around 103–106, it's close to a coin flip.
- **Weekly constituent readings for November:** Kalshi's brand POS markets, especially Celsius and Alani Nu.
- **Four-week Nielsen or Circana reads through mid-November**, especially Alani Nu's c-store growth and Rockstar's decline rate.
- **Analyst scanner notes** (Nielsen four-week data, often published weekly) for Monster and Celsius Holdings.
- **Other factors:** November limited-flavor launches (Alani Nu holiday flavors) and weather or traffic news from c-store chains.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not get any historical level of the Carbon Arc Energy Drinks Array or its components. This is the biggest gap.
- Carbon Arc's data source (scanner data vs card or receipt panel), the channel definition and the brand mapping are all unknown.
- C4's c-store trend for 2026 is not established, and neither is Red Bull's trend after July 2026.
- Alani Nu's November 2025 comparison base is unclear, as is how much of its 2026 c-store growth came from one-off distribution gains that may already have been lapped.
- It's unknown whether Carbon Arc publishes the full November value in time for the December 2 recording.
