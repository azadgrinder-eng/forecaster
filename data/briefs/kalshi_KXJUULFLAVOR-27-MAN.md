# Research Brief: Will JUUL release Mango to the public before Jan 1, 2027?

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** JUUL "releases Mango to the public" before Jan 1, 2027. The market closes 2027-01-01 04:59 UTC, which is about 5 weeks from today (2026-11-27).
- **Who decides:** Not stated. It is probably the market creator's judgment.
- **The biggest ambiguity is where the release has to happen.** This matters more than anything else in the brief.
  - **US reading:** In the US, a mango JUUL pod can only be sold legally after an FDA marketing order (PMTA) for that specific product. None exists for any JUUL or JUUL2 mango pod (vapecityusa.com, 2026; FDA JUUL2 release, Aug 28, 2026).
  - **Anywhere-in-the-world reading:** Juul Labs UK launched a JUUL2 **Mango** pod, along with Apple, Watermelon and Lychee, through UK wholesalers, convenience stores and juul.co.uk starting **3 October 2025** (Talking Retail, Oct 1, 2025; Vape Business, ~Oct 2025; Convenience Store UK, ~Oct 2025). If any market counts and the question was written before October 2025, it may already be YES in substance. If the question was written after that launch and still left open, the creator almost certainly means a US release, or a release they did not consider to have happened yet.
  - **Recommendation:** Forecasters should find out when the question was created and how the creator has clarified it. Under the US reading, the answer is clearly heading to NO.
- **Other edge cases:**
  - JUUL sold mango in the US from about 2015 to 2019. It pulled mango from US stores in November 2018 and stopped all mango sales in October 2019 (CNBC, Nov 9, 2018; NPR, Oct 17, 2019). Whether a "re-release" counts is probably fine, but that history shows the question is about a *new* release.
  - Grey-market and counterfeit "JUUL Mango" and "JUUL2 Mango" listings on US-facing and Dubai retail sites (pricepointny.com, heatedland.com, vapewizarddxb.com, all 2026) are not JUUL releasing the product.
  - Glas's **mango** e-cigarette, sold as "Gold" and authorized by FDA on May 5, 2026, is another company's product and should not count (Quartz, May 7, 2026).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Time from JUUL PMTA filing to FDA authorization:**
   - The original JUUL PMTA was filed in 2020. It was denied in June 2022, the denial was rescinded in 2024, and it was authorized on July 17, 2025, about 5 years after filing (FDA, July 2025).
   - The JUUL2 (next-generation menthol pod) PMTA was filed around December 2023 and authorized on August 28, 2026, about 32 months later (Juul Labs, Dec 2023; FDA, Aug 2026).
   - **0 of 2 JUUL applications took less than 2.5 years.** No mango or fruit JUUL application is publicly known to have been filed.
2. **FDA authorizations of fruit-flavored e-cigarettes, from any company:**
   - Only one exists: Glas mango and blueberry, May 5, 2026. Those PMTAs were filed in July 2021, about 58 months before authorization (FDA Glas Director Summary, May 2026).
   - FDA's fastest recent PMTA decision was a nicotine pouch authorized about 3 months after scientific review began. That was called a record (FDA CTP statement / Vaping360, 2026).
   - Even at that record speed, an application would have had to be in scientific review by roughly late August 2026, and nothing public suggests one was.
3. **Time from authorization to launch:**
   - JUUL has moved quickly once authorized. JUUL2 was described as soon available after the August 2026 order.
   - In the UK, the new flavours went from announcement (late September 2025) to shelves (October 3, 2025) in about a week.
   - So launch speed is not the bottleneck. The bottleneck is getting the FDA order.

**My rough inference:** For a US release, P(FDA authorizes a JUUL mango pod *and* JUUL launches it within the next 5 weeks) looks well below 2%, given that no filing is known and FDA timelines are measured in years.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Oct 22, 2026:** A European Patent Office appeals board revoked a JUUL airflow patent (Nicotine Insider, Oct 22, 2026). This is not relevant to flavors.
- **Sep 3, 2026:** A Delaware patent suit was filed claiming that JUUL and JUUL2 infringe a patent (Nicotine Insider / search summary, Sep 2026). This could become a minor distraction for JUUL.
- **Aug 27–28, 2026:** FDA authorized the **JUUL2 device plus Virginia Tobacco and Polar Menthol pods** (18 mg/mL) (FDA, Aug 28, 2026; AP/News4Jax, Aug 28, 2026).
  - Age verification through an app is **optional** on JUUL2 (CBS News, Aug 2026; Dexerto, Aug 2026).
  - CEO K.C. Crosthwaite said JUUL will build on JUUL2 with "a robust series of FDA filings," including 5% nicotine products and "a portfolio of flavored varieties" beyond tobacco and menthol (Juul Labs statement, Aug 2026; Morningstar/Dow Jones, Aug 28, 2026).
  - The company gave no filing dates, did not name specific flavors, and did not mention mango.
- **May 5, 2026:** FDA's first authorization of fruit-flavored ENDS went to **Glas** (mango and blueberry, plus two menthol pods). Approval depended on a device that requires ID verification, Bluetooth pairing and biometric check-ins (Quartz, May 7, 2026; NatLawReview, May 2026).
  - FDA's own summary said the data showed no statistically significant switching advantage over tobacco flavor (FDA Glas Director Summary).
  - Senators objected (Blumenthal letter, May 15, 2026).
- **Mar 11, 2026:** FDA published draft guidance on flavored ENDS PMTAs. It focuses on youth risk and "device access restrictions," meaning age-gating (Federal Register 2026-04732, Mar 11, 2026). The comment period closed around May 2026. I could not confirm whether the guidance has been finalized.
- **Oct 3, 2025:** JUUL2 Mango, Apple, Watermelon and Lychee pods launched in the UK (Talking Retail, Oct 1, 2025; SLR Magazine, ~Oct 2025).
- **Jul 17, 2025:** Original JUUL device and Virginia Tobacco and Menthol pods (3% and 5%) authorized in the US (FDA, Jul 2025).
- **Scheduled events before close:** None identified. FDA does not pre-announce PMTA decisions. The next relevant thing would be FDA publishing a marketing order on its tobacco marketing orders page.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES:**
- **(a) Interpretive YES:** The resolver counts a non-US mango release. The UK launch already happened in October 2025, and JUUL could release mango in other non-US markets. Whether this works depends entirely on the question's intent and creation date.
- **(b) Surprise US authorization:** JUUL might have quietly filed a flavored JUUL2 PMTA, possibly with mandatory age-gating to fit the Glas precedent and the draft guidance. FDA would then need to issue an order in December 2026 under its faster-review push, and JUUL would need to ship immediately. That chain would require several unlikely steps all happening in 5 weeks.
- **(c) Resolver error:** The resolver counts grey-market listings. This is unlikely.

**Paths to NO (the default):**
- FDA has not authorized any JUUL mango product, and no filing is publicly reported.
- Even if JUUL filed after August 2026, decisions take months to years.
- Selling unauthorized flavored pods in the US would risk FDA enforcement and JUUL's existing marketing orders, which FDA has said it can withdraw over youth marketing. JUUL would not do that.

## 5. Signposts

- **Strongly toward YES (US reading):**
  - FDA newsroom or marketing-orders page lists a JUUL or JUUL2 flavored, especially mango, pod.
  - A JUUL press release announcing a US flavored launch.
- **Moderately toward YES on longer horizons only:**
  - Reports that JUUL has filed fruit-flavored JUUL2 PMTAs with mandatory age-gating.
  - FDA finalizing the flavored-ENDS guidance.
  - FDA authorizing further fruit flavors from other companies, such as Breeze.
- **Toward NO:**
  - Continued silence through mid-December.
  - FDA leadership or political changes, or litigation challenging the Glas order.
- **Resolution-interpretation signposts:**
  - Any comment from the question creator on whether the UK launch counts.
  - The question's creation date relative to October 2025.

## 6. Uncertainties

- **Geography and question creation date:** I could not see the question's creation date or any clarifications. This decides whether the UK launch matters.
- **Whether JUUL has filed a fruit-flavored PMTA:** I could not confirm whether JUUL has submitted one, mango or otherwise. Companies do not have to disclose filings, and FDA does not publish pending applications.
- **Status of the flavored-ENDS guidance:** I could not confirm whether it has been finalized since the comment period closed around May 2026.
- **Source quality:** Several facts came from search summaries rather than full pages, because direct fetches of fda.gov, juullabs.com and betterretailing.com failed (proxy refused). Many 2026 "JUUL flavor" pages are retailer SEO blogs.
- **Small factual conflicts:**
  - The JUUL2 authorization is dated August 27 in some sources and August 28 in others.
  - JUUL2 pod strength is reported as 18 mg/mL in some sources and 1.6% in JUUL's own statement.
