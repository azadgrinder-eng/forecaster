The facts needed for YES appear to be in place already. The remaining risk comes mainly from how the rules are read, especially if Liberty hosts the Dec. 4 CUSA title game.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

- **Path to YES (dominant):** The regular season ends Nov. 28 with an away game. Liberty finishes 6-0 at home, and Kalshi resolves YES. This needs nothing further on the field.
- **Paths to NO (all low probability):**
  1. Kalshi or its data source counts the Dec. 4 CUSA Championship Game as part of the "regular season," and Liberty hosts and loses it. This needs both an unusual reading of the rules and a loss.
  2. A data or resolution error, such as misclassifying a home game or using the wrong team.
  3. A retroactive change to a result, such as a forfeit or vacated win from an NCAA ruling. This is extremely unlikely in this time frame. Vacated wins usually do not turn wins into losses anyway.
