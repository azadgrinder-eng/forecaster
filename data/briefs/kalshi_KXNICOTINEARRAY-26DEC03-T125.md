## Research brief: Carbon Arc Nicotine Products Array for November 2026, above 125?

**Access limits:** WebFetch was refused by the proxy for every page I tried, including Carbon Arc's prism page. So I could not see any past or current array values, and I could not read any page directly. Everything below comes from search-result summaries of filings, transcripts and trade press. Treat the exact figures with some caution.

### 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Carbon Arc's published full-month value of the Nicotine Products Array for November 2026 must be strictly above 125. A value of exactly 125.0 resolves NO.
- **When it is recorded:** Kalshi records the value at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 2, 2026. Later revisions do not count.
- **How the array works:**
  - It is the equal-weighted mean (¼ each) of four brands' year-over-year indices: Zyn, Velo, on! and Rogue.
  - The data is convenience-store point-of-sale **spend** in dollars, comparing November 2026 with November 2025.
  - A brand index of 100 means flat spend. For example, 150 means +50% and 80 means −20%.
  - Above 125 requires the four brands' YoY growth rates to average more than +25%, so their indices must sum to more than 500.
- **Edge cases:**
  1. Carbon Arc may publish late or only a partial month by Dec 2. The rules don't say what happens then.
  2. Brand membership could change after listing. The rules only say "at listing."
  3. Carbon Arc's data is probably a panel (card or receipt data) rather than a census, so small brands such as Rogue may be noisy. This is inferred; I could not confirm the methodology.
  4. Because brands are weighted equally, one brand that is growing fast (Velo) moves the array as much as the market leader (Zyn).

### 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Carbon Arc's own published pattern.** Its case study says Velo's share of c-store oral nicotine spend **doubled over twelve months**, Zyn and on! grew **only single digits**, and Rogue **declined** (Carbon Arc Prisms case study, undated, probably 2025–26).
   - If you plug that pattern in (Velo about 200, Zyn about 105, on! about 105, Rogue about 90), the array is about **125**, right at the threshold.
   - That is the most YES-favourable reading. It needs Velo to still be doubling YoY in November 2026.
2. **Velo's growth slowing as it laps its launch.** BAT's reported growth for Velo/Velo Plus is falling as it laps the Velo Plus rollout:
   - **290%** YoY volume growth (CAGNY, Feb 2026)
   - **>200%** for Velo Plus in H1 2026 (BAT H1 results, 30 Jul 2026)
   - A "Velo +66%" headline in H1 coverage (BigGo, 30 Jul 2026). Its scope is unclear; it may be revenue.
   - Velo's US modern-oral volume share went from **24%** (CAGNY, Feb 2026) to **31%** (value share about 26%) in H1 2026. BAT also cited a +10.4-point YoY share gain (pre-close update, 2 Jun 2026).
   - If Velo's share was about 22–25% in November 2025 and is about 32–36% in November 2026, and category units are up about 10–17%, Velo's volume is up roughly **+50–85%**. Its spend may be up less if heavy promotion pulls prices down.
   - Under this reference class, Velo's index is about 150–185.
3. **Mature or losing incumbents (Zyn, on!, Rogue).**
   - **Zyn:** analysts cited scanner data showing **mid-single-digit declines** in Q3 2026 (PMI Q3 call, 21 Oct 2026). Another source has Zyn at about **−1%** in a recent 4-week period, versus +12.7% over 52 weeks (Tobacco Insider, about Oct 2026).
   - **on!:** its share of the pouch category was **13.5%** in Q3 2026, down 1.3 points YoY and 0.9 points from the prior quarter (Altria 10-Q, Q3 2026). Altria's oral tobacco shipments were down **4.3%**.
   - **Rogue:** no 2026 data found. Carbon Arc says it is declining.
   - Plausible indices: Zyn about 92–105, on! about 92–105, Rogue about 80–100.

**Arithmetic summary:**
- Central case: Zyn 98, Velo 165, on! 97, Rogue 88, giving about **112**.
- For the array to exceed 125 with the others near 95 on average, Velo needs to be above about 215, i.e. spend more than doubled.
- Alternatively, the incumbents would need to show surprise growth.

### 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **18 Nov 2026:** US median shelf price is about $5.00 for a 20-pouch can versus $6.01 for a 15-pouch Zyn can (PouchBuddy price tool, updated 18 Nov 2026). This is a weak, secondary source.
- **Oct 2026:** US c-store smokeless sales were up **11.6%** YoY in dollars to $13.6B, driven by pouches. Zyn's 4-week growth was about −1%, which the source blames on Velo Plus and moist pouches (Tobacco Insider, about Oct 2026).
- **From Oct 2026:** Zyn's flagship 1.5/3/6 mg cans moved from **15 to 20 pouches at the same can price**. PMI says that is about 25% less revenue per pouch.
  - Spend per can is roughly unchanged, so any effect on spend comes through can volume.
  - It could lift Zyn units, or cannibalise them if users buy fewer cans.
- **21 Oct 2026, PMI Q3 results:**
  - US Zyn shipments rose **4.2%** to 3.2B pouches, or +13% excluding the September 2025 promotion. Year-to-date shipments are down 6.0%.
  - PMI says Zyn's offtake share rose 1.4 points from June to September, to about **46.0%**, after falling from 47.5% in March to 44.6% in June.
  - Q4 US profit is expected to be about zero because of heavy investment. SG&A was about $370M in Q3 and is expected to rise about 10% in Q4.
  - Analysts said scanner data showed Zyn retail down mid-single digits in Q3 (PMI Q3 call, 21 Oct 2026; PMI 8-K, Oct 2026).
- **Late Oct 2026, Altria Q3:**
  - on! share of the pouch category was 13.5% (down 1.3 points YoY).
  - Pouches are 61.2% of US oral tobacco, up 5.8 points (Altria 10-Q, Q3 2026).
  - on! PLUS 12 mg has rolled out, but its effect on share is "yet to become evident" (Zacks, 2026).
- **Jun–Jul 2026:**
  - Circana: pouches grew **+29% in dollars and +17% in units** over the 52 weeks to 14 Jun 2026 (via c-store trade press).
  - Zyn ULTRA (moist, 20-count, cheaper per pouch) launched in June 2026.
  - BAT launched Velo Max (higher moisture) from Q3 2026.
- **Scheduled before Dec 2:** BAT's 2026 pre-close trading update is probably in early-to-mid December, which is too late. There are no other scheduled company disclosures before resolution.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (array above 125):**
- Velo's c-store spend is still roughly doubling YoY in November. Possible causes:
  - Velo Plus/Max distribution gains.
  - A weak November 2025 Velo base. For example, the Velo Plus rollout was still ramping or heavily discounted at $2.99/can (Tobacco Insider, about Mar 2025).
  - The panel over-representing Velo's channels.
- Zyn's 20-count can re-accelerates its can volume, giving Zyn an index of about 105–110.
- on! PLUS 12 mg lifts on! spend.
- Rogue's index is noisy and swings high.

**Paths to NO:**
- Velo's growth keeps decelerating to about +50–80% as it laps November 2025, when its share was already in the low-to-mid 20s.
- Zyn stays flat or negative.
- on! keeps losing share.
- Rogue keeps declining.
- Under this path the array lands about 105–118.

### 5. Signposts
- **Most important:** the October 2026 and earlier monthly array values and brand indices on Carbon Arc or Kalshi. I could not access them. The latest month's level is by far the best predictor.
  - If October was 120 or higher with a steady trend, YES is plausible.
  - If October was 115 or lower, and especially if the trend is falling, NO is very likely.
- The trend in Velo's own index: whether it is above or below 200 in the latest months.
- Weekly Nielsen or Circana reads for November from Goldman, Barclays or trade press:
  - Velo dollar growth above +100% points up.
  - Zyn dollar growth below 0 points down.
- Early read on Zyn can volume after the switch to 20-pouch cans.

### 6. Uncertainties
- **No historical array levels.** I have no array values for any month, so I cannot see whether it has recently been near 125. This is the main gap.
- Carbon Arc's methodology is unknown: panel type, coverage, and whether it has re-weighted or restated data.
- Velo's November 2025 base level in Carbon Arc's data is unknown.
- I found no Rogue data at all.
- The scope of BAT's "+66%" and "+200%" figures is unclear (volume vs revenue, Velo vs Velo Plus).
- How Zyn's 20-count switch will affect spend per transaction is unclear.
