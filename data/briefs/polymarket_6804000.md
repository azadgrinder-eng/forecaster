## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What YES needs:** the score after 90 minutes plus stoppage time is not one of the scores the market explicitly lists. Extra time and penalties don't count.
- **The listed scores are not given in the question.** Exact-score markets of this kind usually list every score from 0-0 to 3-3 (16 scores). If that holds here, "Any Other Score" is YES only if **either team scores 4 or more goals** (4-0, 4-1, 5-2, 4-4 and so on). If the market lists fewer scores (for example nothing above 2-2, or no 3-3), YES becomes much more likely. **This is the key thing forecasters should confirm.**
- **Kickoff:** Saturday 21 Nov 2026 at 13:30 Turkey time (10:30 UTC, 5:30 AM ET) at Eryaman Stadium in Ankara. The club's official fixture list and several outlets confirm this (Gençlerbirliği official fixtures; T24/Diken Week 12 schedule, ~9–10 Nov 2026). The question closes at kickoff.
- **If the match doesn't happen:** cancellation, no start by 23 Nov 11:59 PM ET, or abandonment with no official result resolves to **"0-0", which is NO here**. If the match is abandoned, an awarded result is used. TFF forfeits are usually 3-0, which would also be NO under a 0-0 to 3-3 grid.
- **Sources:** TFF first, then Flashscore, then Sofascore. Score changes made after resolution are ignored.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. League-wide scoring (Poisson estimate).** The 2025-26 Süper Lig finished with 812 goals in 306 matches, 2.65 per game, the lowest in 12 seasons (Sporx season goal report, May 2026). The first half of that season averaged about 2.60 (TFF data via Yeni Akit, Jan 2026). Assuming a typical home/away split of 1.45 and 1.15 goals, a Poisson model gives:
- P(home team scores 4+) ≈ 5.9%
- P(away team scores 4+) ≈ 3.0%
- **P(either team scores 4+) ≈ 8.7%**

These are my own calculations. Real score distributions have slightly more 0-0 and 1-0 results than Poisson predicts, but also fatter tails in mismatches. For a match between two weak sides, a range of about **7–9%** is reasonable.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (a team scores 4+):**
1. **Gençlerbirliği hammers a collapsing Gaziantep.** This is the main path, roughly 6–9%. It depends on Gaziantep's disarray (coach turnover, a boardroom change, a losing streak with 2–3 goals conceded most weeks), an early Gençlerbirliği goal, or a Gaziantep red card. Gençlerbirliği showed it can do this with the 5-0 over Alanyaspor.
2. **Gaziantep scores 4+ against a leaky, injury-hit Gençlerbirliği defence.** Roughly 1.5–3%. Gaziantep has managed it once this season (4-2 vs Göztepe), but its attack is the league's weakest or close to it, at 0.82 goals a game.
3. **A high-scoring game where both teams score freely** (for example 4-2 or 4-3). Tail risk only.

**Paths to NO:**
- Any score within the grid. Relegation six-pointers between two weak attacks tend to be cagey and low-scoring.
- A cancelled or postponed match resolves to 0-0, which is NO. That risk is small.

## 5. Signposts

- **The market's list of scores** matters most. If it lists 0-0 to 3-3, the estimate is about 7–12%. If the grid is smaller, YES is substantially higher.
- **Gaziantep's coach situation:** a caretaker plus continuing board chaos makes a heavy defeat a little more likely (up). An experienced appointment such as Demirel or Tekke settling the squad points down slightly.
- **Gençlerbirliği team news:** confirmation that Thalisson and the captain are out, and whether Baltacı is suspended. A weaker Gençlerbirliği defence modestly raises Gaziantep's tail.
- **Lineups about an hour before kickoff,** and any weather or security-related postponement (a postponement means NO).
- **Live:** a red card or a two-goal lead before half-time sharply raises the chance of 4+.
