# Research Brief: Will none of CASHCAT, AI (Artificial Inu), ANSEM or NET reach $1B FDV by Dec 25, 2026?

*Method note: Every direct page fetch was blocked, including CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, DexScreener, OpenSea and Etherscan. The figures below therefore come from search-engine snippets of those pages and from news articles, so treat every number as approximate. I did not look up any prediction-market prices.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **YES** means that none of the four coins shows an FDV of $1,000,000,000 or more on its assigned source at any moment between market creation and **Dec 25, 2026, 11:59 PM ET**. That leaves about 38 days from today, Nov 17.
- **NO** means at least one coin touches $1B FDV even for a moment, as long as it can be verified from the resolution source or from a consensus of credible reporting.
- **Sources for each coin:**
  - CASHCAT, ANSEM and NET use the "Fully Diluted Valuation" figure on CoinGecko.
  - AI uses the "FDV" figure on CoinMarketCap.
  - Each coin is judged only against its own source, and differences in method between the two sites are not adjusted for. Circulating-supply market cap does not count.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Data glitches.** A "momentary crossing" counts. Aggregator FDV is price × total (or max) supply, so a spike from a thin liquidity pool or a supply mis-entry could briefly show a bogus FDV of $1B or more. The pools here are thin: GeckoTerminal showed NET at about $648 in one thin Uniswap pool, while the protocol's own NAV was about $163 (snippets, undated). Whether such a print counts as "verifiable" is unclear.
  - **NET has a rebasing supply.** NetNet works like an OHM-style reserve fund, with staking rebases and bond sales, so total supply grows over time. FDV can rise from supply growth as well as price, although at the current pace this is tiny.
  - **The AI page on CoinMarketCap may be stale.** One snippet described the CMC AI page as "data marked 123 days ago" (undated). If CMC stops publishing data for AI, the "other credible sources" fallback applies.
  - **Ticker confusion.** Several unrelated "ANSEM" tokens and "Artificial Inu" listings exist, including CoinGecko slugs artificial-inu-2 and -3. Only the listed contract addresses and URLs count.
  - **Creation date unknown.** The market's creation date isn't given. None of the four appears to have reached anywhere near $1B at any point since launch, so this probably doesn't matter.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Memecoins that reach $1B FDV at all.** Only a few dozen memecoins have ever passed $1B, including DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, BONK, WIF, FLOKI, TRUMP, POPCAT, MOG, SPX6900, Fartcoin, GOAT and PENGU, out of millions launched. Even among coins that once reached a $100–400M peak, a clear minority went on to $1B. All four coins here peaked at $400M or less, and three peaked at roughly $200M or less.
2. **Coins already down 70–90% from their peak that then rise 10x or more within about 6 weeks.** This is very rare, and I estimate it at well under 5% per coin. Revivals like SPX6900 and Fartcoin took months and needed a strong market for memecoins. Each coin's required move:
   - **CASHCAT:** about 11x from roughly $90M (Nov 6).
   - **ANSEM:** about 17–23x from roughly $43–58M.
   - **AI:** about 35x from roughly $28M (Nov 16).
   - **NET:** about 35–45x from roughly $21–29M. Its price is also tied to a reserve of about $23M in USDG, so a $1B FDV would need about 40x NAV or roughly $1B of new reserves.
3. **Robinhood Chain ecosystem history.** The chain launched July 1, 2026. Its whole meme category peaked at about **$842M combined** in early September (Robinhood Chain guide, Sept 2026). Later it fell to about $358M, with only 5 tokens above $10M (Crypto Briefing, roughly Aug–Oct 2026). No Robinhood Chain token has reached $1B. The largest, PONS, peaked at about $392–576M (Yahoo Finance, Sept 2026).

Etherscan showed $171.55 and about 10.9K holders on Oct 11. A thin pool price spike in GeckoTerminal implied an FDV of about $47.75M.
- **Scheduled events before Dec 25:** I found no scheduled token-specific catalysts. Macro events include the December FOMC meeting (mid-December) and US data releases.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to NO**, roughly in order of plausibility:
- **CASHCAT mania (most plausible).** It is the closest coin and the one with retail access through the Robinhood app. It would need a renewed Robinhood Chain frenzy, for example a new Tenev or Robinhood endorsement, a major CEX listing such as Coinbase or Binance spot, or a broad memecoin rally. Even then it needs about 3x its all-time high.
- **ANSEM revival.** This would need Ansem himself to push it hard, plus a Solana meme season. The Nov 11 news that he backed a new token works against this.
- **AI rally.** The token is very volatile (+33% days), but it needs about 35x.
- **Data or display glitch** on CoinGecko or CMC, mainly from thin pools or supply errors, which could be argued to count as a "momentary crossing." Low probability, but not zero.
- **NET.** This is structurally near-impossible because the price is anchored to NAV and the reserves are about $23M.

**Path to YES:** the status quo. Prices are 70–92% below their peaks, macro is risk-off, the Robinhood Chain meme sector is shrinking, and the window is short at about 38 days.

## 5. Signposts

- **Toward NO:**
  - CASHCAT moving above about $300M FDV, a new all-time high.
  - Any new listing of CASHCAT, AI or ANSEM on Coinbase, Binance or the Robinhood app.
  - A new public endorsement from Tenev or Robinhood.
  - Robinhood Chain DEX volume returning toward its August record of about $989M per day (The Block, Aug 31).
  - BTC reclaiming $80K or more with a memecoin rally.
  - Ansem shilling ANSEM again.
- **Toward YES:**
  - BTC falling further.
  - The Robinhood Chain meme category shrinking below $200M.
  - Robinhood disclaimers about tokens (a similar warning preceded $WALLET's 90% crash, per Crypto Briefing).
  - Ansem's attention moving to the new token.
  - Each week that passes with no catalyst.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I couldn't verify any current FDV directly on CoinGecko or CMC because fetches were blocked. All figures are from snippets, some undated and some conflicting.
- I don't know whether CMC is still updating the AI page live, or exactly how CoinGecko computes NET's FDV given its rebasing supply and an odd circulating figure of about 4.8K.
- The market's creation date is unknown, though no coin appears to have come anywhere near $1B since launch.
- It is unclear how the resolver would treat a brief glitched FDV print on an aggregator.
- I don't know the exact nature of the "new token" Ansem backed on Nov 11, or how it affects ANSEM.
