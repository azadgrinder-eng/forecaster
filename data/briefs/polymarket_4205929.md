## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **This is one option in a multi-outcome market.** The market resolves to the next team Demon1 (Max Mazanov) officially joins by Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET (close is 2027‑01‑01 04:59 UTC). The MIBR option resolves YES only if MIBR, or a consensus of credible media, officially announces that Demon1 has signed with MIBR within that window.
- **The first official signing settles the market.** Any official signing announcement resolves it immediately. If he officially joins any other team first (including an open-qualifier or tier-2 team, if that counts as "officially joins"), MIBR is NO even if he moves to MIBR later.
- **Fallback outcomes contradict each other, but MIBR is NO either way.** If he joins no new team, the market resolves to "Team Envy". If he is released, retires, or has no contract by Dec 31, it resolves to "Other". Those two clauses can collide, for example if his ENVY contract simply expires at the end of 2026, but both outcomes are NO for MIBR.
- **Edge cases:**
  - A reported but unannounced deal (journalist "sources" only) as of Dec 31. The rules say consensus media reporting "may" be used, so a strong multi-outlet report could plausibly count.
  - Trials and stand-ins are probably not an "official join".
  - The title says "play for MIBR in 2027", but the criteria are about signing, not about playing matches in 2027.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Specific rumored destination becomes the actual signing.** Demon1 has been linked to MIBR before. Several vlr.gg fan threads titled "MIBR demon1" date from the 2025 off-season after his LEVIATÁN release (Aug 2025), including one claiming a video from aspas and zekken hinted at it. None of it happened: he went to Dragon Ranger Gaming, then substituted for Cloud9 at Kickoff 2026, then signed with ENVY on Feb 5, 2026. Of the publicly floated landing spots in his last three free-agency periods (post-NRG 2024, post-LEV 2025, now), MIBR went 0 for 1. In general, fan-forum rumors with no reporter behind them come true well under 20% of the time. Mine is a rough estimate, not a measured rate.
2. **Free-agency periods ending in a new signing before Dec 31.** Demon1's past free agencies resolved slowly:
   - LEV released him in Aug 2025, and he had no tier-1 team until a Cloud9 stand-in role about Jan 2026 and the ENVY signing on Feb 5, 2026.
   - In Dec 2025 he said he would probably retire if he got no team (Sheep Esports, Dec 22, 2025).
   - He also said G2 had trialled him for 2026 and picked someone else.
   - So in 2 of his last 2 free agencies, no new tier-1 team was announced by about Dec 31. That puts the base rate for "no signing at all by Dec 31" fairly high.
3. **MIBR signing North American imports.** MIBR has been willing: its 2025–26 roster included the Americans zekken and tex alongside aspas, Verno and Mazino. But MIBR's 2027 rumors so far are all Brazil- or Chile-based players (see section 3), and the 2027 rules appear to cap imports at 2 per roster (section 4).

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (MIBR):**
- **MIBR wants a duelist or flex import.** MIBR is rebuilding from zero after losing aspas, its star duelist, and zekken. Demon1 is a former world champion duelist and costs little, since his ENVY contract runs out at the end of 2026.
- **The rules allow it.** Reported VCT 2027 rules require at least 3 residents and allow at most 2 imports. If Brazil is its own residency territory, an NA player is an import for MIBR, and MIBR would need to spend one of its two import slots on him. If the Americas count as one region, there is no constraint (sources conflict: esportnow.gg vs Dot Esports).
- **Is MIBR still in the league?** MIBR's place in VCT 2027 matters. "No Guaranteed Paths: Inside the New VCT 2027" (Riot) suggests partner teams may no longer have guaranteed slots. If MIBR has to come through Brazil qualifiers, it will probably field a mostly Brazilian roster.

**Paths to NO:**
- **The default is no signing.** Demon1 has no reported link to any team, he leans toward streaming, and he has threatened to retire. If nobody signs him, the market resolves to "Team Envy" or "Other".
- **MIBR builds a Brazilian or LATAM core.** Its current rumors (dgzin, Less, lukxo, erde) are all Brazil or LATAM players, and erde, as a Chilean, could take an import slot.
- **He signs elsewhere first:** an NA tier-2 or open-qualifier team, or an international org.
- **Timing.** About 40 days remain, and many 2027 announcements may land in January.

## 5. Signposts

- **Toward YES (strongly):**
  - A credible report (Sheep Esports, Dot Esports, Spilled, or Brazilian outlets like Mais Esports or Dust2 Brasil) linking Demon1 to MIBR.
  - Demon1 or MIBR social posts hinting at it.
  - Demon1 appearing in MIBR scrims or as a stand-in.
- **Toward YES (moderately):** MIBR confirming 4 players with one slot open for a duelist or import, or MIBR having confirmed VCT Americas partner status for 2027.
- **Toward NO:**
  - MIBR announcing a full 5 with no Demon1, or using both import slots on other players (erde, a European, and so on).
  - Clarification that Brazil is a separate residency territory.
  - Demon1 announcing streaming, retirement, or another team, or appearing on an NA qualifier roster.
  - ENVY confirming that it is exiting VALORANT or that he has been released, without a MIBR link.
