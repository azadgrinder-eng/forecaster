# Research brief: Will The Weeknd be credited on *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*?

*Prepared Nov 15, 2026. Note: every direct page fetch was blocked by the network proxy, so the facts below come from search-result summaries of the cited articles. I could not read the full leaked 34-track list line by line.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** The Weeknd must be credited as a featured or credited artist on at least one track of *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*. The primary source is Rockstar's official page (rockstargames.com/VI/music) and Rockstar announcements. If those don't settle it, official credits on Spotify or Apple Music can be used.
- **Deadline:** The album must be released by Nov 26, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise the market resolves NO.
- **Release date:** Officially Nov 19, 2026, the same day as the game. Rolling Stone and RockstarINTEL report the album slipped a day to Fri Nov 20. Other outlets say Nov 20 is only when physical copies ship. Either date falls inside the deadline.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Wrong product.** "Wanderlust" (from *Kiss Land*, 2013) reportedly plays on the in-game radio. That is a licensed track, not part of the album, so it does not count. The album is billed as "34 original tracks."
  - **Separate score album.** Justice's *Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score* comes out Dec 11 with 30 tracks. It is a different product, so even a Weeknd–Justice score cue would not count.
  - **Writer or producer credits.** Spotify's "Credits" panel lists songwriters and producers. If Abel Tesfaye showed up only as a writer or producer, it's unclear whether that counts as "credited artist." The intent of the question is a performer credit, so a strict resolver would probably not count it.
  - **Name.** The Weeknd dropped his plan to retire the stage name in Aug 2026, so any credit would almost certainly read "The Weeknd."
  - **Late digital additions.** A bonus or extra track added only on streaming after the physical back cover was printed is the main way the leaked list could be wrong.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Tracklists leaked from physical packaging that later prove wrong about which artists appear.** Back covers printed for retail (vinyl and CD) almost always match the final standard edition. Gaps usually come from typos, clean-version title changes, or digital-only bonus tracks. In my judgment (no systematic dataset), an artist missing from a leaked back-cover list ends up credited on the final album well under 5% of the time. Surprise additions for a fixed 34-track soundtrack whose total was announced in September seem even less likely.
2. **Artist "teases" turning into credited soundtrack appearances.** Travis Scott's GTA teases (Complex, Sept 2026) did turn into a credited track. The Weeknd's supposed tease was weaker: an Instagram Story photo with Justice in mid-October. He already has a known working relationship with Justice ("Wake Me Up" on *Hurry Up Tomorrow*, 2025), and he has posted Justice photos before (Mixmag Asia, 2024), so the post carries little signal on its own.
3. **Any given A-list artist appearing on a big curated soundtrack before leaks.** With roughly 34 tracks and about 45–50 credited acts, a specific top-10 global pop/R&B star had maybe a 10–20% chance before any leak. That prior has now been largely replaced by the leak itself.

- **Earlier 2026:** A leaked gameplay clip reportedly had "Wanderlust" playing in a Vice City nightclub. That is in-game radio, not the album (Pop Crave / NewsWeeknd).

Future & Metro Boomin, Morgan Wallen, Keith Richards, PinkPantheress/Fred again.., and others). Pre-orders opened for CD and vinyl, with physical shipping from Nov 20 (Consequence, Sept 2026; MusicBusinessWorldwide; KitGuru). Cardi B's track came out in October.
- **Game date:** Nov 19, 2026, reaffirmed by Take-Two through at least mid-2026 (TweakTown). I found no report of a new delay. Even if the game slipped, the album has a separate deadline buffer to Nov 26.
- **Scheduled before close:** Album out Nov 19 (or Nov 20); game Nov 19; physical copies ship Nov 20.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (all low-probability):**
- **Uncredited back-cover feature.** He is a guest on an existing track but left off the back cover, then credited on Spotify or Apple Music. This would most plausibly be a Justice, Metro Boomin/Future, or Travis Scott track, given his past collaborations with all of them. It requires the packaging to have left out a feature, which is uncommon for big names because they are selling points.
- **Digital-only bonus or surprise track** added at release. This conflicts with the repeatedly announced count of "34 original tracks."
- **Leak is wrong.** The list is fake or comes from an outdated pressing. This seems unlikely: it was corroborated by JustJared and Rolling Stone sources, and the running order matches the positions of the songs already released.
- **Loose resolution.** A writer or producer credit on a streaming platform gets counted as "credited." The market wording ("featured or credited artist") makes this unlikely.

**Paths to NO:**
- **Main case.** The final tracklist matches the leak, which has no Weeknd. His only GTA VI link stays in-game radio ("Wanderlust") and possibly the separate score or nothing.
- **Album slips past Nov 26.** This looks very unlikely given the announced date and that physical copies are already at retailers.

## 5. Signposts

- **Biggest mover:** the official Rockstar tracklist or full streaming credits on Nov 19–20. That settles it almost completely.
- **Toward YES:** The Weeknd, Justice, Metro Boomin, or Travis Scott teasing a "GTA" collaboration in the next 4 days; Rockstar or Atlantic announcing "bonus" or "surprise" tracks; full-credit leaks from streaming pre-saves (Apple Music pre-add pages often show features).
- **Toward NO:** Rockstar or Atlantic publishing the 34-track list matching the leak; more retail copies or photos confirming the same back cover.
- **Separate product:** A Weeknd credit on the Dec 11 score album is irrelevant to this market.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not read the full leaked list myself because page fetches were blocked. That The Weeknd is absent rests on search summaries and on RockstarINTEL's explicit statement.
- Rockstar has not officially confirmed the list. Sources disagree on some details.
- I could not verify whether any listed track has uncredited guest vocals.
- I could not confirm whether the Nov 20 album date is a real one-day slip or just the physical shipping date. It doesn't matter for the deadline.
- I found no Take-Two statement on the game's release date dated after mid-2026, though nothing suggests a delay.
- The Weeknd–Justice Instagram photo is reported only by fan accounts. I could not verify its date or context.
