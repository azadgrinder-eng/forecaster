# Research brief: Will Take-Two report that GTA VI has reached 50 million or more units sold-in before January 1, 2027?

*Prepared November 22, 2026. Note on sourcing: the page fetcher's connection was refused for every page I tried (SEC.gov, CNN, ABC, GameSpot, GamesBeat and others). The facts below therefore come from search-result summaries of those pages, not from my own reading of them. Before trading on exact wording, especially the Take-Two 8-K text, confirm it at the primary source.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What YES needs:** Take-Two Interactive (including Rockstar Games) must *publicly report*, before January 1, 2027, that GTA VI has reached at least 50 million units sold-in. The source is Take-Two or Rockstar itself. Analyst figures, Sensor Tower, Circana and media estimates do not count.
- **What NO means:** If Take-Two publishes nothing that meets the bar by the deadline, the market resolves NO. The "No data" clause means silence counts as NO for this strike. The deadline falls on New Year's Eve at midnight US Eastern time (05:00 UTC on January 1).
- **The question is about disclosure, not sales.** The game probably will pass 50 million units sold-in before December 31. Whether Take-Two *announces* it before then is a separate question.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Wording.** Take-Two traditionally says "sold-in" (units shipped to retailers or sold digitally). The November 20 release reportedly said "32.5 million units sold pre-release," without the word "sold-in." If a later release says "sold more than 50 million units," it would most likely count, but that is a judgment the resolver would have to make.
  - **Dollars, not units.** A revenue-only announcement (for example "$5B+ in the first week") would not count, even if it implies more than 50 million units.
  - **Vague or derived figures.** It is unclear whether wording like "approaching 50 million," or a number calculated from other disclosures, would count. Probably not.
  - **Executive interviews.** If Strauss Zelnick (Take-Two's CEO) states a number on air, for example on CNBC, it would probably count as "publicly reports," but the rules don't say.
  - **Timing of the routine report.** Take-Two's normal disclosure, the fiscal Q3 FY2027 earnings report covering October–December, will almost certainly come in early February 2027. That is after the deadline. Recent Q3 reports came out on Feb 7, 2024, Feb 6, 2025 and Feb 3, 2026 (Fool transcript, Feb 3, 2026).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. When Take-Two discloses unit numbers for its biggest launches (the most important reference class)**
- **GTA V (launched Sept 17, 2013):**
  - Sept 18: press release reporting about $800M on day one.
  - Sept 20: press release reporting $1B in three days.
  - The first official unit number, "nearly 29M sold-in," came only with fiscal Q2 earnings on Oct 29, 2013, about six weeks after launch (Game Informer / Engadget, Oct 29, 2013).
  - The next figure, more than 32M, came at the following earnings report (NZ Herald / Game Developer, early 2014).
  - In short, there were revenue-milestone press releases between earnings reports, but unit numbers appeared only with earnings.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2 (launched Oct 26, 2018):**
  - A press release reported $725M in three days.
  - The first unit figure, 17M sold-in after eight days, came with earnings on Nov 7, 2018 (Gematsu / Fortune / CNBC, Nov 2018).
  - The next figure came at the February 2019 earnings report.
- **Count:** In 2 of 2 comparable launches, Take-Two gave no unit figure outside an earnings release after the launch week. In both cases an earnings date happened to fall soon after launch.
- **GTA VI has already broken that pattern once.** The day-one press release on Nov 20, 2026 included a unit count (32.5M sold before launch). This makes a further release with a unit count, such as a "first week" or "fastest to X" announcement, more plausible than history alone suggests.

**B. How fast sales grow after launch (does the threshold get crossed before Dec 31?)**
- **GTA V:** about 29M sold-in after six weeks, and more than 32M after about 3.5 months. Roughly 90% of the first-quarter volume came in the first six weeks.
- **RDR2:** 17M after eight days, and 23M by the end of December (about 1.35 times the eight-day figure after about two months).
- **GTA VI so far:** 32.5M units sold before launch, plus heavy day-one sales.
  - $3.79B in retail sales through the end of Nov 19 implies roughly 39–47M units if pre-orders are included in that total. The range depends on the average price paid: $79.99 for the Standard edition (about 47.4M units) versus $90–96 if many buyers chose premium editions (about 39.5–42M units) (Shattered / AppleScoop analyses, Nov 20–21, 2026).
  - Applying RDR2's ×1.35 growth from launch week to year-end gives more than 50M sold-in by Dec 31 in almost every plausible case.
- **Conclusion:** the threshold is very likely crossed in reality, probably by early-to-mid December and possibly already by the end of launch week.

**C. Pre-launch forecasts (useful only as background)**
- M Science projected up to 50M copies by the end of 2026.
- Morgan Stanley projected about 40M in the first four months, through the fiscal year ending March 2027 (TweakTown, about mid-2026).
- Actual results are tracking ahead of both forecasts.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 20–22, 2026:** I found no Take-Two or Rockstar sales update after Nov 20, either for three days or for the first weekend (search results, Nov 22).
- **Nov 20, 2026:** Take-Two issued a press release, filed as Exhibit 99.1 to an 8-K, stating:
  - $3.79B in estimated worldwide retail sales through the end of Nov 19, which it called an entertainment record.
  - 32.5M units sold before release, a record across all platforms (SEC 8-K Ex. 99.1; GameSpot; VideoCardz; RockstarINTEL; AP via KNOE/KKTV, Nov 20–21).
  - The release does not split the dollar figure between pre-orders and launch-day sales, and gives no platform or digital/physical breakdown.
- **Platforms and pricing:**
  - The game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PC version yet.
  - Analysts estimate about 75% of sales are on PS5.
  - The Standard edition costs $79.99.
- **Sensor Tower estimates (not official):** about 24.6M digital copies and about $2.36B on day one across PS5 and Xbox. Its PS5 estimate was 18.44M copies and its Xbox estimate 6.15M (Vice / SteamDB, Nov 2026).
- **Nov 19, 2026:** GTA VI launched on schedule, after delays from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026 and then to Nov 19, 2026 (Gematsu / Engadget, Nov 2025). In a TBPN interview recorded that day, Zelnick talked about not declaring success too early.
- **Nov 10, 2026:** Zelnick told CNBC that demand was "vastly exceeding expectations."
- **Nov 9, 2026 (fiscal Q2 2027 earnings):**
  - Net bookings were about $1.60B, down 18% and slightly below guidance.
  - Full-year FY27 bookings guidance of $8.0–8.2B was kept.
  - Guidance for Q3 (October–December) net bookings was $3.35–3.50B.
  - Zelnick said pre-orders were running "far in excess of our initial expectations" but declined to raise guidance before seeing actual sales.
  - There were reports that Zelnick said Take-Two would share more about performance after launch (Take-Two 8-K Q2 FY27; GamesBeat; Stocktwits; Fool, Nov 9–10, 2026).
- **Scheduled events before Dec 31:**
  - No Take-Two earnings report is scheduled. The next one is expected around early February 2027.
  - Possible but unconfirmed: investor conferences in December, such as UBS or Barclays tech conferences, where Zelnick has appeared in past years. Because of SEC fair-disclosure rules (Regulation FD), new numbers usually come out by press release or 8-K, not at conferences.
  - A first-week, Black Friday/Thanksgiving or holiday milestone release is possible but not announced.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES**
1. **A "first week" or "fastest to 50M" press release in late November or December.** The day-one release has already shown that Take-Two is willing to give unit counts mid-quarter for this title. If such a release includes a unit total, that total is likely to be at or above 50M given the math in Section 2B.
   - Depends on: Take-Two's marketing and investor-relations choices, and whether management wants to signal strength to support the stock. The shares reportedly fell about 3.7% on launch day.
2. **A holiday or year-end milestone release** (for example "50 million players" or units sold by Christmas). Note that a "players" figure is not "units sold-in" and could cause a resolution dispute.
3. **Zelnick giving a number in a December media interview or at a conference**, filed with a concurrent 8-K or press release.

**Paths to NO**
1. **Take-Two waits for earnings.** It reverts to its normal practice and saves unit updates for the early-February Q3 report. This is the base-rate behavior for GTA V and RDR2 after launch week.
2. **Revenue-only updates.** Further releases give dollar figures, not units.
3. **An update arrives but is below 50M.** For example, a figure given right after launch week shows 45–49M, and nothing further follows before Dec 31.
4. **Wording problems.** Phrases like "nearly 50M," or a "players" metric, may not meet a strict reading of the criteria.

**Background factors:**
- Q3 guidance of $3.35–3.50B looks conservative given the day-one figure. That makes an upbeat interim disclosure, or a guidance-related 8-K, somewhat more likely. Take-Two could also hold everything for February.

## 5. Signposts

- **Strongest YES signal:** a Take-Two or Rockstar release in roughly Nov 23–30 giving first-weekend or first-week units. A figure at or above 50M resolves YES. A figure of 40–49M combined with stated intent to update again would also be a strong positive.
- **Moderate YES signal:** management comments promising more updates soon, or an 8-K or guidance update in December.
- **Moderate NO signal:** a first-week release that gives only dollar figures. This would suggest unit counts are being saved for earnings.
- **Strong NO signal:** no release at all by mid-December. After the launch window passes, the odds of an unprompted mid-quarter unit disclosure fall sharply, though a year-end announcement remains possible.
- **Watch:** Rockstar Newswire, Take-Two's investor-relations press releases, SEC 8-K filings, and Zelnick's interviews on CNBC, Bloomberg and similar outlets in December.

## 6. Uncertainties

- **Exact 8-K wording.** I could not read the 8-K myself. It is unclear whether it says "sold-in," "sold," or "sold through," and whether the 32.5M figure includes all physical shipments.
- **Pre-orders within the $3.79B.** Whether the dollar total includes pre-order revenue determines whether day-one units were about 40–47M or much higher.
- **Disclosure plans.** I could not confirm whether Zelnick actually said, or committed to, providing "more information after launch," or on what schedule.
- **December events.** I could not confirm any December investor-conference appearances, or the exact date of the Q3 FY27 earnings report (expected early February 2027).
- **How the resolver treats non-standard wording**, such as "units sold" versus "sold-in," or "players" versus units.
- **Leftover search content.** One search summary contained prediction-market prices. I deliberately left them out of this brief.
