## Research brief: Will Avengers: Doomsday's Tomatometer be above 50 the Monday after wide release?

### 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Avengers: Doomsday's Rotten Tomatoes critics score (Tomatometer) must be **strictly above 50** at 10:00 AM ET on the Monday after wide release. A score of exactly 50% resolves NO. The audience score (Popcornmeter) does not count.
- **Timing:** US wide release is **Friday, December 18, 2026**, so the check happens **Monday, December 21, 2026, 10:00 AM ET**. That matches the market close (2026-12-21 15:00 UTC). The international rollout starts December 16 (Wikipedia; Murphy's Multiverse, Nov 2026).
- **Who decides:** Kalshi settles the market from the verified Tomatometer shown on Rotten Tomatoes (Action Network/Kalshi coverage of earlier RT markets, 2026).
- **Edge cases:**
  - If the release date moved, "Monday after wide release" would move with it. One September 2026 Inside the Magic headline claimed Marvel "pulled" the release date, but every current source still gives December 18.
  - The score moves as reviews arrive. By the Monday there will likely be 300+ reviews, and big films usually change only 1–4 points over that stretch.
  - Rotten Tomatoes shows a rounded whole-number percentage. A displayed "50%" would resolve NO.

### 2. Reference classes and base rates
(MCU scores below are the commonly cited Tomatometer values. I could not open RT's own ranking page because the proxy refused it, so these come from secondary reporting and my own knowledge. Treat them as ±1–2 points.)

**A. All MCU theatrical films, 2008–2026: about 34 of 37 scored above 50% (~92%).**
- The only three under 50 are Eternals (47%, 2021), Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania (46%, 2023) and Captain America: Brave New World (48%, 2025).
- The next-lowest are The Marvels (62%) and Thor: Love and Thunder (~63%).

**B. Post-Endgame MCU films, 2019–2026: about 13 of 16 above 50 (~81%).**
- Below 50: Eternals, Quantumania, Brave New World.
- Most recent results:
  - Spider-Man: Brand New Day (Jul 2026): debuted at 92%, settled around 89–90% with 378 reviews (GamesRadar; CBM, Jul–Aug 2026).
  - Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025): 86–87%.
  - Thunderbolts* (2025): 88% (CBM; Pinkvilla, 2025).
  - Brave New World (Feb 2025): 48–49% (GamesRadar, 2025).
- Of the last five MCU films, four were well above 50 and one was just below.

**C. Avengers ensemble films and Russo-directed MCU films: 6 of 6 above 75%.**
- Avengers films: The Avengers ~91%, Age of Ultron ~76%, Infinity War ~85%, Endgame ~94%. All four Avengers films are 4/4 above 75.
- Russo MCU films: Winter Soldier ~90%, Civil War ~90%, Infinity War ~85%, Endgame ~94%. All four are 85% or higher.
- **Counterweight:** the Russos' non-Marvel films all scored under 50:
  - Cherry: 37% (2021)
  - The Gray Man: 45% (2022)
  - The Electric State: 14–23% (2025), their worst score (CBM; GamesRadar, Mar 2025)
- **Other comparison:** big superhero crossover "event" films from other studios did poorly (Batman v Superman ~29%, Justice League ~39%). This shows that event-scale ensembles can fail with critics.

**How the score drifts.** Borderline MCU films tend to lose points between the embargo lift and the Monday after opening. Brave New World opened near 50–51% and fell to 48–49% (IMDb/CBM headline: "Rotten Tomatoes Score Drops Below 50%," Feb 2025). Quantumania and Eternals opened higher and fell into the 40s. Strong films move only a few points (Brand New Day went 92 → ~90). The 50-point line is only really at risk if the debut score is below about 60.

  - **Review embargo:** not announced. One outlet speculates Dec 8–15 (9to5Marvel).
  - **World premiere:** reportedly **Dec 14, Los Angeles** (Marvel Updates on X, citing an LA events site). Social-media first reactions usually follow the premiere.
  - **Release:** international from Dec 16, US Dec 18. **Resolution:** Dec 21, 10 AM ET.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (score above 50):**
- The Russos/Avengers track record holds (6 of 6 MCU films ≥76%).
- The MCU's recent quality rebound continues (Thunderbolts*, First Steps and Brand New Day were all 86–92%).
- Critics reward the event spectacle and Downey's Doom.
- Positive test-screening chatter reflects real quality.
- Even a "mixed but fun" reception (65–80%) clears the line comfortably.

**Paths to NO (50 or below):**
- An overstuffed multiverse/X-Men crossover that serves mainly as setup for Secret Wars, the "Part 1" problem.
- Visible scars from the reshoots and heavy cuts.
- A very dark, violent tone that critics dislike.
- The Russos' weak post-Marvel record (all three recent films under 50%) suggests they may not still be at their peak.
- Franchise fatigue and Multiverse Saga skepticism: the three sub-50 MCU films all come from this saga, 2021–2025.
- For NO to happen, the debut score probably needs to be at or below about 58%, then slip over opening weekend.

### 5. Signposts
- **Embargo timing (strongest early signal):** an embargo lifting a week or more before release (around Dec 8–11) points to studio confidence and favors YES. A very late embargo (Dec 16–17, or on release day) would be a significant warning sign. Brave New World's embargo lifted only two days before release.
- **Premiere-night social reactions (around Dec 14):** these are almost always positive, so they carry little information. Muted or lukewarm wording would be a real negative.
- **Initial Tomatometer when the embargo lifts:** a debut at 70% or higher makes YES nearly certain. A debut at 55–62% puts the result in real doubt, given the usual downward drift. A debut under 55% strongly favors NO.
- **Metacritic score on embargo day:** a score of 60 or more lines up with a comfortable RT score. A score in the low 50s or below is a warning.
- **Late news:** a release-date change, reports of further recuts, or a final runtime that differs a lot from earlier reports.

### 6. Uncertainties
- There are no actual reviews yet. Every quality signal is either rumor (test screenings) or a proxy (presales, marketing).
- The embargo date and the premiere date are unconfirmed by Disney.
- The real extent of the reshoots and recuts, and whether the "starting from scratch" remarks reflect deep problems, could not be verified.
- I could not open Rotten Tomatoes or Wikipedia directly (proxy refused them), so the historical scores come from secondary reports and may be off by 1–2 points.
- I did not pin down the exact score-drift paths of Eternals and Quantumania from embargo to opening Monday. The direction (downward) is well documented, but the size is approximate.
