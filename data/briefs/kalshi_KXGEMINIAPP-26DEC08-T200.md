# Research brief: will Carbon Arc's Gemini app downloads index for November 2026 be above 200?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What YES requires:** Carbon Arc's "Gemini app downloads" index for November 2026 must come in **above 200**. The index is year-over-year, with 100 equal to November 2025. A value above 200 means November 2026 downloads must be **more than double** November 2025's, which is over 100% growth. A value of exactly 200 resolves NO.
- **Who decides and when:** Carbon Arc's finalized value as recorded at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 7, 2026. The market closes December 8, 04:59 UTC.
- **Ambiguities:**
  - **Method unknown.** I could not find Carbon Arc's methodology. It is unclear whether the index covers the world or just the US, iOS plus Google Play or only one store, and whether re-downloads count. Kalshi describes Carbon Arc only as "the neutral data layer" for these KPI contracts (Kalshi news, "Announcing sector KPI markets with Carbon Arc," undated). Kalshi pages were blocked when I tried to fetch them, so I couldn't see past index values.
  - **Revisions.** It is unclear how "finalized" values are handled if Carbon Arc revises its panel after the record time.
  - **Pre-installs.** If Gemini comes pre-installed or is pushed onto Android devices (for example as a Google Assistant replacement), those installs may not count as "downloads," depending on how Carbon Arc measures them.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Gemini's own monthly download path (Appfigures estimates, iOS plus Google Play, worldwide):**

   | Month | Downloads |
   |---|---|
   | Sep 2025 | ~40M (peak; Nano Banana launch) |
   | Oct 2025 | 34.3M |
   | **Nov 2025 (the comparison month)** | **~31M** |
   | Dec 2025 | 35.7M |
   | Feb 2026 | 27M |
   | Apr 2026 | 32M |
   | Aug 2026 | ~26.6M (secondary source, unverified) |

   Sources: Appfigures Oct 2025 report, Nov 21 2025; Appfigures Nov 2025 report, Dec 19 2025; Appfigures Feb/Apr 2026 reports; getpanto/usecarly aggregators.

   - In 2026, no month I found was even close to 2× its value a year earlier. Year-over-year changes in the months I could check ran from about −35% to +15%, a rough estimate on my part.
   - Hitting 200 in November 2026 would take about 62M or more downloads on the Appfigures scale. That is roughly 1.5× Gemini's all-time peak month.
2. **Big AI apps doubling year-over-year after they reach scale:** In November 2025, Sensor Tower had Gemini downloads up 190% YoY and Perplexity up 215%, while ChatGPT was up 85% and the cohort averaged 110% (TechCrunch, Dec 5 2025).
   - Growth above 100% has happened, but only from a low base: Gemini in 2024 was small, before Nano Banana.
   - Once an app is doing 30M or more downloads a month, doubling again the next year is rare. ChatGPT, at a similar scale, managed 85%.
   - My judgment: for an app that is already a top-5 download worldwide, doubling against a month that was itself a launch spike is a low-single-digit-percent event.
3. **The comparison month is unusually high:** November 2025 included the Gemini 3 and Nano Banana Pro launches (around Nov 18, 2025), and Appfigures had Gemini as the #1 iOS app worldwide that month (Appfigures, Dec 19 2025). That makes 200 even harder to reach.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)
- **Nov 21, 2026:** A Gemini app update brings "Gemini 4" features and PDF handling. A secondary blog reports a rollout to Android Go and other lower-memory phones with at least 2GB of RAM, around Nov 18. This could add downloads in emerging markets, but I found no evidence of scale (AndroidSage, Nov 21 2026; Releasebot, Nov 2026).
- **Nov 18, 2026:** The Google Play listing shows more than 1B cumulative installs and 52.4M reviews (Google Play).
- **Nov 12, 2026:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen rolled out to all users, including free users through automatic model selection. The launch post had about 6.4M views. I found no report of an App Store chart spike (Gemini app on X; Chrome Unboxed).
- **Nov 11, 2026:** Gemini started rolling out on Apple CarPlay (MacRumors, Nov 11 2026). Some secondary sources say Tasks became free in the US, India, Canada, Australia, Japan and Korea.
- **Nov 10, 2026:** Gemini 4 Carbon is reportedly rolling out to AI Ultra subscribers only (secondary sources).
- **Oct 28, 2026:** Alphabet said the Gemini app has passed 1B monthly active users, up from 650M a year earlier (ppc.land). That is about 50% user growth, not download doubling. Google had already said "more than 1 billion" on Aug 11, 2026.
- **Oct 13–14, 2026:** Gemini 4 Argon went generally available for Pro and Ultra subscribers.
- **Sep 2026:** Statista had ChatGPT as the top download worldwide (Statista, Oct 6 2026). I found no Gemini figures for September or October 2026.
- **Nothing found for November 2026:** no report of Gemini at #1 on the App Store or of a Nano Banana-style viral spike.
- **Remaining:** November has 8 days left, and Thanksgiving and Black Friday device sales could add a little seasonal lift.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **YES (very unlikely):**
  - A huge viral feature in the last week of November, on the order of Nano Banana, would be needed. Even that would struggle to double a month that already had a launch spike.
  - Alternatively, Carbon Arc's method could count something that inflates November 2026, such as the Android Go rollout, forced installs or Assistant-replacement installs, or a change in its panel.
  - A third possibility is a very low November 2025 value in Carbon Arc's own panel, if its data differs sharply from Appfigures and Sensor Tower.
- **NO (dominant):**
  - Downloads stayed roughly flat at about 25–35M a month through 2026 while the comparison month was high.
  - The Gemini 4 launches mainly targeted paid tiers or existing users.
  - The likely index range is roughly 70–130. The 200 threshold is far out in the tail.

## 5. Signposts
- **Toward YES:**
  - Reports of Gemini at #1 worldwide on the App Store or Google Play for several days in late November 2026.
  - Sensor Tower or Appfigures reporting more than 50M Gemini downloads for November.
  - Carbon Arc's index for September or October 2026 already above about 150. I could not find these values; they would be the single most useful data point.
- **Toward NO:**
  - Carbon Arc's October 2026 value near 100 or below.
  - Appfigures' November 2026 report (due mid-December, after resolution) showing Gemini around 30M.
  - ChatGPT staying #1 through November.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I could not find Carbon Arc's methodology: geography, stores covered, or whether it is panel-based.
- I could not find any past values of Carbon Arc's Gemini index; the Kalshi and Public pages could not be fetched.
- I found no download estimates from any provider for September, October or November 2026.
- Carbon Arc's November 2025 comparison value may differ from Appfigures' 31M.
- The scale of the Android Go rollout, and whether those installs count as downloads, is unknown.
