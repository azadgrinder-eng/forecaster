# Research Brief: Will Predict.fun launch a token by November 15, 2026?

*Research note: every page fetch was refused by the network proxy. This brief therefore rests on search-result summaries of the cited articles, not on full-text reads. Several outlets (CoinGabbar, TechBullion, OpenPR) are aggregators or paid placements, so I weighted primary posts from @predictdotfun, @binance and @BinanceHelpDesk, plus PANews, TechFlow and CNBC/CNN, more heavily.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Predict.fun officially launches a governance token, which will be PREDICT, by 11:59 PM ET on Sunday, November 15, 2026. That is 04:59 UTC on November 16. The token must be "actively and publicly transferable and tradable." Announcements alone do not count.
- **Who decides:** Information from predict.fun is the primary source. A consensus of credible reporting is also used.
- **Time left:** About 6.5 days from November 9, a Monday. November 14–15 is a weekend.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Pre-market IOU trading.** Aspecta runs a "PREDICTFUN" pre-market (CoinGabbar, ~Oct 31). This is almost certainly not a launched, transferable token, so it should not count.
  - **Token exists but no trading.** If the token contract is deployed, or Prime Sale or airdrop tokens sit in wallets while Binance trading is still postponed, the case is ambiguous. The "tradable" requirement most likely means NO until there is real trading on a DEX or an exchange.
  - **Launch without Binance.** If Predict.fun launched on a DEX such as PancakeSwap or on another exchange, that would probably count. There is no sign this is planned, though. The team explicitly tied the TGE to Binance's schedule.
  - **"Governance token" wording.** PREDICT is the project's native token. I don't expect a dispute over whether it qualifies as a "governance" token, but I could not confirm its official utility description.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **How fast exchanges recover after a hack.**
   - Binance's May 2019 hack ($40M BTC): trading continued, and withdrawals were suspended for about one week (TechCrunch, May 15, 2019).
   - The current Binance hack (Oct 29, 2026, ~$6.3B): withdrawals began a phased restart on about Oct 31–Nov 1. That restart was still incomplete on Nov 8 (crypto.news, ~Nov 8). Binance targets more assets "by end of next week," meaning about Nov 13–14 (TokenPost, ~Nov 6).
   - Recovery from this hack is running in weeks, not days. Alpha trading had been paused for 11 days as of Nov 9, with no restart date.
   - My rough prior from hacks at KuCoin (2020), Bybit (2025) and Upbit (2025), based on background knowledge rather than this research: full restoration of non-core services usually takes 2–6 weeks.
2. **Token launches postponed "until further notice."** From background knowledge, not systematically measured here: when a project delays a TGE because of outside conditions and gives no new date, the relaunch usually comes 2–8 weeks later. Relaunches within about 2.5 weeks of the original date are a minority, perhaps 20–35%.
   - The PREDICT delay started Oct 30. A launch by Nov 15 would mean a delay of no more than 16 days.
3. **How far ahead Binance announces listings.** Spot listings and HODLer airdrops are usually announced 1–2 days before trading. Alpha listings can be announced the same day or the day before.
   - So the lack of a date on Nov 9 does not rule out Nov 15.
   - In practice, a re-announcement would need to come by about Nov 13–15.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES**
1. Binance restores Alpha and Spot listing activity this week and quickly reschedules PREDICT for Nov 10–15. PREDICT is the only listing stuck in the queue and the Prime Sale has already happened, so Binance has a reason to clear it first.
   - This depends on Binance declaring the security matter "fully addressed."
   - Restarting new-token flows such as Alpha pools and Wallet distributions is riskier than reopening withdrawals.
2. Predict.fun decouples from Binance and launches on-chain on PancakeSwap or another venue, or with another exchange. This is unlikely. The tokenomics, Prime Sale and airdrop are all built around Binance Wallet. The project is in the Binance/YZi orbit and said it would wait for Binance confirmation.

**Paths to NO**
1. Binance's phased recovery is still underway, with withdrawals for several assets targeted only for about Nov 13–14. Alpha stays paused through Nov 15, or listings resume but PREDICT is scheduled after Nov 15. This is the default path.
2. Binance does no new listings for an extended period, for example because of regulatory scrutiny after the hack, and PREDICT slips into late November or December. Coverage speculates about "late November or December," but that is not official (CoinGabbar, ~Nov 1).
3. Predict.fun chooses to wait for better market conditions after the post-hack sell-off.

## 5. Signposts

- **Strongly toward YES:**
  - Binance or @BinanceHelpDesk announces that Alpha trading has resumed.
  - Binance or Binance Wallet posts a new PREDICT trading time.
  - Predict.fun posts a revised claim or TGE time on or before Nov 15. Given the 1–2 day lead times, this would need to appear by Nov 13–14.
  - Any other new Binance spot or Alpha listing, which would show the listing pipeline has reopened.
- **Moderately toward YES:** Binance finishes restoring the remaining withdrawal assets (INJ, NEAR, LTC, PLASMA) early in the week.
- **Toward NO:**
  - By Nov 12–13, Binance still says Alpha is paused, or no listing announcement has appeared.
  - Binance recovery updates say more security reviews are coming.
  - Predict.fun posts Season 2 updates that don't mention a TGE date.
  - Regulators take action against Binance.
- **Wildcard:** Predict.fun announces a launch without Binance or with another exchange. That would sharply raise YES only if trading actually starts by Nov 15.

## 6. Uncertainties

- I could not confirm whether Alpha trading resumed on Nov 8 or 9. The last confirmation I found that it was still paused was from about Nov 6–7.
- I could not tell whether the PREDICT contract has already been deployed or whether Prime Sale tokens are sitting in wallets. That affects how "transferable" could be interpreted in an edge case.
- Binance's internal criteria and timeline for resuming new listings after the hack are unknown.
- I could not confirm whether Predict.fun has any contractual or strategic room to launch without Binance.
- The base rates for hack recovery and postponed TGEs are partly from background knowledge, not systematically counted here.
- All page fetches failed, so details come from search summaries. Some secondary figures, such as the exact allocation percentages, conflict across sources.
