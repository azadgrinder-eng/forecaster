# Research Brief: Will Sebastian "CBass" Diamond be confirmed as CEO of FaZe Media, Inc. before Jan 1, 2027?

*Prepared 2026-11-28. About 34 days remain before the close.*

**Method note:** Every attempt to open a full article was refused by the network proxy, so everything below comes from search-result summaries, not full articles. Treat details, especially dates, as medium-confidence.

---

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Sebastian Diamond (known as "CBass" or "Cbass," an early FaZe Clan member) must be *confirmed* as CEO of **FaZe Media, Inc.** before **Jan 1, 2027**. The close is 2027-01-01 15:00 UTC.
- **Interim or acting appointments do not count.** A title like "co-CEO" or "head of FaZe" is a gray area, and so is a CEO role at a related company rather than FaZe Media, Inc. itself, such as FaZe Esports (owned by GameSquare) or HardScope (Kalish's company).
- **Who decides:** The market operator, presumably based on an official announcement or credible reporting. I could not open the operator's full rules page, so I don't know which sources it accepts or how it would treat a title like "creative director" versus "CEO."
- **Edge cases:**
  - Corporate structure is murky. FaZe Media, Inc. is the creator and media company. GameSquare's esports arm is separate. Since December 2025, Matt Kalish's HardScope has been described as the "parent" of FaZe. A CEO appointment at HardScope, or at a newly branded entity, might not count as FaZe Media, Inc.
  - It is unclear whether FaZe Media currently has a confirmed CEO at all. The seat was reported empty after Banks left in July 2025. Kalish is described as CEO of HardScope and "new management" of FaZe.
  - The market appears to list many named candidates; this brief covers only Diamond.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **A departed former employee with no current role is named CEO within about one month.** I found no case in the FaZe ownership era of a former, non-executive creator or staffer being installed as CEO. FaZe's CEOs and acting CEOs since 2022 were:
   - Lee Trink, until September 2023
   - Christoph Pachler (CFO/COO), interim, 2023
   - Richard "Banks" Bengtson, as an owner-operator, late 2023 to July 2025
   - since then, effectively Kalish, the controlling owner

   That is about 4 leaders, all of them insiders, owners, or executives, and none a departed rank-and-file member. For a person with no visible connection to the company, the base rate of being named CEO in any given month is well under 1%.
2. **A leaderless or shrinking company fills its CEO seat in a short window.** The seat has reportedly been vacant or informally held since July 2025, about 16 months. If nothing happened in 16 months, the chance of *any* named CEO announcement in a given ~5-week window is modest, roughly 5–10% at a naive monthly hazard. Diamond would have to be the pick out of many possible people.
3. **Founder or OG "returns" to lead a creator brand.** There are examples where it did happen: Banks took over FaZe in 2023 when GameSquare bought it, and founder returns happen at other creator brands. But those returnees were co-owners or very prominent founders. Diamond was the "first employee" and "longest-tenured employee," not an owner, as far as I can find.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 9–12, 2026:** FaZe posted a cryptic "11.11.26" teaser on X. Its first payoff was a $100,000 Call of Duty event, the "Flank Invitational," on Nov 11–12, streamed on twitch.tv/faze and tied to ZooMaa's show *The Flank*. FaZe has not said whether more announcements are coming. Banks says he has no part in it. Reporting says FaZe hasn't disclosed who runs the brand now, and no ownership breakdown has been published since Banks left (FaZe Clan's 11.11.26 Teaser…, TalkEsport, Nov 2026; FaZe Clan Teases 11.11.26 Return…, Shane the Gamer, Nov 2026). **I found nothing linking Diamond to this relaunch.**
- **Sept 2026:** GameSquare issued about 30% of itself for FanEngine Web3 assets. That deal is unrelated to FaZe Media. Reporting restates that GameSquare fully exited FaZe Media in 2025 (EsportsAdvocate, Sept 2026).
- **Mar 31, 2026:** Kalish left his job as President of DraftKings North America (DraftKings DEF 14A proxy, 2026). That frees up his time for HardScope and FaZe. He reportedly stays on the DraftKings board.
- **Apr–Jun 2026:** The esports side carried on (karrigan left in April 2026, Twistzz became captain). This doesn't bear on FaZe Media leadership.
- **Jan 2026:** An ownership summary posted on X by Michael Decker (a social post, not a filing) says FaZe Media is about 49% Kalish, 25.5% Gigamoon Media (Kalish), and 25.5% Banks, with 0% GameSquare. A March 2025 deal summary instead describes the final 25.5% as bought by "Gigamoon Media and FaZe's founders."
- **Dec 25–29, 2025:** Six roster creators left after failed contract talks with Kalish and HardScope: Adapt, Jason, Ronaldo, Lacy, Rage, and Silky. Apex and Rug also left. Kalish called the old financial structure "unsustainable." FaZe said it would focus on esports. Its content house and creator division were reported shut down (TechCrunch, Dec 27, 2025; Tubefilter, Dec 27, 2025; Dust2.us; EsportsAdvocate "FaZe Media experiences hard reset," Dec 2025).
- **Dec 2025:** Kalish launched HardScope as its CEO. It is described as "built on the infrastructure behind the relaunch of FaZe Clan," and creators hold equity in the IP they help build. A PR Newswire release says FaZe was "relaunched under new management including … Matt Kalish as CEO" (PR Newswire, Dec 2025; Tubefilter, Dec 16, 2025; Variety, 2026).
- **Jul 28–29, 2025:** Banks stepped down as CEO of FaZe after the MLG meme-coin collapse and a public feud with Adin Ross. No successor was named at the time (Yahoo/Gaming Amigos, Jul 2025).
- **Apr 1, 2025:** GameSquare sold its last 25.5% of FaZe Media to Gigamoon Media (Kalish) and "FaZe's founders" for $10M, which valued the company at $39.2M (Esports Insider, Apr 2025; MarketScreener).
- **May–Jun 2024:** FaZe Media was formed with Banks as CEO, Temperrr as President, and Apex as COO. Kalish invested $11M for 49%, and Banks bought 25.5% for $9.5M (GamesBeat; Nasdaq press release, Jun 18, 2024).
- **Diamond's status:** On Dec 4, 2023, he announced he was leaving FaZe after about 12 years, following a public contract dispute. He apologized for his negative comments and said both sides had "come to an agreement to part ways" (Cbass on X, Dec 2023; Dexerto). His X bio reads "Former OG @FaZeClan." **I found no 2024–2026 reporting of any role for him at FaZe Media, HardScope, or the November 2026 relaunch.** One low-quality page claims a "Christmas 2025" exit for a group that includes him, which conflicts with the 2023 departure and looks unreliable.

**Scheduled events before close:** none known. Watch for follow-ups to the "11.11.26" teaser and any HardScope or FaZe year-end announcements.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (all low-likelihood):**
- **OG relaunch:** The November 2026 relaunch turns out to be an OG-led reboot, with Diamond installed as the public face and given the formal title "CEO of FaZe Media." This would need Kalish, who controls a majority, to agree, plus an announcement within about 5 weeks.
- **Change of control:** FaZe Media is sold or recapitalized and goes to a group that includes Diamond, who becomes CEO. I found no reporting of a sale.
- **Title mismatch:** A titled appointment happens but is worded as "CEO" while technically sitting at HardScope or a new entity. That creates resolution ambiguity, possibly in YES's favor.

**Paths to NO (dominant):**
- The status quo holds: Kalish runs things through HardScope, and no new CEO is named before Jan 1.
- Someone else is named, such as Kalish formally, another HardScope executive, or a different OG like Temperrr, ZooMaa, or Apex.
- Diamond takes a non-CEO role, such as a creator, advisor, or creative lead.
- Any appointment is labeled interim or acting.

**Key dependency:** Kalish's preferences. He holds about 74.5% and appears to favor a professional, creator-equity model rather than handing the CEO title to an outside creator. The only other owner, Banks, has publicly distanced himself.

## 5. Signposts

- **Toward YES (large moves):**
  - Diamond publicly rejoins FaZe or appears in relaunch content.
  - FaZe or HardScope announces a leadership reveal.
  - Reports surface of Diamond taking an equity stake.
  - A FaZe teaser hints at "the OG" or "the first employee."
- **Toward YES (moderate):**
  - Reporting that FaZe Media, Inc. has been sold or recapitalized.
  - Kalish stepping back.
- **Toward NO:**
  - Kalish or another executive confirmed as FaZe Media CEO.
  - The "11.11.26" campaign ending with no leadership news.
  - Diamond announcing unrelated ventures.
  - Silence through mid-December, which leaves little time.

## 6. Uncertainties

- **Why Diamond is a listed candidate.** I could not find any recent news connecting him to FaZe leadership. He may be on the list simply as a well-known OG, or there may be a rumor (for example, on X or TikTok) that search didn't surface.
- **Who formally holds the CEO title at FaZe Media, Inc. now.** It might be Kalish, it might be vacant, or the company might be largely dormant after its creator division shut down.
- **Exact ownership.** It's unclear what "FaZe's founders" means in the April 2025 deal, which founders hold equity, and whether Diamond is among them (probably not).
- **Whether the November 2026 relaunch has more phases.** And who is behind it.
- **Full resolution rules.** I couldn't read the operator's rules, including which sources it accepts and how it treats a CEO role at HardScope or a successor company.
- **Source quality.** All findings come from search summaries, and several sources are low-quality or conflict with each other.
