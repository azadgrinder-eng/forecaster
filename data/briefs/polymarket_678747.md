# Research brief: Will Israel strike 4 countries in 2026?

*Prepared 2026-11-21. Note on method: every page fetch failed at the proxy, so this brief relies on search-result snippets and headlines rather than full articles. Treat the details below as a strong lead, not a verified count.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts.** The market counts how many different countries had Israeli drone, missile, or air strikes land on their soil between Jan 1, 2026 00:00 ET and Dec 31, 2026 23:59 ET. Each strike must be officially acknowledged by Israel or attributed to Israel by "a consensus of credible reporting."
- **What doesn't count:**
  - Gaza, the West Bank, and any territory Israel controlled as of Dec 31, 2025. Strikes inside Israel's southern Syria buffer zone may not count, but strikes elsewhere in Syria do.
  - Intercepted munitions and surface-to-air fire.
  - Artillery, FPV or anti-tank missile strikes, naval shelling, ground raids, and cyberattacks.
- **Embassies** count toward the host country.
- **Key ambiguity 1: "exactly 4" or "4 or more"?** The title reads like one bucket in a multi-outcome "how many countries" market, which would mean exactly 4. Forecasters should check the market's format:
  - If it means exactly 4, a fifth qualifying country (for example Yemen) before Dec 31 turns it NO.
  - If it means at least 4, a fifth country doesn't matter.
- **Key ambiguity 2: joint strikes.** The rules don't say how to handle joint US-Israeli strikes where nobody claimed the specific strike, which is the case for Iraq. "Consensus of credible reporting" leaves the resolver a lot of discretion.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Countries Israel struck per calendar year, 2022–2025**, from my own background knowledge (not re-checked in this session):
   - 2022: about 2 (Syria; Lebanon rarely).
   - 2023: about 2–3 (Syria, Lebanon from October; possibly Yemen-related intercepts only).
   - 2024: 4 (Lebanon, Syria, Iran, Yemen).
   - 2025: 5–6 (Lebanon, Syria, Iran, Yemen, Qatar in September; Tunisia if you count the flotilla drone incidents, which US officials attributed to Israel).
   - So recent years cluster at 4 to 6 countries, but in 2026 the count is mostly settled already. The remaining question is mainly about attribution (Iraq) plus 40 days of possible new fronts.
2. **Chance a new country is added in the last ~6 weeks of a year.** Historically low. Most "new" targets in a year come with big escalations: Iran in April and October 2024, Qatar in September 2025, Yemen in July 2024. Roughly, Israel opened a new country front about 1–2 times a year in 2024–25, which works out to about 10–20% for any given 6-week window. That rises sharply if the Iran war resumes, since Houthi re-entry in March 2026 shows how a renewed war can pull in other fronts.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to a count of exactly 4:**
- **A. Iraq is counted** because of the joint US-Israeli framing, PMF and Iraqi government attribution, and the Shafaq and Asharq/JPost reporting, and no fifth country is added. I think this is the main path. It depends on how the resolver reads "consensus of credible reporting" when there is no Israeli claim and most reports say "US-Israeli."
- **B. Iraq is not counted**, but Israel strikes one new country by Dec 31. Yemen is most likely, if the Houthis fire at Israel during a renewed Iran war. Iraq could also be struck in a way Israel claims. Less likely: Turkish-linked targets beyond Syria, or a Hamas target abroad, as in Qatar in 2025.

**Paths to NO:**
- **C. Stays at 3.** Iraq isn't counted and nothing new happens. That is plausible if Trump holds the Iran resumption until January and the caretaker-government constraints bind.
- **D. Reaches 5 or more** (NO only if the market means exactly 4). Iraq is counted plus a new front, for example a December Iran war that brings in the Houthis and leads Israel to strike Yemen.

## 5. Signposts

- **Biggest mover: how Iraq is treated.**
  - Watch for any IDF or Israeli-official acknowledgment of an Iraq strike, or a major wire service (Reuters/AP) saying "Israeli strike in Iraq" without hedging. Either strongly favors counting Iraq.
  - Check whether a companion "Will Israel strike Iraq in 2026?" market from the same platform has already resolved or been clarified. That would be decisive evidence of how the resolver reads the rules (I did not look up prices).
- **Iran war resuming before Dec 31.** This raises the odds of new fronts (Yemen, a claimed Iraq strike). Signposts: Trump dropping his "wait until January" line, US force deployments, Netanyahu getting cabinet or legal sign-off.
- **Houthis resuming missile or drone fire at Israel.** Israel retaliated against Yemen repeatedly in 2024–25. Note the reported IDF advice against striking Houthis in 2026, and that Saudi Arabia is already hitting them.
- **New Israeli strikes on Hamas or Iranian targets in third countries.**

## 6. Uncertainties

- **Market format:** whether it is "exactly 4" or "4 or more." This is critical.
- **Iraq:** whether any Israeli strike there was officially acknowledged, and how the resolver weighs "US-Israeli" joint attribution. Dates and details of the Shafaq and JPost Iraq reports could not be checked because the fetches failed.
- **Yemen:** I couldn't completely rule out an Israeli strike on Yemen in 2026. Some Sept-2026-stamped snippets about Sanaa strikes appeared to be republished 2025 stories.
- **Other countries:** I couldn't rule out lesser-known strikes elsewhere (Sudan, Somalia, Tunisia, Jordan). Nothing turned up.
- **Earlier Syria strikes:** whether Syria strikes in Jan–Mar 2026 fell inside the excluded buffer zone. This doesn't matter, because the Idlib strike clearly qualifies.
