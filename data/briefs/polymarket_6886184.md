**A. Serie A away wins in general.** Over the last decade the away side has won roughly 30–34% of Serie A matches, and about 25–27% have been draws. This is from general knowledge; I didn't verify it this session. It's too broad to use on its own.

**B. Title-contending away side at a mid-table home side.** A top-2 team visiting a team in roughly 8th–14th place typically wins about 50–58% of the time, with draws around 25%. This is an approximate heuristic from long-run league data, not something I measured here.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES**
- Inter's overall quality and form: about 2.5 points per game and among the best goal differences in the league.
- Atalanta are fragile. They lost 0-3 at home to Parma, their crowd is unhappy, and they've lost five league games.
- Inter's recent dominance in this fixture.
- Inter can score without their first-choice strikers: Pio Esposito has been scoring.
- Atalanta's defence is short of Hien and Kossounou.

**Paths to NO (draw or Atalanta win)**
- Inter's attack is depleted. Lautaro, Thuram and Bonny are all doubtful or out, and players returning from the international break often lack match rhythm.
- Inter could rotate with Stuttgart three days later, although that risk is modest.
- Bergamo is historically a hard ground for Inter.
- Atalanta can lift their level at home in a big match.
- About a 25% draw rate is the structural baseline.

## 5. Signposts

- **Thuram and Lautaro fitness, in reports around Nov 17–21:**
  - If both are fit to start, YES becomes more likely, by a few points.
  - If both are out, leaving Esposito plus a backup, it becomes less likely, by roughly 3–6 points.
- **Players returning from the international break:** Inter's internationals coming back with new knocks would lower YES. Atalanta's defenders Hien and Kossounou returning early would also lower it.
- **Sarri's job security:** A sacking or a new-coach bounce before the match adds variance, and probably lowers YES slightly.
- **Confirmed line-ups**, published about an hour before kickoff.
