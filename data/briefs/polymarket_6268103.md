# Research brief: Will Anthropic's IPO happen on November 20, 2026?

*Prepared 2026-11-12. Most news pages could not be opened directly because the proxy refused the connection. Anthropic's own pages loaded. Many details below come from search-result summaries of CNBC, Bloomberg, Fortune, TechCrunch and others, and are labelled that way.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** Anthropic's IPO date, in Eastern Time, must be **Friday, November 20, 2026**. The rules define an IPO as "the first public sale of shares… on a recognized stock exchange," and the main source is the exchange's listing page. So the date that matters is almost certainly the **first day ANT trades on Nasdaq**. It is not the roadshow launch (Nov 10) or the pricing date, when shares go to institutions off-exchange.
- **What counts as NO:** first trading on any other date, or no IPO by Nov 30, 2026, 11:59 PM ET. This question is one outcome of a multi-date market. The close date is Nov 21, 04:59 UTC, which is 11:59 PM ET on Nov 20.
- **Who decides:** the exchange listing page (Nasdaq, ticker "ANT"), or a consensus of credible reporting.
- **Edge cases:**
  - Mega-IPOs often open hours after the 9:30 AM bell (around noon to 1 PM ET). A late open on Nov 19 still counts as Nov 19.
  - Under a pricing-date reading, the planned pricing date is Nov 18, so that reading would also give NO.
  - The only clear path to YES is pricing on Thursday Nov 19 after the close, then trading Friday Nov 20.
  - A Nasdaq glitch that pushes the opening cross past midnight is essentially impossible.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **US mega-IPOs that publicly set a pricing and trading timetable at roadshow launch.** These are rough estimates from memory, not a systematic dataset. Large deals have usually traded on the day signalled at launch:
   - Facebook 2012, Alibaba 2014, Uber 2019, Rivian 2021, Arm 2023, Figma and Circle 2025: all on schedule, even when the range was raised (Circle, Figma).
   - SpaceX in June 2026 also kept its timetable exactly: roadshow ~June 4, priced June 11, traded June 12 (TechCrunch, 2026-06-11; Fortune, 2026-06-12).
   - Rare exceptions: one-day accelerations when books are very strong (e.g., Lyft 2019), and postponements of weeks after market shocks (e.g., Klarna and StubHub around the April 2025 tariff selloff).
   - **Rough estimate:** about 85–90% trade on the signalled day, about 5% move by one day either way, and about 5% slip by more or are postponed.
2. **One-day slips specifically, which is what YES needs.** Postponements of exactly one business day after launch are uncommon, roughly ≤5%. Delays tend to be either zero days or weeks.
3. **Anthropic's own timeline.** It has slipped before, by weeks:
   - Commentary said the prospectus moved from early September and the roadshow from mid-October, with an earlier plan to list before the Nov 3 midterms (search summary of vestedfinance/brokerchooser, Sept–Oct 2026).
   - The public S-1 came out Oct 26, about five months after the confidential draft was submitted on June 1 (Anthropic news, 2026-10-26; Leverage Shares).

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

- **NO, trades Nov 19 as planned (base case).** Multiple sources, the book is covered several times over, and the SpaceX precedent was kept to the day. This depends on calm markets and SEC effectiveness being declared on Nov 18.
- **YES, one-day slip to Fri Nov 20.** The WSJ's original "19 or 20" window shows Friday was considered. A slip could come from:
  - raising the range, which needs an amended filing and possibly more marketing time;
  - SEC effectiveness or logistics timing;
  - choosing to wait for a market event, such as Nvidia earnings or the AI selloff, before pricing;
  - pricing on Thursday because of a very large book (anchor allocations for about $64B or more).
- **NO, acceleration (Nov 18 or earlier).** Strong oversubscription can lead banks to close books early. Note that Nov 18 is the pricing date in current plans.
- **NO, longer delay (week of Nov 23, December, or later).** This would follow a sharp AI selloff, a regulatory or national-security shock, a government shutdown affecting the SEC, or a governance or safety controversy.

## 5. Signposts

- **Toward YES:**
  - An amended S-1 or FWP raising the range after about Nov 16.
  - Reports that pricing moved to "Thursday" (Nov 19) or trading to "Friday."
  - Nasdaq's IPO calendar listing ANT for Nov 20.
  - Hearing of a one-day delay "to accommodate demand" or because of market volatility.
- **Toward NO (trades Nov 19):**
  - The SEC acceleration request or effectiveness notice timed for Nov 18.
  - Reports of "pricing tonight" on Nov 18.
  - Brokers such as Schwab, Robinhood, SoFi and HL confirming Nov 19.
- **Toward NO (longer delay):**
  - A sharp Nasdaq or AI drawdown.
  - Reports that the IPO was "postponed" or that bankers are "monitoring markets."
  - A new government or national-security action against Anthropic.
- **Toward NO (acceleration):** reports that books are closing early or pricing has moved up to Nov 17.

## 6. Uncertainties

- Anthropic has not publicly confirmed the pricing or trading dates. Nov 18/19 rests on CNBC and other "people familiar" reporting.
- I could not open primary articles from the WSJ, CNBC, Bloomberg or HL. The HL "20 November" pricing text and the WSJ "19 or 20" wording are unconfirmed.
- I don't know whether bankers will actually raise the range, or whether doing so would change the timetable.
- The 2026 date of Nvidia's earnings, and any government-shutdown risk to SEC operations in mid-November, are unknown.
- The base rates in section 2 are estimates from memory, not a systematic count.
