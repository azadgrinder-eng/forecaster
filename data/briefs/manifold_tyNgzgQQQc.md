# Research Brief: Will "Avengers: Doomsday" beat $360,091,572 for its domestic opening weekend?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Box Office Mojo must report a domestic (US + Canada) opening-weekend gross for *Avengers: Doomsday* above **$360,091,572**. That is the Box Office Mojo figure for *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, which opened July 31 – Aug 2, 2026.
- **Timing:** The film opens Friday **Dec 18, 2026**, so the opening weekend is Dec 18–20. Studios usually issue Sunday estimates on Dec 20 and actuals on Monday Dec 21. The market closes Dec 26, 2026 01:16 UTC, so there is time for actuals to come in.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Estimate vs. actual.** The record itself moved after the first report. Sony's Sunday estimate for *Brand New Day* was about $355M, described as "second biggest." Monday actuals raised it to $360M, a new record (ABC News, ~Aug 3, 2026; Pajiba/JoBlo, ~Aug 2–3, 2026). A close Doomsday result could flip the same way between Sunday and Monday. Resolution should use the final Box Office Mojo number.
  - **Thursday previews and early-access shows** are folded into Friday and the weekend total. That is how *Brand New Day*'s $72M Wed/Thu previews were counted (Variety, late July 2026).
  - **Release-date changes.** A date change before Dec 18 is unlikely but has been speculated about. Earlier coverage floated moving to Dec 11. If the opening weekend falls after the close date, NO is the likely result.
  - **Conflicting record figures.** Some outlets say *Brand New Day* opened at $355M or even $365M. The question fixes the threshold at $360,091,572.
  - **Not inflation-adjusted.** *Endgame*'s $357.1M (Apr 2019) is about $467M in today's dollars, but that doesn't matter here (ABC News, Aug 2026).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. Record-breaking openings in general (nominal domestic record, 2002–2026).** The record changed hands 9 times in about 24 years:

| Film | Year | Opening |
|---|---|---|
| Spider-Man | 2002 | $114.8M |
| Pirates of the Caribbean 2 | 2006 | $135.6M |
| The Dark Knight | 2008 | $158.4M |
| The Avengers | 2012 | $207.4M |
| Jurassic World | 2015 | $208.8M |
| The Force Awakens | 2015 | $248.0M |
| Infinity War | 2018 | $257.7M |
| Endgame | 2019 | $357.1M |
| Brand New Day | 2026 | $360.1M |

- Two records have fallen within about 6 months of each other only once (2015).
- Avengers team-up films set the record 3 times out of 4. *Age of Ultron* (2015) was the exception at $191M.
- So "the next Avengers film breaks the record" is historically common. But the bar now is the highest it has ever been, and it was set only ~4.5 months earlier by a film with similar appeal.

**B. December openings (the most important seasonal class).**
- No December film has ever opened above **~$260M**:
  - *Spider-Man: No Way Home*: ~$260M, Dec 17, 2021. It opened on the same Friday-before-Christmas-week slot.
  - *Star Wars: The Force Awakens*: $247.97M, Dec 18, 2015. This is the same calendar date.
  - *Rogue One*: ~$155M.
  - *Avatar: Fire and Ash*: $88M, Dec 2025.
  
  (Statista/Benzinga December list; Boxoffice Pro on NWH; Rotten Tomatoes, Dec 2025.)
- The pre-Christmas weekend usually produces long runs but less front-loaded openings. Many schools are still in session on Friday Dec 18.
- To win, Doomsday must beat the December record by about **38%**. 0 of the roughly 3 mega-franchise December openings have come within 30% of $360M.

**C. Final opening vs. pre-release tracking for Avengers films.**
- *The Avengers* (2012): tracked ~$150–160M, opened $207M.
- *Infinity War*: tracked ~$200–225M, opened $257.7M.
- *Endgame*: early tracking was $200–250M (Variety, 2019), it opened $357.1M (+40% vs. the top of that early range).
- *Age of Ultron*: tracked above $200M, opened $191M.
- That is 3 of 4 beating tracking, by 20–40%. Doomsday's most-cited professional range is $260–300M. It would need **+20% over the top of that range** to win. That is within the historical Avengers overperformance band, but it is not the median outcome.

  - Its domestic opening projection is **$260–300M**, revised down slightly from $270–300M (ComicBasics; search summaries, ~Oct 29 – Nov 2026).
  - ComicBasics reports trackers saying presales are "closing in on Brand New Day," though they count differently.
  - The low end of analyst forecasts is $230–260M domestic.
- **Oct 31, 2026:** Worldwide presales reportedly topped **$125M**. Variety India treats this as an estimate (Dark Horizons, Oct 31, 2026).
- **Oct 6, 2026:** Worldwide presales passed $100M. *Brand New Day* reportedly reached that mark only about 40 days before release.
- **Sept 2026:**
  - Disney's CFO told an investor conference that **domestic presales topped $50M**, with **over 70% from Infinity Vision** premium screens (Deadline, Sept 2026; Pirates & Princesses).
  - *Endgame* re-released in Infinity Vision on Sept 25.
- **Aug–Sept 2026 tracker comparisons:**
  - TheWrap reported Doomsday's first two days of wide presales ran **about 65% ahead of Brand New Day's** first two days.
  - The comparison has problems. Brand New Day opened sales only ~45 days before release; Doomsday's general sale opened about 4 months early (Aug 14).
  - Other tracker snapshots: Friday sales 71 days out roughly matched where Brand New Day stood 12 days out. At 83 days out, Doomsday passed Brand New Day's pace at 21 days out. A Bulgarian tracker had them level at the same point. (ComicShopLocator, Aug 18; Kursiv, Aug 19; ComicBasics, Aug–Oct.)
  - Brand New Day's final presale total is not consistently reported. One figure is ~$150M by the day before opening, which is unverified.
- **Forecasts from fans and podcasts:**
  - AnthonyJPHer projects $120M previews, a $205M opening day and a **$425–450M** weekend.
  - The Boxoffice Pro podcast says $400M is "by no means a guarantee, but definitely in play."
  - Murphy's Multiverse calls $300M+ a "lock" and says the record is possible. It also says the $400M talk comes from fans projecting presales growth in a straight line, not from trades or standard trackers.
- **July 20, 2026:**
  - The first trailer was released and premium (Infinity Vision) tickets went on sale.
  - Runtime is reported at about **165 min**, the second-longest Avengers film. *Endgame* was 181 min and still set the record.
  - Variety reported $16.5M in first-day ticket sales, with premium Dec 17/18 shows largely sold out.
- **Brand New Day benchmark (released July 31, 2026):**
  - $72M Wed/Thu previews (a record; *Endgame* had $60M).
  - $168M opening day.
  - $360.09M weekend; $932M worldwide.
  - **It also opened without US IMAX**, because *The Odyssey* held those screens. So the "no IMAX" handicap did not stop the record (Variety; Geeks & Gamers; ABC News, late July – Aug 2026).
- **Competition:**
  - ***Dune: Part Three* opens the same day, Dec 18**, with a reported 3-week IMAX exclusive. Some IMAX shows are already sold out (Tribune/Geo/JoBlo, Mar–Jun 2026).
  - Doomsday relies on Infinity Vision. This is a certification of existing large premium screens (Cinemark XD, Regal RX, AMC Prime), not new equipment. One unverified figure says about 75 US screens at launch (Axios, July 20, 2026; TheWrap).
  - Doomsday will play on **more than 4,000** North American screens (Variety).
  - China opens Dec 16, which is irrelevant to the domestic figure.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES:**
1. **Presales convert like Endgame's did.** The fan base is primed: Avengers plus X-Men plus RDJ's Doom, with presales running ahead of Brand New Day on several metrics. Walk-up business in the final week is strong, and previews exceed $90–100M.
2. **Higher ticket prices.** Premium-heavy sales (70% from premium screens) and 2026 ticket prices push average prices well above 2019's.
3. **Excellent reviews and social reaction** (embargo likely early December) widen the audience beyond fans. A Thursday-preview record above $72M would be strong evidence.
4. **Sunday/Monday rounding.** If the result is close, studio actuals can add 1–2%, as happened with Brand New Day.

**Paths to NO:**
1. **Seasonality.** The pre-Christmas weekend has historically capped openings at about $260M. Schools are in session Friday, holiday shopping and travel compete, and December films earn more across the holiday weeks than on opening weekend.
2. **Head-to-head with Dune: Part Three.** It takes IMAX and some large-format screens, showtimes and adult moviegoers. A "Barbenheimer"-style shared weekend lifts the overall total but could split demand.
3. **Presales spread out over time.** Long sale windows make the presales total look big without adding demand. Professional tracking ($260–300M) sits clearly below the bar, and the latest professional revision was downward.
4. **Fewer screenings per day** because of the 165-minute runtime.
5. **Fatigue.** Doomsday is the second giant Marvel/Sony event in 5 months, and post-2019 MCU results have been mixed.
6. **Bad word of mouth** or spoilers or leaks (Brand New Day leaked on X but still broke the record).

## 5. Signposts (direction of effect)
- **Final trade tracking** (Deadline / Boxoffice Pro / NRG, usually 3–4 weeks out, roughly Nov 19 – Dec 1):
  - Above $330M → strongly up.
  - $260–300M confirmed → down.
- **Disney's Nov 17 earnings call:** a domestic presales number above about $100M would point up. Watch for Thursday-preview comparisons with Endgame.
- **Tracker comparisons inside the last 2 weeks** (like for like, same days before release) against Brand New Day and Endgame. These matter most. Comparisons made months out are weak evidence.
- **Social and review embargo (early December):** Rotten Tomatoes above 90% or ecstatic reactions → up; mixed → down.
- **Dune: Part Three presales and tracking.** If Dune tracks above $100M, that hints at heavier competition for adult and large-format audiences.
- **Thursday previews (Dec 17) and Friday estimate (Dec 18)**, the most decisive signals:
  - Brand New Day: $72M previews / $168M Friday → $360M weekend.
  - A December film likely needs a **higher** Friday-to-weekend multiplier: The Force Awakens was about 2.06×, and No Way Home about 2.14×.
  - So Doomsday probably needs a Friday (previews included) of roughly **$165–175M+**.
- **Any release-date change** (e.g. to Dec 11 to get IMAX) would remove Dune head-to-head pressure → up.

## 6. Uncertainties
- **Data quality.** I could not open the primary trade articles (Deadline, Dark Horizons and Murphy's Multiverse pages failed to load). Many presale figures come from fan trackers or aggregators (ComicBasics, FictionHorizon) whose numbers conflict and are often undated or rewritten.
- **Brand New Day's comparable figures** are uncertain: its final presales ($72M or $150M?) and its preview-to-weekend multiplier. Without them, presale comparisons with it are fuzzy.
- **No major-trade long-range domestic tracking** found for November. The latest professional range ($260–300M) comes from a single firm (Global Box Office).
- **Size of the large-screen footprint:** the number of Infinity Vision and other premium screens, and how the overlap with Dune is split.
- **Ticket prices:** average 2026 prices and how much premium pricing raises the per-ticket gross relative to Brand New Day's summer audience.
- **Dates and runtime:** the final runtime and whether the Dec 18 date is firm (no confirmed change found).
