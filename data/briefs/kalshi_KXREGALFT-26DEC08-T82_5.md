# Research Brief: Will Carbon Arc's Regal foot-traffic index for November 2026 be above 82.5?

**Note on method:** Every direct page fetch was refused by the network proxy. All figures below come from search-result snippets, so check the key numbers against primary pages if you can.

---

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What resolves YES:** Carbon Arc's full-month, year-over-year foot-traffic index for Regal for November 2026 must be strictly above 82.5. The index is built from mobile location data. A value of 100 means November 2026 visits equal November 2025 visits. **82.5 means visits fell 17.5%.** So YES requires Regal's November visits to be down by less than about 17.5% from last year, or to be up.
- **Timing and decider:** Kalshi uses the finalized Carbon Arc value recorded at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 7, 2026. The market closes December 8 at 04:59 UTC, which is the same moment.
- **Background:** Kalshi launched these KPI contracts, which settle on Carbon Arc data, on October 23, 2026 (Markets Media / Kalshi News, Oct 2026). The series is new, so there is little settled history for how Carbon Arc's Regal index behaves.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - **Exactly 82.5 resolves NO**, because the rule says "above."
  - Carbon Arc's method is not public: how it defines the panel, whether it adjusts for a shrinking mobile panel, and whether it counts per location or across the whole chain. Theater closures could lower a chain-wide count.
  - Revisions between preliminary and "finalized" values are possible. Only the December 7 snapshot counts.
  - Calendar effects matter (see Section 4).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

**A. U.S. domestic box office, November versus the prior November.** This is the closest public proxy for visits.
- November is driven by a few tentpole releases, and year-over-year swings of ±20–50% are common. I'm recalling these from memory; they are not verified: November 2023 was weak, November 2024 was very strong (Wicked, Moana 2, Gladiator II and a record Thanksgiving), and November 2025 was strong but likely somewhat below 2024.
- In roughly half of recent Novembers, the year-over-year box-office change was larger than ±17.5%. The direction depended almost entirely on the tentpole slate. My rough count, which needs checking, is that November 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 each moved between about 10% and 50% in absolute terms.

**B. Mobile-panel foot traffic versus box office.** Panel data can drift away from ticket sales.
- Kalibrate found Regal visits down 12.2% year over year (comparing Q2–Q3 2024 with Q2–Q3 2025), with major chains down about 15% on average. Box office over that stretch was roughly flat to slightly up (Kalibrate, 2026).
- Placer.ai found AMC, Cinemark and Regal visits up 11.7–17.5% for January–August 2026. Domestic box office was up about 20% year to date over a similar window (Placer.ai, Sept 24, 2026; Deadline, Oct 2026).
- **Takeaway:** mobile-panel visit indices have recently run about 3–15 points below box-office growth. That gap would make a weak month look weaker still.

**C. Single months that face a mega-tentpole comparison month.** When the prior-year month had two $150M+ openers and the current month has one $80–130M opener, theater attendance has typically fallen 10–30% that month. This is a heuristic, not a counted base rate.

## 3. Current state of the world (most recent first)

- **Nov 25, 2026 (scheduled):** Disney Animation's *Hexed* and Universal's *Focker-In-Law* open for Thanksgiving.
  - BoxOfficePro long-range forecasts: *Hexed* $35–45M (3-day) / $50–70M (5-day); *Focker-In-Law* $30–40M (3-day) / $45–65M (5-day). A Deadline-cited report has each at $40–50M over five days (BoxOfficePro Long Range Forecast; Comicbasics, Nov 2026).
  - Thanksgiving is Nov 26 and the holiday weekend runs Nov 27–29, so it falls fully inside November 2026, as it did in 2025 (Nov 27–30).
- **Nov 25, 2026:** *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth* (Netflix) opens in theaters. **Regal is reportedly skipping it** and gave its IMAX screens to *Hunger Games* instead (search-result summary, Pixeltwelve / Regal news, Nov 2026). Whatever traffic that film draws goes to rival chains.
- **Nov 20, 2026 (scheduled):** *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opens. This is the month's only major tentpole, and it is on Regal IMAX.
  - Tracking has risen from $55–65M (BoxOfficePro, about late October) to "$80M+" (Deadline-cited, mid-November).
  - Independent trackers put it at $100–140M, and one forum tracker says $130M+. Presales are reportedly beating *Wicked* and *Michael* (Comicbasics; Todd Thatcher, Nov 12, 2026).
- **Nov 13–15, 2026 weekend:** a "sleepy" frame.
  - *Ebenezer* (Paramount) opened at about $19.0M from 3,535 theaters. *The Cat in the Hat* made $11.4M in its second weekend (down 45%, $38.2M after 10 days). *Godzilla Minus Zero* made about $8.5M (Gold Derby; Rotten Tomatoes, Nov 2026).
  - The comparable 2025 weekend (Nov 14–16) was led by *Now You See Me: Now You Don't* at $21.3M, with *The Running Man* and *Predator: Badlands* holding over. The total for that weekend was probably similar to, or slightly above, 2026's.
- **Nov 6–8, 2026 weekend:** *Godzilla Minus Zero* opened at $30.6–31.6M (a GKIDS record; A CinemaScore; $12.7M of it from IMAX). *The Cat in the Hat* opened at $20.5M, *Drummer Boy* took $9.3M, and six new films entered the top 10 (Rotten Tomatoes; Gold Derby, Nov 2026).
  - The comparable 2025 weekend had *Predator: Badlands* opening at about $40M (from memory). This weekend is probably roughly flat to slightly up year over year.
- **Oct 30–Nov 1, 2026 weekend:** a documentary, *You Can See Everything*, reached #1 with about $11.4M, a sign of a very soft Halloween frame. Only Nov 1 falls in the month.
- **Regal promotions in November 2026:** a "Deep Cuts" repertory series, $1/free-admission Veterans Day promotions on Nov 11, and Friday the 13th horror screenings (Webull / Celluloid Junkie, Nov 2026). These are minor traffic effects.
- **Year to date (as of ~Nov 15, 2026):** domestic box office is about $8.67B, already above Box Office Mojo's full-year 2025 total of about $8.66B and running about 20% ahead year over year (Box Office Mojo; Deadline, Oct 2026). Strong months included *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* in August (Placer.ai, Sept 24, 2026).
- **Regal footprint:** 402 theaters / 5,448 screens in 41 states as of June 30, 2026. Some closures happened in 2026, for example Regal Cross Keys in New Jersey, which closed Jan 30, 2026 (Regal Global Entertainment / PhillyVoice). I found no evidence of a big wave of closures.
- **The prior-year comparison (November 2025) was strong:**
  - *Wicked: For Good* opened Nov 21, 2025 at about $147M (from memory) and made $207.5M in its first 7 days.
  - *Zootopia 2* opened Nov 26 with the second-biggest Thanksgiving opening ever, roughly $156M over five days (from memory).
  - By Nov 30, 2025, *Wicked: For Good* had $269–270M and *Zootopia 2* had about $159M (The Numbers; Rotten Tomatoes, Nov–Dec 2025).
  - The last ten days of November 2025 were exceptionally strong.

Two things likely push attendance lower than dollars:
- 2025's mix leaned toward family films (*Zootopia 2*), where average tickets are cheaper, so more visits per dollar.
- 2026's *Hunger Games* is IMAX-heavy, which means fewer visits per dollar.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (index > 82.5, i.e., decline under 17.5%):**
- *Hunger Games* overperforms ($120M+ opening, strong second weekend) and *Hexed* behaves like a typical Disney Thanksgiving film ($60M+ over five days). The final ten days would then come within about 15–20% of 2025.
- The first half of November is flat or up (*Godzilla*, *Cat in the Hat*) and cushions the late-month gap.
- Regal outperforms the market: it has the *Hunger Games* IMAX screens, and its press releases have claimed market-share gains in 2026.
- Carbon Arc's panel shows less downward drift than Kalibrate's data did in 2025, or the index is per store.

**Paths to NO (index ≤ 82.5):**
- *Hunger Games* lands near the trade's $80M figure, and *Hexed* and *Focker-In-Law* sit at the low end ($35M and $30M three-day). The Thanksgiving gap against *Zootopia 2* + *Wicked* holdovers would then be 30–40%.
- Panel-based visit indices run several points below box office, as Kalibrate's 2025 Regal data (−12.2%) suggests.
- Calendar effect: November 2026 has 4 Saturdays and 5 Mondays, while November 2025 had 5 Saturdays and 5 Sundays. Losing one Saturday could cost about 1–3% of monthly visits.
- Regal skips *Cliff Booth*, and any shrinkage in its theater count lowers a chain-level index.
- The soft Nov 13–15 frame is a sign of weak underlying demand outside tentpoles.

## 5. Signposts (before December 7)

1. **Hunger Games opening weekend (actuals Nov 22–23):** this is the biggest single swing factor. ≥$120M points toward YES; ≤$85M points strongly toward NO. Watch the second-weekend hold over Thanksgiving (Nov 27–29) too.
2. **Thanksgiving 5-day total (Nov 25–29) versus 2025's record-level frame:** a gap under about 25% favors YES; over 35% favors NO.
3. **Weekly charts:** the Nov 20–22 weekend total against 2025's Nov 21–23 *Wicked* weekend, and the Nov 27–29 total against Nov 28–30, 2025.
4. **Any Carbon Arc or Kalshi published value for Regal for October 2026, or other recent months, if released.** This would show how Carbon Arc's index tracks box office, including any downward panel drift. It is very valuable for calibration.
5. **Regal statements:** "biggest weekend" or market-share press releases around Thanksgiving point toward YES.
6. **Placer.ai or other foot-traffic reports for November theater visits:** these would directly show the gap between panel data and box office.

## 6. Uncertainties

- **No historical Carbon Arc Regal index values were found.** I don't know how its series compares with box office, whether it has a downward bias from panel attrition, or how much it fluctuates month to month. This is the largest gap.
- **Exact 2025 figures:** I couldn't confirm the November 2025 monthly box-office total, the Thanksgiving 2025 five-day total, or Regal's November 2025 visits. The 2025 numbers above are partly from memory.
- **Regal's theater count:** the November 2025 versus November 2026 count is unknown beyond a June 2026 snapshot (402 theaters).
- **Late-November films:** actual results for *Hunger Games*, *Hexed* and *Focker-In-Law* are still unknown, and tracking ranges are wide ($55M to $140M for *Hunger Games*).
- **Index construction:** I couldn't establish whether the index uses unique visitors or total visits, or how dwell-time filters treat mall-based theaters. Mall-adjacent Regal locations could pick up holiday-shopping noise.
- **Regal's market share:** how Regal's share in November 2026 compares with November 2025 is not established beyond company PR claims.
