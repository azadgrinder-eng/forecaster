# Research brief: Will Venezuela place in the Top 5 at Miss Universe 2026?

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Venezuela's delegate, Clara Vegas Goetz (Miss Universe Venezuela 2025, crowned 4 Dec 2025), finishes in the Top 5 of the 75th Miss Universe. A win or any of the four runner-up spots counts.
- **When:** The final is scheduled for **24 Nov 2026** at the Coliseo José Miguel Agrelot in San Juan, Puerto Rico. The national costume show is 21 Nov and the preliminary competition is 22 Nov (El Diario, 9 Nov 2026). The question closes 2 Dec 2026, which leaves about 8 days of slack.
- **Who decides:** A panel of human judges for the Narcissist Forecasting Contest (braff.co), not Manifold's usual rules. Their reading could differ from a literal one.
- **Edge cases:**
  - **Format change.** The 2026 page lists 30 placements. In 2024 and 2025 the cuts were Top 30 → Top 12 → Top 5. I could not confirm that the 2026 broadcast keeps a Top 5 round. If it runs, say, Top 12 → Top 3, the judges would have to decide, probably by counting the winner and the named runners-up.
  - **Delay or withdrawal.** If the final slips past 2 Dec, or Vegas withdraws or is disqualified, the likely result is NO or a judges' ruling.
  - **Legal or organizational disruption at MUO.** President Raúl Rocha is under a Mexican investigation; a judge suspended his arrest warrant in Jan 2026. Puerto Rico's funding dispute was settled by April 2026. These are tail risks only.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES**
- Venezuela's strong brand and coaching program, recently 3 Top 5s in 4 years.
- Vegas sits in the consensus top 4–5. Her profile (actress, German-Venezuelan, family pageant legacy, quake relief work) fits MUO's current emphasis on "purpose."
- A Caribbean host with a large Venezuelan diaspora.
- She very likely makes the Top 30 and Top 12. The question is the Top 12 → Top 5 cut, which in recent years has leaned on Q&A or speech performance and on judges' preferences.

**Paths to NO**
- The Top 5 is crowded with other favorites: Puerto Rico as host, France, Philippines, Mexico (which has done well under Rocha's MUO), Colombia and Thailand.
- MUO's judging is opaque. The 2025 edition saw judge resignations and claims of a rigged pre-selection, which adds noise.
- A weak Q&A performance, given the March 2026 speech backlash.
- Possible "Latin quota" crowding if Puerto Rico, Mexico and Colombia all advance.
- Small tail risks: withdrawal, a format change, or postponement.

## 5. Signposts
- **Preliminary night (22 Nov) and costume show (21 Nov):** strong coverage of Vegas's evening gown and swimsuit performance, and her place in final hot picks (Missosology, Pageant Circle, Sash Factor, Angelopedia) published around 22–23 Nov. Staying top 5 in final picks → up; dropping to top 10–15 → down.
- **Closed-door interview reports (around 20–22 Nov):** pageant insiders' reports on how her interview went.
- **Live on 24 Nov:** the Top 30 and Top 12 calls are near-certain conditions. Missing the Top 12 → NO.
- **MUO announcements:** any format change, especially the number of finalists, or legal disruption tied to Rocha.
- **Her health:** any reports of illness or injury.
