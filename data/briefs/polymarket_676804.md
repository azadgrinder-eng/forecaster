# Research brief: Will OpenAI launch a new consumer hardware product by Dec 31, 2026?

*Prepared 2026-11-18. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network proxy, so every fact below comes from search-result summaries of the cited articles. I could not read any article in full. Treat details from small or aggregator sites with caution.*

## 1. Resolution mechanics

- **What counts as YES:** OpenAI must both publicly announce and launch a new consumer hardware product by **Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET**. That leaves about 43 days from today.
- **Who decides:** The rules name "official information from OpenAI" as the source.
- **What qualifies:** A physical device meant for individual consumers. Developer tools, AI chips, and enterprise-only servers are excluded. Rebrands, updates, or new versions of an earlier device are excluded.

**Ambiguities that matter:**
- **Announce vs. launch.** The rules require "announces *and* launches." Showing a device without shipping it probably does not count. An unveiling in December with pre-orders open and shipping in 2027 is a grey area. A strict reading needs the product to be available or shipping. This matters because the only firm official date says customer shipping starts no earlier than end of February 2027. The open question has been whether OpenAI would show the device in 2026 anyway.
- **The Codex Micro.** OpenAI already sold a physical device in 2026: the $230 Codex Micro macro pad, made with Work Louder, launched around July 1, 2026. Tom's Hardware called it "OpenAI's first hardware device." It is a control pad for AI coding agents, so it most likely falls under the "developer tools" exclusion. It is also co-branded and limited-edition. The market still being open suggests it was not counted, or that the market was created after July. Either way, it probably gives no path to YES. A similar co-branded accessory in December aimed at general ChatGPT users would be a real edge case.
- **What "new" means.** If OpenAI's first flagship device ships, it is clearly new. Tie-in merchandise or a partner-made device "for ChatGPT" (for example, a third-party speaker with ChatGPT built in) is unclear. The "official information from OpenAI" wording points toward devices that OpenAI itself announces as its own.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates

1. **Official sworn timelines vs. actual ship dates.** In February 2026, OpenAI vice president Peter Welinder said in a sworn court declaration that the first device would not reach customers before **end of February 2027** (9to5Mac, 2026-02-10; MacRumors/Wired, 2026-02-10). Hardware dates in sworn filings and supply-chain reports almost always slip later, not earlier. Cases I know of where a first-generation consumer device shipped *earlier* than its stated window are close to 0 out of many (Vision Pro, Humane AI Pin, Rabbit R1, Meta's Orion glasses, Google Glass consumer). OpenAI's own device has already slipped once, from "late 2026" to 2027. Base rate for shipping ahead of a sworn date: very low, under 5%.
2. **New entrants' first devices, from reveal to shipping.**
   - Humane AI Pin: revealed Nov 2023, shipped Apr 2024 (about 5 months).
   - Rabbit R1: revealed Jan 2024, shipped Apr 2024 (about 3 months).
   - Apple Vision Pro: revealed Jun 2023, shipped Feb 2024 (about 8 months).
   - Meta Orion: shown in 2024 as a prototype only.
   
   Reveal and shipping are usually months apart. Even if OpenAI unveiled the device in December 2026, shipping by Dec 31 would break this pattern and contradict the February 2027 filing.
3. **Company guidance on reveal timing.** In January 2026, Chris Lehane said OpenAI was "on track" to *unveil* in H2 2026 (9to5Mac, 2026-01-19; Silicon Republic, Jan 2026). By mid-November, about 88% of H2 has passed with no unveiling, including DevDay on Sept 29. OpenAI has made December announcements before (the "12 Days of OpenAI" in December 2024), so a December reveal is possible. But a reveal alone probably does not resolve YES.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to YES (all look unlikely):**
1. **Surprise unveiling plus purchase in December.** OpenAI shows the speaker or another device and opens orders, or ships a small "founders" batch, before Dec 31. That would contradict the sworn February 2027 floor. A reveal with pre-orders is more plausible, but then it depends on whether pre-orders count as a launch.
2. **A smaller consumer accessory.** OpenAI releases another co-branded, limited-edition device (like the Codex Micro) aimed at general ChatGPT users rather than developers, perhaps as holiday merchandise. There is no reporting of this. The Codex Micro shows OpenAI will do quick partner hardware.
3. **Generous resolution.** The resolver counts an unveiling with "coming early 2027" as "announces and launches," or counts the Codex Micro retroactively. I consider both unlikely under the wording.

**Paths to NO (the default):**
- The flagship device follows the sworn timeline, shipping in late February 2027 or later.
- Any reveal slips to CES season or early 2027.
- The Apple litigation, a possible injunction, and supply-chain problems add further delay risk.
- With 43 days left, nothing scheduled, and nothing at DevDay, the status quo strongly favors NO.

## 5. Signposts

These would raise the probability a lot:
- An official OpenAI teaser, event invite, or "12 Days" style announcement that mentions hardware.
- FCC equipment-authorization filings for an OpenAI device. These usually appear weeks before sale.
- Retail listings or pre-order pages on openai.com.
- Reports from The Information, Bloomberg, or Wired of a December ship date.

These would cut it further:
- Judge Davila granting a broad preliminary injunction that touches the hardware team.
- New reporting of slips into mid-2027.
- December passing with only software announcements.

Points that need interpretation:
- An unveiling with pre-orders but shipping in 2027 would raise the probability only as far as it makes resolution ambiguous.
- A new Codex-branded developer peripheral would probably not qualify.
