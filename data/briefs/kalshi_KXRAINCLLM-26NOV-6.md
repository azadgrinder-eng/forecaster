# Research Brief: Will College Station (CLL) get more than 6.00" of rain in November 2026?

As of Monday, Nov 23, 2026, College Station has had about 1.1–1.8" of rain this month, and the forecast for the rest of the week is cold and mostly dry. To resolve YES, Easterwood Field would need roughly 4.2–4.9" more in the last 7–8 days of November. That is well above a normal *whole* November there (about 3.3–3.4").

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts for YES:** total November 2026 precipitation at the official College Station station has to be **more than 6.00"**. Exactly 6.00" resolves NO. The station is "CLICLL", the NWS climate report for Easterwood Field (KCLL / WBAN 03904).
- **Source of truth:** the NWS daily climate report (CLI) for CLL. For the monthly total, the CLI for Nov 30, or the monthly CLM report issued in early December, gives the month-to-date figure. AccuWeather and similar sites are only guidance. An amended clause covers "materially erroneous data", so obvious sensor or reporting errors could be corrected.
- **Window:** Nov 1–30, 2026, local standard time. The market closes Dec 1, 04:59 UTC, which is 10:59 pm CST on Nov 30. Rain after midnight on Nov 30 does not count.
- **Ambiguities:**
  - Gridded or neighborhood totals (Precip.ai, the TAMU campus station, CoCoRaHS) can differ from the airport gauge by a few tenths of an inch.
  - If a CLI report is missing, the settlement source is unclear.
  - Trace amounts count as zero.

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
1. **Unconditional: Novembers at CLL above 6".** The 1991–2020 November normal is about 3.3" (ExtremeWeatherWatch, citing NWS normals). WeatherSpark gives 3.4", and one tracker lists 3.82". Monthly rainfall in southeast Texas is heavily skewed: most Novembers are dry or near normal, and a few are very wet. I couldn't get the NOWData record for CLL. A rough estimate is that **about 8–12% of Novembers** go above 6" (roughly 2× normal). Treat this as approximate and unverified. Recent example: November 2025 had only 1.32" (raingrid.app).
2. **Conditional (the one that matters): 4.2"+ at a single station in the last 7–8 days of November.** In College Station, a 7-day total above 4" in late fall usually needs a major Gulf low, a stalled front, or tropical moisture. Even with no forecast information, a given late-November week does that only about 1–3% of the time (my estimate from climatology). When the 7-day forecast is cold and dry, the chance is much lower.
3. **Drought persistence.** The Brazos Valley has been in moderate to extreme drought through fall 2026 (KBTX, Nov 5 and Nov 12, 2026). A drought pattern doesn't rule out heavy rain, but in this autumn the rain events have mostly been light, apart from Nov 15.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
- **Path to YES (very narrow):** a slow-moving upper low over Mexico and South Texas pulls deep Gulf moisture over the cold air after Thanksgiving. That would mean an overrunning or coastal-low setup giving 4–5"+ of widespread heavy rain at Easterwood, Nov 27–30. This would need much stronger moisture than any current forecast shows. Late-November events like this have happened in southeast Texas (for example, cold-season coastal lows), but they are rare.
- **Path to NO (dominant):** the cold, dry continental air mass holds through Thanksgiving, and any Black Friday or weekend system brings a few tenths to about 1" at most. November ends around 1.5–3", below the normal of about 3.3".

## 5. Signposts
- **Toward YES (each would be a big change):**
  - NWS Houston/Galveston (HGX) forecasts or Weather Prediction Center (WPC) 7-day QPF showing **3"+** for Brazos County, Nov 27–30.
  - A WPC excessive-rainfall risk, or a flood watch, for the Brazos Valley.
  - KBTX "First Alert" heavy-rain messaging for the weekend.
- **Toward NO:**
  - The Black Friday system tracks south or toward the coast, or weakens.
  - The 7-day QPF stays under 1".
  - The CLI reports for Nov 22–25 show only trace or light amounts.
- **Check first:** the exact CLL month-to-date figure in the latest CLI. If it is lower than 1.8", even more rain is needed.

## 6. Uncertainties
- I couldn't access the official CLI or CLM reports or the NWS point forecast (fetches were blocked). The CLL month-to-date figure above is pieced together from a KBTX gauge report (Nov 15) and gridded estimates, not the official number.
- I couldn't confirm the Easterwood amounts for Nov 3, Nov 8 and Nov 22.
- I couldn't confirm the size or track of the post-Thanksgiving system (Nov 27–30). Some search results about a "stormy Thanksgiving week" seem to be from 2025, for example Fox 26's "Sunday, Nov. 23" (Nov 23, 2026 is a Monday), so I set them aside.
- I couldn't verify the historical count of Novembers above 6" at CLL; the 8–12% figure is my estimate.
