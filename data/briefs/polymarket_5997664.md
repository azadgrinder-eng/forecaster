## Research brief: will tkzin play for LOUD in 2027?

**Caveat on sources:** I could not open any article directly because the proxy refused every page fetch. All facts below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Dates marked "~" are inferred from how old the search engine said each article was, counted back from Nov 25, 2026.

### 1. Resolution mechanics

- **This is a "next team" market, and LOUD is the default.** LOUD wins if tkzin (Enzo Zimiani, an 18-year-old Brazilian VALORANT duelist) does not officially join a new team by Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 PM ET.
  - A new permanent contract with LOUD, or an extension of the loan, does not count as joining a new team. Both keep the market on LOUD.
- **Paths that resolve away from LOUD:**
  - **Another team.** An official signing by any other team (for example MIBR, or KRÜ's main roster) resolves to that team, or to "Other" if the team isn't listed. The first official announcement resolves the market immediately.
  - **Return to KRÜ.** The rules say a "return to the team that owns his contract at the end of the loan" counts as joining that team.
  - **"Other" clause.** The market resolves to "Other" if he is released, retires, or is "not under contract with any professional team" by Dec 31.
- **Ambiguities:**
  - **Who owns his contract is disputed.** Riot reportedly treats tkzin as a free agent. KRÜ says he is under contract until the end of 2027. If KRÜ formally announces his "return", it is unclear whether that counts as an official joining.
  - **The "Other" clause could bite.** Riot considers him a free agent and LOUD posted a farewell on Nov 5. If LOUD never formally announces a new deal by Dec 31, a strict reader could say he is "not under contract with any team" and resolve "Other". On the other hand, "does not officially join a new team → LOUD" and media consensus (he is playing for LOUD in December) point to LOUD. This is the biggest technical risk to a LOUD resolution.
  - **The farewell post.** LOUD's Nov 5 post could be read as the loan ending, and so as an implied return to KRÜ. But the rules require an official joining, and no KRÜ announcement has been found.
- **Resolution sources:** official announcements from LOUD or the acquiring team; a consensus of credible media can also be used.

### 2. Reference classes and base rates

These are my own estimates, not counted datasets.

1. **Sheep Esports and THESPIKE "sources" reports of a verbal agreement in VCT rostermania.** In recent offseasons (2023–2026), I estimate roughly 85–95% of such Sheep/THESPIKE scoops turned into official signings. Failures usually come from buyout or third-party problems, which is exactly the risk here.
2. **Riot free-agency rulings against an organization's contract claim.** Riot controls player registration in the partnered VCT league, so its view usually decides whether a player is eligible. Organizations that disagree have generally turned to compensation claims, not to blocking the move. I know of no VCT case where Riot reversed a free-agent finding within weeks.
3. **Loanees who win a title.** Teams that win a world title on a loan usually keep the loanee if they can, especially when the core is leaving. LOUD lost erde and luk xo to Leviatán and Darker to KRÜ (VCTatualizado on X, ~Nov 2026). The team needs continuity, so its motivation to keep tkzin is very high.

### 4. Drivers and scenarios

**Paths to LOUD (the default):**

- **A.** LOUD officially announces a new contract or extension, either after the Riot free-agent ruling or after a settlement or payment to KRÜ.
- **B.** No official announcement by Dec 31, but tkzin plays for LOUD at Home Ground and no other team announces him. This still resolves LOUD unless the "not under contract" clause is applied strictly.

What these depend on: Riot's ruling holding, the player's stated preference for LOUD, and LOUD's motivation as reigning champion after losing three players.

**Paths away from LOUD:**

- **C (KRÜ).** KRÜ's legal challenge succeeds, Riot reverses its ruling, and KRÜ refuses to sell, so KRÜ officially announces him for its main roster. KRÜ did say in July it would test its Spark players for the main roster.
- **D (another team).** Contract talks with LOUD collapse over salary or KRÜ's compensation claim, and MIBR or another team signs him.
- **E ("Other").** The resolver decides he is not under contract on Dec 31 because the paperwork is delayed or frozen by the dispute.

### 5. Signposts

- **Strongly toward LOUD:**
  - An official LOUD announcement or Riot contract-database entry.
  - tkzin appearing in LOUD's Home Ground lineup on Dec 10–13.
  - Reports of a LOUD–KRÜ compensation settlement.
- **Toward KRÜ:**
  - Riot reversing its free-agent ruling.
  - KRÜ obtaining an injunction or filing suit.
  - KRÜ or Red Bull listing tkzin under KRÜ.
  - LOUD signing a different duelist. VCT Centel had earlier linked aspas to LOUD.
- **Toward "Other":**
  - Home Ground passing with tkzin absent or playing as a stand-in.
  - Prolonged silence from LOUD through late December while the dispute continues.

### 6. Uncertainties

- Whether Riot's free-agent view is final or still under review. No formal ruling was found.
- Whether a KRÜ lawsuit was filed. I found none as of mid-November.
- Whether LOUD has signed the contract. Reports from Nov 6 to Nov 24 all say "verbal" or "completed" without official confirmation.
- The exact registration deadline for the 2027 Americas Kickoff.
- How the resolver would treat the "not under contract" clause if he plays for LOUD without an announced contract.
- Whether Red Bull's "back to KRÜ Spark" line is just out of date.
