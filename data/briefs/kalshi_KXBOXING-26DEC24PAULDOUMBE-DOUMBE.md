# Research Brief: Will Cédric Doumbé beat Jake Paul? (Kalshi, closes Jan 7, 2027)

## 1. Resolution mechanics
- **What counts as YES:** Doumbé has to win the Jake Paul vs Cédric Doumbé pro boxing match first scheduled for **Thursday, Dec 24, 2026** at Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai. Any official win counts: KO, TKO, decision or disqualification.
- **What counts as NO:** A Paul win, presumably. A draw or no-contest should also mean NO, because Doumbé would not have "won." The rules don't spell this out.
- **Format:** Eight 3-minute rounds at cruiserweight, contracted at 200 lb (90.7 kg), with 10-oz gloves. Most Valuable Promotions (MVP), Paul's company, calls it "professionally sanctioned." So the official decision should come from the commission or sanctioning body, with Kalshi resolving on that result (Jake Paul vs Cédric Doumbé: December 24 Date, Fight Rules And Dubai Tickets, boxingschedule.co, Nov 2026; Yahoo Sports, Nov 2026).
- **Postponement:** If the fight is postponed, the market stays open and closes within two weeks of the rescheduled fight.
  - The listed close date is Jan 7, 2027. A later reschedule would presumably push resolution out.
- **Edge cases and ambiguities:**
  - The rules say nothing about outright cancellation or an opponent swap. A Paul vs someone-else fight would not satisfy "Doumbé wins," so the likely outcome is NO or a void. This is unclear.
  - Hybrid rules: in Dec 2024 Doumbé floated a mixed-rules fight, but the current contract is pure boxing (Bloody Elbow, Dec 14, 2024).
  - Purse bet: a "winner-takes-all" side bet was reportedly agreed at the Nov 10 press conference. Doumbé later backed out, saying his Muslim faith forbids gambling. This has no bearing on resolution (Yardbarker/Woodley piece, Nov 2026; ClutchPoints, ~Nov 13, 2026).

## 2. Reference classes and base rates
**A. Jake Paul against MMA-background opponents: 6–0.**
- Wins over Ben Askren (TKO1, Apr 2021), Tyron Woodley (SD, Aug 2021), Woodley again (KO6, Dec 2021), Anderson Silva (UD, Oct 2022), Nate Diaz (UD, Aug 2023) and Mike Perry (TKO6, Jul 2024).
- Doumbé differs from all of them: he is an elite stand-up striker, not a wrestler or grappler. The closest analogues are Silva and Perry, and both lost.
- Paul's only losses (12–2, 7 KOs) came against real boxers: Tommy Fury (SD, Feb 2023) and Anthony Joshua (KO6, Dec 19, 2025).
- I compiled this from the record pages above and the Wikipedia fight pages that came up in searches.
- **Base rate for the "MMA/kickboxer beats Paul" path: 0 of 6.**

**B. High-profile MMA or kickboxing stars making their pro-boxing debut, or nearly so, against an established boxer: about 1 win in 10.**
- Losses: McGregor to Mayweather (2017), Ngannou to Fury (2023, though he scored a knockdown) and Joshua (2024), plus the six Paul fights above.
- The one clear upset was Anderson Silva beating Julio César Chávez Jr. (SD, 2021). Silva had real boxing experience, and Chávez Jr. was past his prime.
- This list is from my own knowledge, not sourced in this session.
- **Rough base rate: 10–15% for the crossover fighter.** Ngannou against Fury shows how often elite strikers are competitive even when they lose.

**C. Doumbé's own finishing record, and how hard he is to stop.**
- Kickboxing: about 75–7–1 with 45 KOs, and he avenged all three of his Glory losses. He held the Glory welterweight title in 2016 (beating Holzken) and again from 2019 to 2021 (Wikipedia; Tapology; Glory profile).
- MMA: 6–1. The one loss was a stoppage from a foot injury (a splinter) against Chamsoudinov at PFL Europe 1, Mar 7, 2024. His 2023 knockout of Jordan Zébo took 9 seconds (Tapology; UFCalendar).
- This shows real one-punch power. It is a striking pedigree, though, not a boxing one.

## 4. Drivers and scenarios
**Paths to YES (Doumbé wins):**
1. **Early knockout from a counter.** Doumbé's 45 KOs in kickboxing and his 9-second MMA knockout show elite timing and power. Paul's jaw has been rebuilt twice and was broken less than a year before the fight, which may lower his chin durability or his willingness to take shots. Joshua showed that Paul can be stopped. This is the main path, most likely in rounds 1–4.
2. **Paul's ring rust or psychological hesitancy** after a brutal KO loss and a year out, combined with Doumbé's better footwork and range control from kickboxing.
3. **A cut, an injury, or a Paul DQ.** Unlikely.
4. **A decision win.** Unlikely in an MVP-promoted fight in a market where Paul is the house fighter. Paul won close decisions over Woodley and Silva.

**Paths to NO:**
1. **Size and boxing skill.** Paul has roughly a 30 lb natural-weight edge and 14 pro fights with a full boxing camp. Doumbé has about 10 years away from boxing, rules that remove his kicks, and no fights since May 2024. Paul wins by mid-to-late-round TKO or by decision, which is the historical pattern against crossover fighters.
2. **Doumbé's limitations.** The chronic wrist injury limits his left-hand punching or his training volume. At 34, after a long layoff, his conditioning fades over 8 rounds.
3. **A draw or no-contest.** Low probability, but it resolves NO.
4. **Cancellation or an opponent swap.** Paul's events have changed often: Fury fell through twice in 2021–22, Tyson was postponed from July to November 2024, and Davis was cancelled in 2025. Doumbé is now publicly reporting an injury, and there is no broadcaster yet. An outright cancellation probably resolves NO or void. A postponement just delays resolution.
